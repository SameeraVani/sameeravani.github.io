---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಕಲ್ಪಸಾಧನ ಸಂಧಿ/ಶ್ರೀ ಅಪರೋಕ್ಷ ತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಸಂಧಿ/Kalpa sadhana sandhi/Aparoksha Taratamya sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè/<br/>parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

ekaviṃśati mata pravartaka kāku māygal̤a kuhaka yukti nirākarisi<br/>sarvottamanu hariyèṃdu sthāpisida<br/>śrī kal̤atrana sadana dvijapa pināki sannuta mahima<br/>parama kṛpākaṭākṣadi noḍu madhvācārya guruvarya||1||

veda mòdalāgippa amala mokṣa adhikārigal̤u āda jīvara<br/>sādhanagal̤a aparokṣa naṃtara liṃga bhaṃgavanu<br/>sādhugal̤u cittaipadu ènnaparādhagal̤a noḍadalè<br/>cakra gadādharanu pel̤isida tèradaṃdadali pel̤uvènu||2||

tṛṇa krimi dvija paśu narottama janapa naragaṃdharva gaṇaru<br/>ivarèniparu aṃśa vihīna karma suyogigal̤èṃdu<br/>tanu pratīkadi biṃbana upāsanavagaiyuta iṃdriyaja karma<br/>anavarata harigè arpisuta nirmamaruyènisuvaru||3||

el̤uvidha jīva gaṇa bahal̤a surāl̤i saṃkhyā nemavul̤l̤adu<br/>tāl̤i naradehavanu brāhmaṇara kuladòl̤udbhavisi<br/>sthūla karmava tòrèdu gurugal̤u pel̤ida arthava til̤idu<br/>tattatkāla dharma samarpisuva avaru karma yogigal̤u||4||

hīna karmagal̤iṃda bahuvidha yoniyali saṃcarisi prāṃtakè<br/>mānuṣatvavanaidi sarvottamanu hariyèṃba<br/>jñāna bhaktigal̤iṃda vedokta anusāra sahasrajanma<br/>anyūna karmava māḍi harigarpisida naṃtaradi||5||

hattu janmagal̤ali hari sarvottamanu surāsura gaṇārcita<br/>citra karma viśoka anaṃtānaṃta rūpātma<br/>satya satsaṃkalpa jagadotpatti sthitilaya kāraṇa<br/>jarāmṛtyu varjitanèṃdu upāsanègaida taruvāya||6||

mūru janmagal̤alli dehāgāra paśu dhana patni mitra<br/>kumāra mātā pitṛgal̤alli iha snehagiṃta adhika<br/>māramaṇanali biḍadè māḍuva sūrigal̤u ī ukta janmava mīri<br/>paramātmana svadehadi noḍi sukhisuvaru||7||

deva gāyaka ajāna cirapitṛ devarèllaru<br/>jñāna yogigal̤u āva kālaku puṣkara śanaiścara uṣā svāhā devi<br/>budhasanakādigal̤u meghāval̤i parjanya sāṃśaru<br/>ī ubhaya gaṇadòl̤agivaru vijñāna yogigal̤u||8||

bharata khaṃḍadi nūru janmava dharisi niṣkāmaka sukarma ācarisida anaṃtaradi<br/>daśa sahasra janmadali urutara jñānavanu<br/>mūraidu èraḍu daśa dehadali bhaktiya<br/>niravadhikanali māḍi kāṃbaru biṃba rūpavanu||9||<br/>sādhanātpūrvadali ivarigè anādi kāla aparokṣavilla<br/>niṣedha karmagal̤illa naraka prāpti mòdalilla<br/>veda śāstragal̤allippa virodha vākyava pariharisi<br/>madhusūdananè sarvottamottamanu èṃdu tutisuvaru||10||

satyalokādhipana viḍidu śatastha devagaṇa aṃta èllaru<br/>bhakti yogigal̤èṃdu karèsuvaru āva kāladali<br/>bhakti yogyara madhyadali sadbhakti vijñānādi guṇadiṃda uttama<br/>uttama brahma vāyū vāṇi vāgdevi||11||

ṛjugaṇakè bhakti ādi guṇa sahajavu ènisuvavu<br/>kramadi vṛddhi abjaja padavi paryaṃta biṃba upāsanavu adhika<br/>vṛjina varjita èllaròl̤u triguṇaja vikāragal̤illavu èṃdigu<br/>dvijaphaṇipa mṛḍa śakra mòdalāda avaròl̤u irutihavu||12||

sādhanātpūrvadali ī ṛjvādi sātvikaru ènipa suragaṇa<br/>anādi sāmānya aparokṣigal̤èṃdu karèsuvaru<br/>sādhanottara svasva biṃba upādhi rahita ādityanaṃdadi<br/>sādaradi noḍuvaru adhikāra anusāradali||13||

chinna bhaktaru ènisutiharu suparṇa śeṣādi amarararèlla<br/>accinna bhaktaru nālvarèniparu bhāratī prāṇa sònnòḍala vāgdeviyaru<br/>paṇègaṇṇa mòdalāda avaròl̤agè tattanniyāmakarāgi<br/>vyāpāravanu māḍuvaru||14||

hīna satkarmagal̤u èraḍu pavamāna devanu māl̤panu<br/>idakè anumānavilla èṃdu ènuta dṛḍha bhaktiyali bhajipargè<br/>prāṇapati saṃprītanāgi kuyonigal̤a kòḍa<br/>èlla karmagal̤u ānè māḍuvènèṃba manujara narakakè aidisuva||15||

devarṣi pitṛpa nararènisuva aivaròl̤u nèlèsiddu<br/>avara svabhāva karmava māḍi māḍipa òṃdu rūpadali<br/>bhāvi brahmanu kūrma rūpadi ī viriṃci aṃḍavanu bènnili tā vahisi<br/>lokagal̤a pòrèvanu dvitīya rūpadali||16||

guptanāgiddu anila deva dvisapta lokada jīvaròl̤agè<br/>trisapta sāvirada ārunūru śvāsa japagal̤anu<br/>suptisvapnadi jāgratigal̤ali āptanaṃdadi māḍi māḍisi<br/>klupta bhogagal̤īva prāṃtakè tṛtīya rūpadali||17||

śuddha satvātmaka śarīradòl̤idda kālaku liṃgadehavu baddhavāgadu<br/>dagdha paṭadopādi irutihudu<br/>siddha sādhana sarvaròl̤agè anavadyanu ènisuva<br/>garuḍa śeṣa kapardi mòdalāda amararèllaru dāsarènisuvaru||18||

gaṇadòl̤agè tāniddu ṛjuvèṃdu ènisikòṃbanu<br/>kalpa śata sādhanavagaida anaṃtaradi tā kalkiyènisuvanu<br/>dvinavāśītiya prāṃta bhāgadi anila hanumadbhīma rūpadi<br/>danujarèllara sadèdu madhvācāryarènisidanu||19||<br/>viśvavyāpaka harigè tā sādṛśya rūpava dharisi<br/>brahma sarasvatī bhāratigal̤iṃda òḍagūḍi pavamāna<br/>śāśvata subhaktiyali sujñāna svarūpana rūpaguṇagal̤a<br/>anaśvaravèṃdènuta pògal̤uva śrutigal̤òl̤agiddu||20||

kheṭa kukkuṭa jalaṭavèṃba trikoṭi rūpava dharisi<br/>satata niśāṭaranu saṃharisi salahuva sarva satjanara<br/>kaiṭabhāriya purada prathama kavāṭavènisuva<br/>garuḍa śeṣa lalāṭalocana mukhya surarigè āvakāladali||21||

ī ṛjugal̤òl̤agòbba sādhana nūru kalpadi māḍi karèsuva<br/>cārutara maṃgal̤a sunāmadi kalpa kalpadali<br/>sūrigal̤u saṃstutisi vaṃdisè ghoraduritagal̤anu al̤idu popuvu<br/>māramaṇa saṃprītanāguva sarva kāladali||22||

pāhi kalkisutejadāsanè pāhi dharmādharma khaṃḍanè<br/>pāhi varcasvī khaṣaṇa namo sādhu mahīpatiyè<br/>pāhi saddharmajña dharmaja pāhi saṃpūrṇa śuci vaikṛta<br/>pāhi aṃjana sarṣapanè kharpaṭa: śraddhāhva||23||

pāhi saṃdhyāta vijñānanè pāhi maha vijñāna kīrtana<br/>pāhi saṃkīrṇākhya katthana mahābuddhi jayā<br/>pāhi māhattara suvīryanè pāhimāṃ medhāvi jayājaya<br/>pāhimāṃ raṃtimnamanu māṃ pāhi māṃ pāhi||24||

pāhi moda pramoda saṃtasa pāhi ānaṃda saṃtuṣṭanè<br/>pāhimāṃ cārvāṃgacāru subāhucāru pada<br/>pāhi pāhi sulocananè māṃ pāhi sārasvata suvīranè<br/>pāhi prājña kapi alaṃpaṭa pāhi sarvajña||25||

pāhimāṃ sarvajit mitranè pāhi pāpa vināśakanè<br/>māṃ pāhi dharmavineta śārada oja sutapasvī<br/>pāhimāṃ tejasvi namo māṃ pāhi dāna suśīla<br/>namo māṃ pāhi yajña sukarta yajvī yāga vartakanè||26||

pāhi prāṇa trāṇa amarṣi pāhimāṃ upadeṣṭa tāraka<br/>pāhi kāla krīḍana sukartā sukālajña<br/>pāhi kāla susūcakanè māṃ pāhi kali saṃhartakali<br/>māṃ pāhi kāliśāmareta sadārata subalanè||27||

pāhi pāhi saho sadākapi pāhi gamya jñāna daśakula<br/>pāhimāṃ śrotavya namo saṃkīrtitavya namo<br/>pāhimāṃ maṃtavyakavyanè pāhi draṣṭavyanè saravyanè<br/>pāhi gaṃtavya namo kravyanè pāhi smartavya||28||

pāhi sevya subhavya namo māṃ pāhi svargavya namo bhāvyanè<br/>pāhi māṃ jñātavya namo vaktavya gavya namo<br/>pāhi maṃ lātavyavāyuvè pāhi brahma brāhmaṇapriya<br/>pāhi pāhi sarasvatīpate jagadguruvarya||29||

vāmana purāṇadali pel̤ida ī mahātmara parama maṃgal̤a nāmagal̤a<br/>saṃprīti pūrvaka nitya smarisuvavarā<br/>śrī manorama avaru beḍida kāmitārthagal̤ittu<br/>tanna tridhāmadòl̤agè anudinadaliṭṭu ānaṃda paḍisuvanu||30||

ī samīragè nūru janma mahā sukha prārabdha bhoga<br/>prayāsavilladè aididanu lokādhipatyavanu<br/>bhūsurana òppiḍi avaligè viśeṣa saukhyavanitta dātana<br/>dāsavaryanu lokapatiyènisuvudu accaravè||31||

dviśata kalpagal̤alli biḍadè ī pèsariniṃdali karèsidanu<br/>tannòśaga amararòl̤iddu māḍuvanu avara sādhanava<br/>asadupāsanègaiva kalyādi asurapara saṃharisi<br/>tā pòṃbasira padavaididanu guru pavamāna satiyòḍanè||32||

animiṣara nāmadali karèsuva aniladevanu<br/>òṃdu kalpakè vanaja saṃbhavanènisi èṃbhattel̤ūvarè varṣa<br/>guṇatraya varjitana maṃgal̤a guṇa kriyā surūpaṃgal̤a<br/>upāsanavu avyaktādi pṛthvi aṃtaradirutihudu||33||

mahita ṛjugaṇakè òṃde paramotsāha vivarjitavèṃba doṣavu<br/>vihitavè sari idanu pel̤dirè mukta brahmarigè bahudu sāmyavu<br/>jñāna bhakutiyu druhiṇa pada paryaṃta vṛddhiyu<br/>bahirupāsanè uṃṭu anaṃtara biṃba darśanavu||34||

jñānarahita bhayatva pel̤va purāṇa daityara mohakavu<br/>caturānanagè kòḍuvadè mohājñāna bhaya śoka<br/>bhānumaṃḍala calisidaṃdadi kāṇuvudu dṛg doṣadiṃdali<br/>śrīnivāsana prītigosuga tordanalladalè||35||

kamalasaṃbhava sarvaròl̤aguttamanènisuvanu èlla kāladi<br/>vimala bhakti jñāna vairāgyādi guṇadiṃda<br/>samābhyadhika vivarjitana guṇa ramèya mukhadiṃdaritu nityadi<br/>dyumaṇi koṭigal̤aṃtè kāṃbanu biṃba rūpavanu||36||

jñāna bhaktādi akhil̤a guṇa caturānanòl̤agippaṃtè<br/>mukhyā prāṇanali ciṃtipudu yatkiṃcit kòratèyāgi<br/>nyūna ṛju gaṇa jīvaralli krameṇa vṛddhi jñāna bhakti<br/>samāna bhārati vāṇigal̤ali pada prayuktādhika||37||

sauri sūryana tèradi brahmasamīra gāyatrī girigal̤òl̤u<br/>toruvudu aspaṣṭa rūpadi mukti paryaṃta<br/>vārijāsana vāyu vāṇī bhāratigal̤igè mahā pral̤ayadi bāradu<br/>ajñānādi doṣavu hari kṛpā baladi||38||

nūru varuṣa anaṃtaradali saroruhāsana tanna kalpadali<br/>āru muktiyanu aiduvarò avaravara karèdòydu<br/>śauri purudòl̤agippa nadiyali kāruṇika susnāna nija parivāra sahitadi māḍi<br/>hari udara praveśisuva||39||

vāsudevana udaradali praveśagaida anaṃtaradi<br/>nirdoṣa muktaru udaradiṃ pòramaṭṭu haruṣadali<br/>meśaniṃda ājñava paḍèdu anaṃtāsana sītadvīpa mokṣadi vāsavāgi<br/>vimukta duḥkharu saṃcarisutiharu||40||

satva satva mahā susūkṣamu satva satvātmaka kal̤evara<br/>satyalokādhipanu ènipagè atyalpavu èraḍu guṇa<br/>mukta bhogyavidalla ajāṃḍa utpatti kāraṇavalla<br/>hari prītyarthavāgī jagada vyāpāragal̤a māḍuvanu||41||

pāda nyūna śatābda paryaṃta odi ugratapa ahvayadi lavaṇa udadhiyòl̤agè<br/>kalpadaśa tapavidda anaṃtaradi<br/>sādhisida mahadeva padava āraidu nava kalpa avasānakè<br/>aiduvanu śeṣana padava pārvati sahitanāgi||42||

iṃdra manu daśa kalpagal̤ali sunaṃda nāmadi śravaṇagaidu<br/>mukuṃdana aparokṣārtha nālku sukalpa tapaviddu<br/>nòṃdu pògèyòl̤u koṭi varuṣa puraṃdaranadanuṃḍa anaṃtara<br/>pòṃdidanu nija loka surapati kāmanidaraṃtè||43||

karèsuvaru pūrvadali caṃdrārkaru ati śāṃta surūpa nāmadi<br/>èraḍèrèḍu manu kalpa śravaṇagaidu<br/>manu kalpa vara tapo baladiṃda arvāk śiragal̤āgi īraidu sāvira varuṣa<br/>duḥkhavanīgi kāṃbaru biṃba rūpavanu||44||

sādhanagal̤a aparokṣa anaṃtara aiduvaru mokṣavanu<br/>śiva śakrādi divijaru ukta kramadiṃ kalpa saṃkhyèyali<br/>aidalègè aivattu upeṃdra sahodaranigippattu<br/>dvinava tvagādhipati prāṇanigè guru manugal̤igè ṣoḍaśavu||45||

pravaha marutagè hannèraḍu saiṃdhava divākara dharmarigè daśa<br/>nava sukalpavu mitrarigè śeṣa śata janarigèṃṭu<br/>kavi sanaka susanaṃdana sanatkuvara munigal̤igè el̤u<br/>varuanana yuvati parjanyādi puṣkaragè āru kalpadali||46||

aidu karmaja surarigè ājānādigal̤igè èraḍèrèḍu kalpa<br/>ardhādhika traya gopikā strīyarigè pitṛ trayavu<br/>ī divaukasa manuja gāyakaru aiduvaru èraḍòṃdu kalpa<br/>narādhiparigè arè kalpadòl̤agè aparokṣavu irutihudu||47||

dīpagal̤a anusarisi dīptiyu vyāpisi mahātimira kal̤èdu<br/>paropakārava māl̤pa tèradaṃdadali paramātma<br/>ā payojāsananòl̤agiddu svarūpa śaktiya vyaktigaisuta<br/>tā pòl̤èvanu avaraṃtè ceṣṭèya māḍi māḍisuva||48||

svodarasthita prāṇa rudra iṃdrādi surarigè dehagal̤a kòṭṭu<br/>ādaradi avaravara sevèya kòṃbanu anavarata<br/>moda bodha dayābdhi tannavarādhi rogava kal̤èdu<br/>mahādaparādhagal̤a noḍadalè salahuva satata smarisuvara||49||

prati pratī kalpadali sṛṣṭi sthiti layava māḍutalè modipa<br/>caturamukha pavamānara annava māḍi bhuṃjisuva<br/>ghṛtavè mṛtyuṃjayanènipa devatègal̤èlla upasecanaru<br/>śrīpatigè mūrjagavèlla odana atithiyènisikòṃba||50||

garbhiṇi strī uṃḍa bhojana garbhagata śiśu uṃba tèradali<br/>nirbhayanu tānuṃḍu uṇisuvanu sarva jīvarigè<br/>nirbalāti paramāṇu jīvagè abbuvadè sthūlānna bhojana<br/>arbhakaru pel̤uvaru kovidaru idana òḍaṃbaḍaru||51||

apacayagal̤illa uṃḍududariṃda upacayagal̤illa<br/>amaragaṇadòl̤agè upamarènisuvarilla janmādigal̤u mòdalilla<br/>aparimita sanmahima bhaktara apunarāvartaranu māḍuva<br/>kṛpaṇa vatsala svapada saukhyavanittu śaraṇarigè||52||

bitti bījavu bhūmiyòl̤u nīrètti bèl̤èsida bèl̤asu prāṃtakè<br/>kitti mèluvaṃdadali lakṣmī ramaṇa lokagal̤a mattè jīvara<br/>karma kālotpatti sthiti laya māḍutali<br/>samavartiyènisuva kheda modagal̤u illa anavarata||53||

śvasana rudra iṃdra pramukha sumanasaròl̤iddaru<br/>kṣutpipāsagal̤u vaśadòl̤ippavu sakala bhogakè sādhanagal̤āgi<br/>asura preta piśācigal̤a bhādisutalippavu<br/>dinadinadi mānisaròl̤agè mṛga pakṣi jīvaròl̤iddu poguvavu||54||

vāsudevagè svapnasuptipipāsa kṣut bhaya śoka moha āyāsa apasmṛti<br/>mātsarya mada puṇya pāpādi doṣa varjitanèṃdu<br/>brahma sadāśivādi samasta divijaru upāsanèyagaidu<br/>èlla kāladi muktarāgiharu||55||

parama sūkṣma kṣaṇavu aidu truṭi karèsuvadu aivattu truṭi lava<br/>èraḍu lavavu nimiṣa nimiṣagal̤èṃṭu mātra<br/>yuga guru daśa prāṇaru pal̤a hannèraḍu bāṇavu<br/>ghaṭika triṃśati irul̤u hagalu aravattu ghaṭikagal̤u ahòrātrigal̤u||56||

ī divārātrigal̤u èraḍu hadinaidu pakṣagal̤u<br/>èraḍu māsagal̤u ādapavu māsa dvayavè ṛtu ṛtutrayagal̤u ayana<br/>aiduvadu ayanadvayābda kṛtādi yugagal̤u<br/>deva mānadi dvādaśa sahasra varuṣagal̤u adanu pel̤uvènu||57||

catura sāvirada èṃṭu nūrivu kṛta yugakè<br/>sahasra salè ṣaṭ śatavu tretagè dvāparakè dvisahasra nānūru<br/>ditijapati kaliyugakè sāvira śata dvayagal̤u kūḍi<br/>ī devatègal̤igè hannèraḍu sāvira vihavu varṣagal̤u||58||

prathama yugakè el̤adhika arè viṃśati sulakṣāṣṭottara<br/>suviṃśati sahasra manuṣya mānābdagal̤u<br/>ṣaṇṇavati mita sahasrada lakṣa dvādaśa dvitīya<br/>tṛtīyakè èṃṭu lakṣada catura ṣaṣṭhi sahasra kāligè idarardha ciṃtipudu||59||

mūradhika nālvattu lakṣada āru mūrèrèḍadhika sāvira<br/>īrèrèḍu yuga varuṣa saṃkhyègaiyalu initihudò<br/>sūri pèccisè sāvirada nānūru mūvattèrèḍu koṭi<br/>saroruhāsanagè idu divasavu èṃbaru vipaścitaru||60||

śatadhṛtigè ī divasagal̤u triṃśatiyu māsa dvādaśābdavu<br/>śatavu èraḍaròl̤u sarva jīvotpatti sthiti layavu<br/>śṛti smṛtigal̤u pel̤utihavu acyutagè nimiṣavidèṃdu<br/>sukha śāśvatagè pāsaṭiyèṃbuvarè brahmādi divijaranu||61||

ādi madhyāṃtaragal̤illada mādhavagidu upacāravèṃdu<br/>ṛgādi veda purāṇagal̤u pel̤uvavu nityadali<br/>modamayana anugrahava saṃpādisi ramā brahma rudra iṃdrādigal̤u<br/>tamma tamma padaviyanu aidi sukhisuvaru||62||

ī kathāmṛta pāna sukha suvivekigal̤igalladalè<br/>vaiṣika vyākula kucittarigè dòrèvudāva kāladali<br/>loka vārtèya biṭṭu idanavalokisuta modiparigòlidu<br/>kṛpākara jagannātha viṭhala pòrèvanu anudinadi||63||
