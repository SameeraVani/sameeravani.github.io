---
title: "ಪಂಚಮಹಾಯಜ್ಞ ಸಂಧಿ / Panchamahayagna Sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè

parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

jananipitabhūvāridāṃbaravènipa paṃcāgniyali

nārāyaṇana triṃśati mūrtigal̤a vyāpāra vyāptigal̤a nènèdu

divasāl̤u èṃba samidhègal̤u niraṃtara homisuta

pāvanakè pāvananu ènipa paramana beḍu paramasukha||1||

gagana pāvaka samidhè ravi raśmigal̤e dhūmavu arciyènipudu hagalu

nakṣatragal̤u kiḍigal̤u caṃdrama aṃgāra

mṛgavara udaranòl̤agè aidu rūpagal̤a ciṃtisi

bhaktirasa mātugal̤è maṃtrava māḍi homisuvaru vipaścitaru||2||

pāvakanu parjanya samidhèyu prāvahīpati dhūmagal̤u meghāval̤igal̤u

arci kṣaṇaprabhè garjanavè kiḍiyu bhāvisuvudu aṃgāra siḍilèṃdu

ī vidha agniyòl̤u abdhi jātana kovidaru homisuvaru anudina

parama bhakutiyali||3||

dharaṇiyèṃbude agni saṃvatsarave samidhè vihāyasave pògè

iral̤u uri diśa aṃgāra avāṃtara digvalaya kiḍiyu

varuṣavèṃba āhutigal̤iṃdali hariya mèccisi

sakalaròl̤agè adhvariyanāgiru sarva rūpātmakana ciṃtisuta||4||

puruṣa śikhi vāksamidhè dhūmavu paraṇa arciyu jihvè śrotragal̤u èraḍu kiḍigal̤u

locanagal̤u aṃgāravènisuvuvu

niruta bhuṃjisuvanna yadukulavaranigè avadānagal̤èṃdu

ī pari samarpaṇègaiyè kaigòṃḍu anudinadi pòrèva||5||

mattè yoṣa agniyòl̤u til̤ivudu upastha tattvavè samidhè

kāmotpatti paramātugal̤u dhūmavu yoni mahadarci

tatpraveśa aṃgāra kiḍigal̤u utsaha utsarjanavè

puruṣottamanigè avadānavanè kaikòṃḍu mannisuva||6||

aidagnigal̤alli marèyadè aidu rūpātmakana ippattaidu rūpagal̤a

anudinadi nènèvarigè janmagal̤a aidisanu nal̤inākṣa

raṇadòl̤u maidunana kāydaṃtè salahuva

baidavagè gatiyitta bhayahara bhaktavatsalanu||7||

paṃcanārī turagadaṃdadi paṃcarūpātmakanu tā ṣaṭpaoca rūpava dharisi

tattannāmadiṃ karèsi

paṃca pāvaka mukhadi guṇamaya paṃcabhūtātmaka śarīrava

paṃca vidha jīvarigè kòṭṭu allallè ramisuvanu||8||

vidhi bhavādi samasta jīvara hṛdayadòl̤agè ekātmanènisuva padumanābhanu

acyutānaṃtādirūpadali

adhisubhūtādhyātmava adhidaivadòl̤u karèsuva

prāṇanāgābhidanu daśarūpadali daśavidha prāṇaròl̤agiddu||9||

īraidu sāvirada ippattu āradhika munnūru rūpagal̤a

īrèrèḍu sthānadali ciṃtipudu anudinadi budharu

nūrippattel̤u adhika mūrārusāvira rūpadiṃ

daśa mārutaròl̤iddu avaravara pèsariṃda karèsuvanu||10||

cittaisuvudu èṃṭadhika ippattu sāvira nālku śatada ippattamūru sumūrtigal̤u

ahavalle pariyaṃta

hattu nālku rūpagal̤a nèrèbittavarīpari til̤idu

puruṣottamana sarvatra pūjèya māḍu kòṃḍāḍu||11||

īrèrèḍu śatadviṣṭa adhika hadināru sāvira rūpa sarva śarīradòl̤u

śabdhādigal̤a adhiṣṭhānadòl̤agè ippa

mārutanu nāgādi rūpadi

mūrane guṇamāni śrī durgāramaṇa vidyākumohava kòḍuva karaṇakkè||12||

aidavidyègal̤òl̤agè iha nāgādigal̤a adhiṣṭhānadali lakṣmīdhavanu

kṛddholka mòdalāda aidu rūpagal̤a tā dharisi

sajjanara avidyava chedisuva tāmasarigè ajñānādigal̤a kòṭṭu

avaravara sādhanava māḍisuva||13||

govugal̤òl̤u udgīthaniha prasthāva hiṃkāra èraḍū rūpadi avyājagal̤òl̤ihanu

pratihārāhva hayagal̤òl̤u

jīvanaprada nidhana manujaròl̤u ī vidhadòl̤iha paṃca sāmava

jhāva jhāvakè nènèvarigè aidisanu janmagal̤a||14||

yuga catuṣṭayagal̤ali tāniddu yuga pravartaka dharma karmagal̤igè pravartaka

vāsudevādi īrèrèḍu rūpa tègèdukòṃḍu

yugādi kṛtu tā yuga pravartakayènisi

dharma praghaṭakanu tānāgi bhakutarigīva saṃpadava||15||

talèyòl̤iha nārāyaṇanu gaṃṭalaḍi òḍalòl̤u vāsudevanu

baladaliha pradyumna èḍa bhāgadali aniruddha

kèl̤agina aṃgadi saṃkaruṣaṇana til̤idu

ī pari sakala dehagal̤òl̤agè paṃcātmakana rūpava noḍu kòṃḍāḍu||16||

tanu viśiṣṭadi ippa nārāyaṇanu kaṭi pāda aṃta saṃkaruṣaṇanu

śira jaghana aṃtavāgiha vāsudevākhya

animiṣa īṣa aniruddha pradyumnannu èḍadi bala bhāgadi

ciṃtanèya māl̤parigè uṃṭè mailigè vidhi niṣedhagal̤u||17||

padumanābhanu pāṇiyòl̤agiha vadanadali hṛṣikeśa

nāsika sadanadali śrīdharanu jihvèyòl̤u ippa vāmananu

vidita netradi trivikramanu tvakdeśadòl̤agiha

karṇadali ippanu viṣṇunāmaka śravaṇanèṃdènisi||18||

manadòl̤iha goviṃda mādhava dhanapa sakha tatvadòl̤u

nārāyaṇa mahattatvadòl̤u avyaktadòl̤u keśavanu

initu rūpava dehadòl̤u ciṃtanèya gaiva mahātmaru

il̤èyòl̤u manujaru avaralla amarare sari harikṛpābaladi||19||

nèladòl̤u ippanu kṛṣṇa rūpadi jaladòl̤u ippanu hariyènisi

śikhiyòl̤agè ippanu paraśurāma upèndranu èṃdènisi èllaròl̤u ippanu janārdhananu

bāṃdaladòl̤u acyuta gaṃdha narahari

pòl̤èva adhokṣaja rasagal̤òl̤u rasarūpa tānāgi||20||

rūpa puruṣottamanu sparśa prāpakanu aniruddha

śabdhadi vyāpisiha pradyumna upasthadi vāsudevaniha

tā pòl̤èva pāyusthanāgi jayāpatiyu saṃkaruṣaṇanu

susthāpakanènisi pādadòl̤u dāmodaranu pòl̤èva||21||

catura viṃśati tattvadòl̤u śrīpatiyè aniruddhādi rūpadi

viditanāgiddu akhila jīvara saṃhananadòl̤agè

pratatiyaṃdadi suttu suttuta pitṛgal̤igè tarpakanènisikòṃḍu

atul̤a mahimanu ṣaṇṇavati nāmadali nèlèsihanu||22||

catura viṃśati tattvadòl̤u tatpatigal̤ènisuva brahma mukha devatègal̤òl̤u

hannòṃdu nūraivattèraḍu rūpa

vitatanāgiddèlla jīvara jatana māḍuva gosuga

jagatpatigè enādarū prayojanavillavidariṃda||23||

iṃdirādhava śakti mòdalāda òṃdadhikadaśa rūpadiṃdali

pòṃdihanu sakala iṃdriyagal̤alli puruṣanāmakanu

suṃdaraprada pūrṇajñānānaṃdamaya

citdehadòl̤u tānòṃdarèkṣaṇa vagaladalè paramāptanu āgippa||24||

āradhika daśa rūpadiṃdali toruta ippanu viśva

liṃgaśarīradòl̤u taijasanu prājñanu turyanāmakanu

mūraidu rūpagal̤a dharisutali īraidu karaṇadòl̤u mātradi

khaira śikhi jala bhūmiyòl̤agihanu ātmanāmadali||25||

manadòl̤u ahaṃkāradòl̤u ciṃtanèya māl̤pudu aṃtarātmana

ghana sutattvadi paramana avyaktadali jñānātma

initu paṃcāśayvaraṇa vedyana ajādyaivattu mūrtigal̤anu

sadā sarvatra dehagal̤alli pūjipudu||26||

catura viṃśati tattvadòl̤u tatpatigal̤ènisuva brahma mukha devatègal̤òl̤u

hadimūru sāvirada èṃṭu nūradhika catura viṃśati rūpadiṃdali

vitatanāgiddu èllaròl̤u

prākṛta puruṣanaṃdadali paṃcātmakanu ramisuvanu||27||

keśavādi sumūrti dvādaśa māsa puṃḍragal̤alli

vedavyāsa aniruddhādi rūpagal̤u āru ṛtugal̤ali

vāsavāgihanèṃdu triṃśati vāsaradi satkarma dharma nirāśèyiṃdali māḍu

karuṇava beḍu kòṃḍāḍu||28||

loṣṭha kāṃcana loha śailaja kāṣṭha mòdalāda akhil̤a jaḍa

parameṣṭhi mòdalāda akhil̤a cetanaròl̤agè anudinavu

ceṣṭègal̤a māḍisuta til̤isadè preṣṭanāgiddu èllarigè

sarveṣṭa ādāyaka saṃtaisuvanu sarva jīvaranu||29||

vāsudevāniruddha rūpadi puṃ śarīradòl̤ihanu sarvada

strī śarīradòl̤ihanu saṃkaruṣaṇanu pradyumna

dvāsuparṇa śruti vinuta sarvasunila nārāyaṇana

sadupāsanèya gaivararu jīvanmuktaru ènisuvaru||30||

tannanaṃta anaṃta rūpa hiraṇya garbhādigal̤òl̤agè

kāruṇya sāgara harahi avaravara akhil̤a vyāpāra

bannabaḍadalè māḍi māḍisi dhanyarènisi

samasta divijara puṇyakarmava svīkarisi sukhavittu pālisuva||31||

sāgaradòl̤iha nadiya jala bheda āgasadòl̤u ippa abda ballavu

kāgè gubbigal̤u ariya ballave nadiya jalasthitiya

bhogivara pariyaṃka śayananòl̤u ī guṇatraya baddha jagavihudu

āgamajñaru til̤ivaru ajñānigal̤igè al̤avaḍadu||32||

karaṇa guṇa bhūtagal̤òl̤agè tadvaranènipa brahmādi divijaròl̤aritu

rūpa catuṣṭayagal̤a anudinadi sarvatra smarisuta anumodisuta higguta

paravaśadi pāḍuvarigè tannirava torisi

bhavavimuktara māḍi poṣisuva||33||

mūlarūpanu manadòl̤iha śravaṇa āliyòl̤agiha matsya

kūrmanu kolarūpanu tvakrasanadòl̤agè ippa narasiṃha

bālavaṭu vāmananu nāsika nāl̤adòl̤u vadanadali bhārgava

vālibhaṃjana hastadòl̤u pāda śrī kṛṣṇa||34||

jina vimohaka buddha pāyuga danuja bhaṃjana kalki meḍradi

initu daśa rūpagal̤a daśa karaṇaṃgal̤ali til̤idu

anubhavipa viṣayaṃgal̤u kṛṣṇārpaṇavènalu kaikòṃba

vṛjina ardana vara jagannātha viṭhala viśva vyāpakanu||35||
