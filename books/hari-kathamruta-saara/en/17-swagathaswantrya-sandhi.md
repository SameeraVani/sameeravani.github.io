---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಸ್ವಗತಸ್ವಾತಂತ್ರ್ಯ ಸಂಧಿ / Swagathaswantrya sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè<br/>parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

svagatasvātaṃtrya guṇava hari tègèdu brahmādyarigè kòṭṭadu<br/>bhṛgu munipa pel̤idanu iṃdradyumna kṣitipanigè||

parama viṣṇu svataṃtra māyā taruṇi vakṣasthal̤a nivāsi<br/>sarasijodbhava prāṇarīrvaru sacivaru ènisuvaru<br/>sarva karmagal̤alli tatpriyaru uraga bhūṣaṇa ahaṃkṛtitraya<br/>karèsuva amareṃdra arka mukharu iṃdriyaparu ènisuvaru||1||

ī divaukasara aṃtè kali mòdalāda daityaru sarva dehadi todakaru tāvāgi<br/>vyāpāragal̤a māḍuvaru<br/>vedhanāṃdadi kaliyu ahaṃkārādhipa adhama madhukukaiṭabha<br/>krodhiśaṃbhara mukharu manasigè svāmiyènisuvaru||2||

devatègal̤a upādhi nityadi evamādi nāmadiṃdali<br/>yāvadiṃdriyagal̤ali vyāpāragal̤a māḍuvaru<br/>sevakara seva anuguṇa phalavīva nrupanaṃdadali<br/>tanna svabhāva svātaṃtrya vibhāgava māḍikòṭṭa hari||3||

agaṇita svātaṃtryava nālbagè vibhāgava māḍi<br/>òṃdanutègèdu daśavidhagaisi pādarè paṃca prāṇanali<br/>mòga catuṣṭayanòl̤u sapādaidu guṇavirasida<br/>mattè daśa vidhayugal̤a guṇavannu māḍi èraḍu sadā śivanòl̤iṭṭa||4||

pākaśāsana kāmaròl̤u sārdhaikaviṭṭa daśa iṃdriyara<br/>sudivaukasādyaròl̤òṃdu yāvajjīvaròl̤agòṃdu<br/>nālkūvarè kalyādi daityānīkakittanu<br/>èraḍu trividha vivekagaisi iṃdiragòṃdu èraḍu ātma tannòl̤agè||5||

ī vidhadi svātaṃtryava deva mānava dānavaròl̤u<br/>ramā vinodi vibhāga māḍiṭṭu alle ramisuvanu<br/>mūvaròl̤agiddu avara karmava tā vikāravagaisadalè<br/>kalpavasānakè kòḍuvanu anāyāsa avara gatiya||6||

ālayagal̤òl̤ippa dīpa jvālè vartigal̤anusarisi janara āligòppuva tèradi<br/>hari tā torpa sarvatra<br/>kāla kāladi śrīdharā durgā lalanèyara kūḍi sukhamaya līlègaiyalu<br/>triguṇa kāryagal̤ihavu jīvarigè||7||

iṃdriyagal̤iṃ māl̤pa karma dvaṃdvagal̤a tanagarpisalu<br/>goviṃda puṇyava kòṃḍu pāpava bhasmavanè māl̤pa<br/>iṃdireśanu bhakta janaranu niṃdisuvaròl̤agippa<br/>puṇyava taṃdu tannavagīva pāpagal̤a avariguṇisuvanu||8||

hòttu òṭṭigè pāpa karma pravartakara niṃdisadè<br/>tanagiṃda uttamara guṇa karmagal̤a kòṃḍāḍadalippa martyarigè<br/>gobrāhmaṇa strī hatya mòdalāda akhil̤a doṣagal̤ittapanu<br/>saṃdeha paḍasalla akhil̤a śāstramata||9||

tanna svātaṃtrya guṇagal̤a hiraṇya garbhādyarigè<br/>kalimukha dānavara saṃtatigè avaradhikārava anusarisi<br/>puṇya pāpagal̤īva bahu kāruṇya sāgaranu<br/>alpaśaktigal̤a uṇṇalariyadiralu uṇa kalisidanu jīvarigè||10||

satya vikrama puṇya pāpa samastarigè kòḍalosugadi<br/>nālvattu bhāgava māḍi leśāṃśavanu janakīva<br/>atyalpa paramāṇu jīvagè sāmarthyavanu tā kòṭṭu<br/>sthūla padārthagal̤a uṃḍu uṇipa sarvadāsarva jīvarigè||11||

timira taraṇigal̤u eka deśadi samanisippavè èṃdigādaru?<br/>bhramaṇa chal̤i bisalu aṃjikègal̤uṃṭeno parvatakè?<br/>amita jīvaròl̤iddu lakṣmī ramaṇa vyāpāragal̤a māḍuva<br/>kamalapatra sarovaragal̤òl̤agippa tèradaṃtè||12||

aṃbujodbhava mukhya surā kali śaṃbarādi samasta daitya kadaṃbakè<br/>anudina puṇya pāpa vibhāgavanè māḍi<br/>aṃbudhiya jalavanu mahadghaṭa diṃbanitu tuṃbuva tèradi<br/>prati biṃbaròl̤u tāniddu yogyatèyaṃtè phalavīva||13||

initu viṣṇu rahasyadòl̤u bhrumu munipa iṃdradyumnagè arupidadanu<br/>budharu kel̤uvudu nityadi matsarava biṭṭu<br/>anucitoktigal̤iddarèyu sariyè gaṇanè māḍadirèṃdu<br/>vidvad janakè vijñāpanèya māḍuvè vinaya pūrvakadi||13||

veta bhaya viśveśa vidhi pita mātul̤āṃtaka madhva vallabha<br/>bhūta bhāvana anaṃta bhāskara teja mahārāja<br/>gautamana maḍadiyanu kāyda anātha rakṣaka<br/>gurutama jagannātha viṭhala tanna naṃbida bhakutaranu pòrèva||14||
