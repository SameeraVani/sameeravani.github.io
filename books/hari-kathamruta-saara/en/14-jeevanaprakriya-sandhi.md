---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಪಿತೃಗಣ ಸಂಧಿ / Pithruguna sandhi / Jeevanaprakriya sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè/

parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

kṛtiramaṇa pradyumna vasudevatègal̤u ahaṃkāra trayadòl̤u

caturaviṃśati rūpadiṃdali bhojyanènisuvanu

hutavahākṣa aṃtargata jayāpatiyu tāne mūradhika triṃśati surūpadi

bhoktru ènisuva bhoktrugal̤òl̤iddu||1||

āradhika mūvattu rūpadi vārijāptanòl̤u irutihanu

māyāramaṇa śrī vāsudevanu kālanāmadali

mūruvidha pitṛgal̤òl̤u vasu tripurāri ādityaga aniruddhanu

torikòl̤l̤adè kartṛ karma kriyanu ènisikòṃba||2||

svavaśa nārāyaṇanu tā ṣaṇṇavati nāmadi karèsutali

vasu śiva divākara kartṛ karma kriyègal̤òl̤agiddu

nèvanavilladè nityadali tannavaru māḍuva sevè kaikòṃḍu

avara pitṛgal̤igīva anaṃtānaṃta sukhagal̤anu||3||

taṃtu paṭadaṃdadali lakṣmīkāṃta paṃcātmakanu ènisi

vasu kaṃtu hara ravi kartṛgal̤òl̤iddu

anavarata tanna ciṃtisuta saṃtaranu guru madhvāṃtarātmaka saṃtaisuvanu

saṃtata akhil̤ārthagal̤a pālisi iha paraṃgal̤ali||4||

taṃdè tāygal̤a prītigosuga niṃdya karmava tòrèdu

vihitagal̤u òṃdu mīradè sāṃga karmagal̤anu ācarisuvavaru

vaṃdanīyarāgi il̤èyòl̤agè dainaṃdinadi daiśika daihika sukhadiṃda bāl̤varu

bahu divasadali kīrtiyutarāgi||5||

aṃśi aṃśa aṃtargatatraya haṃsavāhana mukhya divijara asaṃśayadi til̤idu

aṃtarātmaka śrī janārdhanana saṃsmaraṇè pūrvakadi

ṣaḍādhika triṃśatitraya rūpavaritu

vipāṃsagana pūjisuvaru avare kṛtārtharu ènisuvaru||6||

mūruvarè sāvirada melè arè nūraidu rūpadi janārdhana

sūrigal̤u māḍuva samārādhanègè vighnagal̤u bāradaṃtè

bahuprakāra kharāri kāpāḍuvanu sarva śarīragal̤òl̤iddu

avaravara pèsariṃda karèsutali||7||

jaya jaya jayākāṃta dattātraya kapila mahidāsa bhaktapriya

purātana puruṣa pūrṇānaṃda mānaghana

hayavadana hari haṃsa lokatraya vilakṣaṇa

nikhil̤a jagadāśraya nirāmaya dayadi saṃtaisèṃdu prārthisuvanu||8||

ṣaṇṇavatiyèṃba akṣara īḍyanu ṣaṇṇavati nāmadali karèsuta

tannavaru sadbhakti pūrvakadiṃda māḍutiha puṇya karmava svīkarisi

kāruṇya sāgara salahuvanu

brahmaṇyadeva bhavābdhipota bahu prakāradali||9||

dehagal̤a kòḍuvavanu avaravara aharagal̤a kòḍadihanè

sumanasa mahita maṃgal̤a carita sadguṇa bharitanu

anavarata ahika pāratrika sukhaprada vahisi bènnili bèṭṭava

amṛtava druhiṇa mòdalādavarigè uṇisida murida nahitarana||10||

druhiṇa mòdalāda amararigè sanmahita māyāramaṇa

tāne svahanènisi saṃtṛptipaḍisuva sarvakāladali

prahita saṃkaruṣaṇanu pitṛgal̤igè aharanènipa svadhākhyarūpadi

mahija phala tṛṇa pèsarinali pradyumna aniruddha||11||

annanènisuva nṛpaśugal̤igè hiraṇya garbhāṃḍadòl̤u

saṃtata tannana īpariyiṃda upāsanègaiva bhaktarana bannabaḍisadè

bhava samudra maha unnatiya dāṭisi

caturvidha annamayanu ātma pradarśana sukhavanīva hari||12||

manavacana kāyagal̤a dèśèyiṃda anudinadi biḍadè ācarisutippa

anucitocita karmagal̤a sadbhakti pūrvakadi anil̤a devanòl̤ippa

nārāyaṇagè idu annavèṃdu kṛṣṇārpaṇavènuta kòḍu

svīkarisi saṃtaipa karuṇāl̤u||13||

el̤u vidha anna prakaraṇava kel̤i kovidara āsyadiṃdali

ālasava māḍadalè aniruddhādi rūpagal̤a

kālakāladi nènèdu pūjisu sthūla matigal̤igè idanu pel̤adè

śrī lakumi vallabhanè annādannādanu||14||

èṃdaridu saptānnagal̤a dainaṃdinadi marèyadè

sadā goviṃdagè arpisu nirbhayadi mahāyajñavu idèṃdu

iṃdireśanu svīkarisi dayadiṃda beḍisikòl̤adè

tavakadi taṃdu kòḍuvanu parama maṃgal̤a tanna dāsarigè||15||

sūji karadali piḍidu samarava nā jayisuvènu èṃba naranaṃtè

ī jagattinòl̤u ul̤l̤a ajñānigal̤u nityadali

śrī jagatpati caraṇa yugal̤a saroja bhakti jñāna pūrvaka pūjisadè

dharmārtha kāmava bayasi bal̤aluvaru||16||

śakaṭa bhaṃjana sakala jīvara nikaṭaganu tānāgi

lokakè prakaṭanāgadè sakala karmava māḍi māḍisuta

akuṭilātmaka bhakuta janarigè sukhadanènisuva sarvakāladi

akaṭakaṭa ītana mahā mahimègal̤igè enèṃbè||17||

śrī lakumivallabhanu vaikuṃṭha ālayadi praṇava prakṛti

kīlālajāsana mukhya cetanaròl̤agè nèlèsiddu

mūla kāraṇāośi nāmadi līlègaisuta tori kòl̤l̤adè

pālinòl̤u ghṛtavidda tèradaṃtè ippa tristhal̤adi||18||

mūru yugadali mūla rūpanu sūrigal̤a saṃtaisi

ditija kumārakara saṃharisi dharmavanu ul̤uhabekèṃdu

kāruṇika bhūmiyòl̤u nija parivāra sahita avatarisi

bahu vidha toridanu naravat pravṛttiya sakala cetanakè||19||

kāraṇāhvaya prakṛtiyòl̤agiddu āradhika hadinèṃṭu tattvava

tā racisi tadrūpa tannāmagal̤anè dharisi

nīraja bhavāṃḍavanu nirmisi kāruṇika kāryākhya rūpadi toruvanu

sahajāhitācalagal̤ali pratidinadi||20||

jīvaraṃtaryāmi aṃśi kal̤evaragal̤òl̤agè iṃdriyagal̤ali

tā vihārava gaiyuta anudina aṃśa nāmadali

ī viṣayagal̤anuṃḍu sukhamayavīva sukha saṃsāra duḥkhava

deva mānava dānavarigè avirata sudhāma sakha||21||

deśa deśava sutti dehāyāsagòl̤isadè kāmya karma durāśègòl̤agāgadalè

brahmādi akhil̤a cetanaru

bhū salila pāvaka samīra ākāśa mòdalāda akhil̤a tattva

pareśagè ivu adhiṣṭhānavu èṃdaritu arcisu anavarata||22||

èraḍu vidhadali lokadòl̤u jīvarugal̤u ipparu saṃtata

kṣarākṣara viliṃga saliṃga sṛjya asṛjya bhedadali karèsuvadu

jaḍa prakṛti praṇavākṣara mahadaṇu kāla nāmadi

hari sahita bhedagal̤a paṃcaka smarisu sarvatra||23||

jīva jīvara bheda jaḍa jaḍa jīva jaḍagal̤a bheda

paramanu jīva jaḍa suvilakṣaṇanu èṃdaridu nityadali

ī viriṃci aṃḍadòl̤u èlla ṭāvinali til̤idaidu bheda

kal̤evaradòl̤aritu acyutana padavaidu śīghradali||24||

ādiyalli kṣarākṣarākhya dvedha akṣaradòl̤u ramā madhusūdanaru

kṣaragal̤òl̤u prakṛti praṇava kālagal̤u veda mukhya tṛṇāota jīvara

bhedagal̤anaritu ī rahasyava bhodisade maṃdarigè

sarvatradali ciṃtipudu||25||

dīpadiṃ dīpagal̤u pòramaṭṭu āpaṇa ālayagal̤a timiragal̤a tā pariharagaisi

tadgata padārtha torpaṃtè

sauparaṇi varavahanu tā bahu rūpa nāmadi èlla kaḍèyali vyāpisiddu

yatheṣṭa mahimèya torpa til̤isadalè||26||

nal̤ina mitragè iṃdradhanu prati phalisuvaṃtè

jagatrayavu kaṃgòl̤ipudu aṇu upādhiyali pratibiṃba ahvayadi harigè

til̤iyè trikakuddhāmana ati maṃgal̤a surūpava

sarva ṭāvili pòl̤èva hṛdayakè pratidivasa prahlāda poṣakanu||27||

rasa viśeṣadòl̤u ati vimalā sitavasana toyisi agniyòl̤agiḍè

pasarisuvudu prakāśa nasaguṃdadalè sarvatra

triśira dūṣaṇa vairi bhakti surasadi toyda mahātmaranu

bādhisavu bhavadòl̤agè iddarèyu sari durita rāśigal̤u||28||

vārinidhiyòl̤agul̤l̤a akhil̤a nadigal̤u berè berè niraṃtaradi vihāragaiyuta

parama modadalippa tèradaṃtè

mūru guṇagal̤a māniniyènisuva śrī ramā rūpagal̤u hariyali toritippavu

sarva kāladi samarahitavènisi||29||

kokanada sakhana udaya ghokālokanakè sògasadirè

bhāskara tā kal̤aṃkanè? ī kṛtīpati jagannāthanirè

svīkarisi sukhapaḍalu ariyada avivekigal̤u niṃdisidarè enahudu

ī kavitvava kel̤i sukhapaḍadiharè kovidaru||30||

cetanācetanagal̤ali guru mātariśvāṃtargata jagannātha viṭhala

niraṃtaradi vyāpisi til̤isi kòl̤l̤adale

kātarava puṭṭisi viṣayadali dhānara mohisuva

nirbhīta nityānaṃdamaya nirdoṣa niravadya||31||
