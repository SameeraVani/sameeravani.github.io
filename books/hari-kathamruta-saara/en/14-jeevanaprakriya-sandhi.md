---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಪಿತೃಗಣ ಸಂಧಿ / Pithruguna sandhi / Jeevanaprakriya sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè/<br/>parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

kṛtiramaṇa pradyumna vasudevatègal̤u ahaṃkāra trayadòl̤u<br/>caturaviṃśati rūpadiṃdali bhojyanènisuvanu<br/>hutavahākṣa aṃtargata jayāpatiyu tāne mūradhika triṃśati surūpadi<br/>bhoktru ènisuva bhoktrugal̤òl̤iddu||1||

āradhika mūvattu rūpadi vārijāptanòl̤u irutihanu<br/>māyāramaṇa śrī vāsudevanu kālanāmadali<br/>mūruvidha pitṛgal̤òl̤u vasu tripurāri ādityaga aniruddhanu<br/>torikòl̤l̤adè kartṛ karma kriyanu ènisikòṃba||2||

svavaśa nārāyaṇanu tā ṣaṇṇavati nāmadi karèsutali<br/>vasu śiva divākara kartṛ karma kriyègal̤òl̤agiddu<br/>nèvanavilladè nityadali tannavaru māḍuva sevè kaikòṃḍu<br/>avara pitṛgal̤igīva anaṃtānaṃta sukhagal̤anu||3||

taṃtu paṭadaṃdadali lakṣmīkāṃta paṃcātmakanu ènisi<br/>vasu kaṃtu hara ravi kartṛgal̤òl̤iddu<br/>anavarata tanna ciṃtisuta saṃtaranu guru madhvāṃtarātmaka saṃtaisuvanu<br/>saṃtata akhil̤ārthagal̤a pālisi iha paraṃgal̤ali||4||

taṃdè tāygal̤a prītigosuga niṃdya karmava tòrèdu<br/>vihitagal̤u òṃdu mīradè sāṃga karmagal̤anu ācarisuvavaru<br/>vaṃdanīyarāgi il̤èyòl̤agè dainaṃdinadi daiśika daihika sukhadiṃda bāl̤varu<br/>bahu divasadali kīrtiyutarāgi||5||

aṃśi aṃśa aṃtargatatraya haṃsavāhana mukhya divijara asaṃśayadi til̤idu<br/>aṃtarātmaka śrī janārdhanana saṃsmaraṇè pūrvakadi<br/>ṣaḍādhika triṃśatitraya rūpavaritu<br/>vipāṃsagana pūjisuvaru avare kṛtārtharu ènisuvaru||6||

mūruvarè sāvirada melè arè nūraidu rūpadi janārdhana<br/>sūrigal̤u māḍuva samārādhanègè vighnagal̤u bāradaṃtè<br/>bahuprakāra kharāri kāpāḍuvanu sarva śarīragal̤òl̤iddu<br/>avaravara pèsariṃda karèsutali||7||

jaya jaya jayākāṃta dattātraya kapila mahidāsa bhaktapriya<br/>purātana puruṣa pūrṇānaṃda mānaghana<br/>hayavadana hari haṃsa lokatraya vilakṣaṇa<br/>nikhil̤a jagadāśraya nirāmaya dayadi saṃtaisèṃdu prārthisuvanu||8||

ṣaṇṇavatiyèṃba akṣara īḍyanu ṣaṇṇavati nāmadali karèsuta<br/>tannavaru sadbhakti pūrvakadiṃda māḍutiha puṇya karmava svīkarisi<br/>kāruṇya sāgara salahuvanu<br/>brahmaṇyadeva bhavābdhipota bahu prakāradali||9||

dehagal̤a kòḍuvavanu avaravara aharagal̤a kòḍadihanè<br/>sumanasa mahita maṃgal̤a carita sadguṇa bharitanu<br/>anavarata ahika pāratrika sukhaprada vahisi bènnili bèṭṭava<br/>amṛtava druhiṇa mòdalādavarigè uṇisida murida nahitarana||10||

druhiṇa mòdalāda amararigè sanmahita māyāramaṇa<br/>tāne svahanènisi saṃtṛptipaḍisuva sarvakāladali<br/>prahita saṃkaruṣaṇanu pitṛgal̤igè aharanènipa svadhākhyarūpadi<br/>mahija phala tṛṇa pèsarinali pradyumna aniruddha||11||

annanènisuva nṛpaśugal̤igè hiraṇya garbhāṃḍadòl̤u<br/>saṃtata tannana īpariyiṃda upāsanègaiva bhaktarana bannabaḍisadè<br/>bhava samudra maha unnatiya dāṭisi<br/>caturvidha annamayanu ātma pradarśana sukhavanīva hari||12||

manavacana kāyagal̤a dèśèyiṃda anudinadi biḍadè ācarisutippa<br/>anucitocita karmagal̤a sadbhakti pūrvakadi anil̤a devanòl̤ippa<br/>nārāyaṇagè idu annavèṃdu kṛṣṇārpaṇavènuta kòḍu<br/>svīkarisi saṃtaipa karuṇāl̤u||13||

el̤u vidha anna prakaraṇava kel̤i kovidara āsyadiṃdali<br/>ālasava māḍadalè aniruddhādi rūpagal̤a<br/>kālakāladi nènèdu pūjisu sthūla matigal̤igè idanu pel̤adè<br/>śrī lakumi vallabhanè annādannādanu||14||

èṃdaridu saptānnagal̤a dainaṃdinadi marèyadè<br/>sadā goviṃdagè arpisu nirbhayadi mahāyajñavu idèṃdu<br/>iṃdireśanu svīkarisi dayadiṃda beḍisikòl̤adè<br/>tavakadi taṃdu kòḍuvanu parama maṃgal̤a tanna dāsarigè||15||

sūji karadali piḍidu samarava nā jayisuvènu èṃba naranaṃtè<br/>ī jagattinòl̤u ul̤l̤a ajñānigal̤u nityadali<br/>śrī jagatpati caraṇa yugal̤a saroja bhakti jñāna pūrvaka pūjisadè<br/>dharmārtha kāmava bayasi bal̤aluvaru||16||

śakaṭa bhaṃjana sakala jīvara nikaṭaganu tānāgi<br/>lokakè prakaṭanāgadè sakala karmava māḍi māḍisuta<br/>akuṭilātmaka bhakuta janarigè sukhadanènisuva sarvakāladi<br/>akaṭakaṭa ītana mahā mahimègal̤igè enèṃbè||17||

śrī lakumivallabhanu vaikuṃṭha ālayadi praṇava prakṛti<br/>kīlālajāsana mukhya cetanaròl̤agè nèlèsiddu<br/>mūla kāraṇāośi nāmadi līlègaisuta tori kòl̤l̤adè<br/>pālinòl̤u ghṛtavidda tèradaṃtè ippa tristhal̤adi||18||

mūru yugadali mūla rūpanu sūrigal̤a saṃtaisi<br/>ditija kumārakara saṃharisi dharmavanu ul̤uhabekèṃdu<br/>kāruṇika bhūmiyòl̤u nija parivāra sahita avatarisi<br/>bahu vidha toridanu naravat pravṛttiya sakala cetanakè||19||

kāraṇāhvaya prakṛtiyòl̤agiddu āradhika hadinèṃṭu tattvava<br/>tā racisi tadrūpa tannāmagal̤anè dharisi<br/>nīraja bhavāṃḍavanu nirmisi kāruṇika kāryākhya rūpadi toruvanu<br/>sahajāhitācalagal̤ali pratidinadi||20||

jīvaraṃtaryāmi aṃśi kal̤evaragal̤òl̤agè iṃdriyagal̤ali<br/>tā vihārava gaiyuta anudina aṃśa nāmadali<br/>ī viṣayagal̤anuṃḍu sukhamayavīva sukha saṃsāra duḥkhava<br/>deva mānava dānavarigè avirata sudhāma sakha||21||

deśa deśava sutti dehāyāsagòl̤isadè kāmya karma durāśègòl̤agāgadalè<br/>brahmādi akhil̤a cetanaru<br/>bhū salila pāvaka samīra ākāśa mòdalāda akhil̤a tattva<br/>pareśagè ivu adhiṣṭhānavu èṃdaritu arcisu anavarata||22||

èraḍu vidhadali lokadòl̤u jīvarugal̤u ipparu saṃtata<br/>kṣarākṣara viliṃga saliṃga sṛjya asṛjya bhedadali karèsuvadu<br/>jaḍa prakṛti praṇavākṣara mahadaṇu kāla nāmadi<br/>hari sahita bhedagal̤a paṃcaka smarisu sarvatra||23||

jīva jīvara bheda jaḍa jaḍa jīva jaḍagal̤a bheda<br/>paramanu jīva jaḍa suvilakṣaṇanu èṃdaridu nityadali<br/>ī viriṃci aṃḍadòl̤u èlla ṭāvinali til̤idaidu bheda<br/>kal̤evaradòl̤aritu acyutana padavaidu śīghradali||24||

ādiyalli kṣarākṣarākhya dvedha akṣaradòl̤u ramā madhusūdanaru<br/>kṣaragal̤òl̤u prakṛti praṇava kālagal̤u veda mukhya tṛṇāota jīvara<br/>bhedagal̤anaritu ī rahasyava bhodisade maṃdarigè<br/>sarvatradali ciṃtipudu||25||

dīpadiṃ dīpagal̤u pòramaṭṭu āpaṇa ālayagal̤a timiragal̤a tā pariharagaisi<br/>tadgata padārtha torpaṃtè<br/>sauparaṇi varavahanu tā bahu rūpa nāmadi èlla kaḍèyali vyāpisiddu<br/>yatheṣṭa mahimèya torpa til̤isadalè||26||

nal̤ina mitragè iṃdradhanu prati phalisuvaṃtè<br/>jagatrayavu kaṃgòl̤ipudu aṇu upādhiyali pratibiṃba ahvayadi harigè<br/>til̤iyè trikakuddhāmana ati maṃgal̤a surūpava<br/>sarva ṭāvili pòl̤èva hṛdayakè pratidivasa prahlāda poṣakanu||27||

rasa viśeṣadòl̤u ati vimalā sitavasana toyisi agniyòl̤agiḍè<br/>pasarisuvudu prakāśa nasaguṃdadalè sarvatra<br/>triśira dūṣaṇa vairi bhakti surasadi toyda mahātmaranu<br/>bādhisavu bhavadòl̤agè iddarèyu sari durita rāśigal̤u||28||

vārinidhiyòl̤agul̤l̤a akhil̤a nadigal̤u berè berè niraṃtaradi vihāragaiyuta<br/>parama modadalippa tèradaṃtè<br/>mūru guṇagal̤a māniniyènisuva śrī ramā rūpagal̤u hariyali toritippavu<br/>sarva kāladi samarahitavènisi||29||

kokanada sakhana udaya ghokālokanakè sògasadirè<br/>bhāskara tā kal̤aṃkanè? ī kṛtīpati jagannāthanirè<br/>svīkarisi sukhapaḍalu ariyada avivekigal̤u niṃdisidarè enahudu<br/>ī kavitvava kel̤i sukhapaḍadiharè kovidaru||30||

cetanācetanagal̤ali guru mātariśvāṃtargata jagannātha viṭhala<br/>niraṃtaradi vyāpisi til̤isi kòl̤l̤adale<br/>kātarava puṭṭisi viṣayadali dhānara mohisuva<br/>nirbhīta nityānaṃdamaya nirdoṣa niravadya||31||
