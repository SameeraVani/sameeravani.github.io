---
title: "ಕರುಣಾ ಸಂಧಿ / Karuna sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè

parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

śravaṇa manakānaṃdavīvudu bhavajanita duḥkhagal̤a kal̤èvudu

vividha bhogagal̤u ihaparaṃgal̤ali ittu salahuvudu

bhuvana pāvanavènipa lakṣmī dhavana maṃgal̤a kathèya

parama utsavadi kivigòṭṭu ālipudu bhūsuraru dinadinadi||1||

mal̤èya nīru oṇiyòl̤u pariyalu, bal̤asaru ūròl̤agè idda janaru

ā jalavu hèddòrègūḍè majjanapāna gaidaparu

kaluṣa vacanagal̤a ādaḍèyu, bāṃbòl̤èya pèttana pāda mahima

ā jaladi pòkkadariṃda māṇdaparè mahīsuraru||2||

śṛtitatigal̤a abhimāni lakṣmīstutigal̤igè gocarisada

apratihata mahaiśvaryādi akhila sadguṇa gaṇāṃbhodhi

pratidivasa tannaṃghri sevārata mahātmaru māḍutiha

saṃstutigèvaśanāguvènu ivana kāruṇyakè enèṃbè||3||

manavacanakè atidūra nènèvaranu anusarisi tiruguvanu jāhnavi janaka

janaròl̤agiddu janisuva jagadudara tānu

ghanamahima gāṃgeyanuta gāyanava kel̤uta

gaganacara vāhana divaukasaròḍanè carisuva manèmanègal̤alli||4||

malagi paramādaradi pāḍalu kul̤itu kel̤uva

kul̤itu pāḍalu niluva niṃtarè naliva nalidarè òliva nimagèṃba

sulabhano hari tannavaranu araghal̤igè biṭṭagalanu

ramādhavana òlisalariyadè pāmararu bal̤aluvaru bhavadòl̤agè||5||

manadòl̤agè tāniddu manavèṃdu ènisikòṃbanu

manada vṛttigal̤a anusarisi bhogaṃgal̤īvanu trividha cetanakè

manavittarè tannanīvanu tanuva daṃḍisi dinadinadi sādhanava māl̤parigè

ittapanu svargādi bhogagal̤a||6||

parama satpuruṣārtharūpavanu hariyu lokakè èṃdu

paramādaradi sadupāsanèya gaivarigè ittapanu tanna

marèdu dharmārthagal̤a kāmisuvarigè naguta atiśīghradiṃdali

surapatanaya suyodhanarigè ittaṃtè kòḍutippa ||7||

jagavanèllava nirmisuva nālmòganòl̤agè tāniddu salahuva

gaganakeśanòl̤iddu saṃharisuvanu lokagal̤a

svagatabheda vivarjitanu sarvaga sadānaṃdaika dehanu

bagèbagèya nāmadali karèsuva bhakutaranu pòrèva||8||

òbbanali niṃdāḍuvanu mattòbbanali noḍuvanu

beḍuvanu òbbanali nīḍuvanu mātāḍuvanu bèragāgi

abbarada hèddaivanu iva mattòbbarana lèkkisanu

lokadòl̤u òbbane tā bādhya bādhakanāha nirbhīta||9||

śaraṇajana maṃdāra śāśvata karuṇi kamalākāṃta kāmada

parama pāvanatara sumaṃgal̤a carita pārthasakha

nirupamānaṃdātma nirgata durita devavareṇyanèṃdu

ādaradi karèyalu baṃdòdaguvanu tannavara bal̤igè||10||

jananiyanu kāṇadiha bālaka nènènènadu halubutirè

kattalè manèyòl̤u aḍagiddu avana noḍuta naguta haruṣadali

tanayanaṃ bigidappi raṃbisi kanalikèya kal̤èvaṃtè

madhusūdananu tannavaru iddèḍègè baṃdòdagi salahuvanu||11||

iṭṭikallanu bhakutiyiṃdali kòṭṭa bhakutagè mècci taṇṇanè kòṭṭa

baḍabrāhmaṇana òppiḍiyavaligè akhil̤ārtha

kèṭṭa mātugal̤èṃda caidyana pòṭṭèyòl̤agiṃbiṭṭa

bāṇadaliṭṭa bhīṣmana avaguṇagal̤èṇisidanè karuṇāl̤u||12||

dhanava saṃrakṣisuva phaṇi tānuṇadè mattòbbarigè kòḍadè

anudinadi noḍuta sukhisuvaṃdadi

lakumivallabhanu praṇataranu kāydihanu niṣkāmanadi

nityānaṃdamaya durjanara sevèyanu òllanu apratimalla jagakèlla||13||

bālakana kalabhāṣè janani kel̤i sukhapaḍuvaṃtè

lakṣmīlola bhaktaru māḍutiha saṃstutigè higguvanu

tāl̤a tannavaralli māḍva avahel̤anava

hèddaiva vidurana ālayadi pāluṃḍu kurupana mānavanè kòṃḍa||14||

smarisuvavara aparādhagal̤a tāsmarisa sakala iṣṭa pradāyaka

maral̤i tanagè arpisalu kòṭṭuda anaṃtamaḍi māḍi paripariyaliṃda uṇisi

sukha sāgaradi lolāḍisuva maṃgal̤acarita

cinmayagātra lokapavitra sucaritra||15||

enu karuṇanidhiyo hari mattenu bhaktādhīnano

innenu ītana līlè iccāmātradali jagava tāne sṛjisuva pālisuva

nirvāṇa mòdalāda akhila lokasthānadali

mattè avaranu iṭṭu ānaṃda baḍisuvanu||16||

janapa mèccidarè īva dhanavāhana vibhūṣaṇa vasanabhūmi

tanumanagal̤a ittu ādariparu uṃṭeno lokadòl̤u

anavarata nènèvavara anaṃtāsanavè mòdalāda ālayadòl̤iṭṭu

aṇuganaṃdadali avara vaśanāguva mahāmahima||17||

bhuvana pāvana carita puṇyaśravaṇakīrtana pāpanāśana

kavibhirīḍita kairavadal̤aśyāma nissīma

yuvativeṣadi hiṃdè gaurīdhavana mohini kèḍisi ul̤isida

ivana māyava gèluvanāvanu ī jagatrayadi||18||

pāpakarmava sahisuvaḍè lakṣmīpatigè samarāda divijaranu

ī payojabhavāṃḍadòl̤agè āvalli nā kāṇè

gòpaguruvina maḍadibhṛgunagacāpa mòdalādavaru māḍda

mahāparādhagal̤a èṇisidanè karuṇā samudra hari||19||

aṃguṭāgradi janisida amarataraṃgiṇiyu lokatrayagal̤a aghahiṃgisuval̤u

avyākṛtāśāṃta vyāpisida iṃgaḍala magal̤a òḍèyana

aṃgopāṃgagal̤ali ippa

amalānaṃta sumaṃgal̤aprada nāma pāvanamāl̤padenaridu||20||

kāmadhenu sukalpataru ciṃtāmaṇigal̤u

amareṃdra lokadi kāmitārthagal̤u īvavalladè sevè māl̤parigè

śrīmukuṃdana parama maṃgal̤anāma narakastharanu salahitu

pāmarara paṃḍitarènisi puruṣārtha kòḍutihudu||21||

manadòl̤agè suṃdara padārthava nènèdu kòḍè kaikòṃḍu

balu nūtana suśobhita gaṃdha surasopeta phalarāśi

dyunadi nivahagal̤aṃtè kòṭṭu avaranu sadā saṃtaisuvanu

sadguṇava kaddavara aghava kadivanu anaghanèṃdènisi||22||

cetanā cetana vilakṣaṇa nūtana padārthagal̤òl̤agè balunūtana

atisuṃdarakè suṃdara rasakè rasarūpa

jātarūpodara bhava ādyaròl̤u ātata pratima prabhāva

dharātal̤adòl̤u èmmòḍanè āḍutalippa nammappa||23||

taṃdè tāygal̤u tamma śiśuvigè baṃda bhayagal̤a pariharisi

nija maṃdiradi beḍidudanu ittu ādarisuvaṃdadali

hiṃdè muṃdè èḍabaladi òl̤ahòragè iṃdireśanu tannavaranu

èṃdèṃdu salahuvanu āgasadòl̤ ètta noḍidaru||24||

òḍala nèl̤alaṃdadali hari nammòḍanè tiruguvanu

òṃdarèkṣaṇa biḍadè bèṃbalavāgi bhaktādīnanèṃdènisi

taḍèva duritaughagal̤a kāmada kòḍuva sakaleṣṭagal̤a

saṃtata naḍèva nammaṃdadali navisu viśeṣa sanmahima||25||

biṭṭavara bhavapāśadiṃdali kaṭṭuvanu bahukaṭhiṇaniva

śiṣṭeṣṭanèṃdaridu anavarata sadbhakti pāśadali kaṭṭuvara

bhavakaṭṭu biḍisuva siṭṭinavanu ivanalla

kāmada kòṭṭukāvanu sakala saukhyavanu ihaparaṃgagal̤ali ||26||

kaṇṇigè èvèyaṃdadali kai mai tiṇṇigòdaguva tèradi

palgal̤u paṇṇu phalagal̤anagidu jihvègè rasavanīvaṃtè

puṇya phalava īvaṃdadali nuḍivèṇṇi nāṇmāoḍadòl̤u

lakṣmaṇana aṇṇanu òdaguva bhaktara avasarakè amaragaṇa sahita||27||

kòṭṭadanu kaikòṃba arèkṣaṇabiṭṭagala tannavara

duritagal̤a aṭṭuvanu dūradali duritāraṇya pāvakanu

bèṭṭa bènnili hòrisidavaròl̤u siṭṭu māḍidaneno hari

kaṃgèṭṭa surarigè sudhèyanu uṇisida muridanahitaranā||28||

kheda moda jayāpājaya mòdalāda doṣagal̤illa cinmaya sādaradi

tannaṃghrikamalava naṃbi stutisuvara kādukòṃḍiha

paramakaruṇa mahodadhiyu tannavaru māḍda

mahāparādhagal̤a noḍadalè salahuva sarvakāmadanu||29||

mīnakūrma varāha narapaṃcānana atul̤a śaurya

vāmana reṇukātmaja rāvaṇādiniśācaradhvaṃsi

dhenukāsuramathana tripurava hānigainisida nipuṇa

kalimukha dānavara saṃharisi dharmadi kāyda sujanaranā||30||

śrī manorama śamala varjita kāmitaprada

kairavadal̤a śyāma śabala śaraṇya śāśvata śarkarākṣa sakha

sāmasannuta sakala guṇagaṇadhāma

śrī jagannātha viṭhalanu ī mahiyòl̤u avatarisi salahida sakala sujanaranā||31||
