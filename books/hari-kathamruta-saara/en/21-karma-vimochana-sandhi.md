---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಕರ್ಮ ವಿಮೋಚನ ಸಂಧಿ / Karma vimochana sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè

parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

mūla nārāyaṇanu māyā lola anaṃta avatāra nāmaka

vyāl̤a rūpa jayā ramaṇana āvèśanu ènisuvana

līlègaiva anaṃta cetana jāladòl̤u pradyumna

brahmāṃḍa ālayada òl̤a hòragè nèlèsiha śāṃti aniruddha||1||

aidu kāraṇa rūpa ippattaidu kāryagal̤u ènisuvavu

āraidu rūpadi ramisutippanu ī carācaradi

bheda varjita mūrjagadjanmādi kāraṇa muktidāyaka

svaudaradòl̤iṭṭu èllaranu saṃtaipa sarvajña||2||

kārya kāraṇa kartṛgal̤òl̤u svabhāryariṃda òḍagūḍi

kapilācārya krīḍisutippa tannòl̤u tānè sveccèyali

preryanu alle ramābja bhavaru ārya rakṣisi śikṣisuvanu

svavīryadiṃdali divija dānava tatiya dinadinadi||3||

ī samasta jagattinòl̤agè ākāśadòl̤u irutippa

vyāptāveśa avatārāṃtarātmakanāgi paramātma

nāśa rahita jagattinòl̤agè avakāśadanu tānāgi

yogīśāśaya sthita tannòl̤u èllaraniṭṭu salahuvanu||4||

dāru pāṣāṇagata pāvaka berè berè ippaṃtè

kāraṇa kāryagal̤a òl̤agiddu kāraṇa kāryanu èṃdènisi

torikòl̤l̤adè èllaròl̤u vyāpāra māḍuva

yogyatègal̤a anusāra phalagal̤a uṇisi saṃtaisuva kṛpāsāṃdra||5||

ūrmigal̤òl̤ippa karma vikarma janya phala aphalaṃgal̤a

nirmalātmanu māḍi māḍisi uṃḍu uṇisutippa

nirmama nirāmaya nirāśraya dharmavitu dharmātma dharmaga

durmatī janara òllanu apratimalla śrīnalla||6||

jalada vaḍabānal̤agal̤u aṃbudhi jalavanu uṃbuvavu

abda mal̤ègarèdil̤igè śāṃtiyanīvudu analanu tāne bhuṃjipudu

til̤ivudu ī pariyalli lakṣmī nilaya guṇa kṛta karmaja

phalāphalagal̤u uṃḍu uṇisuvanu sāravāga sarva jīvarigè||7||

pustakagal̤a avalokisuta maṃtra stutigal̤a analenu

raviya udayāstamaya paryaṃta japagal̤a māḍi phalavenu

hṛstha paramātmanè samasta avasthègal̤òl̤iddu èllaròl̤agè

nirastakāmanu māḍi māḍipanu èṃdu til̤iyadava||8||

madya bhāṃḍava deva nadiyòl̤agè addi tòl̤èyalu nityadali

pariśuddhavu ahudè èṃdigādaru

hari padābjagal̤a buddhi pūrvaka bhajisadavagè viruddhavu ènisuvavèlla

karma samṛddhigal̤u duḥkhavanè kòḍutihavu adhama jīvarigè||9||

bhakti pūrvakavāgi muktāmukta niyāmakana

sarvodrikta mahimègal̤a anavarata kòṃḍāḍu marèyadalè

saktanāgadè lokavārtè prasaktigal̤anu īḍāḍi

śṛti smṛti ukta karmava māḍutiru hariyājñèyèṃdaridu||10||

lopavādaru sariye karmaja pāpapuṇyagal̤u èraḍu ninnanu lepisavu

niṣkāmakanu nīnāgi māḍutirè

sauparaṇi varavahana ninna mahāparādhagal̤a èṇisadalè

svargāpavargava kòṭṭu salahuva satata sukhasāṃdra||11||

svarata sukhamaya sulabha viśvaṃbhara viṣoka surāsurārcita caraṇa yuga

cāru aṃga śārnga śaraṇya jitamanyu

parama suṃdara tara parātpara śaraṇa jana suradhenu śāśvata karuṇi

kaṃjadal̤ākṣakāyènè kaṃgòl̤ipa śīghra||12||

nirmamanu nīnāgi karma vikarmagal̤u niraṃtaradi

sudharma nāmakagè arpisuta niṣkaluṣa nīnāgu

bharma garbhana janaka dayadali durmatigal̤anu kòḍadè

tannaya harmyadòl̤agiṭṭu èlla kāladi kāva kṛpèyiṃda||13||

kalpakalpadi śaraṇa jana varakalpavṛkṣanu

tanna nija saṃkalpada anusāradali kòḍutippanu phalāphalava

alpasukhada apekṣèyiṃda ahitalpana ārādhisadiru èṃdigu

śilpagana kai sikka śilèyaṃdadali saṃtaipa||14||

deśa bheda ākāśadaṃdadi vāsudevanu sarva bhūta nivāsiyènisi

carācarātmakanu èṃdu karèsuvanu

dveṣa sneha udāsīnagal̤illa ī śarīragal̤òl̤agè

avara upāsanagal̤aṃdadali phalavīvanu parabrahmha||15||

saṃcitāgāmigal̤a karma viriṃci janakana bhajisè kèḍuvavu

miṃcinaṃdadi pòl̤èva puruṣottama hṛdaya aṃbaradi

vaṃcisuva janaròlla śrīvatsāṃcita susadvakṣa

tā niṣkiṃcana priya suramunigeya śubhakāya||16||

kāladravya sukarma śuddhiya pel̤uvaru alparigè

avu nirmūla gaisuvavu alla pāpagal̤a èlla kāladali

taila dhārīyaṃta avana pada olaisi tutisadalè nityadi

bāliṣaru karmagal̤è tārakavèṃdu pel̤uvaru||17||

kamalasaṃbhava śarva śakrādi amararèllaru

ivana duratikrama mahimègal̤a manavacanadiṃ prāṃtagāṇadalè

śramitarāgi padābja kalpadrumada nèl̤ala āśrayisi

lakṣmī ramaṇa saṃtaisèṃdu prārthiparu ati bhakutiyiṃda||18||

vāricaravu ènisuvavu dardura tārakagal̤èṃdaridu

bhekavanu eri jaladhiya dāṭuvènèṃbuvana tèradaṃtè

tāratamya jñāna śūnyaru sūrigamyana til̤iyalariyadè

sauraśaiva matānugara anusarisi kèḍutiharu||19||

kṣoṇipati sutanènisi kaiduggāṇigòḍḍuva tèradi

sumanasa dhenu manèyòl̤agiralu gomaya bayasuvaṃdadali

veṇugānapriyana ahika sukhānubhava beḍadalè

lakṣmī prāṇanāthana pāda bhakutiya beḍu kòṃḍāḍu||20||

kṣudhèya gosuga pogi kānana badari phalagal̤a apekṣèyiṃdali

pòdèyòl̤agè siga biddu bāydèrèdavana tèradaṃtè

vidhipitana pūjisadè ninnaya udara gosuga

sādhuliṃga pradarśakara ārādhisuta bal̤aladiru bhavadòl̤agè||21||

jñāna jñeya jñātṛvèṃba abhidhānadiṃ buddhādigal̤a adhiṣṭhānadali nèlèsiddu

karèsuta tattadāhvayadi

bhānumaṃḍalaga pradarśaka tānènisi vaśanāguvanu

śuka śaunakādi munīṃdra hṛdayākāśagata caṃdra||22||

udaya vyāpisi darśa paurṇima adhika yāmavu śravaṇa abhijitu

sadanavaidirè māl̤pa tèradaṃdadali harisevè

vidhi niṣedhagal̤u enu noḍadè vidhisutiru

nityadali tannaya sadanadòl̤agiṃbiṭṭu salahuva bhaktavatsalanu||23||

naṃdivāhana rātri sādhanè baṃda dvādaśi paitṛka saṃdhisiha samayadali

śravaṇava tyajisuvaṃtè sadā

niṃdyariṃdali baṃda dravyava kaṇdèrèdu noḍadalè

śrīmadānaṃdatīrtha aṃtargatana sarvatra bhajisutiru||24||

śrī manorama meru trikakuddhāma satkalyāṇaguṇa nissīma

pāvananāma divijoddhāma raghurāma

premapūrvaka nitya tanna mahā mahimègal̤a tutisuvarigè

sudhāmagè òlidaṃdadali akhil̤ārthagal̤a kòḍutippa||25||

taṃdè tāygal̤a kuruhanariyada kaṃda deśāṃtaradòl̤agè tannaṃdadali

ippavara jananī janakaranu kaṃḍu

hiṃdèyènnanu paḍèda avaru ī yaṃdadalipparo ala

nānu avara èṃdu kāṇuvènènuta huḍukuva tèradi kovidaru||26||

śruti purāṇa samūhadòl̤u bhārata prati prati padagal̤òl̤u

nirjitana guṇa rūpagal̤a puḍukuta parama haruṣadali

matimataru pratidivasa sārasvata samudradi

śaphariyaṃdadi satata saṃcarisuvaru kāṇuva lavalavikèyiṃda||27||

matsyaketana janaka hari śrīvatsa lāṃchana nija śaraṇa janavatsala

varāroha vaikuṃṭhaālaya nivāsi

citsukhaprada salahènalu govatsa dhvanigòdaguva tèradi

paramotsāhadi baṃdòdagavunu nirmatsarara bal̤igè||28||

sūrigal̤igè samīpaga durācārigal̤igè èṃdèṃdu dūrāddūratara

durlabhanu ènisuvanu daitya saṃtatigè

sāri sārigè nènèvavara saṃsāravèṃba mahoragakè sarvāriyènisi

sadā susaukhyavanu īva śaraṇarigè||29||

cakra śaṃkha gadābjadhara duratikrama durāvāsa

vidhi śiva śakra sūryādya amara pūjya padābja nirlajja

śukra śiṣyara aśvamedhā prakriyava kèḍisi

abjajāṃḍava atikramisi jāhnaviya paḍèda trivikramāhvayanu||30||

śaktarènisuvarèlla hari vyatirikta suragaṇadòl̤agè

sarvodriktanu ènisuva sarvariṃdali sarva kāladali

bhakti pūrvakavāgi anya prasaktigal̤a nīḍāḍi

paramāsaktanu āgiru harikathāmṛta pāna viṣayadali||31||

praṇata kāmada bhakta ciṃtāmaṇi maṇimayābharaṇa bhūṣita

guṇi guṇa traya dūra varjita gahana sanmahima

èṇisa bhaktara doṣagal̤a kuṃbhiṇijèyāṇma śaraṇya

rāmārpaṇavènalu kaikòṃḍa śabariya phalava paramātma||32||

ballènèṃbuvarillavu ītana òllènèṃbuvarilla

lokadòl̤illadiha sthal̤avillavai ajñāta janarilla

bèlladaccina bòṃbèyaṃdadi èllaròl̤agirutippa

śrībhū nalla ivagè èṇèyilla apratimalla jagakèlla||33||

śabda gocara śārvarīkara abda vāhanananuja

yaduvaṃśābdhi caṃdrama nirupama sunissīma samitasama

labdhanāguva tannavagè prārabdha karmagal̤a uṇisi tīvradi

kṣubdha pāvakanaṃtè biḍadippanu dayāsāṃdra||34||

śrī viriṃci ādi amara vaṃdita ī vasuṃdharèyòl̤agè

devaki devi jaṭharadòl̤u avatarisidanu ajanu nararaṃtè

revatī ramaṇa anujanu svapadāvalaṃbigal̤anu salahi

daityāval̤iya saṃharisida jagannātha viṭhalanu||35||
