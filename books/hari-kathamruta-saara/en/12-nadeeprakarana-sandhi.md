---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ನಾಡೀಪ್ರಕರಣ ಸಂದಿ / Nadeeprakarana Sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè<br/>parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

vāsudevanu prāṇamukha tatva īśariṃdali sevè kaikòl̤uta<br/>ī śarīradòl̤u ippa mūvattāru sāvira ī sunāḍigal̤òl̤agè<br/>ī bhūmī sameta vihāragaiva<br/>para īśana amala sumūrtigal̤a ciṃtisuta higgutiru||1||

caraṇagal̤òl̤iha nāḍigal̤u hannèrèḍu sāvira madhya dehadòl̤u irutihavu hadinālku<br/>bāhugal̤òl̤agè īrèrèḍu śiradòl̤u āru sahasra ciṃtisi<br/>irul̤u hagalu abhimāni divijaranaritu<br/>upāsanègaivaru il̤èyòl̤u svarga vāsigal̤u||2||

bṛhatināmaka vāsudevanu vahisi strī rūpa<br/>doṣa virahita èppattèrèḍu sāvira nāḍigal̤òl̤agiddu<br/>druhiṇa mòdalāda amaragaṇa sanmahima<br/>sarva prāṇigal̤a mahāmahima saṃtaisuvanu saṃtata parama karuṇāl̤u||3||

nūru varṣakè divasa mūvattāru sāvira vahavu<br/>nāḍi śarīradòl̤agè initihavu èṃdaritu òṃdu divasadali<br/>sūrigal̤a satkarisidava prati vāradali daṃpatigal̤a arcanè tā racisidava<br/>satya saṃśayavu illavèṃdèṃdu||4||

catura viṃśati tattvagal̤u tatpatigal̤u ènisuva<br/>brahma mukha devatègal̤u anudina prati prati nāḍigal̤òl̤u irutiddu<br/>catura daśa lokadòl̤u jīva pratatigal̤a saṃrakṣisuva<br/>śāśvatana tattat sthānadali noḍutale modiparu||5||

satya saṃkalpanu sadā èppattèrèḍu sahasradòl̤u<br/>mūvattu nālku lakṣada aivattāru sahasra<br/>citprakrutiya òḍagūḍi paramanu nitya maṃgal̤amūrti<br/>bhaktara tèttiganu tānāgi sarvatradali saṃtaipa||6||

maṇigal̤òl̤agiha sūtradaṃdadi praṇava pādyanu<br/>sarva cetana gaṇadòl̤iddu anavarata saṃtaisuvanu tannavara<br/>praṇata kāmada bhakta ciṃtāmaṇi cidānaṃdaika dehanu<br/>aṇu mahadgatanu alparopādiyali nèlèsippa||7||

ī suṣumna ādi akhil̤a nāḍī kośa nābhī mūladali<br/>vṛṣaṇā sadana madhyadalippadu tuṃdi nāmadali<br/>ā sarojāsana mukharu mūleśana anaṃdādi suguṇa upāsanèya gaivutali<br/>dehadòl̤agè irutiharu||8||

sūrigal̤u cittaisuvudu bhāgīrathiyè mòdalāda tīrthagal̤u<br/>eradhika èppattu sāvira nāḍigal̤òl̤ihavu<br/>ī rahasyavanu alpa janarigè tori pel̤adè<br/>nāḍi nadiyòl̤u dhīraru anudina majjanava māḍutale sukhisuvaru||9||<br/>til̤ivudu ī dehadòl̤agiha èḍa balada nāḍigal̤òl̤agè divijaru<br/>jalaja saṃbhava vāyu vāṇi ādigal̤u baladalli<br/>èlaruṇiga vihaga iṃdra cal̤ivèṭṭal̤iya ṣaṇmahiśiyaru<br/>vāruṇi kuliśa dhara kāmādigal̤u èḍa bhāgadòl̤iharu||10||

ikkaladòl̤iha nāḍiyòl̤u devarkal̤iṃda òḍanāḍutali<br/>pòṃbakki deranu jīvara adhikārānusāradali<br/>takka sādhanava māḍi māḍisuta akkaradi saṃtaipa bhaktara<br/>dakkagòḍanu asurargè satpuṇyagal̤anu apaharipa||11||

tuṃdiviḍidu āśirada pariyaṃta òṃdu vyāpisihudu<br/>tāvara kaṃdanihanu adaròl̤agè adakè īraidu śākhègal̤u<br/>òṃdadhika daśa karaṇadòl̤u saṃbaṃdhagaidihavalli<br/>ravi śaśi siṃdhu nāsatyādigal̤u nèlègòṃḍiharu satata||12||

pòkkal̤adiviḍidu òṃde nāḍiyu sukkadalè dhārāl̤a rūpadi<br/>sikkihudu ī dehadòl̤agè suṣumna nāmadali<br/>rakkasaranu òl̤a pogagòḍadè daśa dikkinòl̤agè samīra devanu<br/>lèkkisadè mattòbbaranu saṃcaripa dehadòl̤u||13||

initu nāḍī śākhègal̤u tanuvinòl̤agè ihavèṃdaridu ekātmanu<br/>dvi saptati sāvira ātmakanu āgi nāḍiyòl̤u<br/>vanitèyiṃda òḍagūḍi nārāyaṇa divā rātrigal̤ali<br/>ī pari vanajāṃḍadòl̤u akhil̤a jīvaròl̤iddu mohisuva||14||

dinadinadi vardhisuva kumudāptana mayūkhada sòbaga<br/>gata locana vilokisi moda paḍaballanè niraṃtaradi<br/>kunaragè ī sukatha amṛtada bhojanada sukha dòrakuvude<br/>lakṣmī manoharana sadguṇava kīrtipa bhakutagè alladalè||15||

ī tanuvinòl̤agihavu ota prota rūpadi nāḍigal̤u<br/>puruhūta mukharu alliharu tammiṃda adhikāra òḍagūḍi<br/>bhītigòl̤isuta dānavara saṃghāta nāmaka hariya guṇa<br/>saṃprītiyali sadupāsanèya gaivutalè modiparu||16||

jalāṭa kukkāṭa khāṭa jīvara kal̤evaragal̤òl̤u iddu kāṇisikòl̤adè<br/>tattadrūpa nāmagal̤iṃda karèsutali<br/>jalaruhekṣaṇa vividha karmagal̤a anudinadi māḍi<br/>tattatphalagal̤a uṇṇadè saṃcarisuvanu nitya sukha pūrṇa||17||

til̤idupāsanègaivuta īpari malinanaṃtiru durjanara kaṃgal̤igè gocarisadè<br/>vipaścitara òḍavè garvisadè<br/>mal̤è bisilu hasi tṛṣi jayājaya khal̤ara niṃdyāniṃdya bhayagal̤igè<br/>al̤ukadalè maddānèyaṃdadi carisu dharèyòl̤agè||18||

kānana grāmastha sarva prāṇigal̤u prati divasadali<br/>enenu māḍuva karmagal̤u hari pūjèyaṃdaridu denisuta<br/>sadbhaktiyali pavamānavaṃdita devāṃtarātmaka<br/>śrīnivāsanigè arpisuta modisuta nalivutiru||19||

nokanīyanu lokadòl̤u śunisūkarādigal̤òl̤agè nèlèsiddu<br/>ekamevādvitīya bahurūpāhvayagal̤iṃda<br/>tā karèsuta òl̤agiddu til̤isadè śrī kamalabhava mukhya sakala<br/>divaukasagaṇa ārādhya kaikòṃḍu anavarata pòrèva||20||

initu upāsanègaivutiha sajjanaru saṃsāradali pratiprati dinagal̤ali<br/>enenu māḍuvudu èlla haripūjè ènisikòṃbudu satyavu<br/>ī mātinali saṃśayapaḍuva naranu alpanu suniścaya<br/>bāhya karmava māḍi phalavenu||21||

bhogya bhokṭruga l̤òl̤agè hari tā bhogya bhoktanu ènisi<br/>yogyāyogya rasagal̤a deva dānava gaṇakè uṇisuvanu<br/>bhāgyanidhi bhaktarigè sadvairāgya bhakti jñānavīva<br/>ayogyarigè dveṣādigal̤a tannalli kòḍutippa||22||

ī caturdaśa bhuvanadòl̤agè carācarātmaka jīvaralli<br/>virocanātmaja vaṃcakanu nèlèsiddu dinadinadi<br/>yācakanuyèṃdènisikòṃba marīcirdamana suhaṃsa rūpa<br/>niṣecaka āhvayanu āgi janarabhilāṣè pūraipa||23||

annadannādannamaya svayaṃ anna brahmādi akhil̤a cetanakè<br/>anna kalpakanāhanu aniruddhādi rūpadali<br/>anyaranu apekṣisadè guṇa kāruṇya sāgara sṛṣṭisuvanu<br/>hiraṇya garbhanòl̤iddu pālisuvanu jagatrayava||24||

tripada tridaśādhyakṣa tristha tripathagāmini pita trivikrama<br/>kṛpaṇa vatsala kuvalaya dal̤a śyāma nissīma<br/>aparimita citsukha guṇātmaka vapuṣa vaikuṃṭha ādi lokādhipa<br/>trayīmaya tannavara niṣkapaṭadiṃ pòrèva||25||

lavaṇa miśrita jalavu torpadu lavaṇadopādiyali jihvègè<br/>vivaragaisalu śakyavāguvadeno nol̤parigè<br/>svavaśa vyāpiyènisi lakṣmīdhava carācaradòl̤agè tuṃbihanu<br/>aviditana sākalya ballavaru āru suraròl̤agè||26||

vyāpyanaṃdadi sarvajīvaròl̤ippa cinmaya ghora bhava<br/>saṃtapya mānaru bhajisè bhakutiyal̤iṃdali ihaparadi prāpyanāguvanu<br/>avaravaguṇagal̤a òppugòṃbanu bhaktavatsala tappisuva<br/>janmādi doṣagal̤a avarigè anavarata||27||

halavu bagèyali hariya manadali òlisi nillisi<br/>enu māḍuva kèlasagal̤u avanaṃghri pūjègal̤u èṃdu nènèvutiru<br/>haladharanuja tāne sarva sthal̤agal̤ali nèlèsiddu<br/>niścaṃcala bhakuti sujñāna bhāgyava kòṭṭu saṃtaipa||28||

doṣa gaṃdha vidūra nānā veṣa dhāraka ī jagatraya poṣaka<br/>purātana puruṣa puruhūta mukha vinuta<br/>śeṣavara pariyaṃka śayana vibhīṣaṇa priya vijaya sakha<br/>saṃtoṣabaḍisuva janarigè iṣṭārthagal̤a pūraisi||29||

śrī mahī sevita padāṃbuja bhūma sadbhakti aika labhya<br/>pitāmahādi amarāsura pāda paṃkaja<br/>vāma vāmana rāma saṃsāramaya auṣadha he mama kulasvāmi<br/>saṃtaisènalu baṃdòdaguvanu karuṇāl̤u||30||

dunuja divijaròl̤iddu avaravara anusarisi karmagal̤a māl̤panu<br/>janana maraṇādi akhil̤a doṣa vidūra<br/>èmmòḍanè janisuvanu jīvisuva saṃrakṣaṇèya māḍuva èlla kāladi<br/>dhanava kāydiha sarpanaṃtè animitta bāṃdhavanu||31||

bisaruha āpta āgasadi tānu udayisalu vṛkṣaṃgal̤a nèl̤alu pasarisuvavu<br/>il̤èyòl̤u astamisalalla alle līnahavu<br/>śvasana mukhya amarāṃtarātmakana òṣadòl̤u irutippavu<br/>ī jagatraya basiròl̤u iṃbiṭṭu èlla karmava torpa nol̤parigè||32||

tribhuvana aika ārādhya lakṣmī subhuja yugal̤a ālaṃgitāṃga<br/>svabhu sukhātma suvarṇavarṇa suparṇa varavāhana<br/>abhayada anaṃtārka śaśi sannibha niraṃjana nityadali tanagè<br/>abhinamisuvarigè eva sarvārthagal̤a taḍèyadalè||33||

kavigal̤iṃdali til̤idu prātaḥ savana madhyaṃ dinavu<br/>sāyama savanagal̤a vasu rudraru ādityaròl̤u rājisuva<br/>pavananòl̤u kṛti jaya sumāyādhavana mūrtitrayava ciṃtisi<br/>divasavèṃba āhutigal̤iṃda arcisuta sukhisutiru||34||

catura viṃśati abdha vasu devatègal̤òl̤u pradyumnanu ippanu<br/>catura catvāriṃśatigal̤ali saṃkaruṣaṇākhya<br/>hutavaha akṣanòl̤ihanu māyapatiyu hadināradhika<br/>dvātriṃśati varuṣagal̤alippanu ādityanòl̤u sitakāya||35||

ṣoḍaśa uttara śata varuṣadali ṣoḍaśa uttara śata surūpadi<br/>krīḍisuva vasu rudraru ādityaròl̤u satisahita<br/>vrīḍavilladè bhajipa bhaktara pīḍisuta durita aughagal̤a<br/>dūra oḍisuta bal̤iyali biḍadè nèlèsippa bhayahāri||36||

mūradhika èṃbhattu sahasra ainūrippattu ènipa rūpadi<br/>torutippa divā niśādhiparòl̤agè nityadali<br/>bhāratī prāṇaròl̤agiddu nivārisuta bhaktara durita<br/>hiṃkāra nidhana prathama rūpadi pitṛgal̤anè pòrèva||37||

buddhi pūrvaka uttamottama śuddha ūrṇa aṃbarava<br/>paṃkadòl̤u aḍḍi tègèyalu lepavāguvade parīkṣisalu<br/>padmanābhanu sarvajīvaròl̤iddarenu<br/>guṇatrayagal̤iṃ baddhanu āguvaneno? nitya sukhātma cinmayanu||38||

sakala doṣa vidūra śaśi pāvaka sahasra anaṃta sūrya prakāra sannibha gātra<br/>lakumi kal̤atra suramitra<br/>vikhana sāṃḍadòl̤ippa brahmādi akhil̤a cetana gaṇakè<br/>tāne sakhanu ènisikòṃḍu akuṭila ātmakanu ippanu avaraṃtè||39||

deśa bhedagal̤alli ippa ākāśadopādiyali cetana rāśiyòl̤u nèlèsippanu<br/>avyavadhānadali niruta<br/>śrī sahita sarvatradi niravākāśa kòḍuvaṃdadali kòḍuta<br/>nirāśèyali sarvāṃtarātmaka śobhisuva sukhada||40||

śrī viriṃci ādi amaragaṇa saṃsevita aṃghri saroja<br/>ī jaḍa jīva rāśigal̤a òl̤a hòragè nèlèsiddu nityadali<br/>sāvakāśanu ènisi tanna kal̤evaradòl̤u iṃbiṭṭu salahuva<br/>deva devakī ramaṇa dānava haraṇa jitāmaraṇa||41||

māsa òṃdakè prati divasadali śvāsagal̤u ahavu<br/>aṣṭa catvāriṃśati sahasrādhika āru sulakṣa saṃkhyèyali<br/>haṃsanāmaka hariya ṣoḍaśa ī śatābdadi bhajisè òliva<br/>dayāsamudra kucelagè òlidaṃdadali dinadinadi||42||

sthūla dehadòl̤iddu varuṣakè el̤adhika èppattu lakṣada<br/>melè èppattāru sāvira śvāsa japagal̤anu<br/>gāl̤i devanu karuṇadali īrel̤u lokadòl̤ul̤l̤a<br/>cetana jāladòl̤u māḍuvanu trijagadvyāpta paramāpta||43||

īrèrèḍu dehagal̤òl̤agiddu samīra devanu śvāsa japa<br/>nānūru adhikavu āgippa èṃbhattāru sahasra tā racisuvanu divasa òṃdakè<br/>mūru vidha jīvaròl̤agiddu<br/>kharāri karuṇābalavu adèṃtuṭò pavana rāyanòl̤u||44||

śrīdhava jagannātha viṭhala tā dayadi vadanadòl̤u nuḍida upādiyali<br/>nā nuḍidènu alladè kel̤i budha janaru<br/>sādhu liṃga pradarśakaru niṣedhagaisidarè enahudu<br/>ènnaparādhavu enidaròl̤agè pel̤vudu til̤idu kovidaru||45||
