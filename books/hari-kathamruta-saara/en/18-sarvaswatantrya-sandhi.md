---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಕ್ರೀಡಾವಿಲಾಸ ಸಂಧಿ/Kreedavilasa sandhi / sarvaswatantrya sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè/

parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

śrīnivāsana caritègal̤a paramānurāgadi bèsègòl̤alu

muni śaunakādyarigè arupidanu sūtārya dayadiṃda||

pacana bhakṣaṇa gamana bhojana vacana maithuna śayana vīkṣaṇa

acalanā calana prayatnadi sādhyave janakè

śuci sadana dayadiṃda jīvara nicayadòl̤u tā niṃtu māḍuva

ucitānucita karmagal̤anèṃdaridu kòṃḍāḍu||1||

viṣṭara śrava dehadòl̤agè praviṣṭanāgi niraṃtaradi

bahu ceṣṭègal̤a māḍutirè kaṃḍu sajīviyènutiharu

hṛṣṭarāguvaru noḍi kaniṣṭaru èllaru sevè māl̤paru

biṭṭa kṣaṇadali kuṇapa samavèṃdaridu anupekṣiparu||2||

krīḍègosuga avaravara gati nīḍalosuga dehagal̤a kòṭṭu āḍuvanu sveccèyali

brahma īśādyaròl̤u pòkku

māḍuvanu vyāpāra bahu vidha mūḍha daityaròl̤iddu pratidina

keḍu lābhagal̤illavu idariṃda āva kāladali||3||

akṣara īḍyanu brahma vāyu tryakṣa surapāsura asuraròl̤u

adhyakṣanāgiddu èllaròl̤u vyāpāra māḍutiha

akṣayanu satyātmaka parāpekṣèyilladè

sarvaròl̤agè vilakṣaṇanu tānāgi lokava rakṣisutalippa||4||

śrī sarasvati bhāratī girijā śacī rati rohiṇī saṃjñā śata surūpādi

akhil̤a strīyaròl̤u strī rūpa vāsavāgiddèllarigè

viśvāsa tannali kòḍuva

avarabhilāṣègal̤a pūraisutippanu yogyatègal̤aritu||5||

kolu kudurèya māḍi āḍuva bālakara tèradaṃtè

lakṣmī lola svātaṃtrya guṇava brahmādyaròl̤agiṭṭu

līlègaivanu tannavarigè anukūlanāgiddu èlla kāladi

khullarigè pratikūlanāgiha prakaṭanāgadalè||6||

sauparṇi varavahana nānā rūpa nāmadi karèsuta avara samīpadalliddu

akhil̤a vyāpāragal̤a māḍuvanu

pāpa puṇyagal̤èrèḍu avara svarūpagal̤a anusarisi uṇipa

paropakāri pareśa pūrṇānaṃda jñāna ghana||7||

āhāra nidrā maithunagal̤a aharāhara bayasi bal̤aluva

lakṣmī mahitana mahā mahimègal̤anu èṃtariva nityadali

ahika saukhyava marèdu manadali grahisi śāstrārthagal̤a

paramotsāhadi kòṃḍāḍutalè maimarèdavarigalladalè||8||

baṃdhamokṣa pradana jñānavu maṃdamatigal̤igèṃtu dòrèvudu

biṃdu mātra sukhānubhava parvatakè sama duḥkhavèṃdu til̤iyadè

anya daivagal̤iṃda sukhava apekṣisuvaru

mukuṃdana ārādhanèya biṭṭavagè uṃṭè mukti sukha||9||

rāja tanna amātya karuṇadi naija janarigè kòṭṭu kārya niyojisuta

mānāpamānava māl̤pa tèradaṃtè

śrī janārdhana sarvaròl̤agè aparājitanu tānāgi

sarva prayojanava māḍisuta māḍuva phalakè gurimāḍi||10||

vāsudeva svataṃtrava saròjāsanādi amarāsurarigè īyalosuga ardhava tègèdu

adaròl̤ardhava caturbhāgagaisi

vaṃdanu śatavidha dvi paṃcāśatābjajagè

aṣṭa catvāriṃśad anilagitta vāṇī bhāratīgardha||11||

dvitīya pādava tègèdukòṃḍu ada śata vibhāgava māḍi

tā viṃśati umeśanòl̤iṭṭa iṃdranòl̤u aidadhika hattu

ratipanòl̤agè initiṭṭa akhil̤a devatègal̤òl̤agè īraidu

jīva pratatiyòl̤u daśa aidadhika nālvattu daityaròl̤u||12||

kāruṇika svātaṃtryatvava mūru vidhagaisi èraḍu tannòl̤u

nārigòṃdanu kòṭṭa svātaṃtryava sarvarigè dhāruṇipa tanna anugarigè

vyāpāra kòṭṭu guṇāguṇagal̤a vicāra māḍuva tèradi

triguṇa vyaktiyanè māl̤pa||13||

puṇya karmakè sahāyavāguva dhanyarigè kalyādi daityara

puṇya phalagal̤anīva divijara pāpa karma phalānya karmava māl̤parigè

anuguṇya janarigè kòḍuva

bahu kāruṇya sāgaranu ī tèradi bhaktaranu saṃtaipa||14||

nirupamagè sariyuṃṭèṃdu uccarisuvava tadbhaktaròl̤u matsarisuvava

guṇaguṇigal̤igè bhedagal̤a pel̤uvava

dara sudarśana ūrdhva puṃḍrava dharisuvaròl̤u dveṣisuva

hari caritègal̤a kel̤adalè logara vārtè kel̤uvava||15||

evamādī dveṣavul̤l̤a kujīvarèllaru daityarèṃbaru

kovidara vijñāna karmava noḍi niṃdiparu

deva devana biṭṭu yāvatjīva paryaṃtaradi tuccara sevèyiṃda

upajīvisuvaru ajñānakè òl̤agāgi||16||

kāma lobha krodha mada hiṃsāmaya anṛta kapaṭa

tridhāmana avatāragal̤a bhedāpūrṇa sukhabaddha

āmiṣa anivedita abhojyadi tāmasa annavanu uṃba tāmasa

śrī madāṃdhara saṃgadiṃdali tamavè vardhipudu||17||

jñāna bhakti virakti vinaya purāṇa śravaṇa śāstra ciṃtana

dāna śama dama yajña satya ahiṃsa bhūtadaya

dhyāna bhagavannāma kīrtana mauna japa tapa vrata

sutīrtha snāna maṃtra stotra vaṃdana sajjanara guṇavu||18||

leśa svātaṃtrya guṇavanu praveśagaisida kāraṇadi

guṇa doṣagal̤u toruvavu satyāsatya jīvaròl̤u

śvāsa bhojana pāna śayana vilāsa maithuna gamana haruṣa

kleṣa svapna suṣupti jāgratiyu ahavu cetanakè||19||

ardha tannòl̤agirisi ul̤idòṃdardhava vibhāgagaisi

vṛjina ardananu pūrvadali svātaṃtryava kòṭṭaṃtè

svardhunīpita kòḍuva avara sukha vṛddhi gosuga

brahma vāyu kapardi mòdalāda avaròl̤iddu avara yogyatèyanaritu||20||

haladharānuja māl̤pa kṛtyava til̤iyadè ahaṃkāradiṃda

ènnul̤idu vidhi niṣedha pātrarillavèṃbuvagè

phalagal̤a dvayakòḍuva daityara kaluṣa karmava biṭṭu puṇyava sèl̤èdu

tanūl̤agiṭṭu kramadiṃ kòḍuva bhaktarigè||21||

toyajāptana kiraṇa vṛkṣa chāya vyaktisuvaṃtè

kamaladal̤āyatākṣanu sarvaròl̤u vyāpisida kāraṇadi

heya sadguṇa karma torpavu nyāya kovidarigè

niraṃtara śrīyarasa sarvottamottamanu èṃdu pel̤uvaru||22||

mūla kāraṇa prakṛtiyènipa mahālakumi èllaròl̤agiddu sulīlègaivuta

puṇya pāpagal̤arpisalu patigè

pālagaḍalòl̤u bidda jala kīlālavu ènipudè

jīvakṛta karmāl̤i tadvatu śubhavènipavu èlla kāladali||23||

jñāna sukha bala pūrṇa viṣṇuvigè enu māl̤pavu triguṇa kārya

kṛśānuvina kṛmikavidu bhakṣipaduṃṭè lokadòl̤u

ī nal̤inajāṃḍavanu brahma īśāna mukhya surāsurara

kālānal̤anavòl̤ nuṃguvagè ī pāpagal̤a bhayavè||24||

moda śira dakṣiṇa supakṣa pramoda uttara pakṣavèṃdu

ṛgādi śrutigal̤u pel̤uvavu ānaṃdamaya harigè

moda vaiṣika sukha viśiṣṭa pramoda pāratrika sukhapradanu

āda kāraṇadiṃda moda pramodanu ènisidanu||25||

èṃdigādaru vṛṣṭiyiṃda vasuṃdharèyòl̤agippa

akhil̤a jaladiṃ siṃdhu vṛddhiyanu aiduvadè bāradirè baridahudè

kuṃdu kòratègal̤illadiha svānaṃda saṃpūrṇa svabhāvagè

baṃdu māḍuvadenu karmākarma janya phala||26||

deha vṛkṣadòl̤u èraḍu pakṣigal̤ihavu èṃdigu biḍadè parama snehadiṃdali

karmaja phalagal̤uṃba jīva khaga

śrī hariyu tā sārabhoktanu drohisuva kalyādi daitya samūhakè

īva viśiṣṭa pāpava leśavèllarigè||27||

dyumaṇi kiraṇava kaṃḍa mātradi timiravu oḍuva tèradi

lakṣmī ramaṇa noḍida mātradiṃda agha nāśavu aiduvadu

kamala saṃbhava mukhya èllā sumanasaròl̤u iha pāpa rāśiya

amaramukhanaṃdadali bhasmava māl̤pa hari tānu||28||

catura śata bhāgadi daśāṃśadòl̤u itara jīvarigīva

leśava ditija devakkal̤igè kòḍuva viśiṣṭa duḥkha sukha

mativihīna prāṇigal̤igè āhutiya sukha mṛti duḥkha

avara yogyatèyanaritu pipīlamaśakādigal̤igīva hari||29||

nitya nanirayāṃdhākhya kūpadi bhṛtyariṃdòḍagūḍi

punarāvṛtti varjita lokavaiduva kaliyu dveṣadali

satya lokādhipa caturmukha tatva devakkal̤a sahita

nijamuktiya aiduva hari padābjava bhajisi bhakutiyali||30||

vidhi niṣedhagal̤u èraḍu marèyadè madhu virodhiya pādakarpisu

aditi makkal̤igīva puṇyava pāpa daityarigè

sudarśana dharègè īyadirè baṃdòdagi òyvaru puṇya daityaru

adhiparillada vṛkṣagal̤a phaladaṃtè nityadali||31||

tilaja kalmaśa tyajisi dīpavu til̤iya tailava grahisi

maṃdiradòl̤agè vyāpisippa kattalè bhaṃgisuva tèradi

kali mòdalugòṃḍa akhil̤a dānava kulajaru anudina māl̤pa puṇyaja phalava

brahmādyarigè kòṭṭu allalle ramisuvanu||32||

iddalèyu nityadali medhyāmedhya vastugal̤uṃḍu

lokadi śuddha śuciyèṃdènisi kòṃbanu veda smṛtigal̤òl̤u

buddhipūrvakavāgi vibudharu śraddhèyiṃda arpisida karma

niṣiddhavādaru sariye kaikòṃḍu uddharisutippa||33||

òḍèyaridda vanastha phalagal̤a baḍidu tiṃbuvaruṃṭè

kaṃḍarè hòḍèdu bisuṭuvarèṃba bhayadiṃ noḍalaṃjuvaru

biḍadè māḍuva karmagal̤a manè maḍadi makkal̤u baṃdhugal̤u

kāròḍalana āl̤gal̤èṃda mātradali oḍuvavu durita||34||

jñāna karma iṃdriyagal̤iṃda enenu māḍuva karmagal̤a

lakṣmī nivāsanigè arpisutaliru kālakāladali

prāṇa pati kaikòṃḍu nānā yoniyaidisanu

òmmè kòḍadirè dānavaru sèl̤èdòyvaru èllā puṇya rāśigal̤a||35||

śruti smṛti arthava til̤idu ahaṃmati viśiṣṭanu karma māḍalu

pratigrahisanu pāpagal̤anu kòḍutippa nitya hari

catura daśa bhuvana adhipati kṛta kṛta kṛtajña niyāmakanuyènè

matibhraṃśa pramāda saṃkaṭa doṣavāgilla||36||

vārijāsana mukhyaru ājñādhārakaru sarva svataṃtra ramāramaṇanu

èṃdaridu iṣṭāniṣṭa karmaphala

sārabhoktanigè arpisalu svīkāra māḍuva

pāpaphalava kubera nāmaka daityarigè kòṭṭu avara noyisuva||37||

krūra daityaròl̤iddu tāne prerisuva kāraṇadi harigè kuberanèṃbaru

èllaròl̤u nirgata ratigè nirati

sūri gamyagè sūryanèṃbaru dūra śokagè śukla liṃga śarīra

illada kāraṇadi akāyanènisuvanu||38||

pel̤alu vaśavallada mahā pāpāl̤igal̤anu òṃde kṣaṇadi nirmūlagaisalu beku

èṃbuvagè òṃde harināma

nāligèyòl̤ul̤l̤avagè parama kṛpāl̤u kṛṣṇanu kaiviḍidu

tanna ālayadòl̤iṭṭu anudinadi ānaṃda paḍisuvanu||39||

rogi auṣadha pathyadiṃda nirogiyènisuva tèradi

śrīmadbhāgavata suśravaṇagaidu bhavākhya rogavanu nīgi

śabdhādi akhil̤a viṣaya niyogisu daśa iṃdriya anilanòl̤u

śrī guru jagannātha viṭhala prītanāguvanu||40||
