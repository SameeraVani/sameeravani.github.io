title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಕ್ರೀಡಾವಿಲಾಸ ಸಂಧಿ/Kreedavilasa sandhi / sarvaswatantrya sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè/<br/>parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

śrīnivāsana caritègal̤a paramānurāgadi bèsègòl̤alu<br/>muni śaunakādyarigè arupidanu sūtārya dayadiṃda||

pacana bhakṣaṇa gamana bhojana vacana maithuna śayana vīkṣaṇa<br/>acalanā calana prayatnadi sādhyave janakè<br/>śuci sadana dayadiṃda jīvara nicayadòl̤u tā niṃtu māḍuva<br/>ucitānucita karmagal̤anèṃdaridu kòṃḍāḍu||1||

viṣṭara śrava dehadòl̤agè praviṣṭanāgi niraṃtaradi<br/>bahu ceṣṭègal̤a māḍutirè kaṃḍu sajīviyènutiharu<br/>hṛṣṭarāguvaru noḍi kaniṣṭaru èllaru sevè māl̤paru<br/>biṭṭa kṣaṇadali kuṇapa samavèṃdaridu anupekṣiparu||2||

krīḍègosuga avaravara gati nīḍalosuga dehagal̤a kòṭṭu āḍuvanu sveccèyali<br/>brahma īśādyaròl̤u pòkku<br/>māḍuvanu vyāpāra bahu vidha mūḍha daityaròl̤iddu pratidina<br/>keḍu lābhagal̤illavu idariṃda āva kāladali||3||

akṣara īḍyanu brahma vāyu tryakṣa surapāsura asuraròl̤u<br/>adhyakṣanāgiddu èllaròl̤u vyāpāra māḍutiha<br/>akṣayanu satyātmaka parāpekṣèyilladè<br/>sarvaròl̤agè vilakṣaṇanu tānāgi lokava rakṣisutalippa||4||

śrī sarasvati bhāratī girijā śacī rati rohiṇī saṃjñā śata surūpādi<br/>akhil̤a strīyaròl̤u strī rūpa vāsavāgiddèllarigè<br/>viśvāsa tannali kòḍuva<br/>avarabhilāṣègal̤a pūraisutippanu yogyatègal̤aritu||5||

kolu kudurèya māḍi āḍuva bālakara tèradaṃtè<br/>lakṣmī lola svātaṃtrya guṇava brahmādyaròl̤agiṭṭu<br/>līlègaivanu tannavarigè anukūlanāgiddu èlla kāladi<br/>khullarigè pratikūlanāgiha prakaṭanāgadalè||6||

sauparṇi varavahana nānā rūpa nāmadi karèsuta avara samīpadalliddu<br/>akhil̤a vyāpāragal̤a māḍuvanu<br/>pāpa puṇyagal̤èrèḍu avara svarūpagal̤a anusarisi uṇipa<br/>paropakāri pareśa pūrṇānaṃda jñāna ghana||7||

āhāra nidrā maithunagal̤a aharāhara bayasi bal̤aluva<br/>lakṣmī mahitana mahā mahimègal̤anu èṃtariva nityadali<br/>ahika saukhyava marèdu manadali grahisi śāstrārthagal̤a<br/>paramotsāhadi kòṃḍāḍutalè maimarèdavarigalladalè||8||

baṃdhamokṣa pradana jñānavu maṃdamatigal̤igèṃtu dòrèvudu<br/>biṃdu mātra sukhānubhava parvatakè sama duḥkhavèṃdu til̤iyadè<br/>anya daivagal̤iṃda sukhava apekṣisuvaru<br/>mukuṃdana ārādhanèya biṭṭavagè uṃṭè mukti sukha||9||

rāja tanna amātya karuṇadi naija janarigè kòṭṭu kārya niyojisuta<br/>mānāpamānava māl̤pa tèradaṃtè<br/>śrī janārdhana sarvaròl̤agè aparājitanu tānāgi<br/>sarva prayojanava māḍisuta māḍuva phalakè gurimāḍi||10||

vāsudeva svataṃtrava saròjāsanādi amarāsurarigè īyalosuga ardhava tègèdu<br/>adaròl̤ardhava caturbhāgagaisi<br/>vaṃdanu śatavidha dvi paṃcāśatābjajagè<br/>aṣṭa catvāriṃśad anilagitta vāṇī bhāratīgardha||11||

dvitīya pādava tègèdukòṃḍu ada śata vibhāgava māḍi<br/>tā viṃśati umeśanòl̤iṭṭa iṃdranòl̤u aidadhika hattu<br/>ratipanòl̤agè initiṭṭa akhil̤a devatègal̤òl̤agè īraidu<br/>jīva pratatiyòl̤u daśa aidadhika nālvattu daityaròl̤u||12||

kāruṇika svātaṃtryatvava mūru vidhagaisi èraḍu tannòl̤u<br/>nārigòṃdanu kòṭṭa svātaṃtryava sarvarigè dhāruṇipa tanna anugarigè<br/>vyāpāra kòṭṭu guṇāguṇagal̤a vicāra māḍuva tèradi<br/>triguṇa vyaktiyanè māl̤pa||13||

puṇya karmakè sahāyavāguva dhanyarigè kalyādi daityara<br/>puṇya phalagal̤anīva divijara pāpa karma phalānya karmava māl̤parigè<br/>anuguṇya janarigè kòḍuva<br/>bahu kāruṇya sāgaranu ī tèradi bhaktaranu saṃtaipa||14||

nirupamagè sariyuṃṭèṃdu uccarisuvava tadbhaktaròl̤u matsarisuvava<br/>guṇaguṇigal̤igè bhedagal̤a pel̤uvava<br/>dara sudarśana ūrdhva puṃḍrava dharisuvaròl̤u dveṣisuva<br/>hari caritègal̤a kel̤adalè logara vārtè kel̤uvava||15||

evamādī dveṣavul̤l̤a kujīvarèllaru daityarèṃbaru<br/>kovidara vijñāna karmava noḍi niṃdiparu<br/>deva devana biṭṭu yāvatjīva paryaṃtaradi tuccara sevèyiṃda<br/>upajīvisuvaru ajñānakè òl̤agāgi||16||

kāma lobha krodha mada hiṃsāmaya anṛta kapaṭa<br/>tridhāmana avatāragal̤a bhedāpūrṇa sukhabaddha<br/>āmiṣa anivedita abhojyadi tāmasa annavanu uṃba tāmasa<br/>śrī madāṃdhara saṃgadiṃdali tamavè vardhipudu||17||

jñāna bhakti virakti vinaya purāṇa śravaṇa śāstra ciṃtana<br/>dāna śama dama yajña satya ahiṃsa bhūtadaya<br/>dhyāna bhagavannāma kīrtana mauna japa tapa vrata<br/>sutīrtha snāna maṃtra stotra vaṃdana sajjanara guṇavu||18||

leśa svātaṃtrya guṇavanu praveśagaisida kāraṇadi<br/>guṇa doṣagal̤u toruvavu satyāsatya jīvaròl̤u<br/>śvāsa bhojana pāna śayana vilāsa maithuna gamana haruṣa<br/>kleṣa svapna suṣupti jāgratiyu ahavu cetanakè||19||

ardha tannòl̤agirisi ul̤idòṃdardhava vibhāgagaisi<br/>vṛjina ardananu pūrvadali svātaṃtryava kòṭṭaṃtè<br/>svardhunīpita kòḍuva avara sukha vṛddhi gosuga<br/>brahma vāyu kapardi mòdalāda avaròl̤iddu avara yogyatèyanaritu||20||

haladharānuja māl̤pa kṛtyava til̤iyadè ahaṃkāradiṃda<br/>ènnul̤idu vidhi niṣedha pātrarillavèṃbuvagè<br/>phalagal̤a dvayakòḍuva daityara kaluṣa karmava biṭṭu puṇyava sèl̤èdu<br/>tanūl̤agiṭṭu kramadiṃ kòḍuva bhaktarigè||21||

toyajāptana kiraṇa vṛkṣa chāya vyaktisuvaṃtè<br/>kamaladal̤āyatākṣanu sarvaròl̤u vyāpisida kāraṇadi<br/>heya sadguṇa karma torpavu nyāya kovidarigè<br/>niraṃtara śrīyarasa sarvottamottamanu èṃdu pel̤uvaru||22||

mūla kāraṇa prakṛtiyènipa mahālakumi èllaròl̤agiddu sulīlègaivuta<br/>puṇya pāpagal̤arpisalu patigè<br/>pālagaḍalòl̤u bidda jala kīlālavu ènipudè<br/>jīvakṛta karmāl̤i tadvatu śubhavènipavu èlla kāladali||23||

jñāna sukha bala pūrṇa viṣṇuvigè enu māl̤pavu triguṇa kārya<br/>kṛśānuvina kṛmikavidu bhakṣipaduṃṭè lokadòl̤u<br/>ī nal̤inajāṃḍavanu brahma īśāna mukhya surāsurara<br/>kālānal̤anavòl̤ nuṃguvagè ī pāpagal̤a bhayavè||24||

moda śira dakṣiṇa supakṣa pramoda uttara pakṣavèṃdu<br/>ṛgādi śrutigal̤u pel̤uvavu ānaṃdamaya harigè<br/>moda vaiṣika sukha viśiṣṭa pramoda pāratrika sukhapradanu<br/>āda kāraṇadiṃda moda pramodanu ènisidanu||25||

èṃdigādaru vṛṣṭiyiṃda vasuṃdharèyòl̤agippa<br/>akhil̤a jaladiṃ siṃdhu vṛddhiyanu aiduvadè bāradirè baridahudè<br/>kuṃdu kòratègal̤illadiha svānaṃda saṃpūrṇa svabhāvagè<br/>baṃdu māḍuvadenu karmākarma janya phala||26||

deha vṛkṣadòl̤u èraḍu pakṣigal̤ihavu èṃdigu biḍadè parama snehadiṃdali<br/>karmaja phalagal̤uṃba jīva khaga<br/>śrī hariyu tā sārabhoktanu drohisuva kalyādi daitya samūhakè<br/>īva viśiṣṭa pāpava leśavèllarigè||27||

dyumaṇi kiraṇava kaṃḍa mātradi timiravu oḍuva tèradi<br/>lakṣmī ramaṇa noḍida mātradiṃda agha nāśavu aiduvadu<br/>kamala saṃbhava mukhya èllā sumanasaròl̤u iha pāpa rāśiya<br/>amaramukhanaṃdadali bhasmava māl̤pa hari tānu||28||

catura śata bhāgadi daśāṃśadòl̤u itara jīvarigīva<br/>leśava ditija devakkal̤igè kòḍuva viśiṣṭa duḥkha sukha<br/>mativihīna prāṇigal̤igè āhutiya sukha mṛti duḥkha<br/>avara yogyatèyanaritu pipīlamaśakādigal̤igīva hari||29||

nitya nanirayāṃdhākhya kūpadi bhṛtyariṃdòḍagūḍi<br/>punarāvṛtti varjita lokavaiduva kaliyu dveṣadali<br/>satya lokādhipa caturmukha tatva devakkal̤a sahita<br/>nijamuktiya aiduva hari padābjava bhajisi bhakutiyali||30||

vidhi niṣedhagal̤u èraḍu marèyadè madhu virodhiya pādakarpisu<br/>aditi makkal̤igīva puṇyava pāpa daityarigè<br/>sudarśana dharègè īyadirè baṃdòdagi òyvaru puṇya daityaru<br/>adhiparillada vṛkṣagal̤a phaladaṃtè nityadali||31||

tilaja kalmaśa tyajisi dīpavu til̤iya tailava grahisi<br/>maṃdiradòl̤agè vyāpisippa kattalè bhaṃgisuva tèradi<br/>kali mòdalugòṃḍa akhil̤a dānava kulajaru anudina māl̤pa puṇyaja phalava<br/>brahmādyarigè kòṭṭu allalle ramisuvanu||32||

iddalèyu nityadali medhyāmedhya vastugal̤uṃḍu<br/>lokadi śuddha śuciyèṃdènisi kòṃbanu veda smṛtigal̤òl̤u<br/>buddhipūrvakavāgi vibudharu śraddhèyiṃda arpisida karma<br/>niṣiddhavādaru sariye kaikòṃḍu uddharisutippa||33||

òḍèyaridda vanastha phalagal̤a baḍidu tiṃbuvaruṃṭè<br/>kaṃḍarè hòḍèdu bisuṭuvarèṃba bhayadiṃ noḍalaṃjuvaru<br/>biḍadè māḍuva karmagal̤a manè maḍadi makkal̤u baṃdhugal̤u<br/>kāròḍalana āl̤gal̤èṃda mātradali oḍuvavu durita||34||

jñāna karma iṃdriyagal̤iṃda enenu māḍuva karmagal̤a<br/>lakṣmī nivāsanigè arpisutaliru kālakāladali<br/>prāṇa pati kaikòṃḍu nānā yoniyaidisanu<br/>òmmè kòḍadirè dānavaru sèl̤èdòyvaru èllā puṇya rāśigal̤a||35||

śruti smṛti arthava til̤idu ahaṃmati viśiṣṭanu karma māḍalu<br/>pratigrahisanu pāpagal̤anu kòḍutippa nitya hari<br/>catura daśa bhuvana adhipati kṛta kṛta kṛtajña niyāmakanuyènè<br/>matibhraṃśa pramāda saṃkaṭa doṣavāgilla||36||

vārijāsana mukhyaru ājñādhārakaru sarva svataṃtra ramāramaṇanu<br/>èṃdaridu iṣṭāniṣṭa karmaphala<br/>sārabhoktanigè arpisalu svīkāra māḍuva<br/>pāpaphalava kubera nāmaka daityarigè kòṭṭu avara noyisuva||37||

krūra daityaròl̤iddu tāne prerisuva kāraṇadi harigè kuberanèṃbaru<br/>èllaròl̤u nirgata ratigè nirati<br/>sūri gamyagè sūryanèṃbaru dūra śokagè śukla liṃga śarīra<br/>illada kāraṇadi akāyanènisuvanu||38||

pel̤alu vaśavallada mahā pāpāl̤igal̤anu òṃde kṣaṇadi nirmūlagaisalu beku<br/>èṃbuvagè òṃde harināma<br/>nāligèyòl̤ul̤l̤avagè parama kṛpāl̤u kṛṣṇanu kaiviḍidu<br/>tanna ālayadòl̤iṭṭu anudinadi ānaṃda paḍisuvanu||39||

rogi auṣadha pathyadiṃda nirogiyènisuva tèradi<br/>śrīmadbhāgavata suśravaṇagaidu bhavākhya rogavanu nīgi<br/>śabdhādi akhil̤a viṣaya niyogisu daśa iṃdriya anilanòl̤u<br/>śrī guru jagannātha viṭhala prītanāguvanu||40||<br/>harikathAmRutasAra gurugaLa karuNadindApanitu kELuve/<br/>parama BagavadBaktaru idanAdaradi kELuvudu||
