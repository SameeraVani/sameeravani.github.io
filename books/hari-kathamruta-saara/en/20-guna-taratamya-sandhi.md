---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಗುಣತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಸಂಧಿ / Guna Taratamya sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè<br/>parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

śrīdharā durgā manorama vedhamukha sumanasa gaṇa samārādhita padāṃbuja<br/>jagadaṃtar bahirvyāpta<br/>godhara phaṇipa varātapatra niṣedha śeṣa vicitra karma<br/>subodha sukhamaya gātra parama pavitra sucaritra||1||

nitya nirmala nigama vedaya utpatti sthiti laya dūravarjita<br/>stutya pūjya prasiddha muktāmukta gaṇa sevya<br/>satyakāma saśaraṇya śāśvata bhṛtyavatsala bhaya nivāraṇa<br/>atyadhika saṃpriyatama jagannātha māṃ pāhi||2||

parama puruṣana rūpa guṇava anusarisi kāṃbal̤u pravahadaṃdadi<br/>nirupamal̤u nirduṣṭa sukha saṃpūrṇal̤u ènisuval̤u<br/>harigè dhāmatrayal̤u ènisi ābharaṇa vasana āyudhagal̤u āgiddu<br/>arigal̤anu saṃharisuval̤u akṣaral̤u ènisikòṃḍu||3||

ītagiṃta anaṃta guṇadali śrī taruṇi tā kaḍimèyènipal̤u<br/>nityamuktal̤u nirvikāral̤u triguṇa varjital̤u<br/>dhauta pāpa viriṃci pavanara mātèyènipa mahālakumi<br/>vikhyātal̤u āgihal̤u èlla kāladi śruti purāṇadòl̤u||4||

kamala saṃbhava pavanarīrvaru samaru samavartigal̤u<br/>rudrādi amaragaṇa sevitaru aparabrahma nāmakaru<br/>yamal̤arigè mahālakṣmi tānuttamal̤u koṭi sajāti guṇadiṃda<br/>amita suvijāti adhamaru èniparu brahma vāyugal̤u||5||

patigal̤iṃda sarasvatī bhāratigal̤u adhamaru nūru guṇa parimita vijāti avararu<br/>bala jñānādi guṇadiṃda atiśayaru vāgdevi śrī bhāratigè<br/>padaprayukta vidhi mārutaravol̤ ciṃtipudu<br/>sadbhaktiyali kovidaru||6||

khagapa phaṇipati mruḍaru sama vāṇigè śataguṇa avararu mūvaru<br/>migilènisuvanu śeṣa padadiṃdali triyaṃbakagè<br/>nagadharana ṣaṇma hiṣiyaru pannaga vibhūṣaṇagaidu<br/>menaki magal̤u vāruṇi sauparṇigal̤igè adhikavu èraḍu guṇa||7||

garuḍa śeṣa maheṣarigè sauparaṇi vāruṇi pārvati mūraru daśādhama<br/>vāruṇigè kaḍimè ènisuval̤u gaurī<br/>harana maḍadigè hattu guṇadali surapa kāmaru kaḍimè<br/>iṃdragè kòratèyènisuva manmathanu padadiṃdali āvāga||8||

īraidu guṇa kaḍimè ahaṃkārika prāṇanu manoja nagārigal̤igè<br/>aniruddha rati manu dakṣa guru śaciyu āru jana sama<br/>prāṇaniṃdali hauraga èniparu hattu guṇadali<br/>mārajādyarigè aidu guṇadiṃda adhama pravahākhya||9||

guṇadvayadiṃ kaḍimè pravahagè ina śaśāṃkayama svayaṃbhuva manu maḍadi śatarūpa<br/>nālvaru pāda pādārdha vanadhi nīca<br/>pādārdha nārada munigè bhṛgu agni prasūtigal̤u<br/>ènisuvaru pādārdha guṇadiṃda adhamarahudèṃdu||10||

hutavahagè dviguṇa adhamaru vidhisuta marīcādigal̤u<br/>vaivasvatanu viśvāmitrarigè kiṃcid guṇādhamanu<br/>vrativara jaganmitra vara nirṛti prāvahi tārarigè<br/>kiṃcit guṇa adhama dhanapa viśvaksenaru ènisuvaru||11||

dhanapa viśvaksena gaurī tanayarigè ukta itararu samarènisuvaru<br/>èṃbhattaidu jana śeṣa śatarèṃdu<br/>dinaparāru el̤adhika nālvattanilaru el̤u vasu<br/>rudrarīraidu anitu viśve deva ṛbhu aśvinī pitṛ dharaṇī||12||

ivarigiṃtali kòratèyèniparu cyavana sanakādigal̤u<br/>pāvaka kavi uciththya jayaṃta kaśyapa manugal̤u èkadaśa<br/>dhruva nahuṣa śaśibiṃdu haihaya dauṣyaṃti virocanana nija kuvara<br/>bali mòdalāda sapta iṃdraru kakutstha gaya||13||

pṛthu bharata māṃdhāta priyavrata maruta prahlāda suparīkṣita<br/>hariścaṃdra aṃbarīṣa uttanapāda mukha<br/>śata supuṇya ślokaru gadā bhrutagè adhiṣṭhānaru<br/>supriyavratagè dviguṇa adhamaru karmajaru èṃdu karèsuvaru||14||

nal̤ini saṃjñā rohiṇī śyāmala virāṭ parjanyaru adhamaru<br/>yalaru mitrana maḍadi dviguṇa adhamal̤u bāṃbòl̤agè<br/>jala maya budha adhamanu dviguṇadi kèl̤agènisuval̤u uṣā<br/>śanaiścaral̤igè īru guṇādhamaru uṣā devi dyasiyiṃdā||15||

èraḍu guṇa karmādhipati puṣkara kaḍimè<br/>ājānu divijaru cira pitṛgal̤iṃda uttamaru kiṃkararu puṣkaragè<br/>surapanālaya gāyaka uttama èraḍaidu guṇadiṃdādhama<br/>tuṃburagè sama nūrukoṭi ṛṣigal̤u nūrujanarul̤idu||16||

avaravara patniyaru apsara yuvatiyaru sama<br/>uttamaranul̤idu avararèniparu manuja gaṃdharvaru dviṣaḍguṇadi<br/>kuvalayādhiparu īru aidu guṇa avanipa strīyaru<br/>daśottara navati guṇadiṃda adhamaru èniparu mānuṣottamaru||17||

satvasatvaru satvarājasa satvatāmasa mūvaru<br/>rajassatva adhikārigal̤u bhagavadbhaktaru ènisuvaru<br/>nitya baddharu rajorajaru utpatti bhūsvargadòl̤u<br/>narakadi pṛthviyòl̤u saṃcarisutipparu rajastāmasaru||18||

tamassātvikaru ènisikòṃbaru amitana ākhyāta asurara gaṇa<br/>tamo rājasaru ènisikòṃbaru daitya samudāya<br/>tamastāmasa kali puraṃdhriyu amita durguṇa pūrṇa<br/>sarvādhamaròl̤u adhamādhama durātmanu kaliyènisikòṃba||19||

ivana poluva pāpi jīvaru bhuvana mūvaròl̤illa noḍalu<br/>nava vidha dveṣagal̤igè ākāranu ènisikòl̤utippa<br/>bavaradòl̤u baṃgāradòl̤u naṭa yuvati dyūtā peya mṛṣadòl̤u<br/>kavisi mohadi kèḍisuvanuyèṃdaridu tyajisuvadu||20||

trividha jīva pratatigal̤a sagga āvòl̤èyāṇma ālayanu nirmisi<br/>yuvatiyaròḍagūḍi krīḍisuvanu kṛpāsāṃdra<br/>divija dānava tāratamyada vivara til̤iva mahātmarigè<br/>bānnavira sakha tānòlidu uddharisuvanu dayadiṃda||21||

deva daityara tāratamyavu pāvamāni matānugarigè idu kevala avaśyakavu<br/>til̤ivudu sarva kāladali<br/>dāvaśikhi pāpāṭavigè nava nāvèyènipudu bhava samudrakè<br/>pāvaṭigè vaikuṃṭha lokakè idèṃdu karèsuvudu||22||

tāratamya jñāna mukti dvāravu ènipudu bhakta janarigè tori pel̤i<br/>sukhābdhiyòl̤u lolyāḍuvudu budharu<br/>krūra mānavarigidu karṇa kaṭoravu ènipudu<br/>nityadali adhikārigal̤igidana arupuvudu dustarkigal̤a biṭṭu||23||

harisiriviriṃci īrabhārati garuḍa phaṇi pati ṣaṇmahiṣiyaru<br/>girijanāka īśa smara prāṇa aniruddha śacīdevī<br/>guru ratī manu dakṣa pravahā maruta mānavi yama śaśi divākara<br/>varuṇa nārada surāsya prasūti bhṛgu munipa||24||

vratatijāsana putrarènisuva vrativara marīci atri<br/>vaivasvatanu tārā mitra nirṛti pravaha mārutana sati<br/>dhana īśa aśvinigal̤a īrgaṇapatiyu viśvaksena śeṣanu śataru<br/>manugal̤u ucithya chāvaṇa munigal̤igè namipè||25||

śata supuṇya ślokaru ènisuva kṣitiparigè namisuvènu<br/>bāgīrathi virāṭ parjanya rohiṇi śyāmalā saṃjñāhuta vahana mahil̤ā<br/>budha uṣā kṣiti śanaiścara puṣkararigè<br/>ānatisi binnayisuvènu bhakti jñāna kòḍalèṃdu||26||

nūradhikavu āgippa mattè hadināru sāvira naṃda gopa kumārana<br/>ardhāṃgiyaru agastya āde munīśvararu<br/>ūrvaśī mòdalāda apsara nāriyaru śata tuṃburaru<br/>kaṃsāri guṇagal̤a kīrtanèya māḍisali ènniṃda||27||

pāvanaru śuci śuddha nāmaka devatègal̤u ājāna cira pitṛ<br/>deva nara gaṃdharvaru avanipa mānuṣottamaru<br/>ī vasumatiyòl̤u ul̤l̤a vaiṣṇavara aval̤iyòl̤u ihanèṃdu<br/>nityaḍi sevipudu saṃtoṣadiṃ sarva prakāradali||28||

mānuṣottamarana viḍidu caturānana aṃta śata uttamatva<br/>krameṇa ciṃtipa bhaktarigè catura vidha puruṣārtha<br/>śrīnidhi jagannātha viṭhala tāne òlidu īvanu<br/>niraṃtara sānurāgadi paṭhisuvudu saṃtarida marèyadalè||29||
