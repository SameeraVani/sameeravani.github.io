---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ದತ್ತಸ್ವಾತಂತ್ರ್ಯ ಸಂಧಿ / Dhatthasvathanthriya sandhi / SwAtantrnya vibhajane Sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè|

parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

kāruṇika hari tannòl̤ippa apāra svātaṃtrya guṇava nānūru tègèdu

sapāda āròṃdadhika aravattu nārigitta

dvi ṣoḍaśa adhika nūru pādatrayava tanna śarīradòl̤agè

īpari vibhāgava māḍi tripadāhva||1||

satya lokādhipanòl̤agè aivattèrèḍu pavamānanòl̤u nālvattu melèṃṭu

adhika śivanòl̤agiṭṭanippattu cittaja iṃdraròl̤u aidadhika daśa

tatvamānigal̤u ènipa suraròl̤u hattu

īraidu akhil̤a jīvaròl̤iṭṭa niravadya||2||

kali mòdalugòṃḍa akhil̤a dānavaròl̤agè nālvattaidu

ī pari til̤idu upāsanè māḍu marèyadè parama bhakutiyali

il̤èyòl̤agè saṃcarisu lakṣmī nilayana āl̤ānèṃdu

sarva sthal̤agal̤ali saṃtaisutippanu gèl̤èyanaṃdadali||3||

avanipa svāmitva dharmava svavaśa mātyarigittu

tā mattavara mukhadali rāja kāryāva māḍisuva tèradi

kavibhirīḍita tanna kal̤ègal̤a divija dānava mānavaròl̤iṭṭu

avirata guṇatrayaja karmava māḍi māḍisuva||4||

puṇya pāpagal̤u ī tèradi kāruṇya sāgara devadānava mānavaròl̤iṭṭu

avara phala vyatyāsavanè māḍi

banna baḍisuva bhaktihīnara sannuta sukarma phalatègèdu

prapannarigè kòṭṭu avara sukhapaḍisuvanu subhujāhva||5||

māṇikava kòṃḍu aṃgaḍiyòl̤u ajivāna kòṭṭu ā puruṣana

samādhāna māḍuva tèradi daityaru nityadali māl̤pa

dāna yajñādigal̤a phala pavamāna pitanu apaharisi

asamīcīna sukhagal̤a kòṭṭu asurara mattaranu māl̤pa||6||

eṇa lāṃchanana amala kiraṇa krameṇa vṛddhiyanu aidi

logara kāṇagòḍadiha kattalèya bhaṃgisuva tèradaṃtè

vainateyāṃgasana mūrti dhyānavul̤l̤a mahātmarigè

sujñāna bhaktyādigal̤u vardhisi sukhave kòḍutiharu||7||

janapana arikèya cora pòl̤alòl̤u dhanava kaddòyda īyalu

avana avaguṇagal̤u èṇisadè pòrèva kòḍadirè śikṣisuva tèradi

anucitocita karma kṛṣṇārpaṇavènalu

kaikòṃḍu tannaramanèyòl̤iṭṭu ānaṃda baḍisuva mādhavānatara||8||

annada annāda anna nāmaka munna pel̤da prakāra

jīvaròl̤u anna rūpa praveśagaidu avaravara vyāpāra

bannabaḍadalè māḍi māḍisi dhanyarivaru ahudu èṃdènisi

traiguṇya varjita tattadāhvayanu āgi karèsuvanu||9||

salilabiṃdu payābdhiyòl̤u bīl̤alu vikāravanu aida balladè

jalavu tadrūpavanè aiduvudu èlla kāladali

kalilamalāpahana arcisuva satkulajara kukarmagal̤u

tāniṣkaluṣa karmagal̤āgi puruṣārthagal̤a kòḍutiharu||10||

mògadòl̤agè mògaviṭṭu muddisi maguvinaṃ bigidappi raṃbisi

nehadi tègèdu tannaya stanagal̤u uṇisuva jananiyaṃdadali

agaṇitātmanu tanna pādābjagal̤a dhenipa bhakta janarigè

praghaṭakanu tānāgi saukhyagal̤u eva sarvatra||11||

toṭiganu bhūmiyòl̤u bījava nāṭabekèṃdènuta

hitadali moṭèyiṃ nīrètti sasigal̤a saṃtaisuvaṃtè

pāṭubaḍadalè jagadi jīvara ghoṭakāsyanu sṛjisi

yogyatè dāṭagòḍadalè salahutippanu sarva kāladali||12||

bhūmiyòl̤u jalavirè tṛṣārtanu tā marèdu

mògavètti èṇdèsèvyoma maṃḍaladòl̤agè kāṇadè miḍukuvaṃdadali

śrī manorama sarvara aṃtaryāmiyu āgirè til̤iyalariyadè

bhrāmakaru bhajisuvaru bhakutiyali anya devatèya||13||

mukhya phala vaikuṃṭha mukhyāmukhya phala mahadādi lokā

amukhya phala vaiṣayikavu èṃdaridu ati bhakutiyiṃda

rakkasa ariya bhajisutali nirduḥkhanāgu

niraṃtaradi mòrè pòkkavara biḍa bhṛtyavatsala bhāratīśa pita||14||

vyādhiyiṃ pīḍita śiśuvigè guḍodakava nèrèdu adakè auṣadha tedu

kuḍisuva tāyiya upādiyali

sarvajña bādarāyaṇa bhakta janakè prasāda rūpakanāgi

bhāgavatādiyali pel̤idanu dharmādigal̤e phalavèṃdu||15||

dūradalliha parvata ghanākāra torpudu nol̤pa janarigè

sāragaiyalu sarva vyāghragal̤iṃda bhayavihudu

ghoratara saṃsāra saukhya asārataravèṃdu aritu

nitya ramāramaṇana ārādhisuvaru adariṃda ballavaru||16||

kèsara ghaṭagal̤a māḍi besigè bisilòl̤agè iṭṭu òṇagisidaru adu

ghana rasavu tuṃbalu bahude? sarva svātaṃtra nānèṃba

paśupa naranu enenu māl̤pa anaśana dāna snāna karmagal̤u

òsari popavu baridè deha āyāsavanè kòṭṭu||17||

èraḍu dīkṣègal̤ihavu bāhyāṃtaravu ènipa nāmadali

budhariṃdaritu dīkṣitanu āgu dīrgha dveṣagal̤a biṭṭu

hariye sarvottama kṣarākṣara puruṣa pūjita pāda

janmādi aravi dūra sukhātma sarvaganu èṃdu smarisutiru||18||

heyavastugal̤illavu upādeya vastugal̤illa

nyāyānyāya dharmagal̤illa dveṣa asūyè mòdalilla

tāyi taṃdègal̤illa kamaladal̤āyata akṣagèyènalu

īsuva kāyiyaṃdadi mul̤agagòḍadè bhavābdhi dāṭisuva||19||

maṃdanādaru sariyè gopīcaṃdana śrī mudrègal̤a naliviṃda dharisuta

śrī tul̤asi padumākṣi saragal̤anu kaṃdharada madhyadali dharisi

mukuṃda śrī bhūramaṇa trijagadvaṃdya

sarva svāmi mama kuladaivavu ènè pòrèva||20||

prāya dhana madadiṃda janarigè nāyaka prabhuvèṃbi

pūrvadi tāyi pòṭṭèyòl̤iralu prabhuvèṃdekè karèyaralai?

kāya ninnanu biṭṭu pogalu rāyā nīnèṃbuva prabhutva

palāyanavanaiditu samīpadaliddarè adu toru||21||

vāsudeva eka prakāradi īśanènisuva

brahma rudra śaci īśa mòdalāda amararèllaru dāsarènisuvaru

ī sumārgava biṭṭu sohamupāsanèyagaiva nara

dehaja daiśika kleśagal̤u baralu avanu ekè biḍisikòl̤a||22||

ā para brahmanali trijagadvyāpakātva niyāmakātva

sthāpakātva vaśātva īśātvādi guṇagal̤igè lopavilla

eka prakāra svarūpavu ènipavu sarva kāladi popavallavu

jīvarigè dāsātvadupādi||23||

nityanūtana nirvikāra suhṛttama praṇavastha

varṇotpatti kāraṇa vāgmanaḥmaya sāmagānarata

datta kapila hayāsya rūpadi pṛth prutag jīvaròl̤agiddu pravartisuvanu

avaravara yogyatè karmava anusarisi||24||

śṛtigal̤u ātana mātu vimalā smṛtigal̤u ātana śikṣè

jīva pratati prakṛtigal̤u èraḍu pratimègal̤u ènisikòl̤utihavu

itara karmagal̤u èlla lakṣmī patigè pūjègal̤èṃdu smarisuta

catura vidha puruṣārthagal̤a beḍadiru svapnadali||25||

bhūtal̤ādhipanu ājña dhāraka dūtarigè

sevānusāradi vetanava kòṭṭu avara saṃtoṣisuva tèradaṃtè

mātariśva priyanu parama prīti pūrvaka

sadguṇaṃgal̤a gādhakara saṃtoṣa paḍisuva iha paraṃgal̤ali||26||

dīpa divadali kaṃḍarādaḍè lopagaisuvaru ā kṣaṇa

hari samīpadallirè naṃda nāma sunaṃdavu ènisuvavu

aupacārikavalla sujanara pāpa karmavu puṇyavu ènipavu

pāpigal̤a satpuṇya karmavu pāpavu ènisuvavu||27||

dhanava saṃpādisuva pradrāvaṇikaraṃdadadi

kovidara manèmanègal̤ali saṃcarisu śāstra śravaṇa gosugadi

mananagaidu upadeśisuta durjanara kūḍi āḍadiru svapnadi

praṇata kāmada kòḍuva saukhyagal̤a iha paraṃgal̤ali||28||

kāraka kriya dravya vibhrama mūru vidha jīvarigè

bahu saṃsārakè ivu kāraṇavu ènisuvavu èlla kāladali dūra oḍisi

bhrāmakatraya mārigè òl̤agāgadalè

sarvārādhakana ciṃtisutaliru sarvatra marèyadalè||29||

karaṇa karmava māḍidarè vismaraṇè kāladi mātugal̤igè

uttarava kòḍadalè summanippanu

jāgarāvasthè karuṇisalu vyāpāra māḍuva

baralu nālkavasthègal̤u pariharisi kòl̤anu etakè? svataṃtranu tānèyèṃbuvanu||30||

yukti mātugal̤alla śruti smṛti ukta mātugal̤ivu

vicārisi muktigivu sopānavu ènipavu pratiprati padavu

bhaktipūrvaka paṭhisuvavarigè vyakti kòḍuva svarūpa sukha

praviviraktana māḍuvanu bhavabhayadiṃda bahu rūpa||31||

śrīnivāsana suguṇa maṇigal̤a prāṇamāta vayunākhya sūtradi poṇisida mālikèya

vāgmayagè samarpisida

jñānigal̤a dṛk viṣayavuhuda ajñānigal̤igè asahya torpudu

māṇikava markaṭana kaiyali kòṭṭa tèradaṃtè||32||

śrīvidhi īra vipāhipa īśa śacī varātmabhava arka śaśi

digdeva ṛṣi gaṃdharva kinnara siddha sādhya gaṇa sevita padāṃbuja

tvatpādālavaṃbigal̤u āda bhaktara kāva

karuṇā sāṃdra lakṣmī hṛtkumuda caṃdra||33||

ādariśa agatākṣa bhāṣā bhedadiṃdali karèyalu adanu

niṣedhagaidu avalokisadè biḍuvarè vivekigal̤u

mādhavana guṇa pel̤va prākṛtavu ādarèyu sari

kel̤i parama āhlādabaḍadipparè? niraṃtara balla kovidaru||34||

bhāskarana maṃḍalava kaṃḍu namaskarisi modisadè

dveṣadi taskaranu niṃdisalu kuṃdahude divākaragè

saṃskṛtavu idalla èṃdu kuhaka tiraskarisalu enahudu?

bhakti puraskaradi kel̤varigòlivanu puṣkarākṣa sadā||35||

patitana kapāladòl̤u bhāgīrathiya jalavirè peyavu ènipudè?

itara kavi nirmita kukāvya aśrāvya budhariṃda

kṛti patikathānvitavu ènipa prākṛtavè tā saṃskṛtavu ènisi

sadgatiyanīvudu bhakti pūrvaka kel̤i pel̤varigè||36||

śāstra sayukti graṃthagal̤u odi kel̤davanalla

saṃtata sādhugal̤a sahavāsa sallāpagal̤u mòdalilla

moda tīrtha āryara matānugaru ādavara karuṇadali pel̤dè

ramādhava jagannātha viṭhala til̤isidadaròl̤agè||37||
