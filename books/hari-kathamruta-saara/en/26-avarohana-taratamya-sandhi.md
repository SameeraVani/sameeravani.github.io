---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಅವರೋಹಣ ತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಸಂಧಿ/Avarohana taratamya sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè<br/>parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

śrīramaṇa nijabhaktarènisuva vārijāsana mukhya nirjara<br/>tāratamyava pel̤vè saṃkṣepadali gurubaladi||

keśavagè nārāyaṇagè kamalāsana samīrarigè vāṇigè<br/>vīśa phaṇipa maheśarigè ṣaṇmahiṣiyara padakè<br/>śeṣa rudrara patniyarigè suvāsada pradyumnarigè<br/>saṃtoṣadali vaṃdisuvè bhakti jñāna kòḍalèṃdu||1||

prāṇadevagè namipè kāmana sūnu manu guru dakṣa śaci rati māniniyarigè<br/>pravaha devagè sūrya soma yama mānavigè varuṇanigè<br/>vīṇā pāṇi nārada munigè napisuvè<br/>jñāna bhakti virakti mārgava til̤isali ènagèṃdu||2||

anal̤a bhṛgu dākṣāyaṇiyarigè kanaka garbhaja saptarṣigal̤igè èṇèyènipa<br/>vaivasvata manu gādhi saṃbhavagè<br/>danuja nirṛti tāra prāvahi vanaja mitragè aśvinī gaṇapā<br/>dhanapa viśvaksenarigè vaṃdisuvènu anavarata||3||

ukta devarkal̤anu ul̤idèṃbhattaidu janarugal̤u manugal̤u<br/>cithya cāvana yamal̤arigè karmajaru ènisutippa<br/>kārtavīryārjuna pramukha śatastharigè parjanya gaṃga<br/>āditya yama soma aniruddhara patniyara padakè||4||

hutavahana ardhāṃginigè caṃdrama suta budhagè nāmātmika uṣā satigè<br/>chāyātmaja śanaiścaragè anamipè satata<br/>prati divasadali biḍadè jīva pratati māḍuva karmagal̤igè<br/>adhipatiyu ènipa puṣkarana pādāṃbujagal̤igè namipè||5||

ā namipè ajānajarigè sukṛśānu sutarigè govrajadòl̤iha<br/>māniniyarigè cirapitaru śatanūna śatakoṭi mauni janarigè<br/>devamānava gāna prauḍharigè avaniparigè<br/>ramā nivāsana dāsavargakè namipènu anavarata||6||

anukramaṇika tāratamyava anudinadi sadbhakti pūrvaka nènèvarigè<br/>dharmārtha kāmādigal̤u phalisuvavu<br/>vanaja saṃbhava mukhyaru avayavaru ènisuva jagannātha viṭhalana<br/>vinayadiṃdali namisi kòṃḍāḍutiru marèyadalè||7||
