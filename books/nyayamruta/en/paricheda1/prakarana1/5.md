# prathamaparicchedaḥ - 1. mithyātvaniruktibhaṅgaḥ - mithyātve paroktānumānāni - bhāga - 3

<details>
  <summary>samīravāṇī</summary>


**nyāyāmṛtam**

1. navīnāstu \- etatpaṭātyantābhāvaḥ etattantuniṣṭhaḥ, etatpaṭānādyabhāvatvāt, etatpaṭānyonyābhāvavat | 2. etattantunāśajanyasya etatpaṭanāśasya etattantuvṛttitvādanādīti viśeṣaṇam | 3. vimataṃ vā brahmapramānyenetyādi vipratipattivākyoktaviśeṣaṇatrayavattve sati avyāpyavṛttitvānadhikaraṇaṃ vā svasamānādhikaraṇātyantābhāvapratiyogi, anātmatvāt saṃyogavaditi | 4. atra ca pakṣadharma(tā)balānmithyātvasiddhiḥ | 5. vimataṃ nityadravyānyat, kevalānvayyatyantābhāvapratiyogi, padārthatvāt, nityadravyavat | 6. tārkikamate nityadravyāṇāmavṛttitvena tadatyantābhāvaḥ kevalānvayīti na dṛṣṭāntaḥ sādhyavikalaḥ | 7. ata eva nātmani vyabhicāraḥ | 8. mithyātvasiddhistu pakṣadharmatābalāt | 9. ātmatvāvacchinnadharmitāko bhedo na paramārthasatpratiyogikaḥ, anātmamātrapratiyogikatvāt, śuktirūpyapratiyogikabhedavadityāhuḥ |  
---

**samīravāṇī \-**

**1. vākyam:** navīnāstu \- etatpaṭātyantābhāvaḥ etattantuniṣṭhaḥ, etatpaṭānādyabhāvatvāt, etatpaṭaanyonyābhāvavat |

* **padārthāḥ:**  
  * **navīnāḥ:** nūtanāḥ vidvāṃsaḥ (Newer scholars/logicians).  
  * **etat-paṭa-atyantābhāvaḥ:** asya paṭasya (vastrasya) atyantaḥ abhāvaḥ |  
  * **etat-tantu-niṣṭhaḥ:** eteṣu tantuṣu (threads) vartate |  
  * **etat-paṭa-anādi-abhāvatvāt:** yatohi ayaṃ paṭasya anādiḥ (yasya ādiḥ nāsti) abhāvaḥ asti |  
  * **etat-paṭa-anyonyābhāvavat:** yathā paṭasya anyonyābhāvaḥ (tantuṣu) bhavati, tathā |  
* **samīravāṇī \-**  nūtanāḥ tārkikāḥ kathayanti yat \- paṭasya atyantābhāvaḥ tasya tantuṣu (kāraṇeṣu) eva tiṣṭhati | kutaḥ? yatohi saḥ paṭasya 'anādi' abhāvaḥ asti | yathā 'paṭaḥ tantuḥ na' iti anyonyābhāvaḥ tantuṣu asti, tathaiva atyantābhāvaḥ api tantuṣu asti iti anumānam |

**navīnāstu iti prayogasya viśiṣṭāni kāraṇāni**   
śāstragrantheṣu (viśeṣataḥ nyāya-vedānta-diṣu) **'navīnāstu'** (The moderns/later scholars however...) iti prayogasya kānicit viśiṣṭāni kāraṇāni bhavanti | asya paṅkteḥ sandarbhe asya āvaśyakatā adhaḥ spaṣṭīkṛtā asti \-

1. matabheda-pradarśanam (Showing a difference in Viewpoint)  
śāstre yadā kaścana viṣayaḥ 'prācīnaiḥ' (Old scholars) uktaḥ bhavati, tatra yadi kecana doṣāḥ dṛśyante athavā tataḥ api uttamaḥ tarkaḥ labhyate, tadā 'navīnāḥ' (Newer scholars) svamataṃ prastauti | 'navīnāstu' iti padena jñāyate yat itaḥ pūrvaṃ yat anumānaṃ dattaṃ, tasmāt **ayaṃ tarkaḥ bhinnaḥ pariṣkṛtaḥ (Refined) ca asti** |

2. pariṣkāraḥ (Refinement of Logic)  
prācīnānāṃ lakṣaṇeṣu prāyaḥ kecana tārkika-doṣāḥ (yathā- avyāptiḥ, ativyāptiḥ vā) sambhavanti | navīnāḥ tārkikāḥ (yathā- gadādharabhaṭṭācāryaḥ vā anye vedāntinaḥ) **'atisūkṣmaṃ'** lakṣaṇaṃ kurvanti |

* **atra kiṃ navīnam?** prācīnāḥ sāmānyataḥ 'jagat mithyā' iti vadanti sma | kintu navīnāḥ atra **'etatpaṭātyantābhāvaḥ etattantuniṣṭhaḥ'** iti pakṣa-hetu-dṛṣṭāntānāṃ prayogaṃ kṛtvā adhikaṃ sudṛḍhaṃ anumānaṃ prastuvanti |

3. 'siddhasādhana' doṣa-nivāraṇam (Avoiding 'Siddhasadhana')  
yadi vayaṃ vadāmaḥ \- "paṭaḥ tantuṣu nāsti", tarhi naiyāyikāḥ vadanti \- "ām, yadā paṭaḥ naṣṭaḥ bhavati (dhvaṃsaḥ), tadā saḥ tantuṣu na tiṣṭhati | etat tu vayaṃ jānīmaḥ eva" (This is already proven).

ataḥ navīnāḥ **'anādi'** iti viśeṣaṇaṃ yojayanti | te vadanti \- "vayaṃ paṭasya vināśasya (dhvaṃsasya) carcāṃ na kurmaḥ, apitu tasya **anādi-atyantābhāvasya** carcāṃ kurmaḥ, yaḥ paṭasya sthitikāle api tantuṣu asti |" eṣaḥ sūkṣmaḥ pariṣkāraḥ navīnaiḥ kṛtaḥ |  
4. sādhyasya spaṣṭatā (Clarity of the Probandum)  
atra navīnānāṃ mukhyaṃ lakṣyam asti \- **mithyātvasya** tādṛśaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ siddhaṃ karaṇīyam, yat kenāpi tarkeṇa khaṇḍayituṃ na śakyate | te 'atyantābhāvasya' ādhāraṃ (Locus) 'tantuṣu' (Material cause) eva sthāpayanti, yena 'mithyātvam' (svādhiṣṭhāne traikālikaniṣedhapratiyogitvam) samyak siddhaṃ bhavati |

**'navīnāstu'** iti padasya āvaśyakatā asti \-

1. pūrvatana-tarkebhyaḥ **uttamaṃ tarkādhāraṃ** dātum |  
2. virodhinaḥ (prativādinaḥ) praśnānāṃ **nūtanaṃ samādhānaṃ** dātum |  
3. anumānavākyaṃ **adhikaṃ vaijñānikaṃ tārkikaṃ ca** kartum |

ataḥ atra 'navīnāḥ' tādṛśaṃ sūkṣmaṃ tarkaṃ vadanti yena 'mithyātva-siddhiḥ' niṣkaṇṭakā bhavati |

---

**2. vākyam:** etattantunāśajanyasya etatpaṭanāśasya etattantuvṛttitvādanādīti viśeṣaṇam |

* **padārthāḥ:**  
  * **etat-tantu-nāśa-janyasya:** tantūnāṃ nāśena utpannasya |  
  * **etat-paṭa-nāśasya:** paṭasya dhvaṃsasya (Destruction of cloth).  
  * **etattantu-vṛttitvāt:** tantuṣu vidyamānatvāt (It does exist in threads).  
  * **anādi iti viśeṣaṇam:** hetumadhye 'anādi' iti padaṃ yojitam |  
* **samīravāṇī \-**  yadi hetumadhye 'anādi' iti na vadāmaḥ, tarhi 'dhvaṃsābhāve' (Destruction) vyabhicāraḥ bhavet | yadā tantavaḥ naśyanti, tadā paṭasya nāśaḥ bhavati | saḥ paṭa-nāśaḥ (dhvaṃsaḥ) tantuṣu na tiṣṭhati | ataḥ kevalaṃ 'paṭābhāvatvāt' iti na uktvā 'anādi-paṭābhāvatvāt' iti uktam, yena dhvaṃsaḥ vyāvṛttaḥ bhavati |

---

**3. vākyam:** vimataṃ vā brahmapramānyenetyādi vipratipattivākyoktaviśeṣaṇatrayavattve sati avyāpyavṛttitvānadhikaraṇaṃ vā svasamānādhikaraṇātyantābhāvapratiyogi, anātmatvāt saṃyogavaditi |

* **padārthāḥ:**  
  * **vimatam:** vivādāspadaṃ jagat (The disputed world).  
  * **viśeṣaṇatrayavattve sati:** mithyātvasya tribhiḥ lakṣaṇaiḥ yuktaṃ sat |  
  * **avyāpyavṛttitva-anadhikaraṇam:** yat deśataḥ kālataḥ ca sīmitaṃ nāsti |  
  * **svasamānādhikaraṇa-atyantābhāva-pratiyogi:** yatra vastu asti, tatraiva tasya abhāvaḥ api asti (etat mithyātvasya lakṣaṇam) |  
  * **anātmatvāt:** ātmanaḥ bhinnatvāt |  
  * **saṃyogavat:** yathā dvayoḥ vastunoḥ saṃyogaḥ (Connection) |  
* **samīravāṇī \-**  jagat (yasmin vivādaḥ asti) 'mithyā' asti | arthāt jagat yatra pratīyate (adhiṣṭhāne), tatraiva tasya atyantābhāvaḥ asti | kimartham? yatohi jagat 'anātmā' asti | yathā saṃyogaḥ (Contact) yasmin vṛkṣe asti, tasyaiva mūle abhāvaḥ api bhavati (avyāpyavṛttiḥ), tathā jagat api svādhiṣṭhāne abhāvātmakam asti |

---

**4. vākyam:** atra ca pakṣadharma(tā)balānmithyātvasiddhiḥ |

* **padārthāḥ:**  
  * **pakṣadharmatā-balāt:** hetoḥ pakṣe (jagati) vidyamānatvakāraṇāt |  
  * **mithyātva-siddhiḥ:** jagat mithyā iti siddhaṃ bhavati |  
* **samīravāṇī \-**  asmin anumāne yadā 'anātmatva' iti hetuḥ jagati (pakṣe) tiṣṭhati, tadā tasya prabhāveṇa jagataḥ mithyātvaṃ siddhaṃ bhavati |

---

**5. vākyam:** vimataṃ nityadravyānyat, kevalānvayyatyantābhāvapratiyogi, padārthatvāt, nityadravyavat |

* **padārthāḥ:**  
  * **nityadravyānyat:** paramāṇu-ākāśa-ādibhyaḥ bhinnam |  
  * **kevalānvayi-atyantābhāva-pratiyogi:** sarvatra vidyamānasya abhāvasya pratiyogī |  
  * **padārthatvāt:** vastutvāt (Being a category).  
* **samīravāṇī \-**  vivādāspadaṃ vastu (jagataḥ vastūni) atyantābhāvasya pratiyogī asti (arthāt mithyā asti) | yatohi tat 'padārthaḥ' asti | yathā nityadravyāṇi (tārkikamate) kevalānvayyabhāvasya pratiyogī bhavanti |

---

**6. vākyam:** tārkikamate nityadravyāṇāmavṛttitvena tadatyantābhāvaḥ kevalānvayīti na dṛṣṭāntaḥ sādhyavikalaḥ |

* **padārthāḥ:**  
  * **nityadravyāṇām:** paramāṇūnām |  
  * **avṛttitvena:** kutrapi na vartante iti kāraṇena |  
  * **sādhyavikalaḥ:** yatra sādhyaṃ nāsti tādṛśaḥ dṛṣṭāntaḥ (Defective example).  
* **samīravāṇī \-**  nyāyamate (Tarkika) nityadravyāṇi (yathā paramāṇuḥ) kutrapi na tiṣṭhanti (avṛttayaḥ) | ataḥ teṣām atyantābhāvaḥ 'kevalānvayī' (sarvatra vidyamānaḥ) bhavati | ataḥ asmākaṃ dṛṣṭāntaḥ (nityadravyavat) yogyaḥ asti, tatra sādhyasya abhāvaḥ nāsti |

---

**7. vākyam:** ata eva nātmani vyabhicāraḥ |

* **padārthāḥ:**  
  * **nātmani vyabhicāraḥ:** ātmani ayaṃ niyamaḥ na bhajyate |  
* **samīravāṇī \-**  yatohi vayaṃ pakṣe 'nityadravyānyat' (Other than eternal substances) iti uktavantaḥ, ataḥ ātmani (yaḥ nityaḥ asti) ayaṃ hetuḥ na gacchati, ataḥ vyabhicāradoṣaḥ na bhavati |

---

**8. vākyam:** mithyātvasiddhistu pakṣadharmatābalāt |

* **padārthāḥ:** (pūrvoktavat)  
* **samīravāṇī \-**  atrāpi hetoḥ pakṣe vidyamānatvāt sādhyasya (mithyātvasya) niścayaḥ bhavati |

---

**9. vākyam:** ātmatvāvacchinnadharmitāko bhedo na paramārthasatpratiyogikaḥ, anātmamātrapratiyogikatvāt, śuktirūpyapratiyogikabhedavadityāhuḥ |

* **padārthāḥ:**  
  * **ātmatvāvacchinna-dharmitākaḥ:** ātmani vidyamānaḥ |  
  * **bhedaḥ:** pṛthaktvam (Difference).  
  * **na paramārthasat-pratiyogikaḥ:** vāstavika-satyena saha tasya bhedaḥ nāsti |  
  * **anātma-mātra-pratiyogikatvāt:** yatohi ātmanaḥ bhedaḥ kevalaṃ anātmanā (jagataḥ vastunā) saha eva dṛśyate |  
  * **śuktirūpya-pratiyogika-bhedavat:** yathā śuktau (shell) rajatasya (silver) bhedaḥ kālpanikaḥ bhavati |  
* **samīravāṇī \-** ātmani yaḥ bhedaḥ pratīyate, saḥ pāramārthikaḥ (Real) nāsti | saḥ bhedaḥ kevalaṃ 'anātma' vastūni prati asti | yathā śuktau dṛśyamānaṃ rajataṃ mithyā asti, tasya bhedaḥ api tādṛśaḥ eva | ataḥ ātmanaḥ dvaitaṃ mithyā iti vidvāṃsaḥ vadanti |
