## Word Meanings and Purports (Verse by Verse)

<a id="meaning-1-2"></a>
### Verse 1-2
`śrīpūrṇabōdhagurutīrthapayōbdhipārā kāmārimākṣaviṣamākṣaśiraḥ spr̥śantī | pūrvōttarāmitataraṅgacaratsuhaṁsā dēvālisēvitaparāṅghripayōjalagnā || 1 ||`  
`jīvēśabhēdaguṇapūrtijagatsusattva- -nīcōccabhāvamukhanakragaṇaiḥ samētā | durvādyajāpatigilairgururāghavēndra- -vāgdēvatāsaridamuṁ vimalī karōtu || 2 ||`

**Word Meanings:**
* **śrīpūrṇabōdhagurutīrthapayōbdhipārā** – Originating from the ocean (payodhi) of knowledge (tirtha) of Sri Madhvacharya (Poornabodha), who is the supreme guru; on the speech side: having crossed to the other shore of the ocean of scriptures/knowledge of Sri Madhvacharya
* **kāmārimākṣaviṣamākṣaśiraḥ spr̥śantī** – Touching the head of Lord Shiva (Kamari / Vishamaksha - who has the eye of knowledge), meaning Shiva honors this speech; on the river side: flowing from Shiva's head (Ganga)
* **pūrvōttarāmitataraṅgacaratsuhaṁsā** – Having many waves in the form of Purvapaksha and Siddhanta (Purva & Uttara Mimamsa), where great ascetic saints (suhamsas) swim/study; on the river side: having East-West flowing waves where beautiful swans swim
* **dēvālisēvitaparāṅghripayōjalagnā** – Originating from the lotus feet of the Supreme Lord Sri Hari, who is served by the assembly of gods; on the river side: originating from the washings of Lord Vishnu's feet
* **jīvēśabhēdaguṇapūrtijagatsusattva-nīcōccabhāva** – Containing the core concepts of Jiva-Isvara difference (Jiveshabheda), fullness of infinite qualities in God (gunapoorti), absolute reality of the universe (jagatsusattva), and the hierarchy of souls (neechocchabhava)
* **nakragaṇaiḥ samētā** – Filled with these core philosophical tenets like crocodiles (nakraganas)
* **durvādyajāpatigilair** – Which swallow/defeat the elephant-like false logicians and disputants (durvadis)
* **gururāghavēndravāgdēvatāsarid** – The river of speech/works of Guru Sri Raghavendra
* **amuṁ vimalī karōtu** – Let this river purify this servant/devotee completely.

**Meaning:**
May the holy river of speech of Guru Sri Raghavendra Swamy—which originates from the ocean of knowledge of Sri Madhvacharya, is revered by Lord Shiva, is studied by great ascetic saints, flows from the feet of Sri Hari, contains the crocodiles of Dvaita principles (such as Jiva-Isvara difference and world reality), and defeats all false logicians—purify my mind and remove all impurities.

### Verse 3
`śrīrāghavēndraḥ sakalapradātā svapādakañjadvayabhaktimadbhyaḥ | aghādrisambhēdanadr̥ṣṭivajraḥ kṣamāsurēndrō:’vatu māṁ sadā:’yam || 3 ||`

**Word Meanings:**
* **ayam** – This Sri Raghavendra Guru
* **svapādakañjadvayabhaktimadbhyaḥ** – To those who have devotion towards his two lotus feet
* **sakalapradātā** – Bestower of all desires/boons
* **aghādrisambhēdanadr̥ṣṭivajraḥ** – He whose glance (drishti) is like a thunderbolt (vajra) in shattering the mountains (adri) of sins (agha)
* **kṣamāsurēndraḥ** – The chief/lord (indra) among the earthly gods/Brahmins (kṣamāsura)
* **sadā mām avatu** – Protect me always.

**Meaning:**
May this Sri Raghavendra Swamy, who bestows all desires on those devoted to his lotus feet, whose glance is like a thunderbolt in shattering the mountains of sins, and who is the chief among the earthly gods, protect me always.

<a id="meaning-4"></a>
### Verse 4
`śrīrāghavēndrō haripādakañja- -niṣēvaṇāllabdhasamastasampat | dēvasvabhāvō divijadrumō:’ya- -miṣṭapradō mē satataṁ sa bhūyāt || 4 ||`

**Word Meanings:**
* **haripādakañjaniṣēvaṇāt** – From the constant service/devotion to the lotus feet of Sri Hari
* **labdhasamastasampat** – Who has obtained all spiritual and material wealth
* **dēvasvabhāvaḥ** – Of divine nature (incarnation of a celestial being/devata)
* **divijadrumaḥ** – Like a wish-fulfilling tree of heaven (Kalpavriksha)
* **ayam saḥ** – May this famous Sri Raghavendra Swamy
* **mē satataṁ iṣṭapradō bhūyāt** – Always bestow my desired boons upon me.

**Meaning:**
May Sri Raghavendra Swamy, who attained all glorious wealth by worshipping the lotus feet of Lord Hari, who is of divine nature (incarnation of a devata), and is like the wish-fulfilling Kalpavriksha tree to his devotees, always grant my desires.

<a id="meaning-5"></a>
### Verse 5
`bhavyasvarūpō bhavaduḥkhatūla- -saṅghāgnicaryaḥ sukhadhairyaśālī | samastaduṣṭagrahanigrahēśō duratyayōpaplavasindhusētuḥ || 5 ||`

**Word Meanings:**
* **bhavyasvarūpaḥ** – Having an auspicious and beautiful form/nature
* **bhavaduḥkhatūlasaṅghāgnicaryaḥ** – Being like fire (agnicharya) to the cotton bale (toola-sangha) of worldly miseries (bhava-duhkha)
* **sukhadhairyaśālī** – Endowed with happiness (sukha) and courage (dhairya)
* **samastaduṣṭagrahanigrahēśaḥ** – The lord/remover who controls and destroys all evil planetary/ghostly influences (dushta-graha)
* **duratyayōpaplavasindhusētuḥ** – A bridge (setu) to cross the ocean (sindhu) of insurmountable calamities/miseries (duratyaya-upaplava)

**Meaning:**
Sri Raghavendra Swamy is of auspicious nature, acts like a blazing fire that destroys the cotton bale of worldly miseries, possesses absolute happiness and courage, is the supreme destroyer of all evil planetary/ghostly afflictions, and serves as a bridge to cross the ocean of insurmountable calamities.

### Verse 6
`nirastadōṣō niravadyavēṣaḥ pratyarthimūkattvanidānabhāṣaḥ | vidvatparijñēyamahāviśēṣō vāgvaikharīnirjitabhavyaśēṣaḥ || 6 ||`

**Word Meanings:**
* **nirastadōṣaḥ** – One who has expelled all faults/desires (raga-dwesha)
* **niravadyavēṣaḥ** – One who wears an unblemished, pure form (sanyasa dress)
* **pratyarthimūkatvanidānabhāṣaḥ** – He whose speech (bhasha) is the cause (nidana) of the silence/dumbness of opponent disputants (pratyarthi)
* **vidvatparijñēyamahāviśēṣaḥ** – He whose great glory (maha-vishesha) is fully understood only by wise scholars (vidvat)
* **vāgvaikharīnirjitabhavyasēṣaḥ** – One who has conquered the great Adi Shesha (the lord of speech) by his extraordinary eloquence (vagvaikhari)

**Meaning:**
Free of all defects, wearing the pure sanyasa attire, his words render disputing opponents speechless. His supreme greatness is understood only by the learned, and by his extraordinary eloquence, he can surpass even Adi Shesha.

### Verse 7
`santānasampatpariśuddhabhakti- -vijñānavāgdēhasupāṭavādīn | datvā śarīrōtthasamastadōṣān hattvā sa nō:’vyādgururāghavēndraḥ || 7 ||`

**Word Meanings:**
* **saḥ gururāghavēndraḥ** – May that Guru Sri Raghavendra Swamy
* **santānasampatparishuddhabhaktivijñānavāgdēhasupāṭavādīn** – Progeny (santana), wealth (sampat), pure devotion (parishuddha-bhakti), special wisdom (vijnana), and excellent health and vigor of speech and body (vaag-deha-supatava)
* **dattvā** – Bestowing
* **śarīrōtthasamastadōṣān** – All bodily-born sins and diseases
* **hattvā** – Destroying
* **naḥ avyāt** – Protect us always.

**Meaning:**
May Guru Sri Raghavendra, by bestowing progeny, wealth, pure devotion, wisdom, and health of speech and body, and by destroying all our bodily sins and diseases, protect us always.

### Verse 8
`yatpādōdakasañcayaḥ suranadīmukhyāpagāsāditā- -saṅkhyānuttamapuṇyasaṅghavilasatprakhyātapuṇyāvahaḥ | dustāpatrayanāśanō bhuvi mahāvandhyāsuputrapradō vyaṅgasvaṅgasamr̥ddhidō grahamahāpāpāpahastaṁ śrayē || 8 ||`

**Word Meanings:**
* **yatpādōdakasañcayaḥ** – The collection of water from washing whose feet (padodaka)
* **suranadīmukhyāpagāsa-ditāsaṅkhyānuttamapuṇyasaṅghavilasatprakhyātapuṇyāvahaḥ** – Brings the famous, glowing virtue (punya) which is superior to the infinite virtues obtained from bathing in all holy rivers led by Ganga (suranadi)
* **dustāpatrayanāśanō** – Destroyer of the three intolerable miseries (adhibhautika, adhidaivika, adhyatmika)
* **bhuvi mahāvandhyāsuputrapradō** – Bestower of good sons even to highly barren women on earth
* **vyaṅgasvaṅgasamr̥ddhidō** – Bestower of perfect limbs to the disabled/deformed
* **grahamahāpāpāpahaḥ** – Destroyer of evil planetary influences and great sins (like Brahmin-killing)
* **taṁ śrayē** – I surrender to and serve that Guru Raghavendra.

**Meaning:**
I surrender to Guru Raghavendra, the water from whose feet washes away all three types of miseries, surpasses the merits of all holy rivers like Ganga, bestows good progeny to barren women, restores health and limbs to the disabled, and removes all planetary afflictions and major sins.

### Verse 9
`yatpādakañjarajasā paribhūṣitāṅgā yatpādapadmamadhupāyitamānasā yē | yatpādapadmaparikīrtanajīrṇavāca -staddarśanaṁ duritakānanadāvabhūtam || 9 ||`

**Word Meanings:**
* **yē** – Those devotees who
* **yatpādakañjarajasā paribhūṣitāṅgāḥ** – Decorate their bodies with the dust (rajas) of whose lotus feet
* **yatpādapadmamadhupāyitamānasāḥ** – Whose minds act like honeybees (madhupa) drinking from his lotus feet
* **yatpādapadmaparikīrtanajīrṇavācaḥ** – Whose words are matured/spent in singing praises of his lotus feet
* **taddarśanaṁ** – Their sight/darshana
* **duritakānanadāvabhūtam** – Acts like a forest fire (dava) to the forest of sins (durita) of others.

**Meaning:**
Those whose bodies are decorated with the dust of the Guru's feet, whose minds hover like bees around his feet, and whose speech is dedicated to singing his praises—their very sight acts like a forest fire that burns away the sins of others.

### Verse 10
`sarvatantrasvatantrō:’sau śrīmadhvamatavardhanaḥ vijayīndrakarābjōtthasudhīndravaraputrakaḥ || 10 ||`

**Word Meanings:**
* **asau** – This Guru Sri Raghavendra Swamy
* **sarvatantrasvatantraḥ** – Master of all scriptures/doctrines (sarva-tantra-svatantra)
* **śrīmadhvamatavardhanaḥ** – The augmenter of the sacred Dvaita philosophy of Sri Madhvacharya
* **vijayīndrakarābjōtthasudhīndravaraputrakaḥ** – The beloved disciple (varaputraka - beloved son) of Sri Sudhindra Teertha, who was born from the lotus hands of Sri Vijayindra Teertha.

**Meaning:**
Guru Sri Raghavendra is the master of all scriptures, the propagator of Madhva philosophy, and the beloved disciple of Sri Sudhindra Teertha, who was the illustrious disciple of Sri Vijayindra Teertha.

### Verse 11
`śrīrāghavēndrō yatirāṭ gururmē syādbhayāpahaḥ jñānabhaktisuputrāyuryaśaḥ śrīpuṇyavardhanaḥ || 11 ||`

**Word Meanings:**
* **yatirāṭ** – The king of ascetics (yati-rat)
* **mē guruḥ śrīrāghavēndraḥ** – My guru Sri Raghavendra Swamy
* **bhayāpahaḥ syāt** – May he be the remover of my fears
* **jñānabhaktisuputrāyuryaśaḥśrīpuṇyavardhanaḥ** – And may he increase my wisdom, devotion, good children, longevity, fame, wealth, and merit (punya).

**Meaning:**
May the king of ascetics, my Guru Sri Raghavendra Swamy, remove all my fears and constantly increase my wisdom, devotion, progeny, long life, fame, prosperity, and spiritual merits.

### Verse 12
`prativādijayasvāntabhēdacihnādarō guruḥ | sarvavidyāpravīṇō:’nyō rāghavēndrānna vidyatē || 12 ||`

**Word Meanings:**
* **prativādijayasvāntabhēdacihnādarō** – He who is keen to manifest the signs of victory over opponents and the shattering of their pride (mental defeat)
* **guruḥ** – The master
* **sarvavidyāpravīṇaḥ** – Fully proficient in all forms of learning/knowledge
* **rāghavēndrāt anyaḥ** – Other than Sri Raghavendra Swamy
* **na vidyatē** – Does not exist in this world.

**Meaning:**
There is no other guru in this world equal to Sri Raghavendra Swamy, who is proficient in all sciences and who is always victorious, shattering the arguments and pride of opposing disputants.

### Verse 13
