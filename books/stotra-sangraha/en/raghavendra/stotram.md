# Guru Sri Raghavendra
## Sri Raghavendra Stotram

śrīpūrṇabōdhagurutīrthapayōbdhipārā kāmārimākṣaviṣamākṣaśiraḥ spr̥śantī | pūrvōttarāmitataraṅgacaratsuhaṁsā dēvālisēvitaparāṅghripayōjalagnā || 1 ||  
  
jīvēśabhēdaguṇapūrtijagatsusattva- -nīcōccabhāvamukhanakragaṇaiḥ samētā | durvādyajāpatigilairgururāghavēndra- -vāgdēvatāsaridamuṁ vimalī karōtu || 2 ||  
  
śrīrāghavēndraḥ sakalapradātā svapādakañjadvayabhaktimadbhyaḥ | aghādrisambhēdanadr̥ṣṭivajraḥ kṣamāsurēndrō:’vatu māṁ sadā:’yam || 3 ||  
  
śrīrāghavēndrō haripādakañja- -niṣēvaṇāllabdhasamastasampat | dēvasvabhāvō divijadrumō:’ya- -miṣṭapradō mē satataṁ sa bhūyāt || 4 ||  
  
bhavyasvarūpō bhavaduḥkhatūla- -saṅghāgnicaryaḥ sukhadhairyaśālī | samastaduṣṭagrahanigrahēśō duratyayōpaplavasindhusētuḥ || 5 ||  
  
nirastadōṣō niravadyavēṣaḥ pratyarthimūkattvanidānabhāṣaḥ | vidvatparijñēyamahāviśēṣō vāgvaikharīnirjitabhavyaśēṣaḥ || 6 ||  
  
santānasampatpariśuddhabhakti- -vijñānavāgdēhasupāṭavādīn | datvā śarīrōtthasamastadōṣān hattvā sa nō:’vyādgururāghavēndraḥ || 7 ||  
  
yatpādōdakasañcayaḥ suranadīmukhyāpagāsāditā- -saṅkhyānuttamapuṇyasaṅghavilasatprakhyātapuṇyāvahaḥ | dustāpatrayanāśanō bhuvi mahāvandhyāsuputrapradō vyaṅgasvaṅgasamr̥ddhidō grahamahāpāpāpahastaṁ śrayē || 8 ||  
  
yatpādakañjarajasā paribhūṣitāṅgā yatpādapadmamadhupāyitamānasā yē | yatpādapadmaparikīrtanajīrṇavāca -staddarśanaṁ duritakānanadāvabhūtam || 9 ||  
  
sarvatantrasvatantrō:’sau śrīmadhvamatavardhanaḥ vijayīndrakarābjōtthasudhīndravaraputrakaḥ || 10 ||  
  
śrīrāghavēndrō yatirāṭ gururmē syādbhayāpahaḥ jñānabhaktisuputrāyuryaśaḥ śrīpuṇyavardhanaḥ || 11 ||  
  
prativādijayasvāntabhēdacihnādarō guruḥ | sarvavidyāpravīṇō:’nyō rāghavēndrānna vidyatē || 12 ||  
  
aparōkṣīkr̥taśrīśaḥ samupēkṣitabhāvajaḥ | apēkṣitapradātā:’nyō rāghavēndrānna vidyatē || 13 ||  
  
dayādākṣiṇyavairāgyavākpāṭavamukhāṅkitaḥ | śāpānugrahaśaktō:’nyō rāghavēndrānna vidyatē || 14 ||  
  
ajñānavismr̥tibhrāntisaṁśayā:’pasmr̥tikṣayāḥ | tandrākampavacaḥkauṇṭhyamukhā yē cēndriyōdbhavāḥ | dōṣāstē nāśamāyānti rāghavēndraprasādataḥ || 15 ||  
  
ōṁ śrīrāghavēndrāya namaḥ itya:’ṣṭākṣaramantrataḥ | japitādbhāvitānnityaṁ iṣṭārthāḥ syurna saṁśayaḥ || 16 ||  
  
hantu naḥ kāyajān dōṣān ātmātmīyasamudbhavān | sarvānapi pumarthāṁśca dadātu gururātmavit || 17 ||  
  
iti kālatrayē nityaṁ prārthanāṁ yaḥ karōti saḥ | ihāmutrāptasarvēṣṭō mōdatē nātra saṁśayaḥ || 18 ||  
  
agamyamahimā lōkē rāghavēndrō mahāyaśāḥ | śrīmadhvamatadugdhābdhicandrō:’vatu sadā:’naghaḥ || 19 ||  
  
sarvayātrāphalāvāptyai yathāśaktipradakṣiṇam | karōmi tava siddhasya br̥ndāvanagataṁ jalam || 20 ||  
  
śirasā dhārayāmyadya sarvatīrthaphalāptayē | sarvābhīṣṭārthasiddhyarthaṁ namaskāraṁ karōmyaham | tava saṅkīrtanaṁ vēdaśāstrārthajñānasiddhayē || 21 ||  
  
saṁsārē:’kṣayasāgarē prakr̥titō:’gādhē sadā dustarē sarvāvadyajalagrahairanupamaiḥ kāmādibhaṅgākulē | nānāvibhramadurbhramē:’mitabhayastōmādiphēnōtkaṭē duḥkhōtkr̥ṣṭaviṣē samuddhara gurō mā magnarūpaṁ sadā || 22 ||  
  
rāghavēndragurustōtraṁ yaḥ paṭhēdbhaktipūrvakam | tasya kuṣṭhādirōgāṇāṁ nivr̥ttistvarayā bhavēt || 23 ||  
  
andhō:’pi divyadr̥ṣṭiḥ syādēḍamūkō:’pi vākpatiḥ | pūrṇāyuḥ pūrṇasampattiḥ stōtrasyā:’sya japādbhavēt || 24 ||  
  
yaḥ pibējjalamētēna stōtrēṇaivābhimantritam | tasya kukṣigatā dōṣāḥ sarvē naśyanti tat kṣaṇāt || 25 ||  
  
yadvr̥ndāvanamāsādya paṅguḥ khañjō:’pi vā janaḥ | stōtrēṇānēna yaḥ kuryāt pradakṣiṇanamaskr̥tī || 26 ||  
  
sa jaṅghālō bhavēdēva gururājaprasādataḥ | sōmasūryōparāgē ca puṣyārkādisamāgamē || 27 ||  
  
yō:’nuttamamidaṁ stōtramaṣṭōttaraśataṁ japēt | bhūtaprētapiśācādi pīḍā tasya na jāyatē || 28 ||  
  
ētat stōtraṁ samuccārya gurōrvr̥ndāvanāntikē | dīpasamyōjanājjñānaṁ putralābhō bhavēddhruvam || 29 ||  
  
paravādijayō divyajñānabhaktyādivardhanam | sarvābhīṣṭapravr̥ddhiḥ syānnātra kāryā vicāraṇā || 30 ||  
  
rājacōramahāvyāghrasarpanakrādipīḍanam | na jāyatē:’sya stōtrasya prabhāvānnātra saṁśayaḥ || 31 ||  
  
yō bhaktyā gururāghavēndracaraṇadvandvaṁ smaran yaḥ paṭhēt stōtraṁ divyamidaṁ sadā na hi bhavēttasyāsukhaṁ kiñcana | kiṁ tviṣṭārthasamr̥ddhirēva kamalānāthaprasādōdayāt kīrtirdigviditā vibhūtiratulā sākṣī hayāsyō:’tra hi || 32 ||  
  
pūjyāya rāghavēndrāya satyadharmaratāya ca | bhajatāṁ kalpavr̥kṣāya namatāṁ kāmadhēnavē ||  
  
āpādamauliparyantaṁ gurūṇāmākr̥tiṁ smarēt | tēna vighnaḥ praṇaśyanti siddhyanti ca manōrathāḥ ||  
  
durvādidhvāntaravayē vaiṣṇavēndīvarēndavē | śrīrāghavēndra guravē namō:’tyantadayālavē ||  
  
mūkō:’pi yatprasādēna mukundaśayanāya tē | rājarājāyatē riktō rāghavēndraṁ tamāśrayē |  
  
iti śrī appaṇṇācāryaviracitaṁ śrīrāghavēndra stōtraṁ sampūrṇam ||

**Word Meanings:**
* **Sri Poornabodha-Guru-Teertha-Payobdhipaaraa** – Originating from the ocean of knowledge of Sri Madhvacharya (Poornabodha).
* **Kaamaarimaaksha-Vishamaaksha-Shirah Sprishantee** – Touching the head of Lord Shiva (Kaamaari / Vishamaaksha), indicating Shiva's deep respect for these words.
* **Poorvottara-Amita-Taranga-Charat-Suhamsa** – Having many waves in the form of Purvapaksha and Uttarapaksha arguments, where self-realized saints (Suhamsas) swim.
* **Devali-Sevita-Paranghri-Payoja-Lagnaa** – Originating from the lotus feet of the Supreme Lord Sri Hari, who is worshipped by all the Devas.
* **Jeevesha-Bheda-Guna-Poorti-Jagatsu-Sattva-Neechoccha-Bhava** – Represented by the core tenets of Dvaita: the distinction between Jiva and God, the fullness of virtues in God, the reality of the universe, and the spiritual hierarchy of souls.
* **Nakra-Ganaih Sametaa** – Filled with crocodiles (which are these core philosophical tenets).
* **Durvaadya-Jaapati-Gilaih** – Capable of swallowing the elephant-like false logicians/arguments.
* **Guru-Raghavendra-Vaagdevata-Sarit** – The river of speech/works of Guru Sri Raghavendra.
* **Amum Vimali-Karotu** – Let this river purify me (remove all my mental impurities).

**Meaning:**
May the river of words of Guru Sri Raghavendra Swamy—which originates from the ocean of knowledge of Sri Madhvacharya, is honored by Lord Shiva, is populated by great saints, flows from the feet of Sri Hari, contains the crocodiles of Dvaita principles (like Jiva-Isvara difference and world reality), and swallows the elephant-like false doctrines—purify my mind and remove all my flaws.
