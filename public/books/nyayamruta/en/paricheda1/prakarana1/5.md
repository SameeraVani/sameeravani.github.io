**Sameervani\-**

**1. Sentence:** The new ones are \- the absence of the end of this fabric is fixed to this fiber, since it is the absence of this fabric and the beginning, as the absence of this fabric is mutual.

* **Items:**
* **Newer:** Newer scholars/logicians.
* **Extreme absence of this fabric:** There is an extreme absence of this fabric (cloth).
* **This-thread-loyal:** Presents in these threads.
* **Because of the eternal absence of this fabric:** Because this is the eternal (which has no beginning) absence of the fabric.
* **This is like the interdependence of the fabric:** As the interdependence of the fabric (in the fibers).
* **Sameervani \-** New logicians say that the extreme absence of \- cloth rests in its fibers (causes). Where is it from? Because that is the 'eternal' absence of the fabric. Just as 'the fabric is not a fiber' means that there is mutual absence in the fibers, so there is an absence of extremity in the fibers.

**Specific reasons for using Navinastu**
There are some specific reasons for the use of **'navinastu'** (The moderns/later scholars however...) in scriptures (especially in Nyaya and Vedanta). This requirement is clarified below in the context of this line \-

1. Showing a difference in Viewpoint
When a subject is stated by the 'Old scholars' in the scriptures, if some errors are found or even better arguments are found, the 'Newer' scholars present their opinion. The term 'be new' indicates that **this argument is different and refined** from the estimates given earlier.

2. Refinement of Logic
The characteristics of the older ones often contain some logical errors (e.g., non-overlap or overlap). New logicians (such as Gadadhar Bhattacharya or other Vedantists) characterize it as **'very subtle'**.

* **What's new here?** The ancients commonly said that 'the world is false'. But the newcomers here present a more robust inference by using the aspect-cause illustrations that **'the absence of this fabric is the end of this fiber'**.

3. Avoiding 'Siddhasadhana'
If we say \- "The cloth is not in the fibers", then the jurists say \- "Yes, when the cloth is lost (destruction), it does not remain in the fibers. This is what we already know" (This is already proven).

Therefore, the newcomers add the adjective **'eternal'**. They say \- "We are not talking about the destruction (destruction) of the fabric, but about its **eternal extremity**, which is in the fibers even when the fabric is in position." This is a subtle refinement made by the newcomers.
4. Clarity of the Probandum
The main goal of the newcomers here is to \- prove such a characteristic of **falsehood** that it cannot be refuted by any argument. They place the locus of 'extreme absence' in the 'material cause', which perfectly proves 'falsehood' (the competition of three-fold prohibition in its own position).

The term **'be new'** is required \-

1. Give a **better argument** to previous arguments.
2. Give a **new solution** to the opponents’ (respondents’) questions.
3. To make inference statements **more scientific and logical**.

Therefore, here the 'newcomers' speak such subtle logic that the 'proof of falsehood' becomes thornless.

**2. Sentence:** The adjective for the destruction of this fabric caused by the destruction of this fiber is the instinct of this fiber.

* **Items:**
* **This is caused by fiber destruction:** caused by the destruction of fibers.
* **This is the destruction of the cloth:** Destruction of cloth.
* **Because it is fiber-involved:** Because it exists in threads (It does exist in threads).
* **Adjective eternal:** The term 'eternal' is added in the middle of the cause.
* **Samiravani \-** If we do not say 'eternal' in the middle of cause, then there would be a deviation from 'destruction' When the fibers are destroyed, the fabric is destroyed. That fabric destruction (destruction) does not remain in the fibers. Therefore, instead of saying only 'because of the absence of the fabric', it is said 'because of the absence of the eternal fabric', which inverts the destruction.

**9. Statement:** They say that the difference of dharmita detached from the Self is not competitive of the ultimate truth, since it is competitive of the non-self alone, like the competitive difference of the form of purity.

* **Items:**
* **Self-detached-religious:** existing in the Self.
* **Difference:** Separation (Difference).
* **Not a competitor of the ultimate truth:** It has no difference with the real truth.
* **Because it is competitive with the non-self alone:** Because the difference of the self is only seen with the non-self (the object of the world).
* **Like the competitive difference between pure silver:** For example, the difference between silver and silver in pure shell is imaginary.
* **Samiravani \-** The difference that is perceived in the self is not transcendental (Real). That distinction is only to 'non-spiritual' things. Just as silver seen in pureness is false, so is its difference. Therefore, scholars say that the duality of the Self is false.
