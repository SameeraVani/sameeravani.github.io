# prathamaparicchedaḥ - 1. mithyātvaniruktibhaṅgaḥ - 4. mithyātvanirvacanam bhāga - 1

<details>
  <summary>samīravāṇī</summary>


**nyāyāmṛtam**  
mithyātvaṃ ca yadyapi nātyantāsattvam, apasiddhāntāt | nāpyanirvācyatvam, aprasiddheḥ | nāpi sadviviktatvam, sato'pi sadantaraviviktatvāt | nāpi sattvānadhikaraṇatvam, nirdharmake brahmaṇyapi sattvāt | na ca nirdharmakatvādeva tatra sattvānadhikaraṇatvamapi neti vācyam | nirdharmakatvarūpahetoḥ sattvānadhikaraṇatvābhāvarūpasādhyasya ca bhāvābhāvābhyāṃ vyāghātenābhāvarūpadharmaniṣedhāyogāt | anyathā'sattvānadhikaraṇatvasyāpi tatrābhāvena tadvyāvṛttyarthamanirvacyalakṣaṇe sattvānadhikaraṇatvarūpaviśeṣaṇāyogāt | na ca pramityaviṣayatvaṃ mithyātvam, brahma tu vedāntajanyavṛttivyāpyamiti vācyam | śuktirūpyāderapi vyavasāyadvārā anuvyavasāyaṃ prati sākṣācca bādhakajñānaṃ prati niṣedhyatvena viṣayatvāt | sattvaprakārakapramāṃ prati sākṣādaviṣayatvaṃ ced, āvaśyakatvāt sattvābhāva eva mithyātvaṃ syāt | 

**samīravāṇī \-**

advaitī svayaṃ (svasiddhāntaṃ sudṛḍhaṃ kartum) mithyātvasya vibhinnānāṃ lakṣaṇānāṃ parīkṣaṇaṃ karoti| advaitagrantheṣu (yathā— advaitasiddhiḥ) eṣaḥ **'svapakṣa-vimarśaḥ'** asti| advaitī mithyātvasya pariṣkṛtaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ vaktuṃ prayatate, tasmin krame saḥ sambhāvitāni lakṣaṇāni upasthāpya svayaṃ teṣāṃ doṣaṃ pradarśayati| 

**1. nyāyāmṛta vākyam \- mithyātvaṃ ca yadyapi nātyantāsattvam, apasiddhāntāt |**  
advaitavedāntasya ekaṃ mahattvapūrṇaṃ tarkaṃ prastauti| advaitavedānte yat 'mithyātvaṃ' svīkṛtaṃ, tat 'atyantāsattvam' (Absolute non-existence) na bhavati| yadi vayaṃ mithyātvam atyantāsattvaṃ manyāmahe, tarhi asmākaṃ siddhāntasyaiva hāniḥ bhaviṣyati, yaḥ 'apasiddhāntaḥ' iti ucyate|

**'atyantāsattvam' kima? (What is absolute non-existence?):**  
loke tādṛśāni vastūni 'atyantāsat' ucyante yāni kadāpi, kutrāpi, kenāpi pramāṇena na dṛśyante| yathā— **'śaśaśṛṅgam'** (rabbit's horn) athavā **'vandhyāputraḥ'** (son of a barren woman)| etāni vastūni kevalaṃ śabdātmakāni santi, eteṣāṃ pratītiḥ (appearance) kadāpi na bhavati|  
**mithyātvaṃ kutaḥ nātyantāsattvam? (Why Mithyatva is not absolute non-existence?):**  
advaitasiddhānte 'mithyā' iti tad ucyate yat pratyakṣaṃ pratīyate kintu jñānena tasya bādhaḥ bhavati| yathā— **'rajjusarpaḥ'**| andhakāre rajjau sarpasya jñānaṃ bhavati| saḥ sarpaḥ tatra pratīyate (appears), tena bhayaṃ api bhavati| ataḥ saḥ 'śaśaśṛṅgavat' sarvathā abhāvasvarūpaḥ nāsti| yadi saḥ sarpaḥ atyantāsat abhaviṣyat, tarhi tasya darśanameva na abhaviṣyat|  
tathaiva, idaṃ **jagat** mithyā asti, parantu atyantāsat nāsti| yataḥ vayaṃ sarve jagat paśyāmaḥ, atra vyavahāraṃ kurmaḥ, sukha-duḥkhādīnāṃ anubhavaṃ ca kurmaḥ| ataḥ jagataḥ mithyātvaṃ nāma 'śūnyatvaṃ' na|

**'apasiddhāntāt' ityasya āśayaḥ (The implication of 'Apasiddhanta'):**  
atra 'apasiddhānta' iti śabdaḥ atīva mahattvapūrṇaḥ| advaitavedānte trividhā sattā svīkṛtā asti:  
1. pāramārthikasattā (brahma)  
2. **vyāvahārikasattā (jagat)**  
3. prātibhāsikasattā (svapnaḥ, rajjusarpādiḥ)

yadi vayaṃ mithyātvasya arthaṃ 'atyantāsat' iti svīkurmaḥ, tarhi:

* **vyāvahārikasattāyāḥ nāśaḥ bhavet:** advaitasiddhānte jagat 'vyāvahārikadṛṣṭyā' satyaṃ manyate| yadi tat atyantāsat syāt, tarhi ayaṃ vyavahāraḥ (eating, walking, studying) kathaṃ sambhavati?  
* **śūnyavādāpattiḥ:** yadi sarvaṃ mithyā iti nāma atyantāsat (void), tarhi advaitavādasya bauddha-śūnyavādena saha abhedaḥ bhaviṣyati| parantu vedāntaḥ śūnyavādī nāsti|  
* **pramāṇavirodhaḥ:** asmākaṃ cakṣuḥ ādi indriyāṇi jagat paśyanti| yadi jagat atyantāsat syāt, tarhi pratyakṣapramāṇasyaiva virodhaḥ bhaviṣyati|

**niṣkarṣaḥ (Conclusion):**  
ataḥ spaṣṭayati yat mithyātvasya arthaḥ 'atyantāsat' iti kadāpi na karaṇīyaḥ| mithyātvaṃ nāma tādṛśaṃ vastu yat pratīyate (pratīti-yogyam), kintu tat vastu trikālasatyaṃ nāsti| ataḥ svasiddhāntasya rakṣārthaṃ (apasiddhāntaṃ nivārayituṃ) mithyātvaṃ atyantāsattvāt bhinnaṃ mantavyam|

**2. nyāyāmṛta vākyam \- nāpyanirvācyatvam, aprasiddheḥ |**  
mithyātvaṃ nāma 'anirvācyatvam' (Indefinability) api vaktuṃ na śakyate, yataḥ tādṛśaṃ kimapi vastu loke **'aprasiddham'** (Not well-known or not established) asti|

**'anirvācyatvam' iti kima? (What is Anirvacyatvam?):**  
advaitavedāntinaḥ vadanti yat jagat 'anirvācyaṃ' vartate| anirvācyatvaṃ nāma — yat vastu 'sat' (Real) iti vaktuṃ na śakyate (yataḥ tasya bādhaḥ bhavati), tathā ca 'asat' (Unreal) iti api vaktuṃ na śakyate (yataḥ tasya pratītiḥ bhavati)| etādṛśaṃ vilakṣaṇaṃ svarūpam 'anirvācyam' ucyate|

**'aprasiddheḥ' iti hetoḥ vivecanam (Logic of 'Aprasiddheḥ'):**  
yadā kasyacit vastunaḥ lakṣaṇaṃ (Definition) kriyate, tadā tat lakṣaṇaṃ loke prasiddhaṃ bhavet| atra ākṣepaḥ asti yat 'anirvācyatvaṃ' nāma kimapi vastu loke prasiddhameva nāsti|

* **laukika-anubhava-virodhaḥ:** loke padārthadvayameva prasiddham asti — **'sat'** (yathā ātmā) athavā **'asat'** (yathā śaśaśṛṅgam)| tṛtīyaḥ kaścana prakāraḥ (category) yaḥ 'sadasadbhyām' bhinnaḥ asti, saḥ asmākaṃ anubhave nāsti|  
* **pramāṇābhāvaḥ:** kena pramāṇena 'anirvācyatvaṃ' siddhyati? pratyakṣapramāṇena tu kevalaṃ vastunaḥ astitvaṃ (Existence) vā abhāvaḥ (Absence) vā jñāyate| 'anirvācya' iti kaścana vilakṣaṇaḥ padārthaḥ pratyakṣeṇa na dṛśyate|  
* **avyāptiḥ / asiddhiḥ:** yadi anirvācyatvaṃ kutrapi prasiddhaṃ nāsti, tarhi tasya lakṣaṇarūpeṇa prayogaḥ kathaṃ bhavitum arhati? yat svayaṃ prasiddhaṃ nāsti, tat anyasya (mithyātvasya) lakṣaṇaṃ bhavituṃ nārhati|

**gūḍhārthaḥ (Deep implication):**  
atra praśnaḥ asti yat — "kiṃ asti tādṛśaṃ kimapi vastu yat sat api nāsti asat api nāsti?"  
yadi vadanti 'rajjusarpaḥ' anirvācyaḥ, tarhi tārkikaḥ pṛcchati — rajjusarpaḥ api jñāne sati 'asat' eva bhavati| ataḥ saḥ 'asat' koṭau eva āgacchati, na tu kasyāpi tṛtīyakoṭau| ataḥ **'anirvācyatvaṃ'** nāma kevalaṃ pāribhāṣikaḥ śabdaḥ asti, loke tasya aprasiddhiḥ asti|

**niṣkarṣaḥ (Conclusion):**  
ataḥ paṅkteḥ āśayaḥ asti yat — mithyātvasya vivecane 'anirvācyatvaṃ' iti śabdaprayogaḥ tārkikadṛṣṭyā kaṭhinaḥ, yataḥ nyāyaśāstre vā sāmānyavyavahāre vā 'sat' 'asat' iti dvau eva mārgau staḥ| tṛtīyaḥ 'anirvācya' mārgaḥ loke aprasiddhaḥ asti|

**3. nyāyāmṛta  vākyam \- nāpi sadviviktatvam, sato'pi sadantaraviviktatvāt |**  
mithyātvasya tṛtīyaṃ sambhāvitaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ khaṇḍayati| atra 'sadviviktatvaṃ' (Difference from the Real) iti lakṣaṇe yaḥ tarkadoṣaḥ asti, tasya vivecanaṃ kriyate| 

**pūrvapakṣaḥ (Proposed Definition):**  
kaścit evaṃ vaktuṃ śaknoti yat— **"mithyātvaṃ nāma sadviviktatvam"**|  
asya arthaḥ— yat 'sataḥ' (satyavastunaḥ) bhinnaṃ (viviktaṃ) bhavati, tat 'mithyā' iti ucyate| yathā— brahma satyaṃ vartate, tasmāt bhinnaṃ yat kimapi asti (jagadādi), tat mithyā|

**khaṇḍanasya ādhāraḥ (The Reason for Rejection):**  
advaitavedāntinaḥ vadanti — **"sato'pi sadantaraviviktatvāt" (distinct / different from the Real)**| asya padacchedaḥ— "sataḥ api sat-antara-viviktatvāt"| asya tarkaḥ evam asti:

* loke bahūni vastūni 'sat' (Real/Existent) iti manyante (vyavahāradṛṣṭyā)| yathā— 'ghaṭaḥ' sat asti, 'paṭaḥ' (vastram) api sat asti|  
* parantu 'ghaṭaḥ' 'paṭāt' bhinnaḥ asti| arthāt ghaṭaḥ paṭāpekṣayā 'viviktaḥ' (Distinct) asti|  
* atra ghaṭaḥ api 'sat' asti, paṭaḥ api 'sat' asti|  
* yadi vayaṃ vadāmaḥ yat **'sadviviktatvaṃ mithyātvam'** (yat sataḥ bhinnaṃ tat mithyā), tarhi ghaṭaḥ paṭāt bhinnaḥ asti iti kṛtvā ghaṭaḥ 'mithyā' bhavet|  
* tathaiva, paṭaḥ ghaṭāt (yaḥ sat asti) bhinnaḥ asti iti kṛtvā paṭaḥ api 'mithyā' bhavet|


yadā advaitavedāntinaḥ 'sadviviktatvaṃ mithyātvam' iti lakṣaṇaṃ vadanti, tadā **siddhasādhana** doṣaḥ bhavati|

**siddhasādhanasya arthaḥ (Meaning of Siddha-Sadhana):**  
"siddhasya sādhanaṃ siddhasādhanam|" arthāt yat vastu prativādinā (Opponent) pūrvameva svīkṛtaṃ (Established/Proven) asti, tasyaiva punaḥ siddhikaraṇaṃ 'siddhasādhana' iti ucyate| tarke eṣaḥ doṣaḥ manyate, yataḥ anena kimapi navīnaṃ na siddhyati|

**prakṛte siddhasādhanasya prasaṅgaḥ (Context in the present argument):**  
yadā advaitī vadati— **"jagat mithyā, sadviviktatvāt"** (jagat mithyā asti yataḥ tat 'sataḥ' bhinnam asti)|  
tadā advaitinaḥ lakṣyaṃ tu jagataḥ 'asatyatvaṃ' sādhayitum asti| parantu atra 'sadviviktatvaṃ' (Difference from the real) iti hetuḥ doṣapūrṇaḥ bhavati:

* **prativādinaḥ (dvaitinaḥ/naiyāyikasya) matam:** naiyāyikāḥ manyante yat jagati bahavaḥ padārthāḥ santi, te ca sarve 'satyāḥ' (Real) santi| yathā— 'ghaṭaḥ' api satyaḥ, 'paṭaḥ' (cloth) api satyaḥ|  
* **bhedasvīkāraḥ:** naiyāyikaḥ pūrvameva etat svīkaroti yat **ghaṭaḥ paṭāt bhinnaḥ asti|** arthāt ghaṭaḥ 'paṭātmaka-sataḥ' viviktaḥ (bhinnaḥ) asti|  
* **doṣasya udayaḥ:**  
  1. advaitī sādhayituṃ icchati — **"jagat mithyā"**|  
  2. naiyāyikaḥ vadati — "ahaṃ pūrvameva svīkaromi yat 'ghaṭaḥ' anyasmāt 'sataḥ' (paṭāt) bhinnaḥ asti| etad 'sadviviktatvaṃ' tu mama mate api siddhameva asti|"  
  3. ataḥ advaitī yaṃ hetuṃ (sadviviktatvaṃ) dadāti, saḥ hetuḥ naiyāyikena pūrvameva 'ghaṭa-paṭādiṣu' svīkṛtaḥ| parantu naiyāyikasya mate saḥ bhedaḥ (Difference) 'mithyātvaṃ' na sādhayati, api tu 'vastu-bhedaṃ' sādhayati|


**niṣkarṣātmakaṃ vivecanam (Final Analysis):**  
yadi 'sadviviktatvam' eva mithyātvaṃ syāt, tarhi:

* **advaitī vadati:** ghaṭaḥ 'sataḥ' (paṭāt) bhinnaḥ asti, ataḥ ghaṭaḥ mithyā|  
* **naiyāyikaḥ vadati:** ghaṭaḥ 'sataḥ' (paṭāt) bhinnaḥ asti, etad tu mayā **siddhameva**| mama mate ayaṃ bhedaḥ 'satyaḥ' asti| bhavān kevalaṃ mama siddhaṃ viṣayaṃ punaḥ sādhayati|

evam atra **'siddhasādhanam'** bhavati| advaitī kimapi navīnaṃ 'mithyātvaṃ' sādhayituṃ na śaknoti, kevalaṃ padārthānāṃ parasparaṃ 'bhedaṃ' sādhayati, yaḥ bhedaḥ dvaitināṃ mate pūrvameva siddhaḥ asti|

**4. nyāyāmṛta  vākyam \- nāpi sattvānadhikaraṇatvam, nirdharmake brahmaṇyapi sattvāt | na ca nirdharmakatvādeva tatra sattvānadhikaraṇatvamapi neti vācyam | nirdharmakatvarūpahetoḥ sattvānadhikaraṇatvābhāvarūpasādhyasya ca bhāvābhāvābhyāṃ vyāghātenābhāvarūpadharmaniṣedhāyogāt | anyathā'sattvānadhikaraṇatvasyāpi tatrābhāvena tadvyāvṛttyarthamanirvacyalakṣaṇe sattvānadhikaraṇatvarūpaviśeṣaṇāyogāt |**

advaitī sādhayituṃ icchati yat jagat 'mithyā' asti| tadarthaṃ saḥ ekaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ prastauti — **"sattvānadhikaraṇatvaṃ mithyātvam"**| arthāt, jagati 'sattvaṃ' (Reality/Existence) nāsti, ataḥ jagat mithyā|

saḥ paśyati yat yadi ahaṃ **'sattva-dharmādhikaraṇatva-abhāvaṃ'** mithyātvaṃ vadāmi, tarhi **brahmaṇi** eva mama lakṣaṇasya ativyāptiḥ bhaviṣyati| kathamiti cet:

* **brahmaṇaḥ nirdharmakatvam:** advaitasiddhānte brahma 'nirdharmakam' asti| tatra ko'pi 'dharmaḥ' (Attribute) nāsti|  
* **sattvaṃ kaścana dharmaḥ:** 'sattvam' (Existence-ness) iti kaścana dharmaḥ asti| (A property like 'Ghatatva').  
* **brahmaṇi sattvābhāvaḥ:** yataḥ brahma nirdharmakam, ataḥ brahmaṇi 'sattva' nāmakaḥ dharmaḥ api na tiṣṭhati| (Note: Brahman *is* Existence, but it does not *have* the property of existence).  
* **ativyāptiḥ:** ataḥ brahma api 'sattvānadhikaraṇam' (sattva-dharmarahitam) abhavat| yadi etat mithyātvasya lakṣaṇaṃ syāt, tarhi brahma api 'mithyā' iti ucyeta|

yadi advaitī svasyaiva brahma 'mithyā' iti ghoṣayet, tarhi tasya sarvaḥ siddhāntaḥ eva vinaśyet|

* **sādhyam:** jagat mithyā|  
* **bhayam:** brahmaṇi api mithyātvasya āpattiḥ|

ataḥ advaitī etat lakṣaṇaṃ khaṇḍayati — **"nirdharmake brahmaṇyapi sattvāt"** (iti hetoḥ etat lakṣaṇaṃ na grāhyam)|

**'sattvaṃ' ca brahma ca (Technical Distinction):**  
atra advaitī sūkṣmaṃ bhedaṃ karoti:

1. **brahma \= sat svarūpam (Pure Existence):** brahma svayameva sat asti, tatra sattvaṃ dharmarūpeṇa na tiṣṭhati| (Brahman is not the locus of Sattva, Brahman *is* Sat).  
2. **sattvam \= dharmaḥ (Attribute of being real):** ayaṃ dharmaḥ jagati sādhayituṃ śakyate, parantu brahmaṇi na|

**niṣkarṣaḥ:**  
advaitī evaṃ niṣkarṣaṃ dadāti yat— **'sattva-dharmābhāvaḥ'** mithyātvaṃ bhavituṃ nārhati| yataḥ evaṃ sati nirdharmaka-brahmaṇaḥ api mithyātvāpattiḥ bhavati, yā asmākaṃ siddhāntasya virodhinī asti| 

**|| mithyātva-nirūpaṇa-saṃvādaḥ ||**

**śiṣyaḥ:** bhagavan\! advaitasiddhānte jagat 'mithyā' iti ucyate| asya mithyātvasya kiṃ lakṣaṇam? kiṃ mithyātvaṃ nāma 'atyantāsattvam' (Absolute non-existence)? yathā 'śaśaśṛṅgam'?

**guruḥ:** na vatsa\! **"mithyātvaṃ ca yadyapi nātyantāsattvam | apasiddhāntāt |"**

yadi jagat atyantāsat syāt, tarhi tasya pratītiḥ (Appearance) eva na bhavet| vayaṃ tu jagat paśyāmaḥ, atra vyavahāraṃ ca kurmaḥ| yadi atyantāsattvaṃ svīkurmaḥ, tarhi pratyakṣa-virodhaḥ bhaviṣyati, sa ca asmākaṃ **'apasiddhāntaḥ'** (Fallacy against our own doctrine) bhavet|

**śiṣyaḥ:** tarhi kiṃ mithyātvaṃ nāma **'anirvācyatvam'** (Indefinability)?

**guruḥ:** **"nāpyanirvācyatvam, aprasiddheḥ |"**

loke 'sat' 'asat' iti dvau eva prakārau prasiddhau| 'sadasadbhyām vilakṣaṇaṃ' kimapi vastu loke na prasiddham| ataḥ aprasiddhatvāt etat lakṣaṇaṃ prārambhe kartuṃ na śakyate|

**śiṣyaḥ:** astu| tarhi evaṃ vadāmaḥ— **'sadviviktatvaṃ mithyātvam'** (Being different from the Real)| yat satyāt bhinnaṃ tat mithyā|

**guruḥ:** **"nāpi sadviviktatvam, sato'pi sadantaraviviktatvāt |"**

atra 'siddhasādhana' doṣaḥ bhavati| loke ghaṭaḥ api 'sat' (Real) iti manyate, paṭaḥ api 'sat' iti manyate| ghaṭaḥ paṭāt bhinnaḥ asti| yadi 'sadviviktatvaṃ' (satyāt bhinnatvaṃ) mithyātvaṃ syāt, tarhi ghaṭaḥ paṭāt bhinnaḥ iti kṛtvā 'mithyā' bhavet| evaṃ tu jagataḥ sarve satyapadārthāḥ mithyā iti ucyeran| dvaitinaḥ (naiyāyikāḥ) tu bhedaṃ satyaṃ manyante| ataḥ teṣāṃ mate eṣaḥ bhedaḥ pūrvameva siddhaḥ, tena 'mithyātvaṃ' na siddhyati|

**śiṣyaḥ:** tarhi caturthaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ paśyāmaḥ— **'sattvānadhikaraṇatvaṃ mithyātvam'** (Not being the locus of the property 'Existence')| yasmin 'sattva-dharmaḥ' nāsti, tat mithyā|

**guruḥ:** vatsa\! atra ativyāptiḥ bhavati— **"nirdharmake brahmaṇyapi sattvāt |"**

advaitasiddhānte brahma 'nirdharmakam' asti| tatra ko'pi dharmaḥ nāsti, ataḥ 'sattva' nāmakaḥ dharmaḥ api tatra nāsti| evaṃ ca brahma api 'sattvānadhikaraṇaṃ' jātam| yadi etat mithyātvaṃ syāt, tarhi brahma api mithyā bhavet\!

**śiṣyaḥ:** bhagavan\! "sattvānadhikaraṇatvaṃ mithyātvam" (sattva-dharmānāśrayatvaṃ mithyātvam) iti lakṣaṇe bhavān **brahmaṇi ativyāptim** uktavān| yataḥ brahma nirdharmakam asti, ataḥ tatra sattva-dharmaḥ nāsti| evaṃ ca brahma api 'sattvānadhikaraṇaṃ' jātam|

parantu mama manasi ekā śaṅkā asti— advaitasiddhānte brahma tu **'nirdharmakam'** (dharmarahitam) asti| 'sattvānadhikaraṇatvam' nāma 'sattva-abhāvaḥ'| abhāvaḥ api kaścana 'dharmaḥ' eva| yadi brahmaṇi ko'pi dharmaḥ nāsti, tarhi **'sattvānadhikaraṇatva'** rūpaḥ dharmaḥ api tatra na bhaviṣyati| evaṃ ca brahmaṇi ativyāptiḥ svataḥ eva nivṛttā, khalu?

**guruḥ (ācāryaḥ):** vatsa\! **"na ca ... iti vācyam"**| tava eṣaḥ tarkaḥ ayuktaḥ|

**śiṣyaḥ:** kutaḥ ācārya?

**guruḥ:** śṛṇu| atra **'vyāghāta'** nāmakaḥ tarkadoṣaḥ bhavati|  bhavān vadati yat brahma 'nirdharmakam' asti, ataḥ tatra 'sattvānadhikaraṇatvaṃ' (sattva-abhāvaḥ) nāsti| tarkaśāstrasya niyamānusāram— **'abhāvasya abhāvaḥ bhāvaḥ bhavati'** (Negation of an absence is a presence)|  
yadi brahmaṇi 'sattvānadhikaraṇatvaṃ' (sattva-abhāvaḥ) nāsti, tarhi tasya arthaḥ bhavati yat brahmaṇi **'sattva-dharmaḥ' asti**| yadi brahmaṇi 'sattva-dharmaḥ' asti, tarhi bhavataḥ **'nirdharmakatva'** pratijñā kathaṃ siddhyati? dharmarahite brahmaṇi dharmaḥ asti iti vacanaṃ vyāhatam (Self-contradictory) khalu? eṣa eva **"bhāvābhāvābhyāṃ vyāghātaḥ"**|

**śiṣyaḥ:** yadi brahmaṇi 'abhāvarūpa-dharmāḥ' svīkriyante, tarhi advaita-hāniḥ (brahmaṇaḥ nirdharmakatva-hāniḥ) na bhaviṣyati kim?

**guruḥ:**  asya samādhānaṃ advaitavedāntinaḥ **'svarūpātmaka-abhāva'** siddhāntena kurvanti|

**abhāvaḥ kimapi atiriktaṃ tattvaṃ na (Abhava is not a separate entity)**  
advaitasiddhānte (viśeṣataḥ vedāntaparibhāṣāyāṃ kaumudyāṃ ca) **'abhāvaḥ adhikaraṇasvarūpaḥ'** iti manyate|

* yadā vayaṃ vadāmaḥ "bhūtale ghaṭo nāsti" (ghaṭābhāvaḥ), tadā tārkikadṛṣṭyā saḥ abhāvaḥ **'kevalaṃ bhūtalam'** eva asti| bhūtalam atiricya 'abhāvaḥ' nāma kaścana pṛthak padārthaḥ tatra na tiṣṭhati|  
* tathaiva, yadā vayaṃ vadāmaḥ "brahmaṇi sattvānadhikaraṇatvam (dharmābhāvaḥ) asti", tadā saḥ abhāvaḥ brahmaṇi sthitaḥ kaścana 'dharmaḥ' na, apitu **brahma-svarūpam** eva| brahma svayameva dharmābhāvasvarūpam asti| ataḥ atirikta-dharma-asvīkārāt 'nirdharmakatva' siddhāntasya hāniḥ na bhavati|

**kalpita-dharma-niṣedhaḥ (Negation of Imagined Attributes)**  
advaitinaḥ vadanti yat brahmaṇaḥ nirdharmakatvaṃ sādhayituṃ vayaṃ yeṣāṃ 'abhāvānāṃ' prayogaṃ kurmaḥ (yathā— advaitam, nirguṇatvam, aśarīratvam), te sarve abhāvāḥ **'vyāvahārika-dṛṣṭyā'** ucyante|

* yadā vayaṃ 'advaitam' (dvaita-abhāvaḥ) iti vadāmaḥ, tadā uddeśyaṃ dvaitasya niṣedhaḥ asti|  
* ekavāraṃ dvaite niṣiddhe sati, saḥ 'abhāvaḥ' api brahmasvarūpe eva vilīyate|  
* yathā agniḥ kāṣṭhaṃ dagdhvā svayaṃ api śāmyati, tathaiva niṣedhamukhena prayuktāḥ dharmāḥ (niṣedha-rūpāḥ) prapañcaṃ niṣidhya svayaṃ brahmasvarūpe antarbhavanti|

**'vyāghāta' tarkasya punarāvṛttiḥ (Re-addressing the Contradiction)**  
bhavatāṃ praśnaḥ āsīt— "abhāvarūpa-dharmāḥ santi cet advaita-hāniḥ"| parantu atra tarkaḥ evaṃ bhavati:

* yadi vayaṃ **'abhāvarūpa-dharmān'** api niṣedhāmaḥ (arthāt brahmaṇi abhāvaḥ api nāsti iti vadāmaḥ), tarhi agatyā asmābhiḥ **'bhāvarūpa-dharmāḥ'** svīkaraṇīyāḥ bhaveyuḥ (Double negation leads to a positive)|  
* yadi brahmaṇi 'sattvānadhikaraṇatvaṃ' (abhāvaḥ) nāsti iti vadāmaḥ, tarhi brahmaṇi 'sattva-dharmaḥ' (Property of existence) siddhaḥ bhaviṣyati|  
* abhāvāpekṣayā bhāvarūpa-dharmaḥ advaitasiddhāntasya kṛte adhikaṃ hānikārakaḥ| yataḥ bhāvarūpa-dharme svīkṛte brahma 'saguṇam' (Possessing qualities) bhaviṣyati, yena advaitasya mukhyasiddhāntaḥ eva vinaśyet|


ataḥ advaitasiddhānte evaṃ vyavasthā asti:

1. **pāramārthika-dṛṣṭyā (Absolute Reality):** brahmaṇi na bhāvarūpa-dharmāḥ santi, na ca abhāvarūpa-dharmāḥ| tat **'niḥprapañcam'** asti|  
2. **tārkika-vimarśa-dṛṣṭyā (Logical Discourse):** brahmaṇaḥ satyatvaṃ rakṣituṃ tatra 'bhāvarūpa-dharmāṇāṃ' (Properties) abhāvaḥ avaśyaṃ svīkartavyaḥ| saḥ abhāvaḥ brahmaṇaḥ svarūpameva, na tu kaścana pṛthak dharmaḥ|  
   

ataḥ uktaṃ— **"nirdharmake brahmaṇyapi sattvāt"**| asya arthaḥ— brahmaṇi bhāvarūpa-dharmasya abhāvāt ativyāptiḥ bhavati eva| yadi taṃ abhāvaṃ necchāmaḥ, tarhi bhāvarūpa-dharmaḥ āpadyeta, yaḥ sutarāṃ ayuktaḥ|

abhāvarūpa-dharmāṇāṃ svīkāraḥ kevalaṃ bhāvarūpa-dharmāṇāṃ niṣedhārthaṃ kriyate| saḥ abhāvaḥ brahma-atiriktaḥ na bhavati iti kāraṇāt **'advaita-hāniḥ'** na bhavati|

**śiṣyaḥ:** satyam ācārya\! mayā na cintitam| abhāvasya niṣedhena bhāvaḥ āyāti, yena nirdharmakatvaṃ naśyati| ataḥ brahmaṇi 'sattvānadhikaraṇatvaṃ' (sattva-abhāvaḥ) aṅgīkaraṇīyam eva| kintu ācārya, asmin viṣaye agrimā paṅktiḥ **"anyathā'sattvānadhikaraṇatvasyāpi..."** kimartham uktā?

**guruḥ:** tasyāḥ āśayaḥ atīva gambhīraḥ| advaitaśāstre mithyātvasya (anirvacanīyatvasya) lakṣaṇaṃ kṛtam asti— **"sattvānadhikaraṇatve sati asattvānadhikaraṇatvam"**|  
asmin lakṣaṇe padadvayam asti:  
1. **asattvānadhikaraṇatvam:** (śaśaśṛṅgavat 'asat' na bhavet)| etad 'asat' vastunaḥ nivāraṇārtham|  
2. **sattvānadhikaraṇatvam:** (brahmavat 'sat' na bhavet)| etad 'brahmaṇaḥ' nivāraṇārtham (Exclusion of Brahman)|

yadi tava tarkaḥ satyaḥ syāt yat "nirdharmakatvāt brahmaṇi kimapi nāsti", tarhi brahmaṇi **'asattvānadhikaraṇatvam'** api na syāt| yadi brahmaṇi 'asattvānadhikaraṇatvaṃ' nāsti, tarhi brahma asmāt lakṣaṇāt svataḥ eva bahiḥ asti|

tarhi ācāryaiḥ lakṣaṇe **'sattvānadhikaraṇatvam'** iti viśeṣaṇaṃ kimarthaṃ dattam? tasya prayojanaṃ tu brahma-vyāvṛttiḥ (to exclude Brahman) eva asti| yadi brahmaṇi sattvānadhikaraṇatvaṃ na abhaviṣyat, tarhi etat viśeṣaṇaṃ vyartham (Redundant) abhaviṣyat| parantu ācāryaiḥ etat dattam, ataḥ siddhaṃ bhavati yat brahmaṇi **'sattvānadhikaraṇatvam'** (sattva-dharmābhāvaḥ) tārkikadṛṣṭyā tiṣṭhati eva|

**śiṣyaḥ:** aho\! idānīṃ spaṣṭam abhavat|  
1. brahma nirdharmakam asti, ataḥ tatra sattva-dharmaḥ nāsti|  
2. ataḥ brahma **'sattvānadhikaraṇam'** anivāryatayā asti|  
3. ataḥ 'sattvānadhikaraṇatvaṃ mithyātvam' iti lakṣaṇaṃ **brahmaṇi ativyāptam** asti|  
etasmāt kāraṇāt ayaṃ caturthaḥ pakṣaḥ api dūṣitaḥ|

**guruḥ:** sādhu vatsa\! samyak jñātam|

**5. nyāyāmṛta  vākyam \- na ca pramityaviṣayatvaṃ mithyātvam, brahma tu vedāntajanyavṛttivyāpyamiti vācyam | śuktirūpyāderapi vyavasāyadvārā anuvyavasāyaṃ prati sākṣācca bādhakajñānaṃ prati niṣedhyatvena viṣayatvāt | sattvaprakārakapramāṃ prati sākṣādaviṣayatvaṃ ced, āvaśyakatvāt sattvābhāva eva mithyātvaṃ syāt |**

**śiṣyaḥ:** guro\! advaitinaḥ svasya mithyātva-lakṣaṇāni svayaṃ kathaṃ parīkṣante? idānīṃ pañcama-lakṣaṇasya vāraḥ| te kiṃ vadanti?

**guruḥ:** vatsa\! paśya, advaitinaḥ svapakṣaṃ sudṛḍhaṃ kartuṃ prathamaṃ sarala\-lakṣaṇaṃ upasthāpayanti— **"pramityaviṣayatvaṃ mithyātvam"**|  
teṣām āśayaḥ eṣaḥ— yad vastu 'pramāyāḥ' (pramāṇajanya-jñānasya) viṣayaḥ na bhavati, tat 'mithyā' iti| yathā rajjusarpaḥ|

**śiṣyaḥ:** parantu guro\! advaita-siddhānte brahma api tu 'avāṅmanasagocaram' (aviṣayaḥ) asti| tarhi advaitinaḥ svayameva asmin lakṣaṇe **'ativyāpti'** doṣaṃ kathaṃ na paśyanti?

**guruḥ:** paśyanti eva\! ata eva te svayameva evaṃ tarkayanti— **"na ca pramityaviṣayatvaṃ mithyātvam, brahma tu vedāntajanyavṛttivyāpyamiti vācyam |"**  
advaitinaḥ svapakṣaṃ rakṣituṃ evaṃ vimarśaṃ kurvanti:

* te vadanti— "yadyapi brahma 'aviṣayam' ucyate, tathāpi tat sarvathā ajñātaṃ nāsti| yadā kaścana mumukṣuḥ vedānta-vākyāni śṛṇoti, tadā tasya buddhau ekā 'akhaṇḍākāra-vṛttiḥ' jāyate| sā vṛttiḥ brahma vyāpnoti| ataḥ brahma **'vedāntajanya-vṛtti-vyāpyam'** asti| ataḥ brahma 'aviṣayaḥ' (Unknowable) nāsti| evaṃ ca brahmaṇi ativyāptiḥ na bhaviṣyati|"


**śiṣyaḥ:** aho\! teṣāṃ yuktiḥ tu atīva caturā asti| te 'vṛtti-vyāpyatva' mādhyamena brahmaṇaḥ jñānaviṣayatvaṃ sādhayanti| tarhi idaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ tanmate śuddhaṃ jātaṃ khalu?

**guruḥ:** na vatsa\! atraiva advaita-siddhāntasya āntarikaḥ vyāghātaḥ (Internal Contradiction) asti, yaṃ te svayaṃ svīkurvanti| advaita-mate 'jñānaṃ' (Cognition) dvividhaṃ bhavati:

1. **vṛtti-vyāpyatvam:** kevalaṃ vṛtteḥ brahmaṇaḥ āvaraṇa-bhaṅgaḥ|  
2. **phala-vyāpyatvam:** yatra jñānasya prakāśaḥ (cit-phalam) vastuni patati|  
**advaitināṃ svayameva niṣkarṣaḥ eṣaḥ bhavati:**  
brahma 'vṛtti-vyāpyam' tu asti (āvaraṇa-bhaṅgāya), parantu tat brahma **'phala-avyāpyam'** asti (yataḥ brahma svayaṃ prakāśarūpam asti, tasya prakāśanāya anyasya prakāśasya āvaśyakatā nāsti)|  
**śiṣyaḥ:** tarhi asya 'lakṣaṇe' kaḥ pariṇāmaḥ?  
**guruḥ:** advaitināṃ mate 'pramitiḥ' (Valid Knowledge) tadaiva ucyate yadā vastu **'phala-vyāpyaṃ'** bhavati| yato hi brahma **'phala-avyāpyam'** asti, tato hi tārkika-dṛṣṭyā brahma **'pramitya-aviṣayaḥ'** (Not an object of valid cognition) eva tiṣṭhati|  
ataḥ yadi te 'pramityaviṣayatvaṃ' mithyātvaṃ vadanti, tarhi **brahma api mithyā** iti siddhaṃ bhavati|

**śiṣyaḥ:** aho\! idānīṃ mayā advaitināṃ vimarśa-paddhatiḥ jñātā|  
1. te prathamaṃ 'pramityaviṣayatvaṃ' lakṣaṇaṃ kurvanti|  
2. punaḥ 'vṛtti-vyāpyatva' hetunā brahmaṇaḥ rakṣaṇaṃ kartuṃ prayatante|  
3. parantu ante svasyaiva 'phala-avyāpyatva' (svayaṃprakāśatva) siddhāntena teṣāṃ rakṣaṇa-kavacaṃ truṭyati|  
4. ataḥ brahmaṇi **'ativyāptiḥ'** anivārthā|

**guruḥ:** satyam| ata eva advaitinaḥ svayaṃ vadanti— "na ca ... iti vācyam"| arthāt eṣā yuktiḥ api na calati| 

**śiṣyaḥ:** guro\! advaitinaḥ 'pramityaviṣayatvaṃ mithyātvam' iti lakṣaṇaṃ parīkṣante| pūrvaṃ taiḥ brahmaṇaḥ rakṣaṇaṃ kṛtam| idānīṃ te svayameva **śukti-rajate** (mithyā-vastuni) etat lakṣaṇaṃ kathaṃ ghaṭate iti paśyanti?

**guruḥ:** satyaṃ vatsa\! advaitināṃ vimarśaḥ atīva vaiyaktikaḥ tārkikaśca asti| te paśyanti yat yadi 'aviṣayatvaṃ' (jñānasya viṣayaḥ na bhavet iti) mithyātvaṃ syāt, tarhi śukti-rajataṃ jñānasya viṣayaḥ na bhavet| parantu loke tu tat viṣayaḥ bhavati eva|

**śiṣyaḥ:** kathaṃ viṣayaḥ bhavati? tat tu mithyā asti khalu?

**guruḥ:** advaitināṃ tarkeṇa paśya— **"śuktirūpyāderapi vyavasāyadvārā anuvyavasāyaṃ prati sākṣācca bādhakajñānaṃ prati niṣedhyatvena viṣayatvāt |"**  
atra trīṇi kāraṇāni dattāni santi:

1. **vyavasāya-anuvyavasāyau (Primary & Secondary Knowledge):**  
yadā kaścana bhrāntyā śuktau rajataṃ paśyati, tadā saḥ jānāti— "idaṃ rajatam" (eṣaḥ **vyavasāyaḥ**)| tadanantaraṃ saḥ cintayati— "ahaṃ rajataṃ jānāmi" (eṣaḥ **anuvyavasāyaḥ**)| atra 'rajataṃ' jñānasya viṣayaḥ jātaḥ| ataḥ rajataṃ 'aviṣayaḥ' na abhavat|

2. **bādhakajñānaṃ prati viṣayatvam (Sublating Knowledge):**  
yadā prakāśe sati yathārthajñānaṃ bhavati— **"nedaṃ rajatam"** (This is not silver), tadā api rajataṃ jñānasya viṣayaḥ bhavati| kathamiti cet— **'niṣedhyatvena'** (As an object of negation)| kasya niṣedhaḥ bhavati? rajatasya eva| ataḥ niṣedha-jñāne api rajataṃ viṣayaḥ asti eva|

3. **sākṣāt viṣayatvam (Direct Objectivity):**  
yadā vayaṃ vadāmaḥ "rajataṃ nāsti", tadā 'rajataṃ' sākṣāt asmākaṃ buddhau viṣayatvena tiṣṭhati, anyathā tasya niṣedhaḥ eva asambhavaḥ|

**śiṣyaḥ:** aho\! tarhi advaitināṃ niṣkarṣaḥ kaḥ?

**guruḥ:** teṣāṃ vimarśasya phalam etadeva yat— "mithyā-vastu (rajatam) api kenacit prakāreṇa jñānasya viṣayaḥ bhavati eva| ataḥ **'pramityaviṣayatvaṃ'** (Unknowability) iti sarala\-lakṣaṇaṃ 'avyāpti' doṣeṇa grastam asti, yataḥ tat rajata-ādiṣu mithyā-vastuṣu na ghaṭate|"

**śiṣyaḥ:** idānīṃ mayā spaṣṭaṃ jñātam| advaitinaḥ kimarthaṃ "na ca ... iti vācyam" iti uktvā asya lakṣaṇasya pariṣkāraṃ kurvanti| te jānanti yat yasya niṣedhaḥ kriyate (rajatasya), saḥ api jñānasya viṣayaḥ bhavati eva| ataḥ 'viṣayatvābhāvaḥ' (Unknowability) mithyātvaṃ bhavituṃ nārhati|

śukti-rajataṃ (mithyā-vastu) api jñānasya viṣayaḥ bhavati| bhrānti-kāle 'idaṃ rajatam' iti jñāne, paścāt 'ahaṃ rajataṃ jānāmi' iti anuvyavasāye, ante ca 'nedaṃ rajatam' iti bādhaka-jñāne (niṣedhyatvena) rajataṃ viṣayaḥ asti eva| ataḥ 'pramityaviṣayatvaṃ' (aviṣayatvaṃ) mithyātvasya lakṣaṇaṃ na bhavituṃ śaknoti, yataḥ etat rajata-ādiṣu na ghaṭate (avyāptiḥ)|

**śiṣyaḥ:** guro\! pūrvaṃ tu siddhaṃ jātaṃ yat śukti-rajataṃ (mithyā-vastu) jñānasya viṣayaḥ bhavati| tarhi advaitinaḥ svasya 'pramityaviṣayatva' lakṣaṇaṃ rakṣituṃ kiṃ navīnaṃ pariṣkāraṃ (Refinement) kurvanti?

**guruḥ:** vatsa\! te atīva buddhimantaḥ| te svayameva evaṃ pariṣkurvanti— **"sattvaprakārakapramāṃ prati sākṣādaviṣayatvaṃ ced..."**|

teṣāṃ vicāraḥ asti— "kevalaṃ 'viṣayatvaṃ' mā astu| vayaṃ vadāmaḥ— yad vastu **'satyatvena'** (as real) pramā-jñānasya viṣayaḥ na bhavati, tat mithyā|"  
śukti-rajataṃ jñānasya viṣayaḥ tu asti, parantu tat 'satyam' iti rūpeṇa kenāpi pramā-pramāṇena (Valid knowledge) na jñāyate| ataḥ rajataṃ mithyā iti siddham|

**śiṣyaḥ:** aho\! eṣaḥ pariṣkāraḥ tu samīcīnaḥ dṛśyate| anena śukti-rajate 'avyāptiḥ' api gatā, tathā ca brahmaṇi (yat satya-jñānasya viṣayaḥ asti) 'ativyāptiḥ' api na bhaviṣyati|

**guruḥ:** paśya vatsa\! atraiva **lāghava-gaurava-vimarśaḥ** āgacchati| advaitinaḥ svayaṃ cintayanti— **"āvaśyakatvāt sattvābhāva eva mithyātvaṃ syāt |"**

**śiṣyaḥ:** asya ko'rthaḥ?

**guruḥ:** saralaḥ arthaḥ eṣaḥ— "yadi bhavān vadati yat 'idaṃ satya-jñānasya viṣayaḥ na bhavati', tarhi tasya phalitārthaḥ (Direct meaning) kimasti? tasya arthaḥ asti yat— **tasmin vastuni 'satyatvaṃ' (Sattva) nāsti|**"

paśya, advaitināṃ vimarśa-saraṇiḥ (Logic path):  
1. "satya-jñāna-aviṣayatvam" iti kathanaṃ 'gauravam' (Logically heavy/complex) asti|  
2. "sattvābhāvaḥ" (Absence of Reality) iti kathanaṃ 'lāghavam' (Simple/Direct) asti|  
tarke ayaṃ niyamaḥ yat yatra lāghavena kāryaṃ siddhyati, tatra gauravaṃ na aṅgīkriyate|

**śiṣyaḥ:** tarhi advaitinaḥ punaḥ tatraiva āgatāḥ yatra te caturthe lakṣaṇe āsan?

**guruḥ:** satyam\! **'sattvābhāvaḥ'** (sattvānadhikaraṇatvam) iti tu caturthaṃ lakṣaṇam āsīt| tat lakṣaṇaṃ taiḥ pūrvameva kimarthaṃ tyaktam? yataḥ tatra **brahmaṇi ativyāptiḥ** (nirdharmake brahmaṇyapi sattvāt) āsīt|

ataḥ advaitināṃ vimarśasya niṣkarṣaḥ eṣaḥ—

* pañcamaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ (pramityaviṣayatvaṃ) yadi rakṣituṃ prayatante, tarhi tat punaḥ caturthe lakṣaṇe (sattvābhāvaḥ) vilīyate|  
* caturthaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ tu pūrvameva doṣapūrṇaṃ siddham|  
* ataḥ, "pramityaviṣayatvaṃ mithyātvam" iti lakṣaṇamapi vimarśe ayogyaṃ siddhaṃ bhavati|

**śiṣyaḥ:** aho\! advaitināṃ vimarśa-cāturyaṃ adbhutam| te svayaṃ paśyanti yat kathaṃ navīnaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ purātana-doṣe eva paryavasannaṃ bhavati| ataḥ te pañcamaṃ lakṣaṇamapi tyaktvā agre gacchanti|

**guruḥ:** sādhu vatsa\!  asti yat— yadi 'satyatvena jñāna-aviṣayatvaṃ' mithyātvaṃ ucyate, tarhi lāghavāt tasya arthaḥ 'sattvābhāvaḥ' (Lack of reality) ityeva bhavati| 'sattvābhāvaḥ' iti caturthaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ tu pūrvameva brahmaṇi ativyāpti-kāraṇāt khaṇḍitam| ataḥ pañcamaṃ lakṣaṇamapi tārkika-lāghavābhāvāt (Due to lack of parsimony) apariṣkṛtaṃ siddhaṃ bhavati| evaṃ te krameṇa sarvān pakṣān vimṛśya ante siddhāntaṃ vakṣyanti|

**śiṣyaḥ:** guro\! advaitināṃ pañcama-lakṣaṇasya (pramityaviṣayatvasya) vimarśaḥ atīva pradīrghaḥ jātaḥ| kṛpayā asya sarvasya **sārāṃśaṃ** saṃkṣepeṇa vadatu|

**guruḥ:** avaśyaṃ vatsa\! etaṃ vimarśaṃ pañcasu sopāneṣu avagaccha

**1. lakṣaṇa-svarūpam**  
advaitināṃ pañcamaḥ sambhāvitaḥ pakṣaḥ asti— **"pramityaviṣayatvaṃ mithyātvam"**| arthāt yat jñānasya viṣayaḥ na bhavati, tat mithyā|

**2. brahmaṇi ativyāpti-parihāraḥ:**  
**śiṣyaḥ:** brahma api tu aviṣayam asti, tarhi brahma mithyā bhavet?

**guruḥ:** satyam, ataḥ advaitinaḥ pariṣkurvanti— "brahma **vedāntajanya-vṛtti-vyāpyam** asti"| arthāt vedāntavākyaiḥ brahma jñāna-viṣayaḥ bhavati, ataḥ brahmaṇi ativyāptiḥ na bhavati|

**3. mithyā-vastuni avyāpti-doṣaḥ:**  
**śiṣyaḥ:** kiṃ rajata-ādi mithyā-vastūni jñānasya aviṣayāḥ santi?  
**guruḥ:** na, atraiva vimarśaḥ asti| **śukti-rajataṃ** bhrānti-kāle (vyavasāya), "ahaṃ rajataṃ jānāmi" iti jñāne (anuvyavasāya), tathā ca "nedaṃ rajatam" iti niṣedha-kāle api **jñānasya viṣayaḥ** bhavati eva| ataḥ mithyā-vastuni 'aviṣayatvaṃ' nāsti, yena lakṣaṇasya **avyāptiḥ** bhavati|

**4. sattvaprakāraka-pramā-vimarśaḥ:**  
**śiṣyaḥ:** tarhi te kathaṃ rakṣaṇaṃ kurvanti?  
**guruḥ:** te vadanti— "yat **satyatvena** pramā-jñānasya viṣayaḥ na bhavati, tat mithyā"| rajataṃ satyatvena na jñāyate, ataḥ tatra lakṣaṇaṃ ghaṭeta|

**5. antima-niṣkarṣaḥ (paryavasānam):**  
**guruḥ:** parantu ante teṣāṃ vimarśasya eṣaḥ niṣkarṣaḥ— yat 'satya-jñāna-aviṣayatvam' iti kathanam atīva gauravāvaham| lāghavena asya arthaḥ bhavati— **"sattvābhāvaḥ"** (Absence of Reality)|  
'sattvābhāvaḥ' iti tu caturthaṃ lakṣaṇam āsīt, yat pūrvameva **nirdharmake brahmaṇi ativyāptam** iti kṛtvā taiḥ tyaktam|

**śiṣyaḥ:** aho\! idānīṃ sārāṃśaḥ spaṣṭaḥ|  
1. brahma rakṣituṃ te 'vṛtti-vyāpyatvaṃ' vadanti|  
2. mithyā-vastu (rajataṃ) jñāna-viṣayaḥ asti iti jñātvā, te 'satya-jñāna-aviṣayatvaṃ' vadanti|  
3. ante tu etat sarvaṃ **'sattvābhāva'** lakṣaṇe eva paryavasannaṃ bhavati, yat pūrvameva khaṇḍitam|

**guruḥ:** sādhu vatsa\! eṣa eva pañcama-pakṣa-vimarśasya sārāṃśaḥ| ataḥ advaitinaḥ idānīṃ pañcamaṃ pakṣamapi tyaktvā agre gacchanti|
