# prathamaparicchedaḥ - 1. mithyātvaniruktibhaṅgaḥ - 2. vipratipattivicāraḥ

<details>
  <summary>samīravāṇī</summary>


**samīravāṇī \-**  'vipratipattiḥ' nāma ekasmin viṣaye dvayoḥ viruddhayoḥ matayoḥ sthitiḥ (saṃśayaḥ vā virodhaḥ) | atra jagat satyaṃ vā mithyā vā iti viṣaye vicāraḥ kriyate |

**nyāyāmṛtam**   
nanu mithyaiva viśvam | tathā hi tatra vipratipattiḥ- brahmapramānyena vā saprakāreṇa vā abādhyatve sati asadvilakṣaṇatve sati brahmānyat pratipannopādhau traikālikaniṣedhapratiyogi, na vā? pāramārthikatvākāreṇa traikālikaniṣedhapratiyogi na vā? akhaṇḍārthaniṣṭhavedāntajanyaniṣprakārakabrahmapramābādhyamapi viśvaṃ brahmapramānyena vā saprakāreṇa vā na bādhyamiti nāśyāsiddhiḥ | evamuttaratrāpi pūrvottarapakṣānumānapratijñe saṅkalayya vipratipattiruhyā |

**samīravāṇī \-** pūrvapakṣī (advaitavādī) vadati \- "etat sampūrṇaṃ jagat mithyā (asatyam) eva asti |"

**vipratipatteḥ svarūpam (Technical Doubt):**  
atra advaita-dvaita-matayoḥ madhye yaḥ saṃśayaḥ asti, taṃ granthakāraḥ evaṃ varṇayati \-

* **brahmapramānyena abādhyatve sati :** jagat sāmānya-laukika-jñānena na naśyati (yathā śukti-rajataṃ jñānamātreṇa naśyati, tathā jagat na naśyati) |  
* **asadvilakṣaṇatve sati :** jagat 'śaśa-śṛṅgavat' (kharagośa ke sīṃga kī taraha) sarvathā śūnyaṃ vā alīkaṃ vā nāsti |  
* **brahmānyat pratipannopādhau traikālikaniṣedhapratiyogi, na vā? :** praśnaḥ eṣaḥ asti yat \- kiṃ jagat yatra bhāsate, tasmin sthāne kālatraye'pi (bhūta-bhaviṣya-vartamāne) nāsti?  
  * yadi **'asti'** (nāsti iti niṣedhaḥ bhavati), tarhi jagat **mithyā** (advaitamatam) |  
  * yadi **'na'** (niṣedhaḥ na bhavati), tarhi jagat **satyam** (dvaitamatam) |


* advaitināṃ mate kevalaṃ 'brahmajñānam' eva jagat bādhituṃ (nāśayituṃ) śaknoti, anyat kimapi jñānaṃ na | yadyapi brahmajñānena jagataḥ bādhaḥ bhavati, tathāpi sāmānyakāle jagataḥ pratītiḥ bhavati, ataḥ asmin vicāre 'āśrayāsiddhiḥ' (laukika-jagataḥ abhāvaḥ) iti doṣaḥ nāsti |  
* evaṃ prakāreṇa anyeṣu api viṣayeṣu pūrvapakṣasya (advaitasya) uttarapakṣasya (dvaitasya) ca tarkān anumānaṃ ca melayitvā vipratipattiḥ (viruddhaṃ matadvayam) jñātavyā |

**saṃkṣepataḥ \-** asmin bhāge granthakāraḥ vivṛṇoti yat \- advaitinaḥ jagat 'mithyā' iti manyante, yataḥ teṣāṃ mate brahmajñānena jagataḥ bādhaḥ bhavati | dvaitinaḥ (granthakāraḥ) jagat 'satyam' iti manyante | etayoḥ dvayoḥ pakṣayoḥ tārkikaṃ svarūpaṃ atra 'vipratipatti' rūpeṇa sthāpitam asti |

**nyāyāmṛtam**  
idaṃ ca vipratipattipradarśanaṃ tārkikarītyaiva na tu vastutaḥ | tatpakṣe'pi vipratipatti vākyasya pakṣaparigrahekaphalakatvāt | tasya ca kathābāhyena nigrahānarheṇa laukika-rītyanusāriṇā saṃskṛtarūpeṇa vā, bhāṣārūpeṇa vā, 'mayā prapañcamithyātvaṃ sādhyate' iti vādivākyena vā, 'tvayā prapañcamithyātvaṃ sādhya'miti madhyasthaparikalpita-viṣayasvīkāreṇa vā siddhau pratijñāvyatiriktasya uktakusṛṣṭiyuktasya vipratipatti-vākyasya vaiyarthyāt | na ca sādhyopasthityarthaṃ tat | pratijñayaiva tatsiddheḥ | na ca pakṣatvaprayojakasaṃśayārthaṃ tat | vādiprativādinoḥ prāśnikānāñca niścayavattvena tadyogāt | āhāryasaṃśayasya cātiprasaṅgitvena pakṣatvāprayojakatvāt, vipratipatti-vākyānapekṣatvācca, saṃśayaṃ vinā'pi siṣādhayiṣāvirahasahakṛtasādhakamānābhāvarūpasya pakṣatvasya sambhavācca |

**samīravāṇī \-**

* atra darśitaḥ virodhaḥ kevalaṃ tarkaśāstrasya niyamānusāram asti, saḥ vāstavikaḥ virodhaḥ na asti |  
* advaitavādināṃ mate api etādṛśasya vivādavākyasya mukhyam uddeśyaṃ kevalaṃ svasya mataṃ svīkāryaṃ karaṇīyam iti asti |  
* yadi vādī sāmānyavacanena, saṃskṛtabhāṣayā vā anyabhāṣayā vā 'ahaṃ jagat mithyā iti siddhaṃ karomi' iti vadati, athavā madhyasthaḥ viṣayaṃ spaṣṭaṃ karoti, tarhi tāvataiva kāryaṃ bhavati| ataḥ etādṛśasya kliṣṭasya tārkikavākyasya kimapi prayojanaṃ nāsti, tat vyartham asti |  
* sādhyasya (viṣayasya) jñānāya api etasya vivādavākyasya upayogaḥ nāsti |  
* yataḥ mukhyapratijñāvākyena eva sādhyaviṣayaḥ sulabhatayā jñāyate |  
* tarkaśāstre āvaśyakasya saṃśayasya (Doubt) nirmāṇārtham api asya vākyasya āvaśyakatā nāsti |  
* śāstrārthe bhāgaṃ gṛhṇantaḥ vādī, prativādī tathā madhyasthāḥ sarve svamate dṛḍhāḥ bhavanti, ataḥ teṣāṃ manasi kasyāpi saṃśayasya sthānaṃ na bhavati |  
* yaḥ saṃśayaḥ kṛtrimaḥ bhavati, saḥ carcāyāḥ ādhāraḥ bhavituṃ na arhati| vipratipattivākyaṃ vinā api saṃśayaḥ bhavituṃ śaknoti| yadi vādinaḥ manasi kevalaṃ siddheḥ icchā asti, tarhi saṃśayaṃ vinā api śāstrārthaḥ sukhena sambhavati |
