# Paricheda 1 - 1. Mithyatvaniruktibhanga

**Shri Mula Ramo Vijayate**  
**Shrimat Nyayamrutam First Paricheda**   
**Shrimad Hanumad Bhima Madhva Antargata Ramakrishna Vedavyasatmaka Lakshmi Hayagrivaya Namah - Harih Om**

Shri Vedavyasaya Namah. Harih Om.

**1. Laghvamodam - Nrusimha Stuti**

stambhāj janmabhiśailādiva divasakaraḥ projjihānastrilokī-
grāsavyāsakta naktañcaravara timirāriṣṭa muṣṭindhayaśrīḥ |
śakrādyādityacakraprabhayaharakaraḥ klṛptapadmānuṣaṅgaḥ
siṃhaḥ prahlādamodī sa haratu duritaṃ raṃhasā prahvasādhyaḥ || 1 ||

**SameeraVani -**  
1. **stambhāj janmabhiśailādiva divasakaraḥ projjihānaḥ** - Just as the sun rises from the eastern mountain, Lord Nrusimha emerged from the pillar in Hiranyakashipu's assembly.
2. **trilokīgrāsavyāsakta naktañcaravara timirāriṣṭa muṣṭindhayaśrīḥ** - The great demon (Hiranyakashipu) who was bent on devouring the three worlds was like darkness. Lord Nrusimha, with his radiance, completely destroyed this demon-like darkness.
3. **śakrādyādityacakraprabhayaharakaraḥ** - He destroyed the great fear in the minds of the host of gods led by Indra.
4. **klṛptapadmānuṣaṅgaḥ** - He who is eternally associated with Lakshmi (Padma) and who resides in the lotus-like hearts of devotees.
5. **siṃhaḥ prahlādamodī sa haratu duritaṃ raṃhasā prahvasādhyaḥ** - May that Lord Nrusimha, who delights his devotee Prahlada and is easily attainable by the humble, quickly destroy our sins.

**Meaning -**   
Just as the sun destroys darkness, Lord Nrusimha, emerging from the pillar, killed the demon-like darkness and removed the fear of the gods. May that Supreme Lord, dear to Prahlada, quickly destroy our sins.

**2. Laghvamodam - Narayana Stuti**

cīrṇapuṇyajanopāsyaṃ pūrṇabodhaiṣṭadaivatam | arṇavaṃ guṇaratnānāṃ tūrṇaṃ naumi rameśvaram || 2 ||

**SameeraVani -**    
1. **cīrṇapuṇyajanopāsyam** – Worshipped only by those who have accumulated immense merit in previous lives.
2. **pūrṇabodhaiṣṭadaivatam** – The beloved deity of Purnabodha (Madhvacharya).
3. **arṇavaṃ guṇaratnānām** – The ocean of infinite auspicious qualities, like jewels.
4. **tūrṇaṃ naumi rameśvaram** – I quickly bow down to that Lord of Lakshmi (Rameshwara).

**Meaning -**   
I bow down to Narayana, the Lord of Lakshmi, who is worshipped by the meritorious, who is the beloved deity of Acharya Madhva, and who is an ocean of infinite auspicious qualities.

**3. Laghvamodam - Madhvacharya Stuti**

taṃ pāṇipallavaṃ bāhuśākhaṃ saddvijasevitam | vidyāhāsalatāpuṣpaṃ madhvakalpadrumaṃ bhaje || 3 ||

**SameeraVani -**  
1. **madhvakalpadrumaṃ bhaje** – I worship the wish-fulfilling tree (Kalpavriksha) in the form of Sri Madhvacharya.
2. **pāṇipallavaṃ bāhuśākham** – Whose hands are like tender leaves and arms are like branches.
3. **saddvijasevitam** – Just as a tree is resorted to by birds (dvija), Madhvacharya is served by noble brahmanas and scholars (saddvija).
4. **vidyāhāsalatāpuṣpam** – Whose knowledge is the creeper and whose smile is its beautiful flower.

**Meaning -**   
Sri Madhvacharya is like a Kalpavriksha (wish-fulfilling tree). Just as the tree removes difficulties and grants desires, Madhvacharya brings welfare to devotees with his knowledge and smile. I bow to such a great guru.

**4. Laghvamodam - Jayatirtha Stuti**

yasya vāk kāmadhenurnaḥ kāmitārthān prayacchati | seve taṃ jayayogīndraṃ kāmabāṇacchidaṃ sadā || 4 ||

**SameeraVani -**    
1. **yasya vāk kāmadhenuḥ** – Whose words are like the wish-fulfilling cow (Kamadhenu).
2. **naḥ kāmitārthān prayacchati** – Which grants us all desired objects (and deep meanings of scriptures).
3. **kāmabāṇacchidaṃ sadā** – Who constantly destroys the arrows of Cupid (desires), meaning one who has conquered his senses.
4. **seve taṃ jayayogīndraṃ sadā** – I constantly serve that king of yogis, Sri Jayatirtha.

**Meaning -**   
The words of Sri Jayatirtha (who wrote commentaries on Madhvacharya's works) are extremely powerful. Reading them, one attains all knowledge. I bow down to him, the great ascetic free from desire and anger.

**5. Laghvamodam - Vyasatirtha Stuti**

yo vyāsamunidāvāgniḥ sacchāstramarutā saha | dadāha kumatāraṇyaṃ prakāśāya tamāśraye || 5 ||

**SameeraVani -**   
1. **yo vyāsamunidāvāgniḥ** – Vyasatirtha is like a forest fire.
2. **sacchāstramarutā saha** – Just as fire blazes more with wind, he becomes highly effective accompanied by the wind of true scriptures (logic).
3. **dadāha kumatāraṇyam** – He burnt the forest of false doctrines.
4. **prakāśāya tamāśraye** – I take refuge in him for the light of true knowledge.

**Meaning -**   
Sri Vyasatirtha was highly skilled in refuting Advaita and other philosophies. He is compared to a forest fire. Fanned by the wind of scriptures, he destroys false doctrines. I worship that guru who is a beacon of knowledge.

**6. Laghvamodam - Special Stuti of Vyasatirtha**

ramāvarākhyāmbujarājahaṃsollasatkathāśrotrakalāpadaṃ tam |   
kuvādirāṭ kuñjaraśīrṣabhede hariṃ bhaje vyāsamuniṃ munīndram || 6 ||

**SameeraVani -**  
1. **ramāvarākhyāmbujarājahaṃsam** – He delights like a royal swan in the lotus of Lord Vishnu's glory.
2. **ullasatkathāśrotrakalāpadam** – Whose ears are always absorbed in the joyful stories of the Lord.
3. **kuvādirāṭkuñjaraśīrṣabhede harim** – He is a lion (Hari) in tearing apart the heads of the elephants of false debaters.
4. **bhaje vyāsamuniṃ munīndram** – I worship that greatest of sages, Vyasatirtha.

**Meaning -**   
Sri Vyasatirtha plays a dual role—he delights in the stories of Vishnu with devotion like a swan, and with logic, he is terrifying like a lion to his opponents. I bow to such a great guru.

**7. Laghvamodam - Sri Surendratirtha Stuti**

mūko'pi vāvadūkendro jāyate yadanugrahāt | śrīsurendraguroḥ stotraṃ kāraṃ kāraṃ bhaje giram || 7 ||

**SameeraVani -**    
1. **yadanugrahāt mūko'pi vāvadūkendro jāyate** – By whose grace even a mute person becomes a great orator.
2. **śrīsurendraguroḥ stotraṃ kāraṃ kāraṃ** – By repeatedly praising such an influential guru, Sri Surendratirtha.
3. **bhaje giram** – I make my speech successful (devote it to his service).

**Meaning -**   
The glory of Sri Surendratirtha is boundless. By his grace, even an ignorant person becomes a great scholar. Praising him, I purify my speech.

**8. Laghvamodam - Subject of the Book**

śrīmanmadhVamatākhyadugdhajaladherdhmandareṇa kramairāmathyoddhṛtamarpitaṃ ca gurubhirnyāyāmṛtaṃ yat purā |   
sauvarṇe caṣake mahīsuratatermodāya tasyādarādāmodaṃ sumanoguṇairvijiyināmindraḥ kariṣye'dhunā || 8 ||

**SameeraVani -**    
1. **śrīmanmadhVamatākhyadugdhajaladheḥ** – Sri Madhvacharya's philosophy (Dvaita) is a vast ocean of milk.
2. **dhīmandareṇa kramairāmathyoddhṛtam** – Churning this ocean well with the Mandara mountain of intellect, the 'Nyayamruta' (nectar of logic) was extracted by the gurus (Vyasatirtha).
3. **arpitaṃ ca gurubhiḥ... purā** – Previously, this nectar was offered by Vyasatirtha to all seekers.
4. **sauvarṇe caṣake** – Now, I am presenting that same 'Nyayamruta' in a golden goblet, which represents this commentary named 'Amoda'.
5. **mahīsuratatermodāya** – For the joy of brahmanas and scholars.
6. **āmodaṃ sumanoguṇairvijiyināmindraḥ kariṣye'dhunā** – I, Vijayindratirtha, along with the virtues of scholars, will now respectfully compose the commentary named 'Amoda'.

**Meaning -**  
Just as nectar was obtained by churning the ocean, Vyasatirtha churned Madhva philosophy to compose 'Nyayamruta'. To make people taste that nectar, Vijayindratirtha now presents the commentary 'Amoda' like a golden goblet.

**9. Laghvamodam - Purpose of Commentary**

vyākhyānavyājato'nyoktadūṣaṇānāmalagnatām | pradarthya gurupādoktimahimātra prakāśyate || 9 ||

**SameeraVani -**    
1. **vyākhyānavyājataḥ** – Under the pretext of a commentary.
2. **anyoktadūṣaṇānām alagnatām pradarthya** – I will show that all the flaws pointed out in 'Nyayamruta' by opponents are completely baseless (alagna).
3. **gurupādoktimahimā atra prakāśyate** – Thus refuting the opponents, the true glory of my guru Vyasatirtha's words will be revealed here.

**Meaning -**  
The main purpose of this commentary (Amoda) is twofold—to refute the criticisms made by opponents, and to establish the supremacy of Sri Vyasatirtha's philosophy.

**1. Nyayamrutaprakasha - First Shloka:**  
vāsavīhṛdayānandaṃ vedaśāstrapraṇāyakam |  
vedavyāsaṃ gurūpālyaṃ vande'bhīṣṭapradāyakam || 1 ||

**SameeraVani -** I bow down to Vedavyasa. He is the son of Satyavati (Vasavi) and brings joy to her. He is the author of the Vedas and scriptures. He is worshipped by all gurus and grants the desired fruits to devotees.

**2. Nyayamrutaprakasha - Second Shloka:**  
śrīrāmaṃ hanumatsevyaṃ śrīkṛṣṇaṃ bhīmasevitam |  
śrīvyāsa śrīmadānandatīrthasaṃsevyamābhaje || 2 ||

**SameeraVani -** I worship Sri Rama, who is served by Hanuman. I worship Sri Krishna, who is served by Bhimasena. And I worship Sri Vedavyasa, who is served by Srimad Ananda Tirtha (Madhvacharya).

**3. Nyayamrutaprakasha - Third Shloka:**  
hṛttamonāśakaṃ śāstraprabhayā tattvabhāsakam |  
namāmi śrīmadānandatīrthācāryadivākaram || 3 ||

**SameeraVani -** I bow to Srimad Ananda Tirtha (Madhvacharya). He is like the sun. Just as the sun destroys darkness, he destroys the darkness of ignorance in the heart. He illuminates the true philosophy through the light of scriptural knowledge.

**4. Nyayamrutaprakasha - Fourth Shloka:**  
śrutikṣīrābdhimāmathya buddhimandarabhūbhṛtā |  
prāptā nyāyasudhā yena taṃ jayendramahaṃ bhaje || 4 ||

**SameeraVani -** I worship Sri Jayatirtha (Jayendra). He churned the milk ocean of the Vedas with the Mandara mountain of his intellect. From that churning, he obtained the nectar called 'Nyayasudha' (he authored the book 'Nyayasudha').

**5. Nyayamrutaprakasha - Fifth Shloka:**  
brahmaṇyatīrthavādhyutthaṃ vyāsatīrthendumābhaje |  
tātparyacandrikāyuktaṃ sarvatāpāpahaṃ satām || 5 ||

**SameeraVani -** I worship Sri Vyasatirtha. He is like the moon born from the ocean of Brahmanyatirtha. He authored the book 'Tatparyachandrika'. Like the moon, he removes the threefold miseries of good people.

**6. Nyayamrutaprakasha - Sixth Shloka:**  
vedavyāsābdhisañjātaṃ sadā haripadāśrayam |  
padārthakaumudīyuktaṃ vedeśendumahaṃ bhaje || 6 ||

**SameeraVani -** I worship Sri Vedeshatirtha. He is like the moon born from the ocean of Vedavyasa. He always takes refuge at the lotus feet of Sri Hari (Vishnu). He is the author of the book 'Padarthakaumudi'.

**7. Nyayamrutaprakasha - Seventh Shloka:**  
śrīmannyāyasudhāyā yairbhāvaḥ samyakpradarśitaḥ |  
tānvande yādavācāryānsadā vidyāgurūnnaham || 7 ||

**SameeraVani -** I always bow to my knowledge guru, Sri Yadavacharya, who has thoroughly clarified the deep meaning of the book 'Sriman Nyayasudha'.

**8. Nyayamrutaprakasha - Eighth Shloka:**  
atha tatkṛpayā nyāyāmṛtasyedaṃ prakāśanam |  
kriyate śrīnivāsena guruśikṣānusārataḥ || 8 ||

**SameeraVani -** Now, by the grace of those gurus, this commentary (Prakasha) on the book 'Nyayamruta' is being composed by Srinivasa (the author). He does this work according to the instructions of his gurus.
