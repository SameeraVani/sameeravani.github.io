# Chapter III: The Yoga of Devotion (Bhakti Yoga)

Bhakti Yoga is the path of pure love and devotion to the Supreme Reality. It is considered the most accessible path to spiritual liberation.

## The Core Verse

> "He who offers to me with devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or water — I accept it." — Bhagavad Gita, 9.26

Surrendering all actions and thoughts to the Divine leads to inner peace and ultimate union.
