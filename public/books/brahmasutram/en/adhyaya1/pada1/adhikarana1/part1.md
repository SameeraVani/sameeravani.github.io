# Adhyaya 1 - Samanvayadhyaya
## Pada 1 - Spashta Brahmalinga Pada
### Adhikarana 1 - Jijnasadhikaranam

> [!NOTE]
> Brahmasutram — Adhyaya 1, Pada 1, Adhikarana 1: ॥ Jijnasadhikaranam ॥

### ॥ Jijnasadhikaranam ॥

#### Adhikarana Panchangam (Five Limbs of Adhikarana)
* **Vishaya (Subject Matter):** Parabrahman Sri Narayana and the Upanishadic declarations.
* **Samshaya (Doubt):** Should an inquiry into Brahman (Brahma-Jijnasa) be undertaken or not?
* **Purvapaksha (Opponent View):** If Brahman is already known, inquiry is futile; if unknown, it is impossible. Or since Vedic rituals grant heaven, Brahma-inquiry is not required.
* **Siddhanta (Concluded Truth):** Liberation (Moksha) is the supreme goal of life. It cannot be attained without the divine grace of Sri Narayana, and His grace is impossible without correct knowledge of His infinite auspicious attributes. Therefore, Brahma-Jijnasa must be undertaken.
* **Prayojana (Purpose):** True knowledge of Brahman leading to divine grace, which culminates in eternal liberation (Moksha).

#### Sutra 1 — Jijnasa Sutra

**oṃ athāto brahmajijñāsā oṃ || 1 ||**

**Word Breakdown & Sandhi**  
oṃ atha (atoguṇa sandhi / visarga lopa) ataḥ brahmajijñāsā oṃ

**Word-by-Word Meaning**

| Word | Category | Grammatical Description | Meaning |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| oṃ | Invariant | Pranava, auspicious invocation | Invocatory Pranava designating Parabrahman |
| atha | Invariant | Avyaya, signifies succession / auspiciousness | After acquiring the eligibility qualifications |
| ataḥ | Invariant | Avyaya, causal particle | Because liberation is impossible without divine grace |
| brahmajijñāsā | Noun | Feminine, Nominative singular | Inquiry into Brahman [is to be undertaken] |
| oṃ | Invariant | Pranava, concluding auspiciousness | Auspicious concluding Pranava |

**Anvaya (Syntax Order)**  
[After acquiring spiritual qualification] atha, [because divine grace is the sole cause of liberation] ataḥ, brahmajijñāsā [kartavyā].

**Overall Meaning (Bhavartha)**  
After acquiring spiritual qualifications (such as devotion, detachment, and study), since eternal liberation cannot be achieved without the divine grace of Parabrahman Sri Narayana, and that grace is not possible without inquiry and knowledge, therefore an earnest inquiry into Brahman must be undertaken.

### **Part 1 — Sri Madhvacharya's Brahmasutra Bhashyam**

**Invocatory Verse by Srimad Anandatirtha:**

nārāyaṇaṃ nikhilapūrṇaguṇaikadehaṃ nirdoṣamāpyatamamapyakhilaiḥ suhṛdbhiḥ |  
asyodbhavādisadaśeṣaviśeṣato'pi vandyaṃ sadā priyasatanmumṛtāṃ bhaje'ham ||

"The word 'atha' signifies auspiciousness and sequence after eligibility. The word 'ataḥ' signifies cause. Because without the grace of Narayana there is no liberation, and without knowledge there is no abundant grace, therefore Brahma-jijnasa must be done."

**1. Commentary on 'atha':**  
The word *atha* indicates auspicious commencement and the prerequisite spiritual qualification (Sadhana-sampat), such as control of mind and senses (sama-dama), deep devotion to Sri Hari, and detachment from worldly desires.

**2. Commentary on 'ataḥ':**  
The word *ataḥ* provides the rationale: Supreme liberation is achieved only through the grace (prasada) of the Supreme Being, Sri Narayana.

**3. Concluded Essence:**  
Since divine grace demands genuine contemplation and philosophical inquiry into the Supreme Reality, the seeker of liberation must diligently study and reflect upon Brahman.
