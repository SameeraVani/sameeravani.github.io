# The Morlocks & Dark Discoveries

It was only as I began to explore the strange, deep circular wells scattered across the landscape that the truth began to dawn on me. These wells were not ventilation shafts for some ancient utility; they were active, breathing air ducts for a vast, underground civilization.

And then, in the pitch black of a ruinous vault, I met them.

## The Creatures of the Deep

They were small, ape-like creatures, with pale, hairless skin and large, glowing, reddish-grey eyes that could not bear the light. Unlike the Eloi, who lived in the sunlight, these creatures—the **Morlocks**—dwelt in the absolute darkness of the underworld, tending to the massive, thudding machinery that kept the surface world running.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     TWO TRACES OF HUMANITY                  |
+------------------------------+------------------------------+
| THE ELOI (Surface)           | THE MORLOCKS (Underground)   |
+------------------------------+------------------------------+
| - Small, fragile, delicate   | - Pale, ape-like, hairy      |
| - Diurnal (active by day)    | - Nocturnal (active by night)|
| - Frugivorous (fruit eaters) | - Carnivorous (meat eaters)  |
| - Artistic, idle, childlike  | - Industrial, mechanical     |
| - Live in fear of the dark   | - Hunt the Eloi in the dark  |
+------------------------------+------------------------------+
```

## The Horrible Truth

I realized that the social division of my own time—between the wealthy Capitalists who enjoyed the fruits of labor, and the poor Workers who lived in squalor—had been carried to its ultimate, biological conclusion.

> "The Eloi had decayed into beautiful, helpless playthings, while the Morlocks had adapted to the underground dark, becoming something predatory and mechanical."

The machines I heard thudding in the depths were weaving the garments and maintaining the state of the surface, but at a terrible price. When night fell, and the moon was thin, the Morlocks would emerge from their wells to capture and feed upon the helpless Eloi. The golden age was an illusion; the Eloi were merely livestock.
