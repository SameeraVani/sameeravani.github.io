## Padartha, Pratipadartha and Bhavartha (Verse-by-Verse)

<a id="meaning-1"></a>
### Verse 1 (Verse 1)
`tatō dūraṃ gatē rāmē rāvaṇaḥ sahalakṣmaṇē ।`  
`sītēyaṃ nīyata iti matvā ninyē tadākṛtim || 1 ||`  

**Sandhi:** tataḥ (visarga-utva, guṇa) dūram (anusvāraḥ) gatē rāmē rāvaṇaḥ sahalakṣmaṇē sītā (guṇaḥ) iyam (anusvāraḥ) nīyatē (ayādiḥ, lōpaḥ) iti matvā ninyē tadākṛtim ।

**Word Analysis:**

| Word | Category | Grammatical Info | Meaning |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| tataḥ | avyayam | - | Right away |
| dūram | Noun | Indeclinable | About Abroad |
| gatē | Noun | Indeclinable | when gone |
| rāmē | Noun | Indeclinable | In Rama |
| rāvaṇaḥ | Noun | Indeclinable | Ravana |
| sahalakṣmaṇē | Noun | Indeclinable | In the one who sees Lakshmana |
| sītā | Noun | आ-ending feminine stem 'sītā', Nominative, singular | Seetheu |
| iyam | Noun | म-ending feminine stem 'idam', Nominative, singular | This is |
| nīyatē | Verb | णीञ् (प्रापणे)-ending आ.प stem 'laṭ', प्र.पु, singular | Are you being carried away? |
| iti | avyayam | - | what |
| matvā | avyayam | - | knowing |
| ninyē | Verb | णीञ् (प्रापणे)-ending आ.प stem 'liṭ', प्र.पु, singular | read |
| tadākṛtim | Noun | इ-ending feminine stem 'tadākṛti', Accusative, singular | Her replica |

**Anvaya:** tataḥ sahalakṣmaṇē rāmē dūram gatē iyam sītā nīyatē iti matvā rāvaṇaḥ tadākṛtim ninyē ।

**Bhavartha:** Then, when Rama and Lakshmana went away, Ravana took the figure, thinking that this Sita was being taken away.

**Grammatical Analysis (Vyākaraṇa):**

| Word | Type | Analysis |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| सहलक्ष्मणे | सहार्थे बहुव्रीहिसमासः | लक्ष्मणेन सहितः |
| तदाकृतिम् | षष्ठीतत्पुरुषसमासः | तस्याः आकृतिः |
| गते | Kṛt (क्त (सप्तमी)) | √गम् + क्त (सप्तमी) |
| मत्वा | Kṛt (क्त्वा) | √मन् + क्त्वा |
