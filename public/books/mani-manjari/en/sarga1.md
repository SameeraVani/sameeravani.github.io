## Padartha, Pratipadartha and Bhavartha (Verse-by-Verse)

<a id="meaning-1"></a>
### Verse 1 (Verse 1)
`vandē gōvindamānandajñānadēhaṃ patiṃ śriyaḥ ।`  
`śrīmadānandatīrthāryavallabhaṃ paramakṣaram || 1 ||`  

**Sandhi:** vandē gōvindam ānandajñānadēham [anusvāraḥ] patim [anusvāraḥ] śriyaḥ śrīmadānandatīrthāryavallabham [anusvāraḥ] param akṣaram ।

**Word Analysis:**

| Word | Category | Grammatical Info | Meaning |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| vandē | Verb | वदिँ (अभिवादनस्तुत्योः)-ending आ.प stem 'laṭ', उ.पु., singular | Greetings |
| gōvindam | Noun | Indeclinable | Govinda |
| ānandajñānadēham | Noun | Indeclinable | A blissful and enlightened bodyless one |
| patim | Noun | इ-ending masculine stem 'pati', Accusative, singular | the husband |
| śriyaḥ | Noun | ई-ending feminine stem 'śrī', Genitive, singular | Lakshmi |
| śrīmadānandatīrthāryavallabham | Noun | Indeclinable | He who is dear to Srimadanandatirtharya |
| param | Noun | Indeclinable | The great one |
| akṣaram | Noun | Indeclinable | The imperishable one |

**Anvaya:** ānandajñānadēham śriyaḥ patim śrīmadānandatīrthāryavallabham param akṣaram gōvindam vandē ।

**Bhavartha:** I bow to Govinda, whose body is of bliss and knowledge, the Lord of Lakshmi, who is dear to Sri Anandateertha (Madhvacharya), supreme and imperishable.

<a id="meaning-2"></a>
### Verse 2 (Verse 2)
`sasarja bhagavānādau trīnguṇān prakṛtēḥ paraḥ ।`  
`mahattatvaṃ tatō viṣṇuḥ sṛṣṭavān brahmaṇastanum || 2 ||`  

**Sandhi:** sasarja bhagavān ādau trīn guṇān prakṛtēḥ paraḥ mahattatvam[anusvārasandhiḥ] tataḥ [visarga-utvasandhiḥ, guṇa] viṣṇuḥ sṛṣṭavān brahmaṇaḥ [visarga-satvasandhiḥ] tanum ।

**Word Analysis:**

| Word | Category | Grammatical Info | Meaning |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| sasarja | Verb | सृजँ (विसर्गे)-ending प.प stem 'liṭ', प्र.पु., singular | created |
| bhagavān | Noun | त्-ending masculine stem 'bhagavat', Nominative, singular | Lord Vasudeva |
| ādau | Noun | इ-ending masculine stem 'ādi', Locative, singular | at the beginning of creation (prior to the manifestation of Mahat-tattva) |
| trīn | Noun | इ-ending masculine stem 'tri', Accusative, plural | Three |
| guṇān | Noun | Indeclinable | attributes (sattva, rajas, tamas) |
| prakṛtēḥ | Noun | इ-ending feminine stem 'prakṛti', Ablative, singular | from Prakriti (primordial nature) |
| paraḥ | Noun | Indeclinable | supreme / distinct from Prakriti |
| mahattatvam | Noun | Indeclinable | Mahatattva |
| tataḥ | Indeclinable | avyayam | after |
| viṣṇuḥ | Noun | उ-ending masculine stem 'viṣṇu', Nominative, singular | Vishnu |
| sṛṣṭavān | Noun | त्-ending masculine stem 'sṛṣṭavat', Nominative, singular | created |
| brahmaṇaḥ | Noun | अन्-ending masculine stem 'brahman', Genitive, singular | Brahmin |
| tanum | Noun | उ-ending masculine stem 'tanu', Accusative, singular | the body |

**Anvaya:** paraḥ bhagavān ādau prakṛtēḥ trīn guṇān sasarja । tataḥ viṣṇuḥ brahmaṇaḥ tanum mahattattvam sṛṣṭavān ।

**Bhavartha:** Lord Vasudeva, who is supreme and distinct from Prakriti (material nature), created the three attributes (sattva, rajas, tamas) at the beginning of creation (prior to the manifestation of Mahat-tattva). Thereafter, Lord Vishnu created the body of Chaturmukha Brahma, which is the Mahat-tattva.

### Verse 3 (Verse 3)
`mahattatvādahaṅkāram sasarja śivavigraham ।`  
`daivān dēhān manaḥ khāni khaṃ ca sa trividhāt tataḥ || 3 ||`  

**Sandhi:** mahattatvāt [jaśtvasandhiḥ] ahaṅkāram sasarja śivavigraham । daivān dēhān manaḥ khāni kham [anusvārasandhiḥ] ca saḥ [visargalōpasandhiḥ] trividhāt tataḥ ॥

**Word Analysis:**

| Word | Category | Grammatical Info | Meaning |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| mahattatvāt | Noun | Indeclinable | from Mahat-tattva |
| ahaṅkāram | Noun | Indeclinable | Ahakaratattva |
| sasarja | Verb | सृजँ (विसर्गे)-ending प.प stem 'liṭ', प्र.पु., singular | created |
| śivavigraham | Noun | Indeclinable | Shiva's body |
| daivān | Noun | Indeclinable | The gods |
| dēhān | Noun | Indeclinable | the body |
| manaḥ | Noun | अस्-ending neuter stem 'manas', Accusative, singular | the mind |
| khāni | Noun | Indeclinable | The senses |
| kham | Noun | Indeclinable | the sky |
| ca | Indeclinable | avyayam | And |
| saḥ | Noun | द्-ending masculine stem 'tad', Nominative, singular | That Lord |
| trividhāt | Noun | Indeclinable | from the threefold ego (vaikarika, taijasa, tamasa) |
| tataḥ | Indeclinable | avyayam | from that |

**Anvaya:** saḥ mahattattvāt śivavigraham ahaṅkāram sasarja । tataḥ trividhāt daivān dēhān manaḥ khāni kham ca sasarja ।

**Bhavartha:** He created the ego-principle (ahankara-tattva), which is the body of Shiva, from the Mahat-tattva. Then, from that threefold ego (vaikarika/sattvika, taijasa/rajasa, and tamasa), He created the deities, their bodies, the mind, the ten senses, and the space (along with sound-element).

### Verse 4 (Verse 4)
`ākāśādasṛjadvāyuṃ vāyōstējō vyajījanat ।`

**Word Analysis:**

| Word | Category | Grammatical Info | Meaning |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| ākāśāt | Noun | Indeclinable | from the sky / space |
| asṛjat | Verb | सृज् निर्माणे-ending प.प stem 'laṅ', प्र.पु., singular | created |
| vāyum | Noun | उ-ending masculine stem 'vāyu', Accusative, singular | air |
| vāyōḥ | Noun | उ-ending masculine stem 'vāyu', Ablative, singular | from Vayu |
| tējaḥ | Noun | स्-ending neuter stem 'tējas', Accusative, singular | brilliance |
| vyajījanat | Verb | वि-जन् जनने-ending प.प stem 'luṅ', प्र.पु., singular | created |
| tējasaḥ | Noun | स्-ending neuter stem 'tējas', Ablative, singular | from fire / brilliance |
| salilam | Noun | Indeclinable | the water |
| tasmāt | Noun | त्-ending neuter stem 'tat', Ablative, singular | from it |
| pṛthivīm | Noun | ई-ending feminine stem 'pṛthivī', Accusative, singular | the land |
| asṛjat | Verb | सृज् निर्माणे-ending प.प stem 'laṅ', प्र.पु., singular | created |
| vibhuḥ | Noun | उ-ending masculine stem 'vibhu', प्रथमा, singular | God is omnipresent |

**Anvaya:** vibhuḥ ākāśāt vāyum asṛjat, vāyōḥ tējaḥ vyajījanat, tējasaḥ salilam (asṛjat), tasmāt pṛthivīm asṛjat ।

**Bhavartha:** From space, the Lord created air; from air, He generated fire; from fire, He created water; and from water, the all-pervading Lord created the earth.
