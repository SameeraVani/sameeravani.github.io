---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಬಿಂಬಾಪರೋಕ್ಷ ಸಂಧಿ / Bimbaparoksha sandhi/ Bimbopasana sandhi / Bimba pratibimba sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè<br/>parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

mukta biṃbanu turiya jīvanmukta biṃbanu viśva<br/>śruti saṃsakta biṃbanu taijasanu asṛjyarigè prājña<br/>śaktanādaru sariyè sarva udrakta mahimanu<br/>duḥkha sukhagal̤a vaktṛ māḍutalippa kalpāṃtadali bapparigè||1||

aṇṇa nāmaka prakṛtiyòl̤agè accinnanu āgiha prājñanāmadi<br/>sònna òḍala mòdalāda avaròl̤u annāda taijasanu<br/>annada aṃbuda nābha viśvanu bhinna nāma kriyègal̤iṃdali<br/>tannòl̤agè tā ramipa pūrṇānaṃda jñānamaya||2||

būdiyòl̤agè aḍagippa anal̤ana upādi cetana prakṛtiyòl̤u<br/>annāda annāhvayadi karèsuva brahma śiva rūpi<br/>odana prada viṣṇu parama āhlādava īvuta<br/>tṛpti baḍisuva agādha mahimana citra karmavanu āva baṇṇisuva||3||

nāda bhojana śabdadòl̤u biṃbu odana udakadòl̤agè<br/>ghoṣa anuvādadòl̤u śāṃtākhya jaṭharāgniyòl̤agè irutippa<br/>vaidika suśabdadòl̤u putra sahodara anugaròl̤u ati śāṃtana pāda kamala<br/>anavarata ciṃtisu ī pariyaliṃda||4||

veda māni ramā anupāsya guṇa udadhi guṇa traya vivarjita<br/>svodarasthita nikhil̤a brahmāṃḍa ādi vilakṣaṇanu<br/>sādhu sammatavènisutiha niṣusīda gaṇapatiyèṃba śruti pratipādisuvudu<br/>anavarata avana guṇa prāṃta gāṇadalè||5||

karèsuvanu māyā ramaṇa tā puruṣa rūpadi tristhal̤agal̤òl̤u<br/>parama satpuruṣārthada mahattatvadòl̤agiddu<br/>sarasija bhavāṃḍa sthita strī puruṣa tanmātragal̤a<br/>ekottara daśa iṃdriyagal̤a mahā bhūtagal̤a nirmisida||6||

ī śarīraga puruṣa tri guṇadi strī sahita tāniddu<br/>jīvarigè āśè lobha ajñāna mada matsara kumoha kṣudha<br/>hāsa harṣa suṣupti svapna pipāsa jāgrati janma sthiti mṛti<br/>doṣa puṇya jayāpajaya dvaṃdvagal̤a kalpisida||7||

trividha guṇamaya deha jīvakè kavacadaṃdadi tòḍisi<br/>karma pravahadòl̤u saṃcāra māḍisutippa jevaranā<br/>kavisi māyāramaṇa mohava bhavakè kāraṇanāguvanu<br/>saṃśravaṇa mananava māl̤parigè mocakanu ènisutippa||8||

sāśanāhvaya strī puruṣaròl̤u vāsavāgihanu èṃdaridu<br/>viśvāsapūrvaka bhajisi toṣisu svāvarottamara<br/>kleśa nāśana acalagal̤òl̤u prakāśisutaliha<br/>anaśana rūpa upāsanava māl̤parigè torpanu tanna nijarūpa||9||

prakārāṃtara ciṃtisuvudu ī prakṛtiyòl̤u viśvādi rūpava<br/>prakaṭa māl̤pènu yathā matiyòl̤u gurukṛpābaladi<br/>mukura nirmita sadanadòl̤u pògè svakīya rūpava kāṃba tèradaṃtè<br/>akuṭilātma carācaradi sarvatra toruvanu||10||

pariccheda traya prakṛtiyòl̤agè irutihanu viśvādi rūpaka<br/>dharisi ātmādi tri rūpava īṣaṇatrayadi<br/>suruci jñānātma svarūpadi turiya nāmaka vāsudevana smarisu<br/>mukti sukha pradāyakanu ītanahudèṃdu||11||

kamalasaṃbhava janaka jaḍa jaṃgamara òl̤agè nèlèsiddu<br/>krama vyutkramadi karmava māḍi māḍisutippa besaradè<br/>kṣama kṣāma kṣamīhanāhvaya sumanasa asuraròl̤agè<br/>ahaṃ mamanamama èṃdu ī upāsanè eva prāṃtadali||12||

ī samasta jagattu īśāvyāsavu ènipudu<br/>kārya rūpavu nāśavādaru nityave sari kāraṇa prakṛti<br/>śrīśagè jaḍa pratimèyènipudu māsadu òmmigu sannidhānavu<br/>vāsavāgiha nitya śālagrāmada upādi||13||

ekamevādvitīya rūpa aneka jīvaròl̤iddu<br/>tā pratyeka karmava māḍi mohisutippa til̤isadalè<br/>mūka badhira aṃdhādi nāmaka ī kal̤evaradòl̤agè karèsuva<br/>mākal̤atrana laukika mahā mahimègè enèṃbè||14||

loka baṃdhu lokanātha viśoka bhaktara śoka nāśana<br/>śrī karārcita sokadaṃdadalippa sarvaròl̤u<br/>sākuvanu sajjanara parama kṛpākara īśa pināki sannuta<br/>svīkarisuva anataru kòṭṭa samasta karmagal̤a||15||

ahita pratimègal̤u ènisuvavu deha geha apatya sati dhana<br/>loha kāṣṭha śilāmṛd ātmakavu āda dravyagal̤u<br/>nehyadali paramātma ènagè ittīhanu èṃdaridu anudinadi<br/>sammohakè òl̤agāgadalè pūjisu sarva nāmakana||16||

śrī taruṇi vallabhagè jīvaru cetana pratimègal̤u<br/>otaprotanāgiddu èllaròl̤u vyāpāra māḍutiha<br/>hota sarva iṃdriyagal̤òl̤u saṃprītiyiṃda uṃḍuṇisi viṣaya<br/>nirvāta deśaga dīpadiṃdalippa nirbhayadi||17||

bhūta sokida mānavanu bahu mātanāḍuva tèradi<br/>mahā bhūta viṣṇvāveṣadiṃdali vartipudu jagavu<br/>kaitavoktigal̤alla śeṣa phaṇāta patragè<br/>jīva paṃcaka vrātavèṃdigu bhinna pādāhvayadi karèsuvudu||18||

diviyòl̤ippavu mūru pādagal̤u avaniyòl̤agihudòṃdu<br/>ī vidha kavibhirīḍita karèsuva catuṣpātu tānèṃdu<br/>ivana pāda catuṣṭayagal̤a anubhavakè taṃdu niraṃtaradi<br/>uddhavana sakha sarvāṃtarātmakanu èṃdu smarisutiru||19||

vaṃśa bāgilu bèl̤èyè kaṃḍu narāṃśadali śobhipudu<br/>bāgada vaṃśa pāśadi kaṭṭi erupa ḍòṃba mastakakè<br/>kaṃsa mardana dāsarigè nissaṃśayadi èragadalè<br/>nā vidvāṃsanu èṃdu ahaṃkarisè bhavaguṇadi baṃdhisuva||20||

jyoti rūpagè pratimègal̤u sāṃketika āropita<br/>supauruṣa dhātu saptaka dhairya śaurya audārya cāturya<br/>mātu māna mahatva sahana sunīti nirmala deśa brāhmaṇa<br/>bhūta paṃcaka buddhi mòdalāda iṃdriya sthāna||21||

jīva rāśiyòl̤u amṛta śāśvata sthāvaragal̤òl̤u sthāṇu nāmaka<br/>āvakāladalippa ajitānaṃtanu èṃdènisi<br/>govidāṃpati gāyanapriya sāvayava sahasra nāma<br/>parāvareśa pavitrakarma vipaścita susāma||22||

mādhavana pūjārthavāgi niṣedha karmava māḍi<br/>dhana saṃpādisalu satpuṇya karmagal̤u ènisikòl̤utihavu<br/>svodaraṃbharaṇārtha nityaḍi sādhu karmava māḍidaru sari<br/>yaiduvanu dehāṃtarava saṃdehavu initilla||23||

apagatāśraya èllaròl̤agiddu upamanu ènipa anupama rūpanu<br/>śaphara ketana janaka mohipa mohakana tèradi<br/>tapana koṭi samaprabhā sitavapuvu ènipa kṛṣṇādi rūpaka<br/>vipagal̤aṃtè uṃḍuṇipa sarvatradali nèlèsiddu||24||

aḍaviyòl̤u bittadalè bèl̤èdiha giḍada mūlikè<br/>sakala jīvara òḍalòl̤ippa āmayava pariharagaisuvaṃdadali<br/>jaḍaja saṃbhava janaka trijagadvaḍèya saṃtaisènalu<br/>avaru iddèḍègè baṃdòdaguvanu bhaktara bhiḍèya mīradalè||25||

śrī niketana tannavara deha anubaṃdhigal̤aṃtè avyavadhānadali nèlèsippa<br/>sarvada sakala kāmadanu<br/>kòṭṭaru bhuṃjisuta maddānèyaṃdadi saṃcarisu<br/>mattenu beḍadè bhajisutiru avana aṃghri kamalagal̤a||26||

beḍadalè kòḍutippa surarigè beḍidarè kòḍutihanu nararigè<br/>beḍi bal̤aluva daityarigè kòḍanu òmmè puruṣārtha<br/>mūḍharu anudina dharma karmava māḍidaru sari<br/>ahika phalagal̤a nīḍi unmattaranu māḍi mahā nirayavīva||27||

taraṇi sarvatradali kiraṇava harahi tattad vastugal̤anu sarisi<br/>adara adaraṃtè chāyava kaṃgòl̤ipa tèradi<br/>aridhara ejāneja jagadòl̤agiruva chāyā tapavènisi<br/>saṃkaruṣaṇāhvaya avaravara yogyatègal̤aṃtè ippa||28||

ī vidhadi sarvatra lakṣmī bhū vanitèyara kūḍi<br/>tanna kal̤ā viśeṣagal̤a èlla kaḍèyali tuṃbi sevyatama<br/>sevakanu tānènisi māyādevi ramaṇa<br/>praviṣṭa rūpava sevè māl̤pa śaraṇya śāśvata karuṇi kamalākṣa||29||

praṇava kāraṇa kārya pratipādyanu parātpara<br/>cetanācetana vilakṣaṇa anaṃta satkalyāṇa guṇapūrṇa<br/>anupamanu upāsita guṇa udadhi anagha ajita anaṃta<br/>niṣkiṃcana janapriya nirvikāra nirāśraya avyaktā||30||

gopa bhīya bhavāṃdhakārakè dīpavaṭṭigè<br/>sakala sukha sadana uparigrahavu ènisutippudu harikathāmṛtavu<br/>gopati jagannātha viṭhala samīpadali nèlèsiddu<br/>bhaktaranu āpavargara māḍuvanu maha duḥkha bhayadiṃda||31||
