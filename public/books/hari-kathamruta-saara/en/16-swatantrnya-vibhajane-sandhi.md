---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ದತ್ತಸ್ವಾತಂತ್ರ್ಯ ಸಂಧಿ / Dhatthasvathanthriya sandhi / SwAtantrnya vibhajane Sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè|<br/>parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

kāruṇika hari tannòl̤ippa apāra svātaṃtrya guṇava nānūru tègèdu<br/>sapāda āròṃdadhika aravattu nārigitta<br/>dvi ṣoḍaśa adhika nūru pādatrayava tanna śarīradòl̤agè<br/>īpari vibhāgava māḍi tripadāhva||1||

satya lokādhipanòl̤agè aivattèrèḍu pavamānanòl̤u nālvattu melèṃṭu<br/>adhika śivanòl̤agiṭṭanippattu cittaja iṃdraròl̤u aidadhika daśa<br/>tatvamānigal̤u ènipa suraròl̤u hattu<br/>īraidu akhil̤a jīvaròl̤iṭṭa niravadya||2||

kali mòdalugòṃḍa akhil̤a dānavaròl̤agè nālvattaidu<br/>ī pari til̤idu upāsanè māḍu marèyadè parama bhakutiyali<br/>il̤èyòl̤agè saṃcarisu lakṣmī nilayana āl̤ānèṃdu<br/>sarva sthal̤agal̤ali saṃtaisutippanu gèl̤èyanaṃdadali||3||

avanipa svāmitva dharmava svavaśa mātyarigittu<br/>tā mattavara mukhadali rāja kāryāva māḍisuva tèradi<br/>kavibhirīḍita tanna kal̤ègal̤a divija dānava mānavaròl̤iṭṭu<br/>avirata guṇatrayaja karmava māḍi māḍisuva||4||

puṇya pāpagal̤u ī tèradi kāruṇya sāgara devadānava mānavaròl̤iṭṭu<br/>avara phala vyatyāsavanè māḍi<br/>banna baḍisuva bhaktihīnara sannuta sukarma phalatègèdu<br/>prapannarigè kòṭṭu avara sukhapaḍisuvanu subhujāhva||5||

māṇikava kòṃḍu aṃgaḍiyòl̤u ajivāna kòṭṭu ā puruṣana<br/>samādhāna māḍuva tèradi daityaru nityadali māl̤pa<br/>dāna yajñādigal̤a phala pavamāna pitanu apaharisi<br/>asamīcīna sukhagal̤a kòṭṭu asurara mattaranu māl̤pa||6||

eṇa lāṃchanana amala kiraṇa krameṇa vṛddhiyanu aidi<br/>logara kāṇagòḍadiha kattalèya bhaṃgisuva tèradaṃtè<br/>vainateyāṃgasana mūrti dhyānavul̤l̤a mahātmarigè<br/>sujñāna bhaktyādigal̤u vardhisi sukhave kòḍutiharu||7||

janapana arikèya cora pòl̤alòl̤u dhanava kaddòyda īyalu<br/>avana avaguṇagal̤u èṇisadè pòrèva kòḍadirè śikṣisuva tèradi<br/>anucitocita karma kṛṣṇārpaṇavènalu<br/>kaikòṃḍu tannaramanèyòl̤iṭṭu ānaṃda baḍisuva mādhavānatara||8||

annada annāda anna nāmaka munna pel̤da prakāra<br/>jīvaròl̤u anna rūpa praveśagaidu avaravara vyāpāra<br/>bannabaḍadalè māḍi māḍisi dhanyarivaru ahudu èṃdènisi<br/>traiguṇya varjita tattadāhvayanu āgi karèsuvanu||9||

salilabiṃdu payābdhiyòl̤u bīl̤alu vikāravanu aida balladè<br/>jalavu tadrūpavanè aiduvudu èlla kāladali<br/>kalilamalāpahana arcisuva satkulajara kukarmagal̤u<br/>tāniṣkaluṣa karmagal̤āgi puruṣārthagal̤a kòḍutiharu||10||

mògadòl̤agè mògaviṭṭu muddisi maguvinaṃ bigidappi raṃbisi<br/>nehadi tègèdu tannaya stanagal̤u uṇisuva jananiyaṃdadali<br/>agaṇitātmanu tanna pādābjagal̤a dhenipa bhakta janarigè<br/>praghaṭakanu tānāgi saukhyagal̤u eva sarvatra||11||

toṭiganu bhūmiyòl̤u bījava nāṭabekèṃdènuta<br/>hitadali moṭèyiṃ nīrètti sasigal̤a saṃtaisuvaṃtè<br/>pāṭubaḍadalè jagadi jīvara ghoṭakāsyanu sṛjisi<br/>yogyatè dāṭagòḍadalè salahutippanu sarva kāladali||12||

bhūmiyòl̤u jalavirè tṛṣārtanu tā marèdu<br/>mògavètti èṇdèsèvyoma maṃḍaladòl̤agè kāṇadè miḍukuvaṃdadali<br/>śrī manorama sarvara aṃtaryāmiyu āgirè til̤iyalariyadè<br/>bhrāmakaru bhajisuvaru bhakutiyali anya devatèya||13||

mukhya phala vaikuṃṭha mukhyāmukhya phala mahadādi lokā<br/>amukhya phala vaiṣayikavu èṃdaridu ati bhakutiyiṃda<br/>rakkasa ariya bhajisutali nirduḥkhanāgu<br/>niraṃtaradi mòrè pòkkavara biḍa bhṛtyavatsala bhāratīśa pita||14||

vyādhiyiṃ pīḍita śiśuvigè guḍodakava nèrèdu adakè auṣadha tedu<br/>kuḍisuva tāyiya upādiyali<br/>sarvajña bādarāyaṇa bhakta janakè prasāda rūpakanāgi<br/>bhāgavatādiyali pel̤idanu dharmādigal̤e phalavèṃdu||15||

dūradalliha parvata ghanākāra torpudu nol̤pa janarigè<br/>sāragaiyalu sarva vyāghragal̤iṃda bhayavihudu<br/>ghoratara saṃsāra saukhya asārataravèṃdu aritu<br/>nitya ramāramaṇana ārādhisuvaru adariṃda ballavaru||16||

kèsara ghaṭagal̤a māḍi besigè bisilòl̤agè iṭṭu òṇagisidaru adu<br/>ghana rasavu tuṃbalu bahude? sarva svātaṃtra nānèṃba<br/>paśupa naranu enenu māl̤pa anaśana dāna snāna karmagal̤u<br/>òsari popavu baridè deha āyāsavanè kòṭṭu||17||

èraḍu dīkṣègal̤ihavu bāhyāṃtaravu ènipa nāmadali<br/>budhariṃdaritu dīkṣitanu āgu dīrgha dveṣagal̤a biṭṭu<br/>hariye sarvottama kṣarākṣara puruṣa pūjita pāda<br/>janmādi aravi dūra sukhātma sarvaganu èṃdu smarisutiru||18||

heyavastugal̤illavu upādeya vastugal̤illa<br/>nyāyānyāya dharmagal̤illa dveṣa asūyè mòdalilla<br/>tāyi taṃdègal̤illa kamaladal̤āyata akṣagèyènalu<br/>īsuva kāyiyaṃdadi mul̤agagòḍadè bhavābdhi dāṭisuva||19||<br/>maṃdanādaru sariyè gopīcaṃdana śrī mudrègal̤a naliviṃda dharisuta<br/>śrī tul̤asi padumākṣi saragal̤anu kaṃdharada madhyadali dharisi<br/>mukuṃda śrī bhūramaṇa trijagadvaṃdya<br/>sarva svāmi mama kuladaivavu ènè pòrèva||20||

prāya dhana madadiṃda janarigè nāyaka prabhuvèṃbi<br/>pūrvadi tāyi pòṭṭèyòl̤iralu prabhuvèṃdekè karèyaralai?<br/>kāya ninnanu biṭṭu pogalu rāyā nīnèṃbuva prabhutva<br/>palāyanavanaiditu samīpadaliddarè adu toru||21||

vāsudeva eka prakāradi īśanènisuva<br/>brahma rudra śaci īśa mòdalāda amararèllaru dāsarènisuvaru<br/>ī sumārgava biṭṭu sohamupāsanèyagaiva nara<br/>dehaja daiśika kleśagal̤u baralu avanu ekè biḍisikòl̤a||22||

ā para brahmanali trijagadvyāpakātva niyāmakātva<br/>sthāpakātva vaśātva īśātvādi guṇagal̤igè lopavilla<br/>eka prakāra svarūpavu ènipavu sarva kāladi popavallavu<br/>jīvarigè dāsātvadupādi||23||

nityanūtana nirvikāra suhṛttama praṇavastha<br/>varṇotpatti kāraṇa vāgmanaḥmaya sāmagānarata<br/>datta kapila hayāsya rūpadi pṛth prutag jīvaròl̤agiddu pravartisuvanu<br/>avaravara yogyatè karmava anusarisi||24||

śṛtigal̤u ātana mātu vimalā smṛtigal̤u ātana śikṣè<br/>jīva pratati prakṛtigal̤u èraḍu pratimègal̤u ènisikòl̤utihavu<br/>itara karmagal̤u èlla lakṣmī patigè pūjègal̤èṃdu smarisuta<br/>catura vidha puruṣārthagal̤a beḍadiru svapnadali||25||

bhūtal̤ādhipanu ājña dhāraka dūtarigè<br/>sevānusāradi vetanava kòṭṭu avara saṃtoṣisuva tèradaṃtè<br/>mātariśva priyanu parama prīti pūrvaka<br/>sadguṇaṃgal̤a gādhakara saṃtoṣa paḍisuva iha paraṃgal̤ali||26||

dīpa divadali kaṃḍarādaḍè lopagaisuvaru ā kṣaṇa<br/>hari samīpadallirè naṃda nāma sunaṃdavu ènisuvavu<br/>aupacārikavalla sujanara pāpa karmavu puṇyavu ènipavu<br/>pāpigal̤a satpuṇya karmavu pāpavu ènisuvavu||27||

dhanava saṃpādisuva pradrāvaṇikaraṃdadadi<br/>kovidara manèmanègal̤ali saṃcarisu śāstra śravaṇa gosugadi<br/>mananagaidu upadeśisuta durjanara kūḍi āḍadiru svapnadi<br/>praṇata kāmada kòḍuva saukhyagal̤a iha paraṃgal̤ali||28||

kāraka kriya dravya vibhrama mūru vidha jīvarigè<br/>bahu saṃsārakè ivu kāraṇavu ènisuvavu èlla kāladali dūra oḍisi<br/>bhrāmakatraya mārigè òl̤agāgadalè

sarvārādhakana ciṃtisutaliru sarvatra marèyadalè||29||

karaṇa karmava māḍidarè vismaraṇè kāladi mātugal̤igè<br/>uttarava kòḍadalè summanippanu<br/>jāgarāvasthè karuṇisalu vyāpāra māḍuva<br/>baralu nālkavasthègal̤u pariharisi kòl̤anu etakè? svataṃtranu tānèyèṃbuvanu||30||

yukti mātugal̤alla śruti smṛti ukta mātugal̤ivu<br/>vicārisi muktigivu sopānavu ènipavu pratiprati padavu<br/>bhaktipūrvaka paṭhisuvavarigè vyakti kòḍuva svarūpa sukha<br/>praviviraktana māḍuvanu bhavabhayadiṃda bahu rūpa||31||

śrīnivāsana suguṇa maṇigal̤a prāṇamāta vayunākhya sūtradi poṇisida mālikèya<br/>vāgmayagè samarpisida<br/>jñānigal̤a dṛk viṣayavuhuda ajñānigal̤igè asahya torpudu<br/>māṇikava markaṭana kaiyali kòṭṭa tèradaṃtè||32||

śrīvidhi īra vipāhipa īśa śacī varātmabhava arka śaśi<br/>digdeva ṛṣi gaṃdharva kinnara siddha sādhya gaṇa sevita padāṃbuja<br/>tvatpādālavaṃbigal̤u āda bhaktara kāva<br/>karuṇā sāṃdra lakṣmī hṛtkumuda caṃdra||33||

ādariśa agatākṣa bhāṣā bhedadiṃdali karèyalu adanu<br/>niṣedhagaidu avalokisadè biḍuvarè vivekigal̤u<br/>mādhavana guṇa pel̤va prākṛtavu ādarèyu sari<br/>kel̤i parama āhlādabaḍadipparè? niraṃtara balla kovidaru||34||

bhāskarana maṃḍalava kaṃḍu namaskarisi modisadè<br/>dveṣadi taskaranu niṃdisalu kuṃdahude divākaragè<br/>saṃskṛtavu idalla èṃdu kuhaka tiraskarisalu enahudu?<br/>bhakti puraskaradi kel̤varigòlivanu puṣkarākṣa sadā||35||

patitana kapāladòl̤u bhāgīrathiya jalavirè peyavu ènipudè?<br/>itara kavi nirmita kukāvya aśrāvya budhariṃda<br/>kṛti patikathānvitavu ènipa prākṛtavè tā saṃskṛtavu ènisi<br/>sadgatiyanīvudu bhakti pūrvaka kel̤i pel̤varigè||36||

śāstra sayukti graṃthagal̤u odi kel̤davanalla<br/>saṃtata sādhugal̤a sahavāsa sallāpagal̤u mòdalilla<br/>moda tīrtha āryara matānugaru ādavara karuṇadali pel̤dè<br/>ramādhava jagannātha viṭhala til̤isidadaròl̤agè||37||
