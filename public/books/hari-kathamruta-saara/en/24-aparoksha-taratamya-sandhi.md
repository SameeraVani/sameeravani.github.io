---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಕಲ್ಪಸಾಧನ ಸಂಧಿ/ಶ್ರೀ ಅಪರೋಕ್ಷ ತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಸಂಧಿ/Kalpa sadhana sandhi/Aparoksha Taratamya sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè/

parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

ekaviṃśati mata pravartaka kāku māygal̤a kuhaka yukti nirākarisi

sarvottamanu hariyèṃdu sthāpisida

śrī kal̤atrana sadana dvijapa pināki sannuta mahima

parama kṛpākaṭākṣadi noḍu madhvācārya guruvarya||1||

veda mòdalāgippa amala mokṣa adhikārigal̤u āda jīvara

sādhanagal̤a aparokṣa naṃtara liṃga bhaṃgavanu

sādhugal̤u cittaipadu ènnaparādhagal̤a noḍadalè

cakra gadādharanu pel̤isida tèradaṃdadali pel̤uvènu||2||

tṛṇa krimi dvija paśu narottama janapa naragaṃdharva gaṇaru

ivarèniparu aṃśa vihīna karma suyogigal̤èṃdu

tanu pratīkadi biṃbana upāsanavagaiyuta iṃdriyaja karma

anavarata harigè arpisuta nirmamaruyènisuvaru||3||

el̤uvidha jīva gaṇa bahal̤a surāl̤i saṃkhyā nemavul̤l̤adu

tāl̤i naradehavanu brāhmaṇara kuladòl̤udbhavisi

sthūla karmava tòrèdu gurugal̤u pel̤ida arthava til̤idu

tattatkāla dharma samarpisuva avaru karma yogigal̤u||4||

hīna karmagal̤iṃda bahuvidha yoniyali saṃcarisi prāṃtakè

mānuṣatvavanaidi sarvottamanu hariyèṃba

jñāna bhaktigal̤iṃda vedokta anusāra sahasrajanma

anyūna karmava māḍi harigarpisida naṃtaradi||5||

hattu janmagal̤ali hari sarvottamanu surāsura gaṇārcita

citra karma viśoka anaṃtānaṃta rūpātma

satya satsaṃkalpa jagadotpatti sthitilaya kāraṇa

jarāmṛtyu varjitanèṃdu upāsanègaida taruvāya||6||

mūru janmagal̤alli dehāgāra paśu dhana patni mitra

kumāra mātā pitṛgal̤alli iha snehagiṃta adhika

māramaṇanali biḍadè māḍuva sūrigal̤u ī ukta janmava mīri

paramātmana svadehadi noḍi sukhisuvaru||7||

deva gāyaka ajāna cirapitṛ devarèllaru

jñāna yogigal̤u āva kālaku puṣkara śanaiścara uṣā svāhā devi

budhasanakādigal̤u meghāval̤i parjanya sāṃśaru

ī ubhaya gaṇadòl̤agivaru vijñāna yogigal̤u||8||

bharata khaṃḍadi nūru janmava dharisi niṣkāmaka sukarma ācarisida anaṃtaradi

daśa sahasra janmadali urutara jñānavanu

mūraidu èraḍu daśa dehadali bhaktiya

niravadhikanali māḍi kāṃbaru biṃba rūpavanu||9||

sādhanātpūrvadali ivarigè anādi kāla aparokṣavilla

niṣedha karmagal̤illa naraka prāpti mòdalilla

veda śāstragal̤allippa virodha vākyava pariharisi

madhusūdananè sarvottamottamanu èṃdu tutisuvaru||10||

satyalokādhipana viḍidu śatastha devagaṇa aṃta èllaru

bhakti yogigal̤èṃdu karèsuvaru āva kāladali

bhakti yogyara madhyadali sadbhakti vijñānādi guṇadiṃda uttama

uttama brahma vāyū vāṇi vāgdevi||11||

ṛjugaṇakè bhakti ādi guṇa sahajavu ènisuvavu

kramadi vṛddhi abjaja padavi paryaṃta biṃba upāsanavu adhika

vṛjina varjita èllaròl̤u triguṇaja vikāragal̤illavu èṃdigu

dvijaphaṇipa mṛḍa śakra mòdalāda avaròl̤u irutihavu||12||

sādhanātpūrvadali ī ṛjvādi sātvikaru ènipa suragaṇa

anādi sāmānya aparokṣigal̤èṃdu karèsuvaru

sādhanottara svasva biṃba upādhi rahita ādityanaṃdadi

sādaradi noḍuvaru adhikāra anusāradali||13||

chinna bhaktaru ènisutiharu suparṇa śeṣādi amarararèlla

accinna bhaktaru nālvarèniparu bhāratī prāṇa sònnòḍala vāgdeviyaru

paṇègaṇṇa mòdalāda avaròl̤agè tattanniyāmakarāgi

vyāpāravanu māḍuvaru||14||

hīna satkarmagal̤u èraḍu pavamāna devanu māl̤panu

idakè anumānavilla èṃdu ènuta dṛḍha bhaktiyali bhajipargè

prāṇapati saṃprītanāgi kuyonigal̤a kòḍa

èlla karmagal̤u ānè māḍuvènèṃba manujara narakakè aidisuva||15||

devarṣi pitṛpa nararènisuva aivaròl̤u nèlèsiddu

avara svabhāva karmava māḍi māḍipa òṃdu rūpadali

bhāvi brahmanu kūrma rūpadi ī viriṃci aṃḍavanu bènnili tā vahisi

lokagal̤a pòrèvanu dvitīya rūpadali||16||

guptanāgiddu anila deva dvisapta lokada jīvaròl̤agè

trisapta sāvirada ārunūru śvāsa japagal̤anu

suptisvapnadi jāgratigal̤ali āptanaṃdadi māḍi māḍisi

klupta bhogagal̤īva prāṃtakè tṛtīya rūpadali||17||

śuddha satvātmaka śarīradòl̤idda kālaku liṃgadehavu baddhavāgadu

dagdha paṭadopādi irutihudu

siddha sādhana sarvaròl̤agè anavadyanu ènisuva

garuḍa śeṣa kapardi mòdalāda amararèllaru dāsarènisuvaru||18||

gaṇadòl̤agè tāniddu ṛjuvèṃdu ènisikòṃbanu

kalpa śata sādhanavagaida anaṃtaradi tā kalkiyènisuvanu

dvinavāśītiya prāṃta bhāgadi anila hanumadbhīma rūpadi

danujarèllara sadèdu madhvācāryarènisidanu||19||

viśvavyāpaka harigè tā sādṛśya rūpava dharisi

brahma sarasvatī bhāratigal̤iṃda òḍagūḍi pavamāna

śāśvata subhaktiyali sujñāna svarūpana rūpaguṇagal̤a

anaśvaravèṃdènuta pògal̤uva śrutigal̤òl̤agiddu||20||

kheṭa kukkuṭa jalaṭavèṃba trikoṭi rūpava dharisi

satata niśāṭaranu saṃharisi salahuva sarva satjanara

kaiṭabhāriya purada prathama kavāṭavènisuva

garuḍa śeṣa lalāṭalocana mukhya surarigè āvakāladali||21||

ī ṛjugal̤òl̤agòbba sādhana nūru kalpadi māḍi karèsuva

cārutara maṃgal̤a sunāmadi kalpa kalpadali

sūrigal̤u saṃstutisi vaṃdisè ghoraduritagal̤anu al̤idu popuvu

māramaṇa saṃprītanāguva sarva kāladali||22||

pāhi kalkisutejadāsanè pāhi dharmādharma khaṃḍanè

pāhi varcasvī khaṣaṇa namo sādhu mahīpatiyè

pāhi saddharmajña dharmaja pāhi saṃpūrṇa śuci vaikṛta

pāhi aṃjana sarṣapanè kharpaṭa: śraddhāhva||23||

pāhi saṃdhyāta vijñānanè pāhi maha vijñāna kīrtana

pāhi saṃkīrṇākhya katthana mahābuddhi jayā

pāhi māhattara suvīryanè pāhimāṃ medhāvi jayājaya

pāhimāṃ raṃtimnamanu māṃ pāhi māṃ pāhi||24||

pāhi moda pramoda saṃtasa pāhi ānaṃda saṃtuṣṭanè

pāhimāṃ cārvāṃgacāru subāhucāru pada

pāhi pāhi sulocananè māṃ pāhi sārasvata suvīranè

pāhi prājña kapi alaṃpaṭa pāhi sarvajña||25||

pāhimāṃ sarvajit mitranè pāhi pāpa vināśakanè

māṃ pāhi dharmavineta śārada oja sutapasvī

pāhimāṃ tejasvi namo māṃ pāhi dāna suśīla

namo māṃ pāhi yajña sukarta yajvī yāga vartakanè||26||

pāhi prāṇa trāṇa amarṣi pāhimāṃ upadeṣṭa tāraka

pāhi kāla krīḍana sukartā sukālajña

pāhi kāla susūcakanè māṃ pāhi kali saṃhartakali

māṃ pāhi kāliśāmareta sadārata subalanè||27||

pāhi pāhi saho sadākapi pāhi gamya jñāna daśakula

pāhimāṃ śrotavya namo saṃkīrtitavya namo

pāhimāṃ maṃtavyakavyanè pāhi draṣṭavyanè saravyanè

pāhi gaṃtavya namo kravyanè pāhi smartavya||28||

pāhi sevya subhavya namo māṃ pāhi svargavya namo bhāvyanè

pāhi māṃ jñātavya namo vaktavya gavya namo

pāhi maṃ lātavyavāyuvè pāhi brahma brāhmaṇapriya

pāhi pāhi sarasvatīpate jagadguruvarya||29||

vāmana purāṇadali pel̤ida ī mahātmara parama maṃgal̤a nāmagal̤a

saṃprīti pūrvaka nitya smarisuvavarā

śrī manorama avaru beḍida kāmitārthagal̤ittu

tanna tridhāmadòl̤agè anudinadaliṭṭu ānaṃda paḍisuvanu||30||

ī samīragè nūru janma mahā sukha prārabdha bhoga

prayāsavilladè aididanu lokādhipatyavanu

bhūsurana òppiḍi avaligè viśeṣa saukhyavanitta dātana

dāsavaryanu lokapatiyènisuvudu accaravè||31||

dviśata kalpagal̤alli biḍadè ī pèsariniṃdali karèsidanu

tannòśaga amararòl̤iddu māḍuvanu avara sādhanava

asadupāsanègaiva kalyādi asurapara saṃharisi

tā pòṃbasira padavaididanu guru pavamāna satiyòḍanè||32||

animiṣara nāmadali karèsuva aniladevanu

òṃdu kalpakè vanaja saṃbhavanènisi èṃbhattel̤ūvarè varṣa

guṇatraya varjitana maṃgal̤a guṇa kriyā surūpaṃgal̤a

upāsanavu avyaktādi pṛthvi aṃtaradirutihudu||33||

mahita ṛjugaṇakè òṃde paramotsāha vivarjitavèṃba doṣavu

vihitavè sari idanu pel̤dirè mukta brahmarigè bahudu sāmyavu

jñāna bhakutiyu druhiṇa pada paryaṃta vṛddhiyu

bahirupāsanè uṃṭu anaṃtara biṃba darśanavu||34||

jñānarahita bhayatva pel̤va purāṇa daityara mohakavu

caturānanagè kòḍuvadè mohājñāna bhaya śoka

bhānumaṃḍala calisidaṃdadi kāṇuvudu dṛg doṣadiṃdali

śrīnivāsana prītigosuga tordanalladalè||35||

kamalasaṃbhava sarvaròl̤aguttamanènisuvanu èlla kāladi

vimala bhakti jñāna vairāgyādi guṇadiṃda

samābhyadhika vivarjitana guṇa ramèya mukhadiṃdaritu nityadi

dyumaṇi koṭigal̤aṃtè kāṃbanu biṃba rūpavanu||36||

jñāna bhaktādi akhil̤a guṇa caturānanòl̤agippaṃtè

mukhyā prāṇanali ciṃtipudu yatkiṃcit kòratèyāgi

nyūna ṛju gaṇa jīvaralli krameṇa vṛddhi jñāna bhakti

samāna bhārati vāṇigal̤ali pada prayuktādhika||37||

sauri sūryana tèradi brahmasamīra gāyatrī girigal̤òl̤u

toruvudu aspaṣṭa rūpadi mukti paryaṃta

vārijāsana vāyu vāṇī bhāratigal̤igè mahā pral̤ayadi bāradu

ajñānādi doṣavu hari kṛpā baladi||38||

nūru varuṣa anaṃtaradali saroruhāsana tanna kalpadali

āru muktiyanu aiduvarò avaravara karèdòydu

śauri purudòl̤agippa nadiyali kāruṇika susnāna nija parivāra sahitadi māḍi

hari udara praveśisuva||39||

vāsudevana udaradali praveśagaida anaṃtaradi

nirdoṣa muktaru udaradiṃ pòramaṭṭu haruṣadali

meśaniṃda ājñava paḍèdu anaṃtāsana sītadvīpa mokṣadi vāsavāgi

vimukta duḥkharu saṃcarisutiharu||40||

satva satva mahā susūkṣamu satva satvātmaka kal̤evara

satyalokādhipanu ènipagè atyalpavu èraḍu guṇa

mukta bhogyavidalla ajāṃḍa utpatti kāraṇavalla

hari prītyarthavāgī jagada vyāpāragal̤a māḍuvanu||41||

pāda nyūna śatābda paryaṃta odi ugratapa ahvayadi lavaṇa udadhiyòl̤agè

kalpadaśa tapavidda anaṃtaradi

sādhisida mahadeva padava āraidu nava kalpa avasānakè

aiduvanu śeṣana padava pārvati sahitanāgi||42||

iṃdra manu daśa kalpagal̤ali sunaṃda nāmadi śravaṇagaidu

mukuṃdana aparokṣārtha nālku sukalpa tapaviddu

nòṃdu pògèyòl̤u koṭi varuṣa puraṃdaranadanuṃḍa anaṃtara

pòṃdidanu nija loka surapati kāmanidaraṃtè||43||

karèsuvaru pūrvadali caṃdrārkaru ati śāṃta surūpa nāmadi

èraḍèrèḍu manu kalpa śravaṇagaidu

manu kalpa vara tapo baladiṃda arvāk śiragal̤āgi īraidu sāvira varuṣa

duḥkhavanīgi kāṃbaru biṃba rūpavanu||44||

sādhanagal̤a aparokṣa anaṃtara aiduvaru mokṣavanu

śiva śakrādi divijaru ukta kramadiṃ kalpa saṃkhyèyali

aidalègè aivattu upeṃdra sahodaranigippattu

dvinava tvagādhipati prāṇanigè guru manugal̤igè ṣoḍaśavu||45||

pravaha marutagè hannèraḍu saiṃdhava divākara dharmarigè daśa

nava sukalpavu mitrarigè śeṣa śata janarigèṃṭu

kavi sanaka susanaṃdana sanatkuvara munigal̤igè el̤u

varuanana yuvati parjanyādi puṣkaragè āru kalpadali||46||

aidu karmaja surarigè ājānādigal̤igè èraḍèrèḍu kalpa

ardhādhika traya gopikā strīyarigè pitṛ trayavu

ī divaukasa manuja gāyakaru aiduvaru èraḍòṃdu kalpa

narādhiparigè arè kalpadòl̤agè aparokṣavu irutihudu||47||

dīpagal̤a anusarisi dīptiyu vyāpisi mahātimira kal̤èdu

paropakārava māl̤pa tèradaṃdadali paramātma

ā payojāsananòl̤agiddu svarūpa śaktiya vyaktigaisuta

tā pòl̤èvanu avaraṃtè ceṣṭèya māḍi māḍisuva||48||

svodarasthita prāṇa rudra iṃdrādi surarigè dehagal̤a kòṭṭu

ādaradi avaravara sevèya kòṃbanu anavarata

moda bodha dayābdhi tannavarādhi rogava kal̤èdu

mahādaparādhagal̤a noḍadalè salahuva satata smarisuvara||49||

prati pratī kalpadali sṛṣṭi sthiti layava māḍutalè modipa

caturamukha pavamānara annava māḍi bhuṃjisuva

ghṛtavè mṛtyuṃjayanènipa devatègal̤èlla upasecanaru

śrīpatigè mūrjagavèlla odana atithiyènisikòṃba||50||

garbhiṇi strī uṃḍa bhojana garbhagata śiśu uṃba tèradali

nirbhayanu tānuṃḍu uṇisuvanu sarva jīvarigè

nirbalāti paramāṇu jīvagè abbuvadè sthūlānna bhojana

arbhakaru pel̤uvaru kovidaru idana òḍaṃbaḍaru||51||

apacayagal̤illa uṃḍududariṃda upacayagal̤illa

amaragaṇadòl̤agè upamarènisuvarilla janmādigal̤u mòdalilla

aparimita sanmahima bhaktara apunarāvartaranu māḍuva

kṛpaṇa vatsala svapada saukhyavanittu śaraṇarigè||52||

bitti bījavu bhūmiyòl̤u nīrètti bèl̤èsida bèl̤asu prāṃtakè

kitti mèluvaṃdadali lakṣmī ramaṇa lokagal̤a mattè jīvara

karma kālotpatti sthiti laya māḍutali

samavartiyènisuva kheda modagal̤u illa anavarata||53||

śvasana rudra iṃdra pramukha sumanasaròl̤iddaru

kṣutpipāsagal̤u vaśadòl̤ippavu sakala bhogakè sādhanagal̤āgi

asura preta piśācigal̤a bhādisutalippavu

dinadinadi mānisaròl̤agè mṛga pakṣi jīvaròl̤iddu poguvavu||54||

vāsudevagè svapnasuptipipāsa kṣut bhaya śoka moha āyāsa apasmṛti

mātsarya mada puṇya pāpādi doṣa varjitanèṃdu

brahma sadāśivādi samasta divijaru upāsanèyagaidu

èlla kāladi muktarāgiharu||55||

parama sūkṣma kṣaṇavu aidu truṭi karèsuvadu aivattu truṭi lava

èraḍu lavavu nimiṣa nimiṣagal̤èṃṭu mātra

yuga guru daśa prāṇaru pal̤a hannèraḍu bāṇavu

ghaṭika triṃśati irul̤u hagalu aravattu ghaṭikagal̤u ahòrātrigal̤u||56||

ī divārātrigal̤u èraḍu hadinaidu pakṣagal̤u

èraḍu māsagal̤u ādapavu māsa dvayavè ṛtu ṛtutrayagal̤u ayana

aiduvadu ayanadvayābda kṛtādi yugagal̤u

deva mānadi dvādaśa sahasra varuṣagal̤u adanu pel̤uvènu||57||

catura sāvirada èṃṭu nūrivu kṛta yugakè

sahasra salè ṣaṭ śatavu tretagè dvāparakè dvisahasra nānūru

ditijapati kaliyugakè sāvira śata dvayagal̤u kūḍi

ī devatègal̤igè hannèraḍu sāvira vihavu varṣagal̤u||58||

prathama yugakè el̤adhika arè viṃśati sulakṣāṣṭottara

suviṃśati sahasra manuṣya mānābdagal̤u

ṣaṇṇavati mita sahasrada lakṣa dvādaśa dvitīya

tṛtīyakè èṃṭu lakṣada catura ṣaṣṭhi sahasra kāligè idarardha ciṃtipudu||59||

mūradhika nālvattu lakṣada āru mūrèrèḍadhika sāvira

īrèrèḍu yuga varuṣa saṃkhyègaiyalu initihudò

sūri pèccisè sāvirada nānūru mūvattèrèḍu koṭi

saroruhāsanagè idu divasavu èṃbaru vipaścitaru||60||

śatadhṛtigè ī divasagal̤u triṃśatiyu māsa dvādaśābdavu

śatavu èraḍaròl̤u sarva jīvotpatti sthiti layavu

śṛti smṛtigal̤u pel̤utihavu acyutagè nimiṣavidèṃdu

sukha śāśvatagè pāsaṭiyèṃbuvarè brahmādi divijaranu||61||

ādi madhyāṃtaragal̤illada mādhavagidu upacāravèṃdu

ṛgādi veda purāṇagal̤u pel̤uvavu nityadali

modamayana anugrahava saṃpādisi ramā brahma rudra iṃdrādigal̤u

tamma tamma padaviyanu aidi sukhisuvaru||62||

ī kathāmṛta pāna sukha suvivekigal̤igalladalè

vaiṣika vyākula kucittarigè dòrèvudāva kāladali

loka vārtèya biṭṭu idanavalokisuta modiparigòlidu

kṛpākara jagannātha viṭhala pòrèvanu anudinadi||63||
