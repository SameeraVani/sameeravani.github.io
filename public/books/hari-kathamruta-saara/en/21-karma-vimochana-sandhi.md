---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಕರ್ಮ ವಿಮೋಚನ ಸಂಧಿ / Karma vimochana sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè<br/>parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

mūla nārāyaṇanu māyā lola anaṃta avatāra nāmaka<br/>vyāl̤a rūpa jayā ramaṇana āvèśanu ènisuvana<br/>līlègaiva anaṃta cetana jāladòl̤u pradyumna<br/>brahmāṃḍa ālayada òl̤a hòragè nèlèsiha śāṃti aniruddha||1||

aidu kāraṇa rūpa ippattaidu kāryagal̤u ènisuvavu<br/>āraidu rūpadi ramisutippanu ī carācaradi<br/>bheda varjita mūrjagadjanmādi kāraṇa muktidāyaka<br/>svaudaradòl̤iṭṭu èllaranu saṃtaipa sarvajña||2||

kārya kāraṇa kartṛgal̤òl̤u svabhāryariṃda òḍagūḍi<br/>kapilācārya krīḍisutippa tannòl̤u tānè sveccèyali<br/>preryanu alle ramābja bhavaru ārya rakṣisi śikṣisuvanu<br/>svavīryadiṃdali divija dānava tatiya dinadinadi||3||

ī samasta jagattinòl̤agè ākāśadòl̤u irutippa<br/>vyāptāveśa avatārāṃtarātmakanāgi paramātma<br/>nāśa rahita jagattinòl̤agè avakāśadanu tānāgi<br/>yogīśāśaya sthita tannòl̤u èllaraniṭṭu salahuvanu||4||

dāru pāṣāṇagata pāvaka berè berè ippaṃtè<br/>kāraṇa kāryagal̤a òl̤agiddu kāraṇa kāryanu èṃdènisi<br/>torikòl̤l̤adè èllaròl̤u vyāpāra māḍuva<br/>yogyatègal̤a anusāra phalagal̤a uṇisi saṃtaisuva kṛpāsāṃdra||5||

ūrmigal̤òl̤ippa karma vikarma janya phala aphalaṃgal̤a<br/>nirmalātmanu māḍi māḍisi uṃḍu uṇisutippa<br/>nirmama nirāmaya nirāśraya dharmavitu dharmātma dharmaga<br/>durmatī janara òllanu apratimalla śrīnalla||6||

jalada vaḍabānal̤agal̤u aṃbudhi jalavanu uṃbuvavu<br/>abda mal̤ègarèdil̤igè śāṃtiyanīvudu analanu tāne bhuṃjipudu<br/>til̤ivudu ī pariyalli lakṣmī nilaya guṇa kṛta karmaja<br/>phalāphalagal̤u uṃḍu uṇisuvanu sāravāga sarva jīvarigè||7||

pustakagal̤a avalokisuta maṃtra stutigal̤a analenu<br/>raviya udayāstamaya paryaṃta japagal̤a māḍi phalavenu<br/>hṛstha paramātmanè samasta avasthègal̤òl̤iddu èllaròl̤agè<br/>nirastakāmanu māḍi māḍipanu èṃdu til̤iyadava||8||

madya bhāṃḍava deva nadiyòl̤agè addi tòl̤èyalu nityadali<br/>pariśuddhavu ahudè èṃdigādaru<br/>hari padābjagal̤a buddhi pūrvaka bhajisadavagè viruddhavu ènisuvavèlla<br/>karma samṛddhigal̤u duḥkhavanè kòḍutihavu adhama jīvarigè||9||

bhakti pūrvakavāgi muktāmukta niyāmakana<br/>sarvodrikta mahimègal̤a anavarata kòṃḍāḍu marèyadalè<br/>saktanāgadè lokavārtè prasaktigal̤anu īḍāḍi<br/>śṛti smṛti ukta karmava māḍutiru hariyājñèyèṃdaridu||10||

lopavādaru sariye karmaja pāpapuṇyagal̤u èraḍu ninnanu lepisavu<br/>niṣkāmakanu nīnāgi māḍutirè<br/>sauparaṇi varavahana ninna mahāparādhagal̤a èṇisadalè<br/>svargāpavargava kòṭṭu salahuva satata sukhasāṃdra||11||

svarata sukhamaya sulabha viśvaṃbhara viṣoka surāsurārcita caraṇa yuga<br/>cāru aṃga śārnga śaraṇya jitamanyu<br/>parama suṃdara tara parātpara śaraṇa jana suradhenu śāśvata karuṇi<br/>kaṃjadal̤ākṣakāyènè kaṃgòl̤ipa śīghra||12||

nirmamanu nīnāgi karma vikarmagal̤u niraṃtaradi<br/>sudharma nāmakagè arpisuta niṣkaluṣa nīnāgu<br/>bharma garbhana janaka dayadali durmatigal̤anu kòḍadè<br/>tannaya harmyadòl̤agiṭṭu èlla kāladi kāva kṛpèyiṃda||13||

kalpakalpadi śaraṇa jana varakalpavṛkṣanu<br/>tanna nija saṃkalpada anusāradali kòḍutippanu phalāphalava<br/>alpasukhada apekṣèyiṃda ahitalpana ārādhisadiru èṃdigu<br/>śilpagana kai sikka śilèyaṃdadali saṃtaipa||14||

deśa bheda ākāśadaṃdadi vāsudevanu sarva bhūta nivāsiyènisi<br/>carācarātmakanu èṃdu karèsuvanu<br/>dveṣa sneha udāsīnagal̤illa ī śarīragal̤òl̤agè<br/>avara upāsanagal̤aṃdadali phalavīvanu parabrahmha||15||

saṃcitāgāmigal̤a karma viriṃci janakana bhajisè kèḍuvavu<br/>miṃcinaṃdadi pòl̤èva puruṣottama hṛdaya aṃbaradi<br/>vaṃcisuva janaròlla śrīvatsāṃcita susadvakṣa<br/>tā niṣkiṃcana priya suramunigeya śubhakāya||16||

kāladravya sukarma śuddhiya pel̤uvaru alparigè<br/>avu nirmūla gaisuvavu alla pāpagal̤a èlla kāladali<br/>taila dhārīyaṃta avana pada olaisi tutisadalè nityadi<br/>bāliṣaru karmagal̤è tārakavèṃdu pel̤uvaru||17||

kamalasaṃbhava śarva śakrādi amararèllaru<br/>ivana duratikrama mahimègal̤a manavacanadiṃ prāṃtagāṇadalè<br/>śramitarāgi padābja kalpadrumada nèl̤ala āśrayisi<br/>lakṣmī ramaṇa saṃtaisèṃdu prārthiparu ati bhakutiyiṃda||18||

vāricaravu ènisuvavu dardura tārakagal̤èṃdaridu<br/>bhekavanu eri jaladhiya dāṭuvènèṃbuvana tèradaṃtè<br/>tāratamya jñāna śūnyaru sūrigamyana til̤iyalariyadè<br/>sauraśaiva matānugara anusarisi kèḍutiharu||19||

kṣoṇipati sutanènisi kaiduggāṇigòḍḍuva tèradi<br/>sumanasa dhenu manèyòl̤agiralu gomaya bayasuvaṃdadali<br/>veṇugānapriyana ahika sukhānubhava beḍadalè<br/>lakṣmī prāṇanāthana pāda bhakutiya beḍu kòṃḍāḍu||20||

kṣudhèya gosuga pogi kānana badari phalagal̤a apekṣèyiṃdali<br/>pòdèyòl̤agè siga biddu bāydèrèdavana tèradaṃtè<br/>vidhipitana pūjisadè ninnaya udara gosuga<br/>sādhuliṃga pradarśakara ārādhisuta bal̤aladiru bhavadòl̤agè||21||

jñāna jñeya jñātṛvèṃba abhidhānadiṃ buddhādigal̤a adhiṣṭhānadali nèlèsiddu<br/>karèsuta tattadāhvayadi<br/>bhānumaṃḍalaga pradarśaka tānènisi vaśanāguvanu<br/>śuka śaunakādi munīṃdra hṛdayākāśagata caṃdra||22||

udaya vyāpisi darśa paurṇima adhika yāmavu śravaṇa abhijitu<br/>sadanavaidirè māl̤pa tèradaṃdadali harisevè<br/>vidhi niṣedhagal̤u enu noḍadè vidhisutiru<br/>nityadali tannaya sadanadòl̤agiṃbiṭṭu salahuva bhaktavatsalanu||23||

naṃdivāhana rātri sādhanè baṃda dvādaśi paitṛka saṃdhisiha samayadali<br/>śravaṇava tyajisuvaṃtè sadā<br/>niṃdyariṃdali baṃda dravyava kaṇdèrèdu noḍadalè<br/>śrīmadānaṃdatīrtha aṃtargatana sarvatra bhajisutiru||24||

śrī manorama meru trikakuddhāma satkalyāṇaguṇa nissīma<br/>pāvananāma divijoddhāma raghurāma<br/>premapūrvaka nitya tanna mahā mahimègal̤a tutisuvarigè<br/>sudhāmagè òlidaṃdadali akhil̤ārthagal̤a kòḍutippa||25||

taṃdè tāygal̤a kuruhanariyada kaṃda deśāṃtaradòl̤agè tannaṃdadali<br/>ippavara jananī janakaranu kaṃḍu<br/>hiṃdèyènnanu paḍèda avaru ī yaṃdadalipparo ala<br/>nānu avara èṃdu kāṇuvènènuta huḍukuva tèradi kovidaru||26||

śruti purāṇa samūhadòl̤u bhārata prati prati padagal̤òl̤u<br/>nirjitana guṇa rūpagal̤a puḍukuta parama haruṣadali<br/>matimataru pratidivasa sārasvata samudradi<br/>śaphariyaṃdadi satata saṃcarisuvaru kāṇuva lavalavikèyiṃda||27||

matsyaketana janaka hari śrīvatsa lāṃchana nija śaraṇa janavatsala<br/>varāroha vaikuṃṭhaālaya nivāsi<br/>citsukhaprada salahènalu govatsa dhvanigòdaguva tèradi<br/>paramotsāhadi baṃdòdagavunu nirmatsarara bal̤igè||28||

sūrigal̤igè samīpaga durācārigal̤igè èṃdèṃdu dūrāddūratara<br/>durlabhanu ènisuvanu daitya saṃtatigè<br/>sāri sārigè nènèvavara saṃsāravèṃba mahoragakè sarvāriyènisi<br/>sadā susaukhyavanu īva śaraṇarigè||29||

cakra śaṃkha gadābjadhara duratikrama durāvāsa<br/>vidhi śiva śakra sūryādya amara pūjya padābja nirlajja<br/>śukra śiṣyara aśvamedhā prakriyava kèḍisi<br/>abjajāṃḍava atikramisi jāhnaviya paḍèda trivikramāhvayanu||30||

śaktarènisuvarèlla hari vyatirikta suragaṇadòl̤agè<br/>sarvodriktanu ènisuva sarvariṃdali sarva kāladali<br/>bhakti pūrvakavāgi anya prasaktigal̤a nīḍāḍi<br/>paramāsaktanu āgiru harikathāmṛta pāna viṣayadali||31||

praṇata kāmada bhakta ciṃtāmaṇi maṇimayābharaṇa bhūṣita<br/>guṇi guṇa traya dūra varjita gahana sanmahima<br/>èṇisa bhaktara doṣagal̤a kuṃbhiṇijèyāṇma śaraṇya<br/>rāmārpaṇavènalu kaikòṃḍa śabariya phalava paramātma||32||

ballènèṃbuvarillavu ītana òllènèṃbuvarilla<br/>lokadòl̤illadiha sthal̤avillavai ajñāta janarilla<br/>bèlladaccina bòṃbèyaṃdadi èllaròl̤agirutippa<br/>śrībhū nalla ivagè èṇèyilla apratimalla jagakèlla||33||

śabda gocara śārvarīkara abda vāhanananuja<br/>yaduvaṃśābdhi caṃdrama nirupama sunissīma samitasama<br/>labdhanāguva tannavagè prārabdha karmagal̤a uṇisi tīvradi<br/>kṣubdha pāvakanaṃtè biḍadippanu dayāsāṃdra||34||

śrī viriṃci ādi amara vaṃdita ī vasuṃdharèyòl̤agè<br/>devaki devi jaṭharadòl̤u avatarisidanu ajanu nararaṃtè<br/>revatī ramaṇa anujanu svapadāvalaṃbigal̤anu salahi<br/>daityāval̤iya saṃharisida jagannātha viṭhalanu||35||
