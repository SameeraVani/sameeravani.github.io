---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಗುಣತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಸಂಧಿ / Guna Taratamya sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè

parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

śrīdharā durgā manorama vedhamukha sumanasa gaṇa samārādhita padāṃbuja

jagadaṃtar bahirvyāpta

godhara phaṇipa varātapatra niṣedha śeṣa vicitra karma

subodha sukhamaya gātra parama pavitra sucaritra||1||

nitya nirmala nigama vedaya utpatti sthiti laya dūravarjita

stutya pūjya prasiddha muktāmukta gaṇa sevya

satyakāma saśaraṇya śāśvata bhṛtyavatsala bhaya nivāraṇa

atyadhika saṃpriyatama jagannātha māṃ pāhi||2||

parama puruṣana rūpa guṇava anusarisi kāṃbal̤u pravahadaṃdadi

nirupamal̤u nirduṣṭa sukha saṃpūrṇal̤u ènisuval̤u

harigè dhāmatrayal̤u ènisi ābharaṇa vasana āyudhagal̤u āgiddu

arigal̤anu saṃharisuval̤u akṣaral̤u ènisikòṃḍu||3||

ītagiṃta anaṃta guṇadali śrī taruṇi tā kaḍimèyènipal̤u

nityamuktal̤u nirvikāral̤u triguṇa varjital̤u

dhauta pāpa viriṃci pavanara mātèyènipa mahālakumi

vikhyātal̤u āgihal̤u èlla kāladi śruti purāṇadòl̤u||4||

kamala saṃbhava pavanarīrvaru samaru samavartigal̤u

rudrādi amaragaṇa sevitaru aparabrahma nāmakaru

yamal̤arigè mahālakṣmi tānuttamal̤u koṭi sajāti guṇadiṃda

amita suvijāti adhamaru èniparu brahma vāyugal̤u||5||

patigal̤iṃda sarasvatī bhāratigal̤u adhamaru nūru guṇa parimita vijāti avararu

bala jñānādi guṇadiṃda atiśayaru vāgdevi śrī bhāratigè

padaprayukta vidhi mārutaravol̤ ciṃtipudu

sadbhaktiyali kovidaru||6||

khagapa phaṇipati mruḍaru sama vāṇigè śataguṇa avararu mūvaru

migilènisuvanu śeṣa padadiṃdali triyaṃbakagè

nagadharana ṣaṇma hiṣiyaru pannaga vibhūṣaṇagaidu

menaki magal̤u vāruṇi sauparṇigal̤igè adhikavu èraḍu guṇa||7||

garuḍa śeṣa maheṣarigè sauparaṇi vāruṇi pārvati mūraru daśādhama

vāruṇigè kaḍimè ènisuval̤u gaurī

harana maḍadigè hattu guṇadali surapa kāmaru kaḍimè

iṃdragè kòratèyènisuva manmathanu padadiṃdali āvāga||8||

īraidu guṇa kaḍimè ahaṃkārika prāṇanu manoja nagārigal̤igè

aniruddha rati manu dakṣa guru śaciyu āru jana sama

prāṇaniṃdali hauraga èniparu hattu guṇadali

mārajādyarigè aidu guṇadiṃda adhama pravahākhya||9||

guṇadvayadiṃ kaḍimè pravahagè ina śaśāṃkayama svayaṃbhuva manu maḍadi śatarūpa

nālvaru pāda pādārdha vanadhi nīca

pādārdha nārada munigè bhṛgu agni prasūtigal̤u

ènisuvaru pādārdha guṇadiṃda adhamarahudèṃdu||10||

hutavahagè dviguṇa adhamaru vidhisuta marīcādigal̤u

vaivasvatanu viśvāmitrarigè kiṃcid guṇādhamanu

vrativara jaganmitra vara nirṛti prāvahi tārarigè

kiṃcit guṇa adhama dhanapa viśvaksenaru ènisuvaru||11||

dhanapa viśvaksena gaurī tanayarigè ukta itararu samarènisuvaru

èṃbhattaidu jana śeṣa śatarèṃdu

dinaparāru el̤adhika nālvattanilaru el̤u vasu

rudrarīraidu anitu viśve deva ṛbhu aśvinī pitṛ dharaṇī||12||

ivarigiṃtali kòratèyèniparu cyavana sanakādigal̤u

pāvaka kavi uciththya jayaṃta kaśyapa manugal̤u èkadaśa

dhruva nahuṣa śaśibiṃdu haihaya dauṣyaṃti virocanana nija kuvara

bali mòdalāda sapta iṃdraru kakutstha gaya||13||

pṛthu bharata māṃdhāta priyavrata maruta prahlāda suparīkṣita

hariścaṃdra aṃbarīṣa uttanapāda mukha

śata supuṇya ślokaru gadā bhrutagè adhiṣṭhānaru

supriyavratagè dviguṇa adhamaru karmajaru èṃdu karèsuvaru||14||

nal̤ini saṃjñā rohiṇī śyāmala virāṭ parjanyaru adhamaru

yalaru mitrana maḍadi dviguṇa adhamal̤u bāṃbòl̤agè

jala maya budha adhamanu dviguṇadi kèl̤agènisuval̤u uṣā

śanaiścaral̤igè īru guṇādhamaru uṣā devi dyasiyiṃdā||15||

èraḍu guṇa karmādhipati puṣkara kaḍimè

ājānu divijaru cira pitṛgal̤iṃda uttamaru kiṃkararu puṣkaragè

surapanālaya gāyaka uttama èraḍaidu guṇadiṃdādhama

tuṃburagè sama nūrukoṭi ṛṣigal̤u nūrujanarul̤idu||16||

avaravara patniyaru apsara yuvatiyaru sama

uttamaranul̤idu avararèniparu manuja gaṃdharvaru dviṣaḍguṇadi

kuvalayādhiparu īru aidu guṇa avanipa strīyaru

daśottara navati guṇadiṃda adhamaru èniparu mānuṣottamaru||17||

satvasatvaru satvarājasa satvatāmasa mūvaru

rajassatva adhikārigal̤u bhagavadbhaktaru ènisuvaru

nitya baddharu rajorajaru utpatti bhūsvargadòl̤u

narakadi pṛthviyòl̤u saṃcarisutipparu rajastāmasaru||18||

tamassātvikaru ènisikòṃbaru amitana ākhyāta asurara gaṇa

tamo rājasaru ènisikòṃbaru daitya samudāya

tamastāmasa kali puraṃdhriyu amita durguṇa pūrṇa

sarvādhamaròl̤u adhamādhama durātmanu kaliyènisikòṃba||19||

ivana poluva pāpi jīvaru bhuvana mūvaròl̤illa noḍalu

nava vidha dveṣagal̤igè ākāranu ènisikòl̤utippa

bavaradòl̤u baṃgāradòl̤u naṭa yuvati dyūtā peya mṛṣadòl̤u

kavisi mohadi kèḍisuvanuyèṃdaridu tyajisuvadu||20||

trividha jīva pratatigal̤a sagga āvòl̤èyāṇma ālayanu nirmisi

yuvatiyaròḍagūḍi krīḍisuvanu kṛpāsāṃdra

divija dānava tāratamyada vivara til̤iva mahātmarigè

bānnavira sakha tānòlidu uddharisuvanu dayadiṃda||21||

deva daityara tāratamyavu pāvamāni matānugarigè idu kevala avaśyakavu

til̤ivudu sarva kāladali

dāvaśikhi pāpāṭavigè nava nāvèyènipudu bhava samudrakè

pāvaṭigè vaikuṃṭha lokakè idèṃdu karèsuvudu||22||

tāratamya jñāna mukti dvāravu ènipudu bhakta janarigè tori pel̤i

sukhābdhiyòl̤u lolyāḍuvudu budharu

krūra mānavarigidu karṇa kaṭoravu ènipudu

nityadali adhikārigal̤igidana arupuvudu dustarkigal̤a biṭṭu||23||

harisiriviriṃci īrabhārati garuḍa phaṇi pati ṣaṇmahiṣiyaru

girijanāka īśa smara prāṇa aniruddha śacīdevī

guru ratī manu dakṣa pravahā maruta mānavi yama śaśi divākara

varuṇa nārada surāsya prasūti bhṛgu munipa||24||

vratatijāsana putrarènisuva vrativara marīci atri

vaivasvatanu tārā mitra nirṛti pravaha mārutana sati

dhana īśa aśvinigal̤a īrgaṇapatiyu viśvaksena śeṣanu śataru

manugal̤u ucithya chāvaṇa munigal̤igè namipè||25||

śata supuṇya ślokaru ènisuva kṣitiparigè namisuvènu

bāgīrathi virāṭ parjanya rohiṇi śyāmalā saṃjñāhuta vahana mahil̤ā

budha uṣā kṣiti śanaiścara puṣkararigè

ānatisi binnayisuvènu bhakti jñāna kòḍalèṃdu||26||

nūradhikavu āgippa mattè hadināru sāvira naṃda gopa kumārana

ardhāṃgiyaru agastya āde munīśvararu

ūrvaśī mòdalāda apsara nāriyaru śata tuṃburaru

kaṃsāri guṇagal̤a kīrtanèya māḍisali ènniṃda||27||

pāvanaru śuci śuddha nāmaka devatègal̤u ājāna cira pitṛ

deva nara gaṃdharvaru avanipa mānuṣottamaru

ī vasumatiyòl̤u ul̤l̤a vaiṣṇavara aval̤iyòl̤u ihanèṃdu

nityaḍi sevipudu saṃtoṣadiṃ sarva prakāradali||28||

mānuṣottamarana viḍidu caturānana aṃta śata uttamatva

krameṇa ciṃtipa bhaktarigè catura vidha puruṣārtha

śrīnidhi jagannātha viṭhala tāne òlidu īvanu

niraṃtara sānurāgadi paṭhisuvudu saṃtarida marèyadalè||29||
