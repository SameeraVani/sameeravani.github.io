title: "ವ್ಯಾಪ್ತಿ ಸಂಧಿ / Vyapthi sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè

parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

puruṣarūpatraya purāṇa puruṣa puruṣottama

kṣarākṣara puruṣa pūjita pāda pūrṇānaṃda jñānamaya

puruṣasūkta sumeya tattat puruṣa hṛtpuṣkara nilaya

mahāpuruṣa ajāṃḍa aṃtaradi bahiradi vyāpta nirlipta||1||

strī napuṃsaka puruṣa bhū salila anala gagana mana śaśi

bhānu kāla guṇa prakṛtiyòl̤agè òṃdu tānalla

enu ivana mahāmahimè kaḍègāṇaru ajabhava śakra mukharu

nidhānisalu mānavarigè al̤avaḍuvude vicārisalu||2||

gaṃdha rasarūpa sparśa śabda òṃdu tānalla

adaradara pèsariṃda karèsuta jīvarigè tarpakanu tānāgi

pòṃdikòṃḍiha parama karuṇāsiṃdhu śāśvata

manavè mòdalāda iṃdriyagal̤òl̤agè iddu bhogisutihanu viṣayagal̤a||3||

śravaṇa nayana ghrāṇa tvagrasana ivugal̤òl̤u

vākpāṇi pādādi avayavagal̤òl̤u tadguṇagal̤òl̤u tatpatigal̤òl̤agè

pravitatanu tānāgi kṛtipati vividha karmava māḍi māḍisi

bhavakè kāraṇanāgi tirugisutihanu til̤isadalè||4||

guṇiguṇagal̤òl̤agè iddu guṇiguṇanu ènisuvanu guṇabaddhanāgadè

guṇaja puṇyāpuṇya phala brahmādicetanakè uṇisuta avaròl̤agiddu

vṛjina ardana cidānaṃdaika dehavanu

kònègè sacarācāra jagadbhukuvènipanu avyayanu||5||

vidyè tānènèsikòṃba aniruddhadevanu

sarvajīvara buddhiyali tāniddu kṛtipati buddhiyènisuvanu

siddhiyènisuva saṃkaruṣana prasiddhanāmaka vāsudeva

anavadya rūpa catuṣṭayagal̤a aritavanè paṃḍitanu||6||

tanucatuṣṭayagal̤òl̤u nārāyaṇanu hṛtkamalākhya siṃhāsanadòl̤u

aniruddhādi rūpagal̤iṃda śobhisuta

tanagè tāne sevya sevakanènèsi sevāsakta suraròl̤agè

anavarata nèlèsiddu sevèya kòṃbanavaraṃtè||7||

jāgara svapnaṃgal̤òl̤u varabhogīśayananu bahu prakāra vibhāgagaisi

niraṃśajīvara citśarīravanu bhogavittu

suṣuptikāladi sāgarava nadi kūḍuvaṃtè

viyogarahitanu aṃśagal̤anu ekatravaidisuva||8||

bhāryariṃdòḍagūḍi kāraṇakārya vastugal̤alli

prèrakaprerya rūpagal̤iṃda paṭataṃtugal̤avòl̤iddu

sūrya kiraṇagal̤aṃtè tannaya vīryadiṃdali kòḍuta kòl̤utiha

anāryarigè ītana vihāravu gocaripudeno||9||

janaka tanna ātmajagè vara bhūṣaṇadukūlava tòḍisi

tā vaṃdanèya kaikòl̤uta harasuta haruṣabaḍuvaṃtè

vanaruhekṣaṇa pūjya pūjakanu ènisi pūjāsādhana

padārthavanu tanagè tānāgi phalagal̤anīva bhajakarigè||10||

taṃdè bahu saṃbhramadi tannaya baṃdhu bal̤agava nèrahi

maduvèya naṃdanagè tā māḍi manèyòl̤agiḍuva tèradaṃtè

iṃdirādhava tanna iccayaliṃda guṇagal̤a cetanakè saṃbaṃdhagaisi

sukhāsukhātmaka saṃsṛtiyòl̤u iḍuva||11||

tṛṇa kṛta ālayadòl̤agè pogè saṃdaṇisi prati chidradali pòramaṭṭu

anal̤a niravanu tori toradalippa tèradaṃtè

vanajāṃḍadòl̤u akhil̤a jīvara tanuvina òl̤a hòragè iddu

kāṇisadè animiśeṣanu sakala karmava māl̤panu avaraṃtè||12||

pādapagal̤a aḍigè èrèyè salilavu todu kaṃbigal̤u ubbi

puṣpa svādu phalava īvaṃdadali sarveśvaranu

janara ārādhanèya kaikòṃḍu brahma bhavādigal̤a nāmadali phalavittu

ādarisuvanu tanna mahimèya toragòḍa janakè||13||

śṛtitatigal̤igè gocarisada apratima ajānaṃdātmanu acyuta vitata

viśvādhāra vidyādhīśa vidhi janaka

pratidivasa cetanaròl̤agè prākṛta puruṣanaṃdadali saṃcarisuta

niyamya niyāmakanu tānāgi saṃtaipa||14||

mana viṣayadòl̤agè irisi viṣayava manadòl̤agè nèlègòl̤isi

balu nūtanavu susamīcīnavidu upādeyavèṃdènisi

kanasilādaru tanna pādada nènèvanu īyadè

sarvaròl̤agiddu anubhavisuvanu sthūla viṣayava viśvanèṃdènisi||15||

todakanu tānāgi mana mòdalāda karaṇadòl̤u iddu viṣayava naiduvanu

nijapūrṇa sukhamaya grāhya grāhakanu

veda vedyanu til̤iyadavanopādi bhuṃjisuta

èllaròl̤agè āhlāda paḍuvanu bhaktavatsala bhāgyasaṃpanna||16||

nityanigamātīta nirguṇa bhṛtyavatsala bhayavināśana

satyakāma śaraṇya śyāmala komalāṃga sukhi

mattanaṃdadi martyara òl̤a hòragè ètta noḍalu suttuta ippanu

atyadhika saṃtṛpta trijagadvyāpta paramāpta||17||

pavi harinmaṇi vidrumada sacchavigal̤a aṃdadi

rājisuta mādhava niraṃtara mānava dānavaròl̤u iddu

trividha guṇa karma svabhāvava pavanamukha devāṃtarātmaka

divasa divasadi vyaktamāḍutali avaròl̤iddu uṇipa||18||

aṇu mahattinòl̤u ippa ghana paramaṇuvinòl̤u aḍagisuva

sūkṣmava muṇugisuva telisuva sthūlagal̤a avana māyavidu

danuja rākṣasaru èllaru ivanòl̤u munidu māḍuvudenu

ulūkhala ònakègal̤u dhānyagal̤a haṇivaṃdadali saṃharipa||19||

deva mānava dānavaru èṃdu ī vidhadali āvāgali ipparu

mūvaròl̤agè ivagè illa sneha udāsīna dveṣa

jīvara adhikāra anusāradali īva sukha saṃsāra duḥkhava

tā uṇadalè avaravarigè uṇisuva nirgatāśananu||20||

èlli kel̤idarè èlli noḍidarè èlli beḍidarè èlli nīḍidarè

èlli oḍidarè èlli āḍidarè alle irutihanu

ballidarigè ati ballidanu sariyilla ivagè alli noḍalu

khullamānavaròllanu apratimalla jagakèlla||21||

tapta lohavu nol̤pa janarigè sapta jihvava tèradi torpadu

lupta pāvaka loha kāṃbudu pūrvadopādi

saptavāhana nikhil̤a janaròl̤u vyāptanu ādudariṃda

sarvarū āptaru āgiharu èlla kāladi kaikòṃḍu||22||

vāridanu mal̤ègarèyè bèl̤èdiha bhūruhaṃgal̤u citra phalarasa

berè berè ippaṃtè bahuvidha jīvaròl̤agè iddu māramaṇanu

avaravara yogyatè mīradalè guṇakarmagal̤a anusāra naḍèsuva

devanigè vaiṣamyavèllihudo||23||

vārijāptana kiraṇa maṇigal̤a seri tattat varṇagal̤anu

vikāragaisadè nol̤parigè kaṃgòl̤isuvaṃdadali

māramaṇa lokatrayadòl̤u iha mūruvidha jīvaròl̤agè

iddu vihāramāḍuvanu avara yogyatè karmava anusarisi||24||

jalavanu apaharisuva ghal̤igè baṭṭalanu ul̤idu

jayaghaṃṭè kaipiḍidu èl̤èdu hòḍèvaṃdadali

saṃtata kartṛ tānāgi haladharānuja puṇya pāpada phalagal̤anu

devāsurara gaṇadòl̤u vibhāgava māḍi uṇisuta sākṣiyāgippa||25||

pòṃdikòṃḍiha sarvaròl̤u saṃbaṃdhavāgadè

sakalakarmava araṃdadali tā māḍimāḍipa tatphalagal̤uṇadè

kuṃdadè aṇumāhattu ènipa ghaṭamaṃdiradi sarvatra tuṃbiha

bāṃdal̤ada tèrèdaṃtè irutippanu ramāramaṇa||26||

kāda kabbiṇa hiḍidu baḍiyalu vedanèyu lohagal̤igè allavè

ādudenai anal̤agā vyathè enu māḍidaru

ādidevanu sarva jīvara kādukòṃḍihanu òl̤ahòragè

duḥkhādigal̤u saṃbaṃdhavāguvaveno cinmayagè||27||

mal̤ala manègal̤a māḍi makkal̤u kāladalāḍi modadi tul̤idu kèḍisuva tèradi

lakṣmīramaṇa lokagal̤a halavu bagèyali nirmisuva

niścalanu tānāgiddu salahuva

èlaruṇiyavol̤ nuṃguvagè èllihudo sukha duḥkha||28||

veṣabhāṣègal̤iṃda janara pramoṣagaisuva naṭapuruṣanol̤

doṣadūranu lokadòl̤u bahurūpa mātinali toṣisuvanu

avaravara manada abhilāṣègal̤a pūraisuta anudina poṣisuva

pūtātma pūrṇa ānaṃda jñānamaya||29||

adhama mānavanu orva maṃtrauṣadhagal̤anu tānaritu

pāvakodakagal̤a saṃbaṃdhavilladalippanu adaròl̤agè

padumaja aṃḍa udaranu sarvara hṛdayadòl̤agè irè

kālaguṇakarmada kaluṣa saṃbaṃdhavāguvude niraṃjanagè||30||

òṃdu guṇadòl̤u anaṃta guṇagal̤u òṃdu rūpadòl̤u ihavu

lokagal̤a òṃde rūpadi dharisi tadgata padārthada òl̤a hòragè

bāṃdal̤adòl̤iddu bahu pèsariṃda karèsuta

pūrṇa jnānānaṃdamaya paripari vihārava māḍi māḍisuva||31||

èllaròl̤u tānippa tannòl̤agè èllaranu dharisihanu

apratimalla manmathajanaka jagada ādi aṃta madhyagal̤a balla

bahuguṇa bharita dānava dallaṇa jagannātha viṭhala

sòllu lālisi staṃbhadiṃdali baṃda bhakutanigè||32||
