---
title: "ಕರುಣಾ ಸಂಧಿ / Karuna sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè<br/>parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

śravaṇa manakānaṃdavīvudu bhavajanita duḥkhagal̤a kal̤èvudu<br/>vividha bhogagal̤u ihaparaṃgal̤ali ittu salahuvudu<br/>bhuvana pāvanavènipa lakṣmī dhavana maṃgal̤a kathèya<br/>parama utsavadi kivigòṭṭu ālipudu bhūsuraru dinadinadi||1||

mal̤èya nīru oṇiyòl̤u pariyalu, bal̤asaru ūròl̤agè idda janaru<br/>ā jalavu hèddòrègūḍè majjanapāna gaidaparu<br/>kaluṣa vacanagal̤a ādaḍèyu, bāṃbòl̤èya pèttana pāda mahima<br/>ā jaladi pòkkadariṃda māṇdaparè mahīsuraru||2||

śṛtitatigal̤a abhimāni lakṣmīstutigal̤igè gocarisada<br/>apratihata mahaiśvaryādi akhila sadguṇa gaṇāṃbhodhi<br/>pratidivasa tannaṃghri sevārata mahātmaru māḍutiha<br/>saṃstutigèvaśanāguvènu ivana kāruṇyakè enèṃbè||3||

manavacanakè atidūra nènèvaranu anusarisi tiruguvanu jāhnavi janaka<br/>janaròl̤agiddu janisuva jagadudara tānu<br/>ghanamahima gāṃgeyanuta gāyanava kel̤uta<br/>gaganacara vāhana divaukasaròḍanè carisuva manèmanègal̤alli||4||

malagi paramādaradi pāḍalu kul̤itu kel̤uva<br/>kul̤itu pāḍalu niluva niṃtarè naliva nalidarè òliva nimagèṃba<br/>sulabhano hari tannavaranu araghal̤igè biṭṭagalanu<br/>ramādhavana òlisalariyadè pāmararu bal̤aluvaru bhavadòl̤agè||5||

manadòl̤agè tāniddu manavèṃdu ènisikòṃbanu<br/>manada vṛttigal̤a anusarisi bhogaṃgal̤īvanu trividha cetanakè<br/>manavittarè tannanīvanu tanuva daṃḍisi dinadinadi sādhanava māl̤parigè<br/>ittapanu svargādi bhogagal̤a||6||

parama satpuruṣārtharūpavanu hariyu lokakè èṃdu<br/>paramādaradi sadupāsanèya gaivarigè ittapanu tanna<br/>marèdu dharmārthagal̤a kāmisuvarigè naguta atiśīghradiṃdali<br/>surapatanaya suyodhanarigè ittaṃtè kòḍutippa ||7||

jagavanèllava nirmisuva nālmòganòl̤agè tāniddu salahuva<br/>gaganakeśanòl̤iddu saṃharisuvanu lokagal̤a<br/>svagatabheda vivarjitanu sarvaga sadānaṃdaika dehanu<br/>bagèbagèya nāmadali karèsuva bhakutaranu pòrèva||8||

òbbanali niṃdāḍuvanu mattòbbanali noḍuvanu<br/>beḍuvanu òbbanali nīḍuvanu mātāḍuvanu bèragāgi<br/>abbarada hèddaivanu iva mattòbbarana lèkkisanu<br/>lokadòl̤u òbbane tā bādhya bādhakanāha nirbhīta||9||<br/>śaraṇajana maṃdāra śāśvata karuṇi kamalākāṃta kāmada<br/>parama pāvanatara sumaṃgal̤a carita pārthasakha<br/>nirupamānaṃdātma nirgata durita devavareṇyanèṃdu<br/>ādaradi karèyalu baṃdòdaguvanu tannavara bal̤igè||10||

jananiyanu kāṇadiha bālaka nènènènadu halubutirè<br/>kattalè manèyòl̤u aḍagiddu avana noḍuta naguta haruṣadali<br/>tanayanaṃ bigidappi raṃbisi kanalikèya kal̤èvaṃtè<br/>madhusūdananu tannavaru iddèḍègè baṃdòdagi salahuvanu||11||

iṭṭikallanu bhakutiyiṃdali kòṭṭa bhakutagè mècci taṇṇanè kòṭṭa<br/>baḍabrāhmaṇana òppiḍiyavaligè akhil̤ārtha<br/>kèṭṭa mātugal̤èṃda caidyana pòṭṭèyòl̤agiṃbiṭṭa<br/>bāṇadaliṭṭa bhīṣmana avaguṇagal̤èṇisidanè karuṇāl̤u||12||

dhanava saṃrakṣisuva phaṇi tānuṇadè mattòbbarigè kòḍadè<br/>anudinadi noḍuta sukhisuvaṃdadi<br/>lakumivallabhanu praṇataranu kāydihanu niṣkāmanadi<br/>nityānaṃdamaya durjanara sevèyanu òllanu apratimalla jagakèlla||13||

bālakana kalabhāṣè janani kel̤i sukhapaḍuvaṃtè<br/>lakṣmīlola bhaktaru māḍutiha saṃstutigè higguvanu<br/>tāl̤a tannavaralli māḍva avahel̤anava<br/>hèddaiva vidurana ālayadi pāluṃḍu kurupana mānavanè kòṃḍa||14||

smarisuvavara aparādhagal̤a tāsmarisa sakala iṣṭa pradāyaka<br/>maral̤i tanagè arpisalu kòṭṭuda anaṃtamaḍi māḍi paripariyaliṃda uṇisi<br/>sukha sāgaradi lolāḍisuva maṃgal̤acarita<br/>cinmayagātra lokapavitra sucaritra||15||

enu karuṇanidhiyo hari mattenu bhaktādhīnano<br/>innenu ītana līlè iccāmātradali jagava tāne sṛjisuva pālisuva<br/>nirvāṇa mòdalāda akhila lokasthānadali<br/>mattè avaranu iṭṭu ānaṃda baḍisuvanu||16||

janapa mèccidarè īva dhanavāhana vibhūṣaṇa vasanabhūmi<br/>tanumanagal̤a ittu ādariparu uṃṭeno lokadòl̤u<br/>anavarata nènèvavara anaṃtāsanavè mòdalāda ālayadòl̤iṭṭu<br/>aṇuganaṃdadali avara vaśanāguva mahāmahima||17||

bhuvana pāvana carita puṇyaśravaṇakīrtana pāpanāśana<br/>kavibhirīḍita kairavadal̤aśyāma nissīma<br/>yuvativeṣadi hiṃdè gaurīdhavana mohini kèḍisi ul̤isida<br/>ivana māyava gèluvanāvanu ī jagatrayadi||18||

pāpakarmava sahisuvaḍè lakṣmīpatigè samarāda divijaranu<br/>ī payojabhavāṃḍadòl̤agè āvalli nā kāṇè<br/>gòpaguruvina maḍadibhṛgunagacāpa mòdalādavaru māḍda<br/>mahāparādhagal̤a èṇisidanè karuṇā samudra hari||19||

aṃguṭāgradi janisida amarataraṃgiṇiyu lokatrayagal̤a aghahiṃgisuval̤u<br/>avyākṛtāśāṃta vyāpisida iṃgaḍala magal̤a òḍèyana<br/>aṃgopāṃgagal̤ali ippa<br/>amalānaṃta sumaṃgal̤aprada nāma pāvanamāl̤padenaridu||20||<br/>kāmadhenu sukalpataru ciṃtāmaṇigal̤u<br/>amareṃdra lokadi kāmitārthagal̤u īvavalladè sevè māl̤parigè<br/>śrīmukuṃdana parama maṃgal̤anāma narakastharanu salahitu<br/>pāmarara paṃḍitarènisi puruṣārtha kòḍutihudu||21||

manadòl̤agè suṃdara padārthava nènèdu kòḍè kaikòṃḍu<br/>balu nūtana suśobhita gaṃdha surasopeta phalarāśi<br/>dyunadi nivahagal̤aṃtè kòṭṭu avaranu sadā saṃtaisuvanu<br/>sadguṇava kaddavara aghava kadivanu anaghanèṃdènisi||22||

cetanā cetana vilakṣaṇa nūtana padārthagal̤òl̤agè balunūtana<br/>atisuṃdarakè suṃdara rasakè rasarūpa<br/>jātarūpodara bhava ādyaròl̤u ātata pratima prabhāva<br/>dharātal̤adòl̤u èmmòḍanè āḍutalippa nammappa||23||

taṃdè tāygal̤u tamma śiśuvigè baṃda bhayagal̤a pariharisi<br/>nija maṃdiradi beḍidudanu ittu ādarisuvaṃdadali<br/>hiṃdè muṃdè èḍabaladi òl̤ahòragè iṃdireśanu tannavaranu<br/>èṃdèṃdu salahuvanu āgasadòl̤ ètta noḍidaru||24||

òḍala nèl̤alaṃdadali hari nammòḍanè tiruguvanu<br/>òṃdarèkṣaṇa biḍadè bèṃbalavāgi bhaktādīnanèṃdènisi<br/>taḍèva duritaughagal̤a kāmada kòḍuva sakaleṣṭagal̤a<br/>saṃtata naḍèva nammaṃdadali navisu viśeṣa sanmahima||25||

biṭṭavara bhavapāśadiṃdali kaṭṭuvanu bahukaṭhiṇaniva<br/>śiṣṭeṣṭanèṃdaridu anavarata sadbhakti pāśadali kaṭṭuvara<br/>bhavakaṭṭu biḍisuva siṭṭinavanu ivanalla<br/>kāmada kòṭṭukāvanu sakala saukhyavanu ihaparaṃgagal̤ali ||26||

kaṇṇigè èvèyaṃdadali kai mai tiṇṇigòdaguva tèradi<br/>palgal̤u paṇṇu phalagal̤anagidu jihvègè rasavanīvaṃtè<br/>puṇya phalava īvaṃdadali nuḍivèṇṇi nāṇmāoḍadòl̤u<br/>lakṣmaṇana aṇṇanu òdaguva bhaktara avasarakè amaragaṇa sahita||27||

kòṭṭadanu kaikòṃba arèkṣaṇabiṭṭagala tannavara<br/>duritagal̤a aṭṭuvanu dūradali duritāraṇya pāvakanu<br/>bèṭṭa bènnili hòrisidavaròl̤u siṭṭu māḍidaneno hari<br/>kaṃgèṭṭa surarigè sudhèyanu uṇisida muridanahitaranā||28||

kheda moda jayāpājaya mòdalāda doṣagal̤illa cinmaya sādaradi<br/>tannaṃghrikamalava naṃbi stutisuvara kādukòṃḍiha<br/>paramakaruṇa mahodadhiyu tannavaru māḍda<br/>mahāparādhagal̤a noḍadalè salahuva sarvakāmadanu||29||

mīnakūrma varāha narapaṃcānana atul̤a śaurya<br/>vāmana reṇukātmaja rāvaṇādiniśācaradhvaṃsi<br/>dhenukāsuramathana tripurava hānigainisida nipuṇa<br/>kalimukha dānavara saṃharisi dharmadi kāyda sujanaranā||30||

śrī manorama śamala varjita kāmitaprada<br/>kairavadal̤a śyāma śabala śaraṇya śāśvata śarkarākṣa sakha<br/>sāmasannuta sakala guṇagaṇadhāma<br/>śrī jagannātha viṭhalanu ī mahiyòl̤u avatarisi salahida sakala sujanaranā||31||
