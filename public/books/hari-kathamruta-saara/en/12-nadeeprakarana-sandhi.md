---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ನಾಡೀಪ್ರಕರಣ ಸಂದಿ / Nadeeprakarana Sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè

parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

vāsudevanu prāṇamukha tatva īśariṃdali sevè kaikòl̤uta

ī śarīradòl̤u ippa mūvattāru sāvira ī sunāḍigal̤òl̤agè

ī bhūmī sameta vihāragaiva

para īśana amala sumūrtigal̤a ciṃtisuta higgutiru||1||

caraṇagal̤òl̤iha nāḍigal̤u hannèrèḍu sāvira madhya dehadòl̤u irutihavu hadinālku

bāhugal̤òl̤agè īrèrèḍu śiradòl̤u āru sahasra ciṃtisi

irul̤u hagalu abhimāni divijaranaritu

upāsanègaivaru il̤èyòl̤u svarga vāsigal̤u||2||

bṛhatināmaka vāsudevanu vahisi strī rūpa

doṣa virahita èppattèrèḍu sāvira nāḍigal̤òl̤agiddu

druhiṇa mòdalāda amaragaṇa sanmahima

sarva prāṇigal̤a mahāmahima saṃtaisuvanu saṃtata parama karuṇāl̤u||3||

nūru varṣakè divasa mūvattāru sāvira vahavu

nāḍi śarīradòl̤agè initihavu èṃdaritu òṃdu divasadali

sūrigal̤a satkarisidava prati vāradali daṃpatigal̤a arcanè tā racisidava

satya saṃśayavu illavèṃdèṃdu||4||

catura viṃśati tattvagal̤u tatpatigal̤u ènisuva

brahma mukha devatègal̤u anudina prati prati nāḍigal̤òl̤u irutiddu

catura daśa lokadòl̤u jīva pratatigal̤a saṃrakṣisuva

śāśvatana tattat sthānadali noḍutale modiparu||5||

satya saṃkalpanu sadā èppattèrèḍu sahasradòl̤u

mūvattu nālku lakṣada aivattāru sahasra

citprakrutiya òḍagūḍi paramanu nitya maṃgal̤amūrti

bhaktara tèttiganu tānāgi sarvatradali saṃtaipa||6||

maṇigal̤òl̤agiha sūtradaṃdadi praṇava pādyanu

sarva cetana gaṇadòl̤iddu anavarata saṃtaisuvanu tannavara

praṇata kāmada bhakta ciṃtāmaṇi cidānaṃdaika dehanu

aṇu mahadgatanu alparopādiyali nèlèsippa||7||

ī suṣumna ādi akhil̤a nāḍī kośa nābhī mūladali

vṛṣaṇā sadana madhyadalippadu tuṃdi nāmadali

ā sarojāsana mukharu mūleśana anaṃdādi suguṇa upāsanèya gaivutali

dehadòl̤agè irutiharu||8||

sūrigal̤u cittaisuvudu bhāgīrathiyè mòdalāda tīrthagal̤u

eradhika èppattu sāvira nāḍigal̤òl̤ihavu

ī rahasyavanu alpa janarigè tori pel̤adè

nāḍi nadiyòl̤u dhīraru anudina majjanava māḍutale sukhisuvaru||9||

til̤ivudu ī dehadòl̤agiha èḍa balada nāḍigal̤òl̤agè divijaru

jalaja saṃbhava vāyu vāṇi ādigal̤u baladalli

èlaruṇiga vihaga iṃdra cal̤ivèṭṭal̤iya ṣaṇmahiśiyaru

vāruṇi kuliśa dhara kāmādigal̤u èḍa bhāgadòl̤iharu||10||

ikkaladòl̤iha nāḍiyòl̤u devarkal̤iṃda òḍanāḍutali

pòṃbakki deranu jīvara adhikārānusāradali

takka sādhanava māḍi māḍisuta akkaradi saṃtaipa bhaktara

dakkagòḍanu asurargè satpuṇyagal̤anu apaharipa||11||

tuṃdiviḍidu āśirada pariyaṃta òṃdu vyāpisihudu

tāvara kaṃdanihanu adaròl̤agè adakè īraidu śākhègal̤u

òṃdadhika daśa karaṇadòl̤u saṃbaṃdhagaidihavalli

ravi śaśi siṃdhu nāsatyādigal̤u nèlègòṃḍiharu satata||12||

pòkkal̤adiviḍidu òṃde nāḍiyu sukkadalè dhārāl̤a rūpadi

sikkihudu ī dehadòl̤agè suṣumna nāmadali

rakkasaranu òl̤a pogagòḍadè daśa dikkinòl̤agè samīra devanu

lèkkisadè mattòbbaranu saṃcaripa dehadòl̤u||13||

initu nāḍī śākhègal̤u tanuvinòl̤agè ihavèṃdaridu ekātmanu

dvi saptati sāvira ātmakanu āgi nāḍiyòl̤u

vanitèyiṃda òḍagūḍi nārāyaṇa divā rātrigal̤ali

ī pari vanajāṃḍadòl̤u akhil̤a jīvaròl̤iddu mohisuva||14||

dinadinadi vardhisuva kumudāptana mayūkhada sòbaga

gata locana vilokisi moda paḍaballanè niraṃtaradi

kunaragè ī sukatha amṛtada bhojanada sukha dòrakuvude

lakṣmī manoharana sadguṇava kīrtipa bhakutagè alladalè||15||

ī tanuvinòl̤agihavu ota prota rūpadi nāḍigal̤u

puruhūta mukharu alliharu tammiṃda adhikāra òḍagūḍi

bhītigòl̤isuta dānavara saṃghāta nāmaka hariya guṇa

saṃprītiyali sadupāsanèya gaivutalè modiparu||16||

jalāṭa kukkāṭa khāṭa jīvara kal̤evaragal̤òl̤u iddu kāṇisikòl̤adè

tattadrūpa nāmagal̤iṃda karèsutali

jalaruhekṣaṇa vividha karmagal̤a anudinadi māḍi

tattatphalagal̤a uṇṇadè saṃcarisuvanu nitya sukha pūrṇa||17||

til̤idupāsanègaivuta īpari malinanaṃtiru durjanara kaṃgal̤igè gocarisadè

vipaścitara òḍavè garvisadè

mal̤è bisilu hasi tṛṣi jayājaya khal̤ara niṃdyāniṃdya bhayagal̤igè

al̤ukadalè maddānèyaṃdadi carisu dharèyòl̤agè||18||

kānana grāmastha sarva prāṇigal̤u prati divasadali

enenu māḍuva karmagal̤u hari pūjèyaṃdaridu denisuta

sadbhaktiyali pavamānavaṃdita devāṃtarātmaka

śrīnivāsanigè arpisuta modisuta nalivutiru||19||

nokanīyanu lokadòl̤u śunisūkarādigal̤òl̤agè nèlèsiddu

ekamevādvitīya bahurūpāhvayagal̤iṃda

tā karèsuta òl̤agiddu til̤isadè śrī kamalabhava mukhya sakala

divaukasagaṇa ārādhya kaikòṃḍu anavarata pòrèva||20||

initu upāsanègaivutiha sajjanaru saṃsāradali pratiprati dinagal̤ali

enenu māḍuvudu èlla haripūjè ènisikòṃbudu satyavu

ī mātinali saṃśayapaḍuva naranu alpanu suniścaya

bāhya karmava māḍi phalavenu||21||

bhogya bhokṭruga l̤òl̤agè hari tā bhogya bhoktanu ènisi

yogyāyogya rasagal̤a deva dānava gaṇakè uṇisuvanu

bhāgyanidhi bhaktarigè sadvairāgya bhakti jñānavīva

ayogyarigè dveṣādigal̤a tannalli kòḍutippa||22||

ī caturdaśa bhuvanadòl̤agè carācarātmaka jīvaralli

virocanātmaja vaṃcakanu nèlèsiddu dinadinadi

yācakanuyèṃdènisikòṃba marīcirdamana suhaṃsa rūpa

niṣecaka āhvayanu āgi janarabhilāṣè pūraipa||23||

annadannādannamaya svayaṃ anna brahmādi akhil̤a cetanakè

anna kalpakanāhanu aniruddhādi rūpadali

anyaranu apekṣisadè guṇa kāruṇya sāgara sṛṣṭisuvanu

hiraṇya garbhanòl̤iddu pālisuvanu jagatrayava||24||

tripada tridaśādhyakṣa tristha tripathagāmini pita trivikrama

kṛpaṇa vatsala kuvalaya dal̤a śyāma nissīma

aparimita citsukha guṇātmaka vapuṣa vaikuṃṭha ādi lokādhipa

trayīmaya tannavara niṣkapaṭadiṃ pòrèva||25||

lavaṇa miśrita jalavu torpadu lavaṇadopādiyali jihvègè

vivaragaisalu śakyavāguvadeno nol̤parigè

svavaśa vyāpiyènisi lakṣmīdhava carācaradòl̤agè tuṃbihanu

aviditana sākalya ballavaru āru suraròl̤agè||26||

vyāpyanaṃdadi sarvajīvaròl̤ippa cinmaya ghora bhava

saṃtapya mānaru bhajisè bhakutiyal̤iṃdali ihaparadi prāpyanāguvanu

avaravaguṇagal̤a òppugòṃbanu bhaktavatsala tappisuva

janmādi doṣagal̤a avarigè anavarata||27||

halavu bagèyali hariya manadali òlisi nillisi

enu māḍuva kèlasagal̤u avanaṃghri pūjègal̤u èṃdu nènèvutiru

haladharanuja tāne sarva sthal̤agal̤ali nèlèsiddu

niścaṃcala bhakuti sujñāna bhāgyava kòṭṭu saṃtaipa||28||

doṣa gaṃdha vidūra nānā veṣa dhāraka ī jagatraya poṣaka

purātana puruṣa puruhūta mukha vinuta

śeṣavara pariyaṃka śayana vibhīṣaṇa priya vijaya sakha

saṃtoṣabaḍisuva janarigè iṣṭārthagal̤a pūraisi||29||

śrī mahī sevita padāṃbuja bhūma sadbhakti aika labhya

pitāmahādi amarāsura pāda paṃkaja

vāma vāmana rāma saṃsāramaya auṣadha he mama kulasvāmi

saṃtaisènalu baṃdòdaguvanu karuṇāl̤u||30||

dunuja divijaròl̤iddu avaravara anusarisi karmagal̤a māl̤panu

janana maraṇādi akhil̤a doṣa vidūra

èmmòḍanè janisuvanu jīvisuva saṃrakṣaṇèya māḍuva èlla kāladi

dhanava kāydiha sarpanaṃtè animitta bāṃdhavanu||31||

bisaruha āpta āgasadi tānu udayisalu vṛkṣaṃgal̤a nèl̤alu pasarisuvavu

il̤èyòl̤u astamisalalla alle līnahavu

śvasana mukhya amarāṃtarātmakana òṣadòl̤u irutippavu

ī jagatraya basiròl̤u iṃbiṭṭu èlla karmava torpa nol̤parigè||32||

tribhuvana aika ārādhya lakṣmī subhuja yugal̤a ālaṃgitāṃga

svabhu sukhātma suvarṇavarṇa suparṇa varavāhana

abhayada anaṃtārka śaśi sannibha niraṃjana nityadali tanagè

abhinamisuvarigè eva sarvārthagal̤a taḍèyadalè||33||

kavigal̤iṃdali til̤idu prātaḥ savana madhyaṃ dinavu

sāyama savanagal̤a vasu rudraru ādityaròl̤u rājisuva

pavananòl̤u kṛti jaya sumāyādhavana mūrtitrayava ciṃtisi

divasavèṃba āhutigal̤iṃda arcisuta sukhisutiru||34||

catura viṃśati abdha vasu devatègal̤òl̤u pradyumnanu ippanu

catura catvāriṃśatigal̤ali saṃkaruṣaṇākhya

hutavaha akṣanòl̤ihanu māyapatiyu hadināradhika

dvātriṃśati varuṣagal̤alippanu ādityanòl̤u sitakāya||35||

ṣoḍaśa uttara śata varuṣadali ṣoḍaśa uttara śata surūpadi

krīḍisuva vasu rudraru ādityaròl̤u satisahita

vrīḍavilladè bhajipa bhaktara pīḍisuta durita aughagal̤a

dūra oḍisuta bal̤iyali biḍadè nèlèsippa bhayahāri||36||

mūradhika èṃbhattu sahasra ainūrippattu ènipa rūpadi

torutippa divā niśādhiparòl̤agè nityadali

bhāratī prāṇaròl̤agiddu nivārisuta bhaktara durita

hiṃkāra nidhana prathama rūpadi pitṛgal̤anè pòrèva||37||

buddhi pūrvaka uttamottama śuddha ūrṇa aṃbarava

paṃkadòl̤u aḍḍi tègèyalu lepavāguvade parīkṣisalu

padmanābhanu sarvajīvaròl̤iddarenu

guṇatrayagal̤iṃ baddhanu āguvaneno? nitya sukhātma cinmayanu||38||

sakala doṣa vidūra śaśi pāvaka sahasra anaṃta sūrya prakāra sannibha gātra

lakumi kal̤atra suramitra

vikhana sāṃḍadòl̤ippa brahmādi akhil̤a cetana gaṇakè

tāne sakhanu ènisikòṃḍu akuṭila ātmakanu ippanu avaraṃtè||39||

deśa bhedagal̤alli ippa ākāśadopādiyali cetana rāśiyòl̤u nèlèsippanu

avyavadhānadali niruta

śrī sahita sarvatradi niravākāśa kòḍuvaṃdadali kòḍuta

nirāśèyali sarvāṃtarātmaka śobhisuva sukhada||40||

śrī viriṃci ādi amaragaṇa saṃsevita aṃghri saroja

ī jaḍa jīva rāśigal̤a òl̤a hòragè nèlèsiddu nityadali

sāvakāśanu ènisi tanna kal̤evaradòl̤u iṃbiṭṭu salahuva

deva devakī ramaṇa dānava haraṇa jitāmaraṇa||41||

māsa òṃdakè prati divasadali śvāsagal̤u ahavu

aṣṭa catvāriṃśati sahasrādhika āru sulakṣa saṃkhyèyali

haṃsanāmaka hariya ṣoḍaśa ī śatābdadi bhajisè òliva

dayāsamudra kucelagè òlidaṃdadali dinadinadi||42||

sthūla dehadòl̤iddu varuṣakè el̤adhika èppattu lakṣada

melè èppattāru sāvira śvāsa japagal̤anu

gāl̤i devanu karuṇadali īrel̤u lokadòl̤ul̤l̤a

cetana jāladòl̤u māḍuvanu trijagadvyāpta paramāpta||43||

īrèrèḍu dehagal̤òl̤agiddu samīra devanu śvāsa japa

nānūru adhikavu āgippa èṃbhattāru sahasra tā racisuvanu divasa òṃdakè

mūru vidha jīvaròl̤agiddu

kharāri karuṇābalavu adèṃtuṭò pavana rāyanòl̤u||44||

śrīdhava jagannātha viṭhala tā dayadi vadanadòl̤u nuḍida upādiyali

nā nuḍidènu alladè kel̤i budha janaru

sādhu liṃga pradarśakaru niṣedhagaisidarè enahudu

ènnaparādhavu enidaròl̤agè pel̤vudu til̤idu kovidaru||45||
