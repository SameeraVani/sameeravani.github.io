---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಸ್ಥಾವರಜಂಗಮ ಸಂಧಿ/Sthavarajangama sandhi/ Dhyaana prakriyasandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè/

parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

pādukèya kaṃṭakasikta mòdalādavu anudina bādhisuvanè

ekādaśa iṃdriyagal̤ali biḍadè hṛṣīkapana mūrti sādaradi nènèvavanu

enaparādhagal̤a māḍidaru sariye

nirodhagaisavu mokṣamārgakè durita rāśigal̤u||1||

hagalu naṃdādīpadaṃdadi nigama vedyana pūjisuta

kai mugidu nālkaròl̤u òṃdu puruṣārthavanu beḍadale

jagadudara kòṭṭudanu bhuṃjisu maga maḍadi prāṇa iṃdriya ātmādigal̤u

bhagavadhīnavèṃdu aḍigaḍigè nènèvutiru||2||

asvataṃtranu jīva hari sarva svataṃtranu nitya sukhamaya

ni:sva baddha alpajña śakta saduḥkha nirviṇṇa

hrasva dehi sanāthajīvanu viśva vyāpaka kartṛ

brahma sarasvati īśādi amaranuta hariyèṃdu kòṃḍāḍu||3||

mattè viśvādi èṃṭu rūpa òṃbhattariṃdali pèccisalu

èppattèraḍu rūpagal̤u ahavu òṃdòṃdè sāhasra

pṛth pṛthaku nāḍigal̤òl̤agè sarvottamana til̤iyèṃdu

bhīṣmanu bittarisidanu dharma tanayagè śāṃti parvadali||4||

èṃṭu prakṛtigal̤òl̤agè viśvādi èṃṭu rūpadaliddu

bhaktara kaṃṭakava pariharisutali pālisuva pratidinadi

nèṃṭanaṃdadi èḍabiḍadè vaikuṃṭharamaṇanu

tannavara niṣkaṃṭaka sumārgadali naḍèsuva durjanara baḍiva||5||

svaramaṇanu śakti ādi rūpadi mānigal̤òl̤agè nèlèsiddu

aravidūranu sthūla viṣayagal̤a uṃḍuṇipa nitya

ariyadalè nānuṃbènu èṃbuva nirayagal̤a uṃbuvanu

niścaya maral̤i maral̤i bhavāṭaviya saṃcarisi bal̤aluvanu||6||

surucirucira sugaṃdha suciyèṃdu irutihanu ṣaḍrasagal̤òl̤u

hannèraḍu rūpadali ippa śrī bhū durgèyara sahita

svaramaṇanu èppattèraḍu sāvira samīrana rūpadòl̤agiddu

uruparākrama kartṛ ènisuva nāḍiyòl̤agiddu||7||

sarvatradali nènèvaranu anya karmava māḍidaru sari

puṇya karmagal̤u ènisuvavu saṃdehavinitilla

ninna smarisadè snāna japa homa anna vastra gaja aśva bhū dhana dhānya

mòdalāda akhil̤a dharmava māḍi phalavenu||8||

iṣṭa bhogya padārthadòl̤u śipiviṣṭa nāmadi sarva jīvara tuṣṭi baḍisuva

dinadinadi saṃtuṣṭa tānāgi

koṣṭadòl̤u nèlèsiddu rasayamaya puṣṭiyaidisuta iṃdriyagal̤òl̤u

preṣṭanāgiddu èlla viṣayagal̤a uṃba til̤isadalè||9||

kāraṇāhvaya jñāna karma prerakanu tānāgi kriyègal̤a toruvanu

karma iṃdriya adhiparòl̤agè nèlèsiddu

mūru guṇamaya dravyaga tadākāra tannāmadali karèsuva

torikòl̤l̤adè janara mohipa mohakalpakanu||10||

dravyanu ènisuva bhūta mātradòl̤u avyayanu

karma iṃdriyagal̤òl̤u bhavya satkriyanènipa jñāna iṃdriyagal̤òl̤agiddu

stavyakārakanu ènisi sukhamaya sevya sevakanu ènisi jagadòl̤u

havyavāhananu araṇiyòl̤agè ippaṃtè irutippa||11||

manave mòdalāda iṃdriyagal̤òl̤agè aniladevanu śuciyènisikòṃḍu

anavarata nèlèsippa śuciṣat hotanèṃdènisi tanuvinòl̤agippanu

sadā vāmana hṛṣīkeśākhya rūpadali

anubhavakè taṃdīva viṣayaja sukhava jīvarigè||12||

preraka preryaròl̤u prerya prerakanu tānāgi

hari nirvairadiṃda pravartisuva tannāma rūpadali

torikòl̤l̤adè sarvaròl̤u bhāgīrathī janakanu

sakala vyāpāragal̤a tā māḍi māḍisi noḍi nagutippa||13||

hariyè mukhya niyāmakanu èṃdaridu puṇyāpuṇya haruṣāharuṣa

lābhālābha sukhaduḥkhādi dvaṃdvagal̤a

niruta avaraṃghrigè samarpisi naraka bhū svargāpa vargadi

karaṇa niyāmakana sarvatradali nènèvutiru||14||

māṇavaka tatphalagal̤a anusaṃdhānavilladè

karmagal̤a sva iccānusāradi māḍi modisuvaṃtè

pratidinadi jñāna pūrvaka vidhi niṣedhagal̤u enu noḍadè māḍu

karma pradhāna puruṣeśanali bhakutiya māḍu kòṃḍāḍu||15||

hāni vṛddhi jayāpajayagal̤u enu kòṭṭuda bhuṃjisuta

lakṣmī nivāsana karuṇavanè saṃpādisu anudinadi

jñāna sukhamaya tannavara parama anurāgadi saṃtaipa

dehānubaṃdhigal̤aṃtè òl̤ahòragiddu karuṇāl̤u||16||

ā parama sakala iṃdriyagal̤òl̤u vyāpakanu tānāgi

viṣayava tā parigrahisuvanu til̤isadè sarvajīvaròl̤u

pāpa rahita purāṇa puruṣa samīpadali nèlèsiddu

nānā rūpaka dhāraka torikòl̤l̤adè karmagal̤a māl̤pa||17||

khecararu bhūcararu vāri niśācararòl̤iddu avara karmagal̤a

ācarisuvanu ghana mahima parama alpanopādi

gocarisu bahu prakāra ālocanèya māḍidaru manasigè

kīcakāri prīya kavijanageya maharāya||18||

òṃde gotra pravara saṃdhyāvaṃdanègal̤u māḍi

prāṃtakè taṃdè tanayaru berè tammaya pèsarugòṃbaotè

òṃde dehadòl̤iddu niṃdyāniṃdya karmava māḍi māḍisi

iṃdireśanu sarvajīvaròl̤u īśanènisuvanu||19||

kiṭṭagaṭṭida loha pāvaka suttu viṃgaḍa māḍuvaṃtè

gharaṭṭa vrīhigal̤u ippa taṃḍula kaḍègè tègèvaṃtè

viṭhalā èṃdòmmè maimarèdu aṭṭahāsadi karèyè

duritagal̤u aṭṭul̤iya biḍisèvana tannòl̤agiṭṭu salahuvanu||20||

jaladhiyòl̤agè sveccānusāradi jalacara prāṇigal̤u

tattat sthal̤agal̤ali saṃtoṣapaḍutali saṃcarisuvaṃtè

nallina nābhanòl̤u abja bhava muppòl̤ala urigamaigaṇṇa mòdalāda

halavu jīvaragaṇavu varisutihudu nityadali||21||

vāsudevanu òl̤ahòragè avakāśadanu tānāgi

biṃba prakāśisuva tadrūpa tannāmadali sarvatra

ī samanvayavèṃdènipa sadupāsanavagaivavanu

mokṣa anveṣigal̤òl̤uttamanu jīvanmuktana avaniyòl̤u||22||

bhogya vastugal̤òl̤agè yogyāyogya rasagal̤anaritu

yogyāyogyarali nèlèsippa harigè samarpisu anudinadi

bhāgya baḍatana baralu higgadè kuggi sòragadè

sadbhakti vairāgyagal̤anè māḍu nī nirbhāgyanènisadalè||23||

sthal̤a jalādrigal̤alli janisuva phala supuṣpaja gaṃdharasa

śrī tul̤asi mòdalāda akhil̤a pūjā sādhana padārtha

halavu bagèyiṃda arpisuta bāṃbòl̤èya janakagè

nityānityadi til̤ivudidu vyatireka pūjègal̤èṃdu kovidaru||24||

śrīkarana sarvatradali avalokisuta guṇa rūpa kriya

vyatireka til̤iyadalè anvayisu biṃbanali marèyadalè

svīkarisuvanu karuṇadiṃda nirākarisadè kṛpāl̤u

bhaktara śokagal̤a pariharisi sukhavittu anavarata pòrèva||25||

initu vyatireka anvayagal̤u èṃdènipa pūjā vidhigal̤anè til̤idu

animiṣa īśana tṛpti paḍisutaliru niraṃtaradi

ghana mahima kaikòṃḍu sthiti mṛti janumagal̤a pariharisi

sevaka janaròl̤iṭṭu ānaṃda paḍisuva bhaktavatsalanu||26||

jalajanābhanigè èraḍu pratimègal̤u il̤èyòl̤agè jaḍa cetanātmaka

caladòl̤u irbagè strī puruṣa bhedadali jaḍadòl̤agè til̤ivudu āhita pratimè

sahaja ācalagal̤u èṃdu irbagè pratīkadi

lalita paṃcatraya sugol̤akava aritu bhajisutiru||27||

vārijāsana vāyuvīṃdra umāramaṇa nākeśa samara ahaṃkārika

prāṇādigal̤u puruṣara kal̤evaradi

torikòl̤adè aniruddha doṣa vidūra nārāyaṇana rūpa

śarīramānigal̤u āgi bhajisuta sukhava kòḍutiharu||28||

siri sarasvati bhāratī sauparaṇī vāruṇi pārvatī mukharu irutiharu

strīyaròl̤agè abimānigal̤u tāvènisi

aruṇa varṇa nibhāṃga śrī saṃkaruṣaṇa pradyumna rūpagal̤a

irul̤u hagalu upāsanava gaivutalè modiparu||29||

kṛta pratīkadi ṭaṃki bhārgava hutavaha anila mukhya divijaru

tutisikòl̤uta abhimānigal̤u tāvāgi nèlèsiddu

prati divasa śrī tul̤asi gaṃdhākṣatè kusuma phala dīpa paṃcāmṛtadi

pūjipa bhakutarigè kòdutihanu puruṣārtha||30||

nagagal̤a abhimānigal̤u ènipa surarugal̤u

sahaja acalagal̤igè māṇigal̤u ènisi śrī vāsudevana pūjisutali iharu

svāgata bheda vivarjitana nālbagè pratīkadi til̤idu pūjisè

vigata saṃsārābdhi dāṭisi muktaranu māl̤pa||31||

āva kṣetrakè podarenu? innāva tīrthadi mul̤ugalenu?

innāva japa tapa homa dānava māḍi phalavenu?

śrīvara jagannātha viṭhala ī vidhadi jaṃgama sthāvara jīvaròl̤u

paripūrṇanèṃdu ariyadiha mānavanu||32||
