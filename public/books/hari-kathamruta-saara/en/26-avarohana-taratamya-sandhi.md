---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಅವರೋಹಣ ತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಸಂಧಿ/Avarohana taratamya sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè

parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

śrīramaṇa nijabhaktarènisuva vārijāsana mukhya nirjara

tāratamyava pel̤vè saṃkṣepadali gurubaladi||

keśavagè nārāyaṇagè kamalāsana samīrarigè vāṇigè

vīśa phaṇipa maheśarigè ṣaṇmahiṣiyara padakè

śeṣa rudrara patniyarigè suvāsada pradyumnarigè

saṃtoṣadali vaṃdisuvè bhakti jñāna kòḍalèṃdu||1||

prāṇadevagè namipè kāmana sūnu manu guru dakṣa śaci rati māniniyarigè

pravaha devagè sūrya soma yama mānavigè varuṇanigè

vīṇā pāṇi nārada munigè napisuvè

jñāna bhakti virakti mārgava til̤isali ènagèṃdu||2||

anal̤a bhṛgu dākṣāyaṇiyarigè kanaka garbhaja saptarṣigal̤igè èṇèyènipa

vaivasvata manu gādhi saṃbhavagè

danuja nirṛti tāra prāvahi vanaja mitragè aśvinī gaṇapā

dhanapa viśvaksenarigè vaṃdisuvènu anavarata||3||

ukta devarkal̤anu ul̤idèṃbhattaidu janarugal̤u manugal̤u

cithya cāvana yamal̤arigè karmajaru ènisutippa

kārtavīryārjuna pramukha śatastharigè parjanya gaṃga

āditya yama soma aniruddhara patniyara padakè||4||

hutavahana ardhāṃginigè caṃdrama suta budhagè nāmātmika uṣā satigè

chāyātmaja śanaiścaragè anamipè satata

prati divasadali biḍadè jīva pratati māḍuva karmagal̤igè

adhipatiyu ènipa puṣkarana pādāṃbujagal̤igè namipè||5||

ā namipè ajānajarigè sukṛśānu sutarigè govrajadòl̤iha

māniniyarigè cirapitaru śatanūna śatakoṭi mauni janarigè

devamānava gāna prauḍharigè avaniparigè

ramā nivāsana dāsavargakè namipènu anavarata||6||

anukramaṇika tāratamyava anudinadi sadbhakti pūrvaka nènèvarigè

dharmārtha kāmādigal̤u phalisuvavu

vanaja saṃbhava mukhyaru avayavaru ènisuva jagannātha viṭhalana

vinayadiṃdali namisi kòṃḍāḍutiru marèyadalè||7||
