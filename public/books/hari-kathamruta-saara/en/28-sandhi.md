---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ವಿಘ್ನೇಶ್ವರ ಸ್ತೋತ್ರ ಸಂಧಿ/ಶ್ರೀ ಗಣಪತಿ ಸ್ತೋತ್ರ ಸಂಧಿ"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu pel̤uvè<br/>parama bhagavad bhaktaridanādaradi kel̤uvudu ||

śrīśanaṃghrisarojabhṛṃga maheśasaṃbhava manmanadòl̤u<br/>prakāśisanudina prārthisuvè premātiśayadiṃda<br/>nī salahu sajjanara veda vyāsa karuṇāpātra<br/>mahadākāśapati karuṇāl̤u kaipiḍidèmmanuddharisu ||1||

ekadaṃta ibheṃdramukha cāmīkarakṛtabhūṣaṇāṃga<br/>kṛpākaṭākṣadi noḍu vijñāpisuvèninitèṃdu<br/>nokanīyana tutisutippa vivekigal̤a sahavāsa sukhagal̤a<br/>nī karuṇisuvudèmagè saṃtata paramakaruṇāl̤u ||2||

vighnarājanè durviṣayadòl̤u magnavāgiha manava mahado ṣaghna naṃghrisarojayugal̤adi bhaktipūrvakadi<br/>lagnavāgali nityānarakabhayāgnigal̤igānaṃjè guruvara bhagnagaisènnavaguṇagal̤anu pratidivasadalli ||3||<br/>dhanapa viṣvaksena vaidyāśvinigal̤igè sariyènipa ṣaṇmukhananuja śeṣaśatasthadevottama viyadgaṃgā|<br/>vinuta viśvopāsakanè sanmanadi vijñāpisuvè<br/>lakumīvanitèyarasana bhaktijñānava kòṭṭu saluhuvudu ||4||

cārudeṣṇāhvayanènisi avatāramāḍidè rukmiṇīyali<br/>gauriyarasana varadi uddhaṭarāda rākṣasara|<br/>śauriyājñadi saṃharisi bhūbhāravil̤uhida karuṇi<br/>tvatpā dāraviṃdakè namipè karuṇipudèmagè sanmatiya||5||

śūrpakarṇa virājiteṃduva darpahara uditārka sannibha<br/>sarpavara kaṭisūtra vaikṛtagātra sucaritra|<br/>svarpitāṃkuśapāśakara khal̤a darpabhaṃjana karmasākṣiga<br/>tarpakanu nīnāgi tṛptiya baḍisu sajjanara ||6||

kheśaparamasubhaktipūrvaka vyāsakṛtagraṃthagal̤anaritu<br/>prayāsavilladè barèdu vistarisidèyò lokadòl̤u|<br/>pāśapāṇiyè prārthisuvènupadeśisènagadararthagal̤a<br/>karuṇāsamudra kṛpākaṭākṣadi noḍi pratidinadi||7||<br/>śrīśanatinirmalasunābhīdeśavasthita raktaśṛṃgaga<br/>dāsuśobhitagātra lokapavitra suramitra|<br/>mūṣakāsura vāhana prāṇāveśayuta prakhyāta<br/>prabhu pūraisu bhaktaru beḍidiṣṭārthagal̤a prati dinadi||8||

śaṃkarātmaja daityarigatibhayaṃkaragatigal̤īyalosuga saṃkaṭacaturthiganènisi ahitārthagal̤a kòṭṭu|<br/>maṃkugal̤a mohisuvè cakradharāṃkitanè dinadinadi<br/>tvatpada paṃkajagal̤igè èragi binnayisuvènu pālipudu||9||

siddhavidyādharagaṇasamā rādhya caraṇasaroja<br/>sarvasusiddhidāyaka śīghradiṃdālipudu binnapava|<br/>buddhividyājñānabala pariśuddhabhaktiviraktinirutana<br/>vadyana smṛtilīlègal̤a sustavana vadanadali ||10||

raktavāsadvayavibhūṣaṇa uktilālisu parama<br/>bhagavadbhaktavara bhavyātma bhāgavatādiśāstradali|<br/>saktavāgali manavu viṣaya virakti pālisu vidvadādya vimuktanèṃdènisènna bhavabhayadiṃda karuṇadali ||11||

śukraśiṣyara saṃharipudakè śakra ninnanu pūjisidanu<br/>urukrama śrīrāmacaṃdranu setumukhadalli|<br/>cakravarti dharmarājanu cakrapāṇiya nuḍigè bhajisida<br/>vakratuṃḍanè ninnòl̤èṃtuṃṭo īśananugrahavu||12||

kauraveṃdranu ninna bhajisada kāraṇadi nijakulasahita<br/>saṃhāravaidida guruvara vṛkodarana gadèyiṃda|<br/>tārakāṃtakananuja ènna śarīradòl̤u nīniṃtu dharma<br/>prerakanu nīnāgi saṃtaisènna karuṇadali||13||

ekaviṃśati modakapriya mūkaranu vāgmigal̤a māl̤pa<br/>kṛpākareśa kṛtajña kāmada kāyò kaiviḍidu|<br/>lekhakāgraṇi manmanada durvyākulava pariharisu dayadi pinākibhāryātanuja mṛdbhava prārthisuvè ninna||14||

nityamaṃgalacarita jagadutpattisthitilayaniyamana<br/>jñānatrayaprada baṇdhmocaka sumanasāsurara |<br/>cittavṛttigal̤aṃtè naḍèva pramattanalla suhṛjjanāptana<br/>nityadali nènènènèdu sukhisuva bhāgya karuṇipudu||15||<br/>paṃcabhedajñānavarupu viriṃcijanakana toru manadali<br/>vāṃchitaprada òlumèyiṃdali dāsanèṃdaridu|<br/>paṃcavaktrana tanaya bhavadòl̤u vaṃcisadè saṃtayisu viṣayadi saṃcarisaṃdadali māḍu manādikaraṇagal̤a||16||

enu beḍuvudilla ninna kuyonigal̤u baralaṃjè lakṣmī prāṇapatitattveśariṃdòḍagūḍi guṇakārya|<br/>tānè māḍuvanèṃba ī sujñānavè karuṇisuvudèmagè<br/>mahānubhāva muhurmuhu prārthisuvèninitèṃdu||17||

namo namo guruvarya vibudhottama vivarjitanidrakalpa<br/>drumanènipè bhajakarigè bahuguṇabharita śubhacarita|<br/>umèya naṃdana pariharisahaṃ mamatè buddhyādiṃdriya<br/>gal̤ā kramisi daṇisutalihavu bhavadòl̤agāvakāladali ||18||

jayajayatu vighneśa tāpa trayavināśana viśvamaṃgal̤a<br/>jayajayatu vidyāpradāyaka vītabhayaśoka|<br/>jayajayatu cārvāṃga karuṇānayanadiṃdali noḍi<br/>janmāmayamṛtigal̤anu pariharisu bhaktarigè bhavadòl̤agè||19||

kaḍukaruṇi nīnèṃdaridu heròḍala namisuvè ninnaḍigè<br/>bèṃbiḍadè pālisu parama karuṇāsiṃdhu èṃdèṃdu|<br/>naḍunaḍuvè barutippa vighnava taḍèdu bhagavannāmakīrtanè<br/>nuḍinuḍigè ènniṃda pratidivasadali marèyadalè||20||

ekaviṃśati padagal̤ènisuva kokanada navamālikèya<br/>mainākitanayāṃtargata śrīprāṇapatiyènipa|<br/>śrīkarajagannāthaviṭhṭhala svīkarisi svargāpavargadi<br/>tā kòḍuva saukhyagal̤a bhakutarigāva kāladali||21||
