---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ವಿಘ್ನೇಶ್ವರ ಸ್ತೋತ್ರ ಸಂಧಿ/ಶ್ರೀ ಗಣಪತಿ ಸ್ತೋತ್ರ ಸಂಧಿ"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu pel̤uvè

parama bhagavad bhaktaridanādaradi kel̤uvudu ||

śrīśanaṃghrisarojabhṛṃga maheśasaṃbhava manmanadòl̤u

prakāśisanudina prārthisuvè premātiśayadiṃda

nī salahu sajjanara veda vyāsa karuṇāpātra

mahadākāśapati karuṇāl̤u kaipiḍidèmmanuddharisu ||1||

ekadaṃta ibheṃdramukha cāmīkarakṛtabhūṣaṇāṃga

kṛpākaṭākṣadi noḍu vijñāpisuvèninitèṃdu

nokanīyana tutisutippa vivekigal̤a sahavāsa sukhagal̤a

nī karuṇisuvudèmagè saṃtata paramakaruṇāl̤u ||2||

vighnarājanè durviṣayadòl̤u magnavāgiha manava mahado ṣaghna naṃghrisarojayugal̤adi bhaktipūrvakadi

lagnavāgali nityānarakabhayāgnigal̤igānaṃjè guruvara bhagnagaisènnavaguṇagal̤anu pratidivasadalli ||3||

dhanapa viṣvaksena vaidyāśvinigal̤igè sariyènipa ṣaṇmukhananuja śeṣaśatasthadevottama viyadgaṃgā|

vinuta viśvopāsakanè sanmanadi vijñāpisuvè

lakumīvanitèyarasana bhaktijñānava kòṭṭu saluhuvudu ||4||

cārudeṣṇāhvayanènisi avatāramāḍidè rukmiṇīyali

gauriyarasana varadi uddhaṭarāda rākṣasara|

śauriyājñadi saṃharisi bhūbhāravil̤uhida karuṇi

tvatpā dāraviṃdakè namipè karuṇipudèmagè sanmatiya||5||

śūrpakarṇa virājiteṃduva darpahara uditārka sannibha

sarpavara kaṭisūtra vaikṛtagātra sucaritra|

svarpitāṃkuśapāśakara khal̤a darpabhaṃjana karmasākṣiga

tarpakanu nīnāgi tṛptiya baḍisu sajjanara ||6||

kheśaparamasubhaktipūrvaka vyāsakṛtagraṃthagal̤anaritu

prayāsavilladè barèdu vistarisidèyò lokadòl̤u|

pāśapāṇiyè prārthisuvènupadeśisènagadararthagal̤a

karuṇāsamudra kṛpākaṭākṣadi noḍi pratidinadi||7||

śrīśanatinirmalasunābhīdeśavasthita raktaśṛṃgaga

dāsuśobhitagātra lokapavitra suramitra|

mūṣakāsura vāhana prāṇāveśayuta prakhyāta

prabhu pūraisu bhaktaru beḍidiṣṭārthagal̤a prati dinadi||8||

śaṃkarātmaja daityarigatibhayaṃkaragatigal̤īyalosuga saṃkaṭacaturthiganènisi ahitārthagal̤a kòṭṭu|

maṃkugal̤a mohisuvè cakradharāṃkitanè dinadinadi

tvatpada paṃkajagal̤igè èragi binnayisuvènu pālipudu||9||

siddhavidyādharagaṇasamā rādhya caraṇasaroja

sarvasusiddhidāyaka śīghradiṃdālipudu binnapava|

buddhividyājñānabala pariśuddhabhaktiviraktinirutana

vadyana smṛtilīlègal̤a sustavana vadanadali ||10||

raktavāsadvayavibhūṣaṇa uktilālisu parama

bhagavadbhaktavara bhavyātma bhāgavatādiśāstradali|

saktavāgali manavu viṣaya virakti pālisu vidvadādya vimuktanèṃdènisènna bhavabhayadiṃda karuṇadali ||11||

śukraśiṣyara saṃharipudakè śakra ninnanu pūjisidanu

urukrama śrīrāmacaṃdranu setumukhadalli|

cakravarti dharmarājanu cakrapāṇiya nuḍigè bhajisida

vakratuṃḍanè ninnòl̤èṃtuṃṭo īśananugrahavu||12||

kauraveṃdranu ninna bhajisada kāraṇadi nijakulasahita

saṃhāravaidida guruvara vṛkodarana gadèyiṃda|

tārakāṃtakananuja ènna śarīradòl̤u nīniṃtu dharma

prerakanu nīnāgi saṃtaisènna karuṇadali||13||

ekaviṃśati modakapriya mūkaranu vāgmigal̤a māl̤pa

kṛpākareśa kṛtajña kāmada kāyò kaiviḍidu|

lekhakāgraṇi manmanada durvyākulava pariharisu dayadi pinākibhāryātanuja mṛdbhava prārthisuvè ninna||14||

nityamaṃgalacarita jagadutpattisthitilayaniyamana

jñānatrayaprada baṇdhmocaka sumanasāsurara |

cittavṛttigal̤aṃtè naḍèva pramattanalla suhṛjjanāptana

nityadali nènènènèdu sukhisuva bhāgya karuṇipudu||15||

paṃcabhedajñānavarupu viriṃcijanakana toru manadali

vāṃchitaprada òlumèyiṃdali dāsanèṃdaridu|

paṃcavaktrana tanaya bhavadòl̤u vaṃcisadè saṃtayisu viṣayadi saṃcarisaṃdadali māḍu manādikaraṇagal̤a||16||

enu beḍuvudilla ninna kuyonigal̤u baralaṃjè lakṣmī prāṇapatitattveśariṃdòḍagūḍi guṇakārya|

tānè māḍuvanèṃba ī sujñānavè karuṇisuvudèmagè

mahānubhāva muhurmuhu prārthisuvèninitèṃdu||17||

namo namo guruvarya vibudhottama vivarjitanidrakalpa

drumanènipè bhajakarigè bahuguṇabharita śubhacarita|

umèya naṃdana pariharisahaṃ mamatè buddhyādiṃdriya

gal̤ā kramisi daṇisutalihavu bhavadòl̤agāvakāladali ||18||

jayajayatu vighneśa tāpa trayavināśana viśvamaṃgal̤a

jayajayatu vidyāpradāyaka vītabhayaśoka|

jayajayatu cārvāṃga karuṇānayanadiṃdali noḍi

janmāmayamṛtigal̤anu pariharisu bhaktarigè bhavadòl̤agè||19||

kaḍukaruṇi nīnèṃdaridu heròḍala namisuvè ninnaḍigè

bèṃbiḍadè pālisu parama karuṇāsiṃdhu èṃdèṃdu|

naḍunaḍuvè barutippa vighnava taḍèdu bhagavannāmakīrtanè

nuḍinuḍigè ènniṃda pratidivasadali marèyadalè||20||

ekaviṃśati padagal̤ènisuva kokanada navamālikèya

mainākitanayāṃtargata śrīprāṇapatiyènipa|

śrīkarajagannāthaviṭhṭhala svīkarisi svargāpavargadi

tā kòḍuva saukhyagal̤a bhakutarigāva kāladali||21||
