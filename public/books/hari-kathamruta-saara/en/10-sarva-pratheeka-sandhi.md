---
title: "ಸರ್ವಪ್ರತೀಕ ಸಂಧಿ /Sarva Pratheeka sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè

parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

āva parabòmmana ativimala aṃgāva baddharu èṃdènipa

rājīvabhava mòdalāda amararu anudinadi haripadava seviparigè

anukūlaralladè tāvu ivaranu kèḍisaballarè

śrīvilāsāspadana dāsarigè uṃṭè apajayavu||1||

śrīdana aṃghri sarojayugal̤a ekādaśa sthāna ātmadòl̤agiṭṭu

ādaradi saṃtutisi higguvarigè ī navagrahavu

āditeyaru saṃtatādhi vyādhigal̤a pariharisuta avarana

kādukòṃḍiharu èllaròṃdāgi īśana ājñèyali||2||

mediniya melul̤l̤a goṣpāda udakagal̤u èlla āmala tīrthavu

pādapādri dharātal̤avè sukṣetra jīvagaṇa śrīdana pratimègal̤u

avaruṃba odanave naivedya

nityadi hādi naḍèvude nartanagal̤u èṃdaritavanè yogi||3||

sarva deśavu puṇya deśavu sarva kālavu parva kālavu

sarva jīvaru dāna pātraru mūru lokadòl̤u

sarva mātugal̤u èlla maṃtravu sarva kèlasagal̤u èlla pūjèyu

śarva vaṃdyana vimala mūrti dhyānavul̤l̤avagè||4||

devakhāta taṭākavāpi sarovaragal̤a abhimāni suraru

kal̤evaradòl̤agiha roma kūpagal̤òl̤agè tuṃbiharu

ā viyatgaṃgādi nadigal̤u bhāvisuvudu èppattèraḍènipa sāvira

sunāḍigal̤òl̤agè pravahisutalihavu èṃdu||5||

mūru koṭiya melè śobhipa īru adhika èppattu sāvira

māruta aṃtaryāmi mādhava prati divasadalli tā ramisutihanèṃdu

til̤idiha sūrigal̤u devatègal̤u

avara śarīrave sukṣetra avara arcanèye haripūjè||6||

śrīvaranigè abhiṣekavèṃdaridu ī vasuṃdharèyòl̤agè ballavaru

āva jaladali miṃdarèyu gaṃgādi tīrthagal̤u tā òlidu baṃdalli

nèlègòṃḍu īvaru akhil̤ārthagal̤anu

ariyada jīvaru amara taraṃgiṇiyanu aididaru phalavenu||7||

nadanadigal̤u il̤èyòl̤agè parivavu udadhi pariyaṃtaradi

taruvāyadali ramisuvavu alli tanmayavāgi toradalè

vidhi niṣedhagal̤u ācarisuvaru budharu

bhagavadrūpa sarvatradali ciṃtanè baralu tyajisuvaru akhil̤a karmagal̤a||8||

kaliyè mòdalāda akhil̤a dānavaròl̤agè

brahma bhavādi devarkal̤u niyāmakarāgi hariyājñèyali

avaravara kaluṣa karmava māḍi māḍisi

jalaruhekṣaṇagè arpisuta niścala subhakti jñāna pūrṇaru sukhiparu avaròl̤agè||9||

āva jīvaròl̤iddarenu? innāva karmava māḍalenu?

innāva guṇa rūpagal̤a upāsanè māḍalenavaru?

kāvanayyana parama kāruṇya avalokana baladi carisuva

devatègal̤anu muṭṭalu āpavè pāpa karmagal̤u||10||

patiyòḍanè manabaṃda tèradali prati divasadali ramisi modisi

sutara paḍèdu il̤èyòl̤u jita iṃdriyal̤u èṃdu karèsuval̤u

kṛti pati kathāmṛta subhojana rata mahātmarigè

itara doṣa pratatigal̤u saṃbaṃdhisuvavenu acyutana dāsarigè||11||

sūsibaha nadiyòl̤agè tanna sahāsa toruvènènuta

jalakèduru īsidarè kaisotu mul̤uguva

hariya biṭṭavanu kleśavaiduva anādiyali sārveśa kluptiya māḍiduda biṭṭu

āśèyiṃdali anyara ārādhisuva mānavanu||12||

nānu nannadu èṃba jaḍamati mānavanu dinadinadi māḍuva

snāna japa devārcanèye mòdalāda karmagal̤a

dānavaru sèl̤èdòyvaralladè śrīnivāsanu svīkarisa

maddānè pakva kapittha phala bhakṣisida volahudu||13||

dhātriyòl̤agul̤l̤a akhil̤a tīrthakṣetra carisidarenu

pātrāpātravaritu annādi dānava māḍi phalavenu

gātra nirmalanāgi maṃtra stotra paṭhisidaru enu

hari sarvatragatanu èṃdariyadè tā kartṛ èṃbuvanu||14||

kaṃḍa nīròl̤u mul̤ugi dehava daṃḍisida phalavenu

daṃḍa kamaṃḍalaogal̤a dharisi yatiyèṃdènisi phalavenu

aṃḍajādhipanu aṃsagana pada puṃḍarīkadi manavaharniśi

baṃḍuṇiyavol irisi sukhapaḍadè ippa mānavanu||15||

veda śāstra purāṇa kathègal̤a odi pel̤idarenu

sakala niṣedha karmagal̤a tòrèdu satkarmagal̤a māḍi enu

odanaṃgal̤a jaridu śvāsa nirodhagaisidarenu

kāma krodhava al̤iyadè nānu nannadèṃba mānavanu||16||

enu kel̤idarenu noḍidarenu odidarenu

pel̤idarenu pāḍidarenu māḍidarenu dinadinadi

śrīnivāsana janma karma sadā anurāgadi nènèdu

tattat sthānadali tadrūpa tannāmakana smarisadava||17||

buddhi vidyābaladi pel̤ida aśuddha kāvyavu idalla

tatva supaddhatigal̤anu til̤ida mānavanu alla budhariṃda

madhva vallabha tāne hṛdayadòl̤u iddu nuḍidaṃdadali nuḍidènu

apaddhagal̤a noḍadalè kivigòṭṭu ālipudu budharu||18||

kabbinòl̤agiha rasa vidaṃtigè abbu balludè

bhāgya yauvana mabbinali mai marèdavagè hari sucaritāmṛtavu labhyavāgadu

haripadābjadi habbida ati sadbhakti rasa

uṃḍu ubbi kòbbi sukhābdhiyòl̤agè āḍuvavagè alladalè||19||

khagavaradhvajana aṃghri bhakutiya bagèyanu ariyada mānavarigidu

ògaṭinaṃdadi torutippadu èlla kāladali

triguṇa varjitana amala guṇagal̤a pògal̤i higguva bhāgavatarigè

migè bhakuti vijñāna sukhavittu avara rakṣipudu||20||

parama tatva rahasyavu idu bhūsuraru kel̤uvudu sādaradi

niṣṭhurigal̤igè mūḍharigè paṃḍita māni piśunarigè

arasikarigè idu pel̤vudalla anavarata bhagavatpāda

yugal̤a aṃburuha madhukaranu ènisuvavarigè idanu arupu modadali||21||

lokavārtèyi dalla paralo kaikanāthana vārtè kel̤varè |

kāku manujari gidu samarpaka vāgi sògasuvudè ||

kokanada pari mal̤avu ṣaṭpada svīkarisuvaṃ dadali

jalacara bheka balladè idara rasa hari bhaktagalladalè||22||

svaprayojanarahita sakaleṣṭa pradāyaka sarvaguṇa pūrṇa prameya

jarāmaraṇa varjita vigata kleśa vipratama viśvātma

ghruṇi sūrya prakāśa anaṃta mahima

ghṛta pratīka ārādhita aṃghri saroja surarāja||23||

vanacara adri dharā dharanè jaya manuja mṛgavara veṣa jaya

vāmana trivikrama deva jaya bhṛgu rāma bhūma jaya

janakajā vallabhanè jaya rugmiṇi manoratha siddhidāyaka

jina vimohaka kalividāraṇa jaya jayāramaṇa||24||

saccidānaṃdātma brahma karārcita aṃghri saroja

sumanasa procca sanmaṃgal̤ada madhva aṃtaḥkaraṇarūḍha

acyuta jagannātha viṭhala nicca nèccida janara biḍa

kāḍgiccanu uṃḍu araṇyadòl̤u go goparanu kāyda||25||
