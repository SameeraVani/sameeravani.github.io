---
title: "ಶ್ರೀ ಸ್ವಗತಸ್ವಾತಂತ್ರ್ಯ ಸಂಧಿ / Swagathaswantrya sandhi"
type: "Hari Kathamruta Saara"
language: "English"
---

harikathāmṛtasāra gurugal̤a karuṇadiṃdāpanitu kel̤uvè

parama bhagavadbhaktaru idanādaradi kel̤uvudu||

svagatasvātaṃtrya guṇava hari tègèdu brahmādyarigè kòṭṭadu

bhṛgu munipa pel̤idanu iṃdradyumna kṣitipanigè||

parama viṣṇu svataṃtra māyā taruṇi vakṣasthal̤a nivāsi

sarasijodbhava prāṇarīrvaru sacivaru ènisuvaru

sarva karmagal̤alli tatpriyaru uraga bhūṣaṇa ahaṃkṛtitraya

karèsuva amareṃdra arka mukharu iṃdriyaparu ènisuvaru||1||

ī divaukasara aṃtè kali mòdalāda daityaru sarva dehadi todakaru tāvāgi

vyāpāragal̤a māḍuvaru

vedhanāṃdadi kaliyu ahaṃkārādhipa adhama madhukukaiṭabha

krodhiśaṃbhara mukharu manasigè svāmiyènisuvaru||2||

devatègal̤a upādhi nityadi evamādi nāmadiṃdali

yāvadiṃdriyagal̤ali vyāpāragal̤a māḍuvaru

sevakara seva anuguṇa phalavīva nrupanaṃdadali

tanna svabhāva svātaṃtrya vibhāgava māḍikòṭṭa hari||3||

agaṇita svātaṃtryava nālbagè vibhāgava māḍi

òṃdanutègèdu daśavidhagaisi pādarè paṃca prāṇanali

mòga catuṣṭayanòl̤u sapādaidu guṇavirasida

mattè daśa vidhayugal̤a guṇavannu māḍi èraḍu sadā śivanòl̤iṭṭa||4||

pākaśāsana kāmaròl̤u sārdhaikaviṭṭa daśa iṃdriyara

sudivaukasādyaròl̤òṃdu yāvajjīvaròl̤agòṃdu

nālkūvarè kalyādi daityānīkakittanu

èraḍu trividha vivekagaisi iṃdiragòṃdu èraḍu ātma tannòl̤agè||5||

ī vidhadi svātaṃtryava deva mānava dānavaròl̤u

ramā vinodi vibhāga māḍiṭṭu alle ramisuvanu

mūvaròl̤agiddu avara karmava tā vikāravagaisadalè

kalpavasānakè kòḍuvanu anāyāsa avara gatiya||6||

ālayagal̤òl̤ippa dīpa jvālè vartigal̤anusarisi janara āligòppuva tèradi

hari tā torpa sarvatra

kāla kāladi śrīdharā durgā lalanèyara kūḍi sukhamaya līlègaiyalu

triguṇa kāryagal̤ihavu jīvarigè||7||

iṃdriyagal̤iṃ māl̤pa karma dvaṃdvagal̤a tanagarpisalu

goviṃda puṇyava kòṃḍu pāpava bhasmavanè māl̤pa

iṃdireśanu bhakta janaranu niṃdisuvaròl̤agippa

puṇyava taṃdu tannavagīva pāpagal̤a avariguṇisuvanu||8||

hòttu òṭṭigè pāpa karma pravartakara niṃdisadè

tanagiṃda uttamara guṇa karmagal̤a kòṃḍāḍadalippa martyarigè

gobrāhmaṇa strī hatya mòdalāda akhil̤a doṣagal̤ittapanu

saṃdeha paḍasalla akhil̤a śāstramata||9||

tanna svātaṃtrya guṇagal̤a hiraṇya garbhādyarigè

kalimukha dānavara saṃtatigè avaradhikārava anusarisi

puṇya pāpagal̤īva bahu kāruṇya sāgaranu

alpaśaktigal̤a uṇṇalariyadiralu uṇa kalisidanu jīvarigè||10||

satya vikrama puṇya pāpa samastarigè kòḍalosugadi

nālvattu bhāgava māḍi leśāṃśavanu janakīva

atyalpa paramāṇu jīvagè sāmarthyavanu tā kòṭṭu

sthūla padārthagal̤a uṃḍu uṇipa sarvadāsarva jīvarigè||11||

timira taraṇigal̤u eka deśadi samanisippavè èṃdigādaru?

bhramaṇa chal̤i bisalu aṃjikègal̤uṃṭeno parvatakè?

amita jīvaròl̤iddu lakṣmī ramaṇa vyāpāragal̤a māḍuva

kamalapatra sarovaragal̤òl̤agippa tèradaṃtè||12||

aṃbujodbhava mukhya surā kali śaṃbarādi samasta daitya kadaṃbakè

anudina puṇya pāpa vibhāgavanè māḍi

aṃbudhiya jalavanu mahadghaṭa diṃbanitu tuṃbuva tèradi

prati biṃbaròl̤u tāniddu yogyatèyaṃtè phalavīva||13||

initu viṣṇu rahasyadòl̤u bhrumu munipa iṃdradyumnagè arupidadanu

budharu kel̤uvudu nityadi matsarava biṭṭu

anucitoktigal̤iddarèyu sariyè gaṇanè māḍadirèṃdu

vidvad janakè vijñāpanèya māḍuvè vinaya pūrvakadi||13||

veta bhaya viśveśa vidhi pita mātul̤āṃtaka madhva vallabha

bhūta bhāvana anaṃta bhāskara teja mahārāja

gautamana maḍadiyanu kāyda anātha rakṣaka

gurutama jagannātha viṭhala tanna naṃbida bhakutaranu pòrèva||14||
